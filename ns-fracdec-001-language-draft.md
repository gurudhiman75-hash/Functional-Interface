## Explanation Candidate ES-001

Find the HCF of the numerator and denominator.

Divide both by this HCF.

The fraction is now in lowest terms.

## Explanation Candidate ES-002

For an improper fraction, divide the numerator by the denominator.

The quotient becomes the whole part.

The remainder becomes the numerator.

For a mixed fraction, multiply the whole part by the denominator and add the numerator.
