Q1. If \(8:13 = 24:x\), find \(x\).
A. 15
B. 39
C. 37
D. 24
Answer: 39
Difficulty: Easy
Family: rp_missing_term_proportion
Explanation:
Write the proportion as fractions.

\[
\frac{8}{13}=\frac{24}{x}
\]

Cross-multiply.

\[
8x=13\times 24
\]

Solve for the missing term.

\[
x=\frac{13\times 24}{8}=39
\]

Answer: 39

Q2. The incomes of A and B are in the ratio \(4:5\). A's income increases by 50% and B's income increases by 20%. Find the new ratio.
A. 480:750
B. 4:5
C. 1:1
D. 54:25
Answer: 1:1
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_ratio_after_increase
Explanation:
Let the original incomes be ratio parts.

\[
A=4,\quad B=5
\]

Apply the percentage increases.

\[
A':B'=4\left(1+\frac{50}{100}\right):5\left(1+\frac{20}{100}\right)
\]

Simplify the new ratio.

\[
A':B'=600:600=1:1
\]

Answer: 1:1

Q5. \(x\) varies directly as \(y\) and inversely as \(z\). If \(x=384\) when \(y=8\) and \(z=3\), find \(x\) when \(y=12\) and \(z=6\).
A. 1152
B. 384
C. 576
D. 288
Answer: 288
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=384\times \frac{12}{8}\times \frac{3}{6}=288
\]

Answer: 288
