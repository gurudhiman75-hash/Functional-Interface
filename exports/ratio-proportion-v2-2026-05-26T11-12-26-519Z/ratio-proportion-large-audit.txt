Q1. The sides of two similar figures are in the ratio \(10:11\). Find the ratio of their areas.
A. 121:100
B. 1000:1331
C. 100:121
D. 10:11
Answer: 100:121
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_side_area_volume_ratio
Explanation:
Start with the side ratio.

\[
\text{Side ratio}=10:11
\]

Area ratio is the square of side ratio.

\[
\text{Area ratio}=10^{2}:11^{2}=100:121
\]

Answer: 100:121

Q2. A's salary and B's salary are in the ratio \(3:5\). B earns ₹600 more than A. Find A's salary.
A. ₹600
B. ₹900
C. ₹300
D. ₹1500
Answer: ₹900
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_difference_based_ratio_recovery
Explanation:
Let the salaries be in ratio form.

\[
A=3k,\quad B=5k
\]

Use the salary difference.

\[
5k-3k=600
\]

Solve for the multiplier.

\[
2k=600,\quad k=300
\]

Compute the required value.

\[
A=3k=900
\]

Answer: ₹900

C. 600:840
D. 5:7
Answer: 5:7

Family: rp_ratio_after_increase

\[
A=5,\quad B=7
\]

Apply the percentage increases.

\[
A':B'=5\left(1+\frac{20}{100}\right):7\left(1+\frac{20}{100}\right)

Simplify the new ratio.

\[

Q4. A and B have money in the ratio \(7:5\). After ₹600 is given by A to B, the ratio becomes \(13:11\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹7800
B. ₹6000
C. ₹9000
D. ₹8400
Answer: ₹8400
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=7k,\quad B=5k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{7k-600}{5k+600}=\frac{13}{11}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
11(7k-600)=13(5k+600)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
12k=14400,\quad k=1200
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=7k=7\times 1200=8400
\]

Answer: ₹8400

Q5. \(x\) varies directly as \(y\) and inversely as \(z\). If \(x=120\) when \(y=6\) and \(z=4\), find \(x\) when \(y=10\) and \(z=5\).
A. 120
B. 250
C. 200
D. 160
Answer: 160
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=120\times \frac{10}{6}\times \frac{4}{5}=160
\]

Answer: 160

Q8. 16 workers finish a work in 36 days. If the number of workers is 24, how many days will be required?
A. 24
B. 36
C. 54
D. 8
Answer: 24
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_inverse_variation_basic
Explanation:
Workers and days vary inversely.

\[
W_1D_1=W_2D_2
\]

Substitute the values.

\[
16\times 36=24\times D_2
\]

Solve for the days.

\[
D_2=\frac{16\times 36}{24}=24
\]

Answer: 24
