Q1. The present ages of A and B are in the ratio \(5:8\). After 35 years, the ratio will be \(2:3\). Find A's present age.
A. 280 years
B. 175 years
C. 35 years
D. 210 years
Answer: 175 years
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_age_future_ratio
Explanation:
Let present ages be ratio parts.

\[
A=5k,\quad B=8k
\]

Use the future-age ratio.

\[
\frac{5k+35}{8k+35}=\frac{2}{3}
\]

Solve for the multiplier.

\[
-1k=-35,\quad k=35
\]

Find A's present age.

\[
A=5k=175
\]

Answer: 175 years

Q3. \(x\) varies directly as \(y\) and inversely as \(z\). If \(x=120\) when \(y=6\) and \(z=4\), find \(x\) when \(y=10\) and \(z=5\).
A. 250
B. 120
C. 200
D. 160
Answer: 160
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=120\times \frac{10}{6}\times \frac{4}{5}=160
\]

Answer: 160

B. ₹27000
C. ₹33000
D. ₹39000
Answer: ₹33000
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer

\[
A=11k,\quad B=7k
\]

\[
\frac{11k-6000}{7k+6000}=\frac{1}{1}

\[
1(11k-6000)=1(7k+6000)

Find the common multiplier.

\[
4k=12000,\quad k=3000

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=11k=11\times 3000=33000
\]

Answer: ₹33000
