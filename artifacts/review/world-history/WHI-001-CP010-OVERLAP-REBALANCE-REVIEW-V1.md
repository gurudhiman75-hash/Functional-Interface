### WHI-CP010-Q004 — easy

Which term describes Germany’s fast-moving assault using armoured units and air power in Poland in 1939?

- A. The Phoney War
- B. The Atlantic Charter
- C. Scorched-earth policy
- D. Blitzkrieg **(correct)**

**Explanation:** Blitzkrieg, or “lightning war,” combined rapid armoured advances with concentrated air power.

**Canonical fact:** The German invasion of Poland used Blitzkrieg tactics combining fast-moving armoured units with air power.
**Source locator:** Section “Invasion and Partition of Poland,” description of Blitzkrieg tactics
**Source ID:** CP010-S12

### WHI-CP010-Q005 — easy

What name was given to the period of limited fighting on the Western Front after Britain and France declared war in 1939?

- A. The Phoney War **(correct)**
- B. The Battle of Britain
- C. The Winter War
- D. The Blitz

**Explanation:** Although Britain and France had declared war, there was little action on the Western Front for several months. This period became known as the Phoney War.

**Canonical fact:** The early period of limited fighting on the Western Front after the September 1939 declarations became known as the Phoney War.
**Source locator:** Second World War galleries, section “How did the war spread across Europe?”
**Source ID:** CP010-S13

### WHI-CP010-Q006 — hard

On what date did besieged Warsaw surrender to German forces?

- A. 17 September 1939
- B. 28 September 1939 **(correct)**
- C. 3 September 1939
- D. 6 October 1939

**Explanation:** Warsaw surrendered on 28 September 1939 after heavy shelling and bombing during the German siege.

**Canonical fact:** Warsaw surrendered to German forces on 28 September 1939 after heavy shelling and bombing.
**Source locator:** Section “Invasion and Partition of Poland,” paragraph on the siege and surrender of Warsaw
**Source ID:** CP010-S12

### WHI-CP010-Q028 — medium

Which 1943 battle marked the failure of Germany’s last major offensive on the Eastern Front?

- A. The Battle of the Bulge
- B. The Battle of Midway
- C. The Second Battle of El Alamein
- D. The Battle of Kursk **(correct)**

**Explanation:** The German offensive at Kursk failed in July 1943. Soviet forces then kept the initiative on the Eastern Front.

**Canonical fact:** The German offensive at Kursk in July 1943 failed, after which Soviet forces retained the initiative on the Eastern Front.
**Source locator:** Section “World War II in Europe,” Eastern Front discussion of July 1943
**Source ID:** CP010-S02
