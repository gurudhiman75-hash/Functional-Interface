## Authoring and localization rules

- Every question must map to a named claim in this plan and cite its specific source IDs in the corpus. Add stronger sources for invention dates, named inventors, political-economy ideas and any detail not established by this source register.
- Keep stems concise and recognisable as SSC/Banking-style history questions. Use plausible historical distractors; avoid absurd options and generic lead-ins.
- Explanations must state the tested fact or relationship in plain language and address the specific question. Do not reuse one generic explanation across a batch.
- Keep the English question ID, correct answer position, difficulty, fact ID and meaning aligned in both localizations. Punjabi must be written as natural Punjabi, not Hindi wording converted to Gurmukhi.
- Preserve review-only flags. Do not set production registration or student publication to true as part of this rebuild.
