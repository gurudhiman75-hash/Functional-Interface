**Scope:** The Second World War, 1939–1945. Includes outbreak, Axis expansion, European and Pacific theatres, Holocaust, resistance, Allied strategy, surrender and consequences.
**Boundary:** CP009 owns the interwar period through the invasion of Poland. CP011 owns post-war institutions and the division of Germany.

## Review requirements

- Keep the European and Pacific timelines distinct where appropriate.
- Preserve the distinction between the Warsaw Ghetto Uprising (1943) and Warsaw Uprising (1944).
- Treat surrender announcements, signed instruments and effective dates as separate events.
- Describe the Holocaust as the genocide of European Jews by Nazi Germany and its collaborators; identify other persecuted groups without conflating their experiences.
- Do not imply that a single battle or one cause explains the outcome of the war.
- Keep post-war institutional detail and decolonization within CP011–CP013.
- Localize into Hindi and Punjabi only after English factual and editorial approval.
