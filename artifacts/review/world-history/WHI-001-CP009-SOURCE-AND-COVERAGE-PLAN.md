## Authoring and lifecycle rules

- Use exact dates and distinguish policy, agreement and military action.
- Do not state that the Depression alone caused Nazi growth or that appeasement alone caused war.
- Treat diplomatic documents as primary records with institutional perspectives; corroborate consequential claims.
