Which conclusion about the first circumnavigation follows from the expedition’s sequence?

A. Magellan began the voyage, died before its completion, and Elcano led the return to Spain
B. Magellan completed the return voyage after Elcano died
C. Columbus began the voyage and da Gama returned it to Spain
D. Elcano began the expedition in 1519 and Magellan returned in 1522

**Answer:** A. Magellan began the voyage, died before its completion, and Elcano led the return to Spain

**Explanation:** The expedition began under Magellan in 1519. After his death in 1521, Elcano led the surviving crew back to Spain in 1522.
