- Keep original 1945 Charter membership distinct from present membership.
- Distinguish Charter signing from entry into force.
- Do not describe the UN as a world government or treat General Assembly recommendations as universally binding.
- Distinguish the ICJ from criminal tribunals; distinguish the IMF from the IBRD.
- Keep occupation zones, Berlin sectors, and the two German states chronologically distinct.
- Do not introduce current office holders or changing membership data.
