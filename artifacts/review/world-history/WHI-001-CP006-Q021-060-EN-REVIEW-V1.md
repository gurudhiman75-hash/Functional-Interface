### WHI-CP006-Q021 · Easy · Movement and objective

Which economic arrangement helped link many German states before political unification?

- **A.** A customs union that reduced internal trade barriers
- **B.** A dynastic union that abolished all local governments
- **C.** A military alliance that included every European power
- **D.** A colonial federation centred on overseas territories
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The Zollverein encouraged economic cooperation by reducing customs barriers among participating German states; it did not itself create a single German nation-state.
**Sources:** CP006-S01, CP006-S02
