**Status:** Review-only candidate; not learner-deliverable.

## Why these changes

The prior pool used six questions for Ghana's independence milestone, including repeated recall of the year, colonial power and renaming. Several later questions also restated the 1994 South African election or the sovereignty/rights distinction. This revision keeps the distinct Ghana date and Nkrumah leadership items while adding missing countries and independence movements identified in the chapter-wide audit.

## Replacement items

### WHI-CP013-Q003 (hard)

Which territory became independent as the Federation of Nigeria on 1 October 1960?

- A. The Gold Coast
- B. The Federation of Nigeria **(correct)**
- C. Northern Rhodesia
- D. Tanganyika

**Explanation:** Nigeria became independent on 1 October 1960 after British rule. The country was recognized as the Federation of Nigeria.

**Source locator:** CP013-S15 — Recognition — U.S. Recognition of Nigerian Independence, 1960

### WHI-CP013-Q004 (medium)

Which pair of former Portuguese territories became independent in 1975?

- A. Angola and Mozambique **(correct)**
- B. Ghana and Nigeria
- C. Kenya and Tanzania
- D. Algeria and Morocco

**Explanation:** Angola became independent on 11 November 1975, and Mozambique on 25 June 1975.

**Source locator:** CP013-S16 — Angola: Recognition — U.S. Response to Angolan Independence, 1975; Mozambique: Recognition — U.S. Recognition of Mozambique, 1975; CP013-S17 — Angola: Recognition — U.S. Response to Angolan Independence, 1975; Mozambique: Recognition — U.S. Recognition of Mozambique, 1975

### WHI-CP013-Q005 (easy)

Which former British territory became independent as Zimbabwe in 1980?

- A. Zambia
- B. Zimbabwe **(correct)**
- C. Botswana
- D. Malawi

**Explanation:** Southern Rhodesia, previously under British sovereignty, attained independence as Zimbabwe in 1980.

**Source locator:** CP013-S18 — Recognition — U.S. Recognition of Zimbabwe’s Independence, 1980

### WHI-CP013-Q006 (medium)

Which United Nations operation supervised elections during Namibia’s transition to independence?

- A. ONUC
- B. UNTAG **(correct)**
- C. UNEF
- D. UNMOGIP

**Explanation:** The United Nations Transition Assistance Group (UNTAG) oversaw elections in the transition process before Namibia became independent in 1990.

**Source locator:** CP013-S19 — Paragraph beginning “On 1 April 1989”

### WHI-CP013-Q051 (hard)

**Source locator:** CP013-S16 — Summary paragraph on the 1974 coup and Alvor Accords

### WHI-CP013-Q053 (easy)

### WHI-CP013-Q054 (medium)
