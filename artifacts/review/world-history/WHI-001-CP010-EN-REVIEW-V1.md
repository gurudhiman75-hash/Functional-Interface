### WHI-CP010-Q001 · Easy · Outbreak and chronology

What event began the Second World War in Europe?

- **A.** Germany's invasion of Poland on 1 September 1939
- **B.** Germany's invasion of the Soviet Union in 1941
- **C.** Japan's attack on Pearl Harbor in 1941
