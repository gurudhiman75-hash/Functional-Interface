## Question and localization rules

- Author fact-specific exam stems, plausible distractors and short explanations tied to the tested fact. Remove generic lead-ins and filler phrasing.
- Give each question a stable English ID and fact ID; carry those unchanged into Hindi and Punjabi. Keep answer position and difficulty in parity across languages.
- Distribute correct positions without a repeating A-B-C-D sequence. Check for duplicate question intent within each checkpoint and across neighbouring checkpoints.
- Do not use a simplistic “single cause” explanation for industrialisation, nationalism, imperialism or the outbreak of war. State qualifications where a historical claim is debated or the treaty/conference had a narrower legal scope than later events.
- Punjabi must read naturally in Gurmukhi; do not transliterate Hindi constructions. Keep names, dates and established historical terms consistent.
- Add exact source IDs to each item. Where the sources above do not establish a named fact, add a stronger source rather than borrowing an unsupported claim.
- Keep every item review-only. Do not mark runtime registration, localization approval, or student publication complete before the corresponding checks and review.
