Q1. \(x\) varies directly as \(y\) and inversely as \(z\). If \(x=96\) when \(y=8\) and \(z=3\), find \(x\) when \(y=12\) and \(z=6\).
A. 288
B. 72
C. 96
D. 144
Answer: 72
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=96\times \frac{12}{8}\times \frac{3}{6}=72
\]

Answer: 72
