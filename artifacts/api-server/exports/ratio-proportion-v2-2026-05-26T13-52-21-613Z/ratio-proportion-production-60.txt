Q1. A is given as 80% of B. Express \(A:B\).
A. 5:4
B. 4:5
C. 80:100
D. 100:80
Answer: 4:5
Difficulty: Easy
Family: rp_percentage_to_ratio
Explanation:
Write A as a fraction of B.

\[
A=\frac{80}{100}B
\]

Clear the denominator to get the ratio.

\[
A:B=80:100=4:5
\]

Answer: 4:5
