Q1. A:B in age is \(3:5\) and B is 16 years older than A. Find B's present age.
A. 40 years
B. 24 years
C. 16 years
D. 8 years
Answer: 40 years
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_age_difference_constant
Explanation:
Let the ages be proportional parts.

\[
A=3k,\quad B=5k
\]

Use the constant age difference.

\[
5k-3k=16
\]

Find k and the required age.

\[
k=8,\quad B=5\times 8=40
\]

Answer: 40 years
