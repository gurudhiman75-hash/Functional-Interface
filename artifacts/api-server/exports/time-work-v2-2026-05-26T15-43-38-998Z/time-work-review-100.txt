Q1. The efficiencies of A and B are in the ratio 4:5. Together they finish the work in 10 days. Find the time taken by A alone.
A. 8 days
B. 12.5 days
C. 22.5 days
D. 19 days
Answer: 22.5 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_relative_efficiency_percent
Explanation:
Use the governing rate-state formula.
\[
W=(a+b)T
\]
Substitute the given values.
\[
W=(4+5)\times 10
\]
Simplify to get the required value.
\[
T_A=\frac{90}{4}=22.5
\]
Therefore the required answer is obtained.
\[
Ans=22.5
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T_A=\frac{(a+b)T}{a}=\frac{(4+5)10}{4}=22.5
\]
Answer: 22.5 days
