Q1. Starting with A, two workers alternate. A completes 6 records and B completes 4 records on their respective turns. What is the completion time in hours for 120 records?
A. 24 hours
B. 18 hours
C. 22 hours
D. 19 hours
Answer: 24 hours
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_alternating_hours
Explanation:
Find the work completed in one full cycle.
\[
W_{cycle}=\sum r_i
\]
Count full cycles before the last turn.
\[
W_{cycle}=6+4
\]
Add only the needed part of the final turn.
\[
T=24
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=cycles+final\ turn
\]
Answer: 24 hours
