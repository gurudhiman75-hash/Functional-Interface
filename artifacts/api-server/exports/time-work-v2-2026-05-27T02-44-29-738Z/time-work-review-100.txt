Q1. A takes 10 days to finish a work and B takes 30 days for the same work. In how many days can A and B finish it together?
A. 7 1/2 days
B. 10 days
C. 40 days
D. 20 days
Answer: 7 1/2 days
Difficulty: Easy
Family: tw_basic_combined_work
Explanation:
Take the LCM as total work.
\[
W=LCM(10,30)=30
\]
Find each worker's one-period efficiency.
\[
A=30/10=3,\quad B=30/30=1
\]
Divide total work by the combined efficiency.
\[
T=\frac{30}{3+1}=7.5
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{10\times 30}{10+30}=7.5
\]
Answer: 7 1/2 days
