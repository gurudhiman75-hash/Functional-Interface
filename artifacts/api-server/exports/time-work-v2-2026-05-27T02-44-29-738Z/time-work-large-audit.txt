Q1. A contract payment of ₹12000 is shared among A, B and C in the ratio 8:12. What amount goes to A?
A. ₹4804
B. ₹4800
C. ₹4802
D. ₹7200
Answer: ₹4800
Difficulty: Easy
Family: tw_wage_partial_time
Explanation:
Convert each person's work into contribution units.
\[
Share=\frac{c_i}{\sum c}\times W
\]
Use contribution ratio for the share.
\[
Share=\frac{8}{20}\times 12000
\]
Apply the wage or contract amount.
\[
Share=4800
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
Share=contribution\ ratio\times wage
\]
Answer: ₹4800
