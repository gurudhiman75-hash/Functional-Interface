Q1. A car travels from town A to town B at 60 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole journey?
  A. 60 km/h
  B. 58 km/h
  C. 75 km/h
  D. 68 km/h
Answer: 60 km/h
Explanation:
Since the distances are equal, use the equal-distance average speed formula.
\[
S=\frac{2uv}{u+v}
\]
Put the onward and return speeds in the equal-distance formula.
\[
S=\frac{2\times 60\times 60}{60+60}
\]
This gives the average speed for the complete return journey.
\[
S=60
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
S=\frac{2uv}{u+v}
\]
Answer: 60 km/h
Hindi:
एक कार शहर A से शहर B तक 60 किमी/घं की गति से जाती है और 60 किमी/घं की गति से लौटती है। पूरी यात्रा की औसत गति कितनी है?
दूरी समान है, इसलिए समान-दूरी औसत गति सूत्र लगाएँ।
\[
S=\frac{2uv}{u+v}
\]
दोनों गतियाँ रखें।
\[
S=\frac{2\times 60\times 60}{60+60}
\]
औसत गति सरल करें।
\[
S=60
\]
शॉर्टकट / परीक्षा विधि:
\[
S=\frac{2uv}{u+v}
\]
उत्तर: 60 किमी/घं
Punjabi:

Q2. A bus covers 120 km at 36 km/h and then 240 km at 108 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?
  A. 64.8 km/h
  B. 62.8 km/h
  C. 81 km/h
  D. 72.8 km/h
Answer: 64.8 km/h
Explanation:
For unequal distances, use total distance divided by total time.
\[
S=\frac{D_1+D_2}{\frac{D_1}{u}+\frac{D_2}{v}}
\]
Find the two travel times and combine them.
\[
S=\frac{120+240}{\frac{120}{36}+\frac{240}{108}}
\]
Divide the total distance by the combined travel time.
\[
S=64.8
\]

\[
S_{\text{avg}}=\frac{D_1+D_2}{\frac{D_1}{u}+\frac{D_2}{v}}

Q3. A cyclist reaches the coaching centre 10 minutes late at 5 km/h. At 6 km/h, he reaches 8 minutes early. What is the distance to the centre?
  A. 9 km
  B. 3 km
  C. 7.2 km
  D. 1 km
Answer: 9 km
Explanation:
The total time gap is from late to early, so add both differences.
\[
\Delta T=10+8=\frac{18}{60}
\]
Use the early-late distance relation.
\[
D=\frac{uv}{v-u}\times \Delta T
\]
Put the two walking speeds and the total time gap in the relation.
\[
D=\frac{5\times 6}{6-5}\times \frac{18}{60}=9
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
D=\frac{uv}{v-u}\times \Delta T
\]
Answer: 9 km

Q4. A 90 m train runs past a platform at 36 m/s and clears it in 15 seconds. What is the platform length?
  A. 450 m
  B. 446 m
  C. 337.5 m
  D. 440 m

Explanation:
Crossing distance equals speed multiplied by time.
\[
D=vt=36\times 15
\]
Subtract the known train length from the crossing distance.
\[
L_p=D-L_t
\]
This gives the unknown length.
\[
L_p=540-90=450
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
L_x=vt-L
\]

\[
D=\frac{vt}{2}

\[
D=\frac{340\times 2}{2}=340

D=\frac{vt}{2}
