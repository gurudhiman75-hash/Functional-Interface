Q1. A job needs 16 men for 12 days at 6 hours per day. Find the men required for 18 days at 8 hours per day.
A. 8 workers
B. 16 workers
C. 11 workers
D. 24 workers
Answer: 8 workers
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_man_days_hours_basic
Explanation:
Set up the rate relation for this case.
\[
M_1D_1H_1=M_2D_2H_2
\]
Put the given values into that relation.
\[
16\times 12\times 6=M_2\times 18\times 8
\]
Solve for the asked value.
\[
M_2=8
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
M_2=\frac{16\times 12\times 6}{18\times 8}=8
\]
Answer: 8 workers
