Q1. A worker does 15 units per day for 5 days, then slows to 12 units per day. Total work is 180 units. Find total time.
A. 17 3/4 days
B. 15 3/4 days
C. 17.19 days
D. 13 3/4 days
Answer: 13 3/4 days
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_productivity_decay
Explanation:
Set up the rate relation for this case.
\[
T=t_1+\frac{W-r_1t_1}{r_2}
\]
Put the given values into that relation.
\[
T=5+\frac{180-15\times 5}{12}
\]
Solve for the asked value.
\[
T=13.75
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=t_1+t_2
\]
Answer: 13 3/4 days
