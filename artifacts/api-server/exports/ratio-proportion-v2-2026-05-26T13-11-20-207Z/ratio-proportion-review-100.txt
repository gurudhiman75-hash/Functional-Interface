Q1. With 18 workers, a job takes 210 days. Find the time needed when 27 workers do the same job.
A. 210
B. 315
C. 9
D. 140
Answer: 140
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_inverse_variation_basic
Explanation:
Workers and days vary inversely.

\[
W_1D_1=W_2D_2
\]

Substitute the values.

\[
18\times 210=27\times D_2
\]

Solve for the days.

\[
D_2=\frac{18\times 210}{27}=140
\]

Answer: 140

Q3. A's money and B's money are in the ratio \(9:4\). After A transfers ₹400 to B, the ratio is \(8:5\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹1600
B. ₹3600
C. ₹3200
D. ₹4000
Answer: ₹3600
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=9k,\quad B=4k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{9k-400}{4k+400}=\frac{8}{5}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
5(9k-400)=8(4k+400)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
13k=5200,\quad k=400
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=9k=9\times 400=3600
\]

Answer: ₹3600

Q4. \(x\) varies directly as \(y\) and inversely as \(z\). If \(x=144\) when \(y=8\) and \(z=3\), find \(x\) when \(y=12\) and \(z=6\).
A. 144
B. 432
C. 108
D. 216
Answer: 108
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=144\times \frac{12}{8}\times \frac{3}{6}=108
\]

Answer: 108
