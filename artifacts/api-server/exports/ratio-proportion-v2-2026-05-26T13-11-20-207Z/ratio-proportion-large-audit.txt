Q1. The quantities of two mixtures are in the ratio \(6:7\). The first decreases by 25% and the second decreases by 25%. Find the new ratio.
A. 750:875
B. 450:700
C. 450:525
D. 6:7
Answer: 6:7
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_ratio_after_decrease
Explanation:
Let the original quantities be ratio parts.

\[
A=6,\quad B=7
\]

Apply the decreases.

\[
A':B'=6\left(1-\frac{25}{100}\right):7\left(1-\frac{25}{100}\right)
\]

Simplify the ratio.

\[
A':B'=450:525=6:7
\]

Answer: 6:7
