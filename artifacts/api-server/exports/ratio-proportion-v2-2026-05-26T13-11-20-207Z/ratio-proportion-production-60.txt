Q1. A is given as 40% of A+B. Express \(A:B\).
A. 3:2
B. 2:3
C. 40:100
D. 100:40
Answer: 2:3
Difficulty: Easy
Family: rp_percentage_to_ratio
Explanation:
Convert the percentage of total into a fraction.

\[
A=\frac{40}{100}(A+B)
\]

The remaining part belongs to B.

\[
A:B=40:60=2:3
\]

Answer: 2:3

Family: rp_age_past_ratio

\]

\[
B=3k=45
\]

Answer: 45 years

B. 36
C. 48
D. 72
Answer: 36
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=48\times \frac{12}{8}\times \frac{3}{6}=36
\]

Answer: 36
