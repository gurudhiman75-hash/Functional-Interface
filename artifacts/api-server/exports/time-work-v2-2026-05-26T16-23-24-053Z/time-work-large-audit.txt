Q1. 8 workers complete a job in 10 days by working 8 hours daily. For 5 hours daily and 12 days, find the required workers.
A. 9.6 workers
B. 14 workers
C. 10.67 workers
D. 8 workers
Answer: 10.67 workers
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_road_construction_work
Explanation:
Write output as rate multiplied by active time.
\[
M_1D_1H_1=M_2D_2H_2
\]
Add the outputs of all active agents.
\[
8\times 10\times 8=M_2\times 12\times 5
\]
Simplify the total output.
\[
M_2=10\frac{2}{3}
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
M_2=\frac{8\times 10\times 8}{12\times 5}=10\frac{2}{3}
\]
Answer: 10.67 workers
