D. 21 days
Answer: 15 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_team_a_vs_team_b
Explanation:

\[
T=\frac{W}{\sum ne}

Put the given values into that relation.
\[
T=\frac{360}{3\times 4+4\times 3}
\]
Solve for the asked value.
\[
T=15
\]

T=W/R
\]
