Q1. 10 men working 6 hours a day finish a work in 8 days. How many men are needed to finish it in 15 days working 8 hours a day?
A. 10 workers
B. 18.75 workers
C. 7 workers
D. 4 workers
Answer: 4 workers
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_farm_harvest_workers
Explanation:
Write output as rate multiplied by active time.
\[
M_1D_1H_1=M_2D_2H_2
\]
Add the outputs of all active agents.
\[
10\times 8\times 6=M_2\times 15\times 8
\]
Simplify the total output.
\[
M_2=4
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
M_2=\frac{10\times 8\times 6}{15\times 8}=4
\]
Answer: 4 workers
