Q1. Pipe A can fill a tank in 12 hours and pipe B can fill it in 18 hours. Pipe C can empty the tank in 36 hours. If all three pipes are opened together, in how many hours will the tank be filled?
A. 13 1/2 hours
B. 9 hours
C. 5 hours
D. 12 hours
Answer: 9 hours
Difficulty: Medium
Family: pc_two_fillers_one_empty
Explanation:
Write filling rates as positive and emptying rates as negative.
\[
T=\frac{W_l}{r_f-r_e}
\]
Find the effective tank rate.
\[
r=1/12+1/18-1/36
\]
Divide the required tank work by that rate.
\[
T=9
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{W_l}{r_n}
\]
Answer: 9 hours
