Q1. Two grades of sugar cost ₹20/kg and ₹30/kg. To get a blend worth ₹24/kg, in what ratio should they be mixed?
  A. 3:2
  B. 2:3
  C. 2:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 3:2
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 30</div><div>Lower value = 20</div><div>Mean value = 24</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">30</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">20</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">4</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">6</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Cross Differences</div><p>Difference between 24 and 20:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
24-20=4
\]</div><p>Difference between 30 and 24:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
30-24=6
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Interpret Ratio</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=6:4=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(30-24):(24-20)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=6:4=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(30-24):(24-20)=6:4=3:2
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>
