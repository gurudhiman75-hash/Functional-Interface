Q1. A and B run a business with investments in the ratio \(7:9\). The total profit is ₹14400. Find A's profit share.
A. ₹6300
B. ₹900
C. ₹7200
D. ₹8100
Answer: ₹6300
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_partnership_basic
Explanation:
For equal time, profit is divided in the investment ratio.

\[
A:B=7:9
\]

Use the total profit.

\[
7k+9k=14400,\quad k=900
\]

Find A's profit share.

\[
A=7k=6300
\]

Answer: ₹6300

Q2. \(x\) is proportional to \(y\) and inversely proportional to \(z\). Given \(x=240\) for \(y=8\), \(z=3\), find \(x\) for \(y=12\), \(z=6\).
A. 360
B. 720
C. 180
D. 240
Answer: 180
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=240\times \frac{12}{8}\times \frac{3}{6}=180
\]

Answer: 180
