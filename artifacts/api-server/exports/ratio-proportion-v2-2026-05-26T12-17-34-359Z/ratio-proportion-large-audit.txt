Q1. A's money and B's money are in the ratio \(9:4\). After A transfers ₹2800 to B, the ratio is \(8:5\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹25200
B. ₹22400
C. ₹28000
D. ₹11200
Answer: ₹25200
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=9k,\quad B=4k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{9k-2800}{4k+2800}=\frac{8}{5}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
5(9k-2800)=8(4k+2800)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
13k=36400,\quad k=2800
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=9k=9\times 2800=25200
\]

Answer: ₹25200

Q2. Output varies jointly as the number of machines and working hours. 6 machines working for 5 hours produce 720 units. How many units will 9 machines produce in 7 hours?
A. 1080
B. 720
C. 1008
D. 1512
Answer: 1512
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_joint_variation
Explanation:
For joint variation, output is proportional to machines and hours.

\[
O=kMH
\]

Use the ratio form.

\[
\frac{O_2}{O_1}=\frac{M_2H_2}{M_1H_1}
\]

Compute the new output.

\[
O_2=720\times \frac{9\times 7}{6\times 5}=1512
\]

Answer: 1512
