Q1. In a partnership, A's capital and B's capital are in the ratio \(5:8\). The profit is ₹10400. Find A's share.
A. ₹4000
B. ₹6400
C. ₹800
D. ₹5200
Answer: ₹4000
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_partnership_basic
Explanation:
For equal time, profit is divided in the investment ratio.

\[
A:B=5:8
\]

Use the total profit.

\[
5k+8k=10400,\quad k=800
\]

Find A's profit share.

\[
A=5k=4000
\]

Answer: ₹4000

Q3. With 12 workers, a job takes 60 days. Find the time needed when 15 workers do the same job.
A. 48
B. 3
C. 75
D. 60
Answer: 48
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_inverse_variation_basic
Explanation:
Workers and days vary inversely.

\[
W_1D_1=W_2D_2
\]

Substitute the values.

\[
12\times 60=15\times D_2
\]

Solve for the days.

\[
D_2=\frac{12\times 60}{15}=48
\]

Answer: 48
