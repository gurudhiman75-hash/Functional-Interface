Q1. A team takes 10 days for a job. After working for 6 days, what fraction of the job remains?
A. 5/2
B. 2/3
C. 2/5
D. 3/5
Answer: 2/5
Difficulty: Easy
Family: tw_remaining_work_fraction
Explanation:
Convert the given time into the fraction of work completed.
\[
R=1-\frac{t}{T}
\]
Use the remaining or required fraction of work.
\[
R=1-\frac{6}{10}
\]
Scale the time in the same ratio as the work.
\[
R=\frac{4}{10}=2/5
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
R=\frac{10-6}{10}=2/5
\]
Answer: 2/5
