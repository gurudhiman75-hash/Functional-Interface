Q1. The efficiencies of A and B are in the ratio 2:3. Together they complete a work in 6 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
A. 9 days
B. 4 days
C. 15 days
D. 11 days
Answer: 15 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_relative_efficiency_percent
Explanation:
Convert time or efficiency data into work rates.
\[
W=(a+b)T
\]
Combine or compare the rates as required.
\[
W=(2+3)\times 6
\]
Convert the final rate relation back into time or ratio.
\[
T_A=\frac{30}{2}=15
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T_A=\frac{(a+b)T}{a}=\frac{(2+3)6}{2}=15
\]
Answer: 15 days
