Q1. Walking at 6/7 of his usual speed, a man reaches office 15 minutes late. What is his usual time to reach the office?
  A. 90 minutes
  B. 96 minutes
  C. 67.5 minutes
  D. 80 minutes
Answer: 90 minutes
Explanation:
When speed is reduced by a fraction, time increases by the reciprocal fraction.
\[
T'=\frac{7}{6}T
\]
The extra time equals the late time.
\[
T'-T=15
\]
The late time is the extra time caused by reduced speed.
\[
T=90
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
\Delta T=\left(\frac{b}{a}-1\right)T
\]
Answer: 90 minutes
Hindi:
अपनी सामान्य गति के 6/7 से चलने पर एक व्यक्ति कार्यालय 15 मिनट देर से पहुँचता है। कार्यालय पहुँचने का उसका सामान्य समय कितना है?
गति भिन्न में घटे तो समय उसके उलटे अनुपात में बढ़ता है।
\[
T'=\frac{7}{6}T
\]
अतिरिक्त समय ही देरी है।
\[
T'-T=15
\]
सामान्य समय निकालें।
\[
T=90
\]
शॉर्टकट / परीक्षा विधि:
\[
\Delta T=\left(\frac{b}{a}-1\right)T
\]
उत्तर: 90 मिनट
Punjabi:

Q2. A bus and a van travel equal distances with speeds in the ratio 4:4. What will be the ratio of their times?
  A. 16:15
  B. 1:2
  C. 2:3
  D. 3:4
Answer: 16:15
Explanation:
For the same distance, time ratio is the inverse of speed ratio.
\[
T_1:T_2=v_2:v_1
\]
Reverse the given speed ratio.
\[
T_1:T_2=4:4
\]
This gives the required time ratio.
\[
T_1:T_2=16:15
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T_1:T_2=v_2:v_1
\]
Answer: 16:15

\[
T_1:T_2=v_2:v_1
\]

\]

\[
T_1:T_2=16:15
\]

Q3. A boat travels 30 km downstream in 5 hours and 30 km upstream in 6 hours. What is the speed of the stream?

  B. 4.5 km/h
  C. 1 km/h
  D. 9.5 km/h

Explanation:

\[
v_d=\frac{30}{5},\quad v_u=\frac{30}{6}
\]
Boat and stream speeds are obtained by averaging or halving the difference.
\[
v_s=\frac{v_d-v_u}{2}
\]
Use downstream and upstream speeds to isolate the requested speed.
\[
v_s=\frac{6-5}{2}=0.5
\]

\[
v_s=\frac{v_d-v_u}{2}

Q4. A starts from a town at 20 km/h. B starts 1 hours later from the same town at 40 km/h. After how many hours from B's start will B catch A?
  A. 1 hours
  B. 1.25 hours
  C. 9 hours
  D. 10 hours
Answer: 1 hours
Explanation:
B first has to cover the distance already gained by A.
\[
D_h=20\times 1=20
\]
In the same direction, subtract the speeds.
\[
v_r=40-20=20
\]
Catch-up time is head start divided by relative speed.
\[
T=\frac{20}{20}=1
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{D_h}{v_2-v_1}
\]

\[
D=vt=25\times 15
\]
Subtract the known train length from the crossing distance.
\[
L_p=D-L_t

This gives the unknown length.
\[
L_p=375-150=225
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
L_x=vt-L
\]

When A finishes, B has covered race distance minus lead.
