Q1. A and B together finish a job in 10 days. A's individual time is 24 days. What is B's individual time?
A. 17.14 days
B. 17 days
C. 14 days
D. 34 days
Answer: 17.14 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_individual_from_combined
Explanation:
Set up the rate relation for this case.
\[
\frac{1}{B}=\frac{1}{T}-\frac{1}{A}
\]
Put the given values into that relation.
\[
\frac{1}{B}=\frac{1}{10}-\frac{1}{24}
\]
Solve for the asked value.
\[
B=17.14
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
B=\frac{10\times 24}{24-10}=17.14
\]
Answer: 17.14 days
