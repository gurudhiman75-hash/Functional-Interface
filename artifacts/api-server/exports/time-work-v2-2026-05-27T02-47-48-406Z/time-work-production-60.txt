B. 50 litres
C. 49 litres
D. 40 litres
Answer: 50 litres
Difficulty: Medium
Family: pc_overflow_waste_rate
Explanation:

\[
V_w=r\times t
\]
Find the effective tank rate.
\[
V_w=10\times 5
\]
Divide the required tank work by that rate.
\[
V_w=50
