C. 275 items
D. 324 items
Answer: 270 items
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_machine_breakdown_midway
Explanation:
Write output as rate multiplied by active time.
\[
O=\sum r_it_i
\]
Add the outputs of all active agents.
\[
O=12\times 6+15\times 6+18\times 6
\]
Simplify the total output.
\[
O=270
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
O=(r_1+r_2+r_3)t
\]
Answer: 270 items
