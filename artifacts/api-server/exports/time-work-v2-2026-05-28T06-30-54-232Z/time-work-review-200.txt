Q1. A pipe starts filling a tank and works alone for 2 hours. A leak that empties a full tank in 30 hours then starts. If the pipe alone fills in 12 hours, in how many hours will the tank be completely filled?
A. 18 2/3 hours
B. 16 2/3 hours
C. 24 2/3 hours
D. 23 1/3 hours
Answer: 18 2/3 hours
Difficulty: Hard
Family: pc_drain_after_partial_fill
Explanation:
Write filling rates as positive and emptying rates as negative.
\[
T=\frac{W_l}{r_f-r_e}
\]
Find the effective tank rate.
\[
r=1/12-1/30
\]
Divide the required tank work by that rate.
\[
T=18\frac{2}{3}
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{W_l}{r_n}
\]
Answer: 18 2/3 hours

B. 16 days
C. 7 1/2 days
D. 12 days
Answer: 7 1/2 days
Difficulty: Easy
Family: tw_basic_combined_work
Explanation:
Take the LCM as total work.
\[
W=LCM(20,12)=60
\]
Find each worker's one-period efficiency.
\[
A=60/20=3,\quad B=60/12=5
\]
Divide total work by the combined efficiency.
\[
T=\frac{60}{3+5}=7.5
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{20\times 12}{20+12}=7.5
\]
Answer: 7 1/2 days
