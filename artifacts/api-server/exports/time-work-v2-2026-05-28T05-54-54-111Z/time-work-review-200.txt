Q1. 12 workers complete the same job in 10 days at 8 hours a day. How many workers are required to complete the same job in 16 days at 6 hours a day?
A. 10 workers
B. 19 workers
C. 13 workers
D. 12 workers
Answer: 10 workers
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_farm_harvest_workers
Explanation:
Write output as rate multiplied by active time.
\[
M_1D_1H_1=M_2D_2H_2
\]
Add the outputs of all active agents.
\[
12\times 10\times 8=M_2\times 16\times 6
\]
Simplify the total output.
\[
M_2=10
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
M_2=\frac{12\times 10\times 8}{16\times 6}=10
\]
Answer: 10 workers
