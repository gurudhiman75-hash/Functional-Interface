Q1. One pipe fills a tank in 12 hours, while another empties it in 36 hours. In how many hours will the tank be completely filled with both open?
A. 22 1/2 hours
B. 24 hours
C. 21 hours
D. 18 hours
Answer: 18 hours
Difficulty: Medium
Family: pc_basic_fill_empty
Explanation:
Write filling rates as positive and emptying rates as negative.
\[
T=\frac{W_l}{r_f-r_e}
\]
Find the effective tank rate.
\[
r=1/12-1/36
\]
Divide the required tank work by that rate.
\[
T=18
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{W_l}{r_n}
\]
Answer: 18 hours
