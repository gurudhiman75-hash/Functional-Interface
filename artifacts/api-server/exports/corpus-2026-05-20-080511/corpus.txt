Fuel price increased by 50%. If total expenditure is kept the same, by what percent should consumption be reduced?

HI:

Answer: 33.33%
Difficulty: Medium
Topology: none:price_consumption
Realism: 86

Explanation EN:
New price after increase:
100 + 50
= 150

For the same expenditure:
New consumption =
100 x 100 / 150
= 66.67

Reduction in consumption is:
100 - 66.67
= 33.33% reduction

Hence, required reduction = 33.33% reduction
