Q1. A tank is filled using pipes A and B. Their individual times are 12 and 18 hours. If pipe B is opened 2 hours later, how long will the tank take to fill?
A. 10 hours
B. 8 hours
C. 14 hours
D. 6 hours
Answer: 8 hours
Difficulty: Hard
Family: pc_multiple_pipes_timing
Explanation:
Split the work into timeline phases.
\[
W_1=r_At_1,\quad T=t_1+\frac{W-W_1}{r_A+r_B}
\]
Subtract the work already completed.
\[
W_1=3\times 2
\]
Use the active rate for the remaining phase.
\[
T=2+\frac{30}{3+2}=8
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=t_1+\frac{W-W_1}{r_A+r_B}
\]
Answer: 8 hours
