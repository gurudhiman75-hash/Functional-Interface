D. 25 days

Family: tw_resource_rate_equivalence
Explanation:

\[
D=\frac{S}{R}
\]

\[
D=\frac{720}{36}

Divide the remaining stock by the new daily use.
\[
D=20
\]

\[
D=\frac{S}{R}
\]
Answer: 20 days
