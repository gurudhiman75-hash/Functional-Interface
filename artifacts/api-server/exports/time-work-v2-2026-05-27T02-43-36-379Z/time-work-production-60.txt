Q1. A mixed team has 3 men and 4 women. Their daily item counts are 4 and 3 respectively. In how many days can the team finish 300 items?
A. 8 1/2 days
B. 12 1/2 days
C. 18 3/4 days
D. 10 1/2 days
Answer: 12 1/2 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_worker_equivalence_men_women
Explanation:
Set up the rate relation for this case.
\[
T=\frac{W}{\sum ne}
\]
Put the given values into that relation.
\[
T=\frac{300}{3\times 4+4\times 3}
\]
Solve for the asked value.
\[
T=12.5
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=W/R
\]
Answer: 12 1/2 days

Q3. A and B can separately complete a job in 30 days and 8 days. What is the time taken when they work together?

B. 6.32 days
C. 38 days
D. 19 days
Answer: 6.32 days
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_lcm_hidden_total_work
Explanation:
Take the LCM as total work.
\[
W=LCM(30,8)=120
\]
Find each worker's one-period efficiency.
\[
A=120/30=4,\quad B=120/8=15
\]
Divide total work by the combined efficiency.
\[
T=\frac{120}{4+15}=6.32
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{30\times 8}{30+8}=6.32
\]
Answer: 6.32 days
