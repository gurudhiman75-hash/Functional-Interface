Q1. A mixed team has 3 men and 4 women. Their daily item counts are 4 and 3 respectively. In how many days can the team finish 300 items?
A. 17 1/2 days
B. 12 1/2 days
C. 10 1/2 days
D. 15 days
Answer: 12 1/2 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_worker_equivalence_men_women
Explanation:
Set up the rate relation for this case.
\[
T=\frac{W}{\sum ne}
\]
Put the given values into that relation.
\[
T=\frac{300}{3\times 4+4\times 3}
\]
Solve for the asked value.
\[
T=12.5
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=W/R
\]
Answer: 12 1/2 days
