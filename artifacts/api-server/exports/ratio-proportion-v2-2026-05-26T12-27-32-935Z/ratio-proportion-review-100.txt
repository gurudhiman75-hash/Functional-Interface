Q1. With 18 workers, a job takes 150 days. Find the time needed when 27 workers do the same job.
A. 225
B. 100
C. 9
D. 150
Answer: 100
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_inverse_variation_basic
Explanation:
Workers and days vary inversely.

\[
W_1D_1=W_2D_2
\]

Substitute the values.

\[
18\times 150=27\times D_2
\]

Solve for the days.

\[
D_2=\frac{18\times 150}{27}=100
\]

Answer: 100

Q5. \(x\) varies directly as \(y\) and inversely as \(z\). If \(x=48\) when \(y=8\) and \(z=3\), find \(x\) when \(y=12\) and \(z=6\).
A. 144
B. 36
C. 48
D. 72
Answer: 36
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=48\times \frac{12}{8}\times \frac{3}{6}=36
\]

Answer: 36
