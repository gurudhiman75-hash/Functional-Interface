Q1. A and B have present ages in the ratio \(2:3\). 10 years ago, their age ratio was \(4:7\). Find B's present age.
A. 30 years
B. 15 years
C. 45 years
D. 35 years
Answer: 45 years
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_age_past_ratio
Explanation:
Let present ages be ratio parts.

\[
A=2k,\quad B=3k
\]

Use the past-age ratio.

\[
\frac{2k-10}{3k-10}=\frac{4}{7}
\]

Solve for the multiplier.

\[
2k=30,\quad k=15
\]

Find B's present age.

\[
B=3k=45
\]

Answer: 45 years
