Q1. Production is jointly proportional to machines and hours. 8 machines produce 2160 units in 6 hours. Find the output of 10 machines in 9 hours.
A. 4050
B. 2160
C. 3240
D. 2700
Answer: 4050
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_joint_variation
Explanation:
For joint variation, output is proportional to machines and hours.

\[
O=kMH
\]

Use the ratio form.

\[
\frac{O_2}{O_1}=\frac{M_2H_2}{M_1H_1}
\]

Compute the new output.

\[
O_2=2160\times \frac{10\times 9}{8\times 6}=4050
\]

Answer: 4050
