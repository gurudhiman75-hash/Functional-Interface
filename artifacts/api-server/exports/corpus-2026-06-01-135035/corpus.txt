[Q4]
EN:
Between \(137\) and \(289\), how many integers are divisible by \(9\)?

HI:
\(137\) और \(289\) के बीच कितने पूर्णांक \(9\) से विभाज्य हैं?

PA:
\(137\) ਅਤੇ \(289\) ਦੇ ਵਿਚਕਾਰ ਕਿੰਨੇ ਪੂਰਨ ਅੰਕ \(9\) ਨਾਲ ਭਾਗ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹਨ?

Options: \(16\) | \(19\) | \(17\) | \(18\)
Answer: \(17\)
Difficulty: Medium
Topology: number_system:ns_divisibility_multi_condition
Realism: 88

Explanation EN:
Step 1: Find the first valid multiple

Therefore, the first multiple of 9 greater than 137 is 144.

\[
9\times 16=144
\]

Step 2: Find the last valid multiple

Therefore, 288 is the greatest multiple of 9 not exceeding 289.

\[
289=9\times 32+1
\]

\[
289-1=288
\]

Step 3: Count the valid multiples

The multiples form an arithmetic progression with common difference 9.

\[
\frac{288-144}{9}+1=16+1=17
\]

Answer Verification
The total number of multiples is 17.
Hence, there are 17 multiples of 9 in the given range.

Final Answer
Therefore, the correct answer is Option (C). Computed value: \(17\).

Shortcut

Count multiples using quotient floors on the range limits.

\[
\left\lfloor\frac{289}{9}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\frac{136}{9}\right\rfloor
\]

\[
32-15=17
\]

Fast result: \(17\). Therefore, the correct answer is Option (C).
