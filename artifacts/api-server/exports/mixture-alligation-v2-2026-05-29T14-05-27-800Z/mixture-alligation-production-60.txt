Q1. A shopkeeper has two grades available. A trader wants a pulses blend costing ₹32/kg by using grades priced at ₹25/kg and ₹45/kg. What ratio should he use?
  A. 13:7
  B. 7:13
  C. 2:1
  D. 13:8
Answer: 13:7
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 45</li><li>Lower value = 25</li><li>Mean value = 32</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">45</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">25</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">32</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">7</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">13</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 45 and 32:</p><div class="mix-math">\[45-32=13\]</div><p>Difference between 32 and 25:</p><div class="mix-math">\[32-25=7\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=13:7=13:7\]</div><p>Place the lower value, mean value and higher value in the alligation cross.</p><div class="mix-math">\[\begin{array}{ccc}
C=25 & & D=45 \\
& M=32 & \\
D-M=13 & & M-C=7
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(45-32):(32-25)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=13:7=13:7\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(45-32):(32-25)=13:7=13:7</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 13:7. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 13:7. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>

C=64 & & D=100 \\
& M=76 & \\
D-M=24 & & M-C=12
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(100-76):(76-64)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=24:12=2:1\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(100-76):(76-64)=24:12=2:1</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 2:1.   C. 3:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 2:1
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 100</li><li>Lower value = 64</li><li>Mean value = 76</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">100</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">64</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">76</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">12</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 100 and 76:</p><div class="mix-math">\[100-76=24\]</div><p>Difference between 76 and 64:</p><div class="mix-math">\[76-64=12\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=24:12=2:1\]</div><p>Place the lower value, mean value and higher value in the alligation cross.</p><div class="mix-math">\[\begin{array}{ccc}
C=64 & & D=100 \\
& M=76 & \\
D-M=24 & & M-C=12
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(100-76):(76-64)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=24:12=2:1\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(100-76):(76-64)=24:12=2:1</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 2:1. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>
