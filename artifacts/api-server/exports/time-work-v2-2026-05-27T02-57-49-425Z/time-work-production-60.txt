Q1. Three printers work together for 5 hours. They complete 12, 15 and 18 pages per hour. How many pages are completed?
A. 225 pages
B. 230 pages
C. 224 pages
D. 168.75 pages
Answer: 225 pages
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_printer_job_queue
Explanation:
Write output as rate multiplied by active time.
\[
O=\sum r_it_i
\]
Add the outputs of all active agents.
\[
O=12\times 5+15\times 5+18\times 5
\]
Simplify the total output.
\[
O=225
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
O=(r_1+r_2+r_3)t
\]
Answer: 225 pages
