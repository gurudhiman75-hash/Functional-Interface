Q1. A shopkeeper has two grades available. Wheat costing ₹30/kg and ₹50/kg are mixed so that the average cost becomes ₹38/kg. What is the mixing ratio?
  A. 3:2
  B. 2:3
  C. 2:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 3:2
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 50</div><div>Lower value = 30</div><div>Mean value = 38</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">50</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">30</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">38</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Cross difference 1, mean minus lower value:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
38-30=8
\]</div><p>Cross difference 2, higher value minus mean:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
50-38=12
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=12:8=3:2
\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(50-38):(38-30)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=12:8=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(50-38):(38-30)=12:8=3:2
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>

Q3. A merchant prepares goods for sale. How many litres of water should be added to 64 litres of a 50% acid solution to make it a 40% solution?
  A. 16 litres
  B. 21 litres
  C. 11 litres
  D. 26 litres
Answer: 16 litres
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is a concentration question. Since only water changes, the amount of solute stays fixed throughout.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 50</div><div>Lower value = 0</div><div>Mean value = 40</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">50</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">0</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">40</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Cross difference 1, mean minus lower value:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
40-0=40
\]</div><p>Cross difference 2, higher value minus mean:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
50-40=10
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=10:40=1:4
\]</div><p>The amount of acid stays fixed unless pure acid is added.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
0.5\times 64=0.4\times V
\]</div><p>Solve for the changed quantity.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
x=16
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[

Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. 60-48=12
\]</div><p>Cross difference 2, higher value minus mean:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
80-60=20
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=20:12=5:3
\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(80-60):(60-48)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=20:12=5:3
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(80-60):(60-48)=20:12=5:3

Q10. A vessel contains a mixture. A store prepares a spices mixture of average price ₹45/kg from two lots costing ₹36/kg and ₹60/kg. In what ratio are the lots mixed?
  A. 5:3
  B. 3:5
  C. 2:1
  D. 5:4
Answer: 5:3
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 60</div><div>Lower value = 36</div><div>Mean value = 45</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">60</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">36</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">45</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Cross difference 1, mean minus lower value:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
45-36=9
\]</div><p>Cross difference 2, higher value minus mean:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
60-45=15
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=15:9=5:3
\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(60-45):(45-36)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=15:9=5:3
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(60-45):(45-36)=15:9=5:3
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>
