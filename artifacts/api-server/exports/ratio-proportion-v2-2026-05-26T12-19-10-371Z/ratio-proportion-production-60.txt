Q1. 16 workers finish a work in 144 days. If the number of workers is 24, how many days will be required?
A. 216
B. 96
C. 8
D. 144
Answer: 96
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_inverse_variation_basic
Explanation:
Workers and days vary inversely.

\[
W_1D_1=W_2D_2
\]

Substitute the values.

\[
16\times 144=24\times D_2
\]

Solve for the days.

\[
D_2=\frac{16\times 144}{24}=96
\]

Answer: 96
