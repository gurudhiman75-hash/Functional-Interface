Q1. Initially, A's income and B's income are in the ratio \(7:8\). They increase by 20% and 25% respectively. Find the new ratio.
A. 875:960
B. 7:8
C. 27:33
D. 21:25
Answer: 21:25
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_ratio_after_increase
Explanation:
Let the original incomes be ratio parts.

\[
A=7,\quad B=8
\]

Apply the percentage increases.

\[
A':B'=7\left(1+\frac{20}{100}\right):8\left(1+\frac{25}{100}\right)
\]

Simplify the new ratio.

\[
A':B'=840:1000=21:25
\]

Answer: 21:25

Q3. With 16 workers, a job takes 36 days. Find the time needed when 24 workers do the same job.
A. 8
B. 24
C. 36
D. 54
Answer: 24
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_inverse_variation_basic
Explanation:
Workers and days vary inversely.

\[
W_1D_1=W_2D_2
\]

Substitute the values.

\[
16\times 36=24\times D_2
\]

Solve for the days.

\[
D_2=\frac{16\times 36}{24}=24
\]

Answer: 24
