Q1. The combined time of A and B is 10 days. If A can finish alone in 12 days, find B's time.
A. 22 days
B. 2 days
C. 11 days
D. 60 days
Answer: 60 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_individual_from_combined
Explanation:
Set up the rate relation for this case.
\[
\frac{1}{B}=\frac{1}{T}-\frac{1}{A}
\]
Put the given values into that relation.
\[
\frac{1}{B}=\frac{1}{10}-\frac{1}{12}
\]
Solve for the asked value.
\[
B=60
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
B=\frac{10\times 12}{12-10}=60
\]
Answer: 60 days
