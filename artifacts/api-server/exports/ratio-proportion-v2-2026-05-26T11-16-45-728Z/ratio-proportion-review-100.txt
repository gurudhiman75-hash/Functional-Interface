Q1. A and B have money in the ratio \(9:4\). After ₹1200 is given by A to B, the ratio becomes \(8:5\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹10800
B. ₹9600
C. ₹12000
D. ₹4800
Answer: ₹10800
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=9k,\quad B=4k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{9k-1200}{4k+1200}=\frac{8}{5}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
5(9k-1200)=8(4k+1200)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
13k=15600,\quad k=1200
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=9k=9\times 1200=10800
\]

Answer: ₹10800
