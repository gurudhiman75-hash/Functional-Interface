Q1. The sides of two similar figures are in the ratio \(10:11\). Find the ratio of their areas.
A. 100:121
B. 121:100
C. 10:11
D. 1000:1331
Answer: 100:121
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_side_area_volume_ratio
Explanation:
Start with the side ratio.

\[
\text{Side ratio}=10:11
\]

Area ratio is the square of side ratio.

\[
\text{Area ratio}=10^{2}:11^{2}=100:121
\]

Answer: 100:121
