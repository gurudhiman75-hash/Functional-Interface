Q1. If \(2:7 = 10:x\), find \(x\).
A. 3
B. 35
C. 17
D. 10
Answer: 35
Difficulty: Easy
Family: rp_missing_term_proportion
Explanation:
Write the proportion as fractions.

\[
\frac{2}{7}=\frac{10}{x}
\]

Cross-multiply.

\[
2x=7\times 10
\]

Solve for the missing term.

\[
x=\frac{7\times 10}{2}=35
\]

Answer: 35

Q5. \(x\) varies directly as \(y\) and inversely as \(z\). If \(x=504\) when \(y=9\) and \(z=5\), find \(x\) when \(y=15\) and \(z=6\).

B. 840

Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}

Compute the new value.

\]

Answer: 700
