Q1. The sides of two similar figures are in the ratio \(8:11\). Find the ratio of their areas.
A. 64:121
B. 121:64
C. 8:11
D. 512:1331
Answer: 64:121
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_side_area_volume_ratio
Explanation:
Start with the side ratio.

\[
\text{Side ratio}=8:11
\]

Area ratio is the square of side ratio.

\[
\text{Area ratio}=8^{2}:11^{2}=64:121
\]

Answer: 64:121

Q2. Boys and girls in a class are in the ratio \(7:9\). If the class has 400 students, find the number of girls.
A. 50
B. 25
C. 175
D. 225
Answer: 225
Difficulty: Easy
Family: rp_sum_based_ratio_recovery
Explanation:
Let boys and girls be proportional to the ratio.

\[
B=7k,\quad G=9k
\]

Use the total students.

\[
7k+9k=400
\]

Solve the equation.

\[
16k=400,\quad k=25
\]

Find the number of girls.

\[
G=9k=225
\]

Answer: 225

Q4. A and B have money in the ratio \(11:7\). After ₹2400 is given by A to B, the ratio becomes \(1:1\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹8400
B. ₹10800
C. ₹13200
D. ₹15600
Answer: ₹13200
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=11k,\quad B=7k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{11k-2400}{7k+2400}=\frac{1}{1}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
1(11k-2400)=1(7k+2400)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
4k=4800,\quad k=1200
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=11k=11\times 1200=13200
\]

Answer: ₹13200
