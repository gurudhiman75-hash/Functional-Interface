Q1. The incomes of A and B are in the ratio \(8:9\). A's income increases by 20% and B's income increases by 20%. Find the new ratio.
A. 8:9
B. 960:1080
C. 28:29
D. 9:8
Answer: 8:9
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_ratio_after_increase
Explanation:
Let the original incomes be ratio parts.

\[
A=8,\quad B=9
\]

Apply the percentage increases.

\[
A':B'=8\left(1+\frac{20}{100}\right):9\left(1+\frac{20}{100}\right)
\]

Simplify the new ratio.

\[
A':B'=960:1080=8:9
\]

Answer: 8:9

Q2. A and B have money in the ratio \(7:5\). After ₹2400 is given by A to B, the ratio becomes \(13:11\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹36000
B. ₹31200
C. ₹24000
D. ₹33600
Answer: ₹33600
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=7k,\quad B=5k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{7k-2400}{5k+2400}=\frac{13}{11}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
11(7k-2400)=13(5k+2400)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
12k=57600,\quad k=4800
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=7k=7\times 4800=33600
\]

Answer: ₹33600

\[
