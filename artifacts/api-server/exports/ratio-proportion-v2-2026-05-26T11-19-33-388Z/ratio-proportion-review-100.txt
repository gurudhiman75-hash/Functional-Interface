Q1. For two directly varying quantities \(x\) and \(y\), \(x=90\) at \(y=9\). Find \(x\) at \(y=14\).
A. 95
B. 90
C. 140
D. 57.86
Answer: 140
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_direct_variation_basic
Explanation:
For direct variation, the ratios are equal.

\[
\frac{x_1}{y_1}=\frac{x_2}{y_2}
\]

Substitute the values.

\[
\frac{90}{9}=\frac{x_2}{14}
\]

Solve for the new value.

\[
x_2=\frac{90\times 14}{9}=140
\]

Answer: 140

Q83. A and B have money in the ratio \(5:3\). After ₹1800 is given by A to B, the ratio becomes \(3:2\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹45000
B. ₹43200
C. ₹46800
D. ₹27000
Answer: ₹45000
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=5k,\quad B=3k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{5k-1800}{3k+1800}=\frac{3}{2}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
2(5k-1800)=3(3k+1800)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
1k=9000,\quad k=9000
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=5k=5\times 9000=45000
\]

Answer: ₹45000

Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
