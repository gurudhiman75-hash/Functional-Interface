Q1. A completes a job in 12 days, while B completes it in 10 days. Find the time required when both work together.
A. 22 days
B. 10 days
C. 5.45 days
D. 11 days
Answer: 5.45 days
Difficulty: Easy
Family: tw_basic_combined_work
Explanation:
Use the governing rate-state formula.
\[
W=LCM(12,10)=60
\]
Substitute the given values.
\[
A=60/12=5,\quad B=60/10=6
\]
Simplify to get the required value.
\[
T=\frac{60}{5+6}=5.45
\]
Therefore the required answer is obtained.
\[
Ans=5.45
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{12\times 10}{12+10}=5.45
\]
Answer: 5.45 days
