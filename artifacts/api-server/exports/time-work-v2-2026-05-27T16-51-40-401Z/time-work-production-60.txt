Q1. A tank has two filling pipes. Pipe A fills it in 12 hours and pipe B in 18 hours. Pipe A is closed after 2 hours. In how many hours will the tank be completely filled?
A. 21 hours
B. 17 hours
C. 15 hours
D. 12 hours
Answer: 15 hours
Difficulty: Medium
Family: pc_pipe_closed_before_completion
Explanation:
Split the work into timeline phases.
\[
W_1=(r_A+r_B)t_1,\quad T=t_1+\frac{W-W_1}{r_B}
\]
Subtract the work already completed.
\[
W_1=(3+2)\times 2
\]
Use the active rate for the remaining phase.
\[
T=2+\frac{26}{2}=15
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=t_1+\frac{W-W_1}{r_B}
\]
Answer: 15 hours
