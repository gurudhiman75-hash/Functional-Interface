Q3. A and B can separately complete a job in 10 days and 15 days. What is the time taken when they work together?
A. 6 days
B. 25 days
C. 12 1/2 days
D. 10 days
Answer: 6 days
Difficulty: Easy
Family: tw_basic_combined_work
Explanation:

\[
W=LCM(10,15)=30
\]
Find each worker's one-period efficiency.
\[
A=30/10=3,\quad B=30/15=2
\]
Divide total work by the combined efficiency.
\[
T=\frac{30}{3+2}=6
\]

\[
T=\frac{10\times 15}{10+15}=6
\]
Answer: 6 days
