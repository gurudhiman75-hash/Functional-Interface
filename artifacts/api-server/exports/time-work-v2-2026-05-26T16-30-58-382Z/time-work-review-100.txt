Q1. The third worker joins only on the third turn of each cycle. Turn outputs are 6, 4, 4 units. Find the time for 60 units.
A. 16 2/3 days
B. 19 days
C. 12 2/3 days
D. 9 2/3 days
Answer: 12 2/3 days
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_conditional_activation
Explanation:
Find the work completed in one full cycle.
\[
W_{cycle}=\sum r_i
\]
Count full cycles before the last turn.
\[
W_{cycle}=6+4+4
\]
Add only the needed part of the final turn.
\[
T=12\frac{2}{3}
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=cycles+final\ turn
\]
Answer: 12 2/3 days
