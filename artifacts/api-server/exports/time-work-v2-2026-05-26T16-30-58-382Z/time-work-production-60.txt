Q1. A group consumes food at 36 adult-equivalent units per day. The stock is 720 units. Find how many days it will last.
A. 21 days
B. 16 days
C. 24 days
D. 20 days
Answer: 20 days
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_resource_rate_equivalence
Explanation:
Convert the stock into consumption units.
\[
D=\frac{S}{R}
\]
Subtract the stock already consumed.
\[
D=\frac{720}{36}
\]
Divide the remaining stock by the new daily use.
\[
D=20
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
D=\frac{S}{R}
\]
Answer: 20 days
