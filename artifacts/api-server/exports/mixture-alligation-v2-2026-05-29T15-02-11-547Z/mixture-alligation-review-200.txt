Q1. A trader works with two lots. Cheap oil at ₹54/kg is mixed with dearer oil at ₹90/kg. If the blend is worth ₹66/kg, find the ratio?
  A. 2:1
  B. 1:2
  C. 3:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 2:1
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 90</div><div>Lower value = 54</div><div>Mean value = 66</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">90</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">54</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">66</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">12</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 66 and 54:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
66-54=12
\]</div><p>Difference between 90 and 66:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
90-66=24
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=24:12=2:1
\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(90-66):(66-54)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=24:12=2:1
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(90-66):(66-54)=24:12=2:1
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 2:1. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 2:1. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>
