Q1. A seller uses the following mixture. A shopkeeper mixes tea costing ₹80 per kg with tea costing ₹120 per kg to make a mixture worth ₹95 per kg. In what ratio should the two types be mixed?
  A. 5:3
  B. 3:5
  C. 2:1
  D. 5:4
Answer: 5:3
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 120</div><div>Lower value = 80</div><div>Mean value = 95</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">120</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">80</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">95</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">15</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">25</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 95 and 80:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
95-80=15
\]</div><p>Difference between 120 and 95:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
120-95=25
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=25:15=5:3
\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(120-95):(95-80)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=25:15=5:3
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(120-95):(95-80)=25:15=5:3
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>

<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. 65-50=15

\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=25:15=5:3

Q10. A trader makes the required blend. Cheap rice at ₹40/kg is mixed with dearer rice at ₹60/kg. If the blend is worth ₹48/kg, find the ratio?
  A. 3:2
  B. 2:3
  C. 2:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 3:2
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 60</div><div>Lower value = 40</div><div>Mean value = 48</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">60</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">40</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">48</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">8</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">12</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 48 and 40:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
48-40=8
\]</div><p>Difference between 60 and 48:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
60-48=12
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=12:8=3:2
\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(60-48):(48-40)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=12:8=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(60-48):(48-40)=12:8=3:2
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>
