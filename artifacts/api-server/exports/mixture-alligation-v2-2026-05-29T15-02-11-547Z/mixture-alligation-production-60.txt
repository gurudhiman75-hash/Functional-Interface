Q1. A seller prepares the final blend. A trader wants a wheat blend costing ₹38/kg by using grades priced at ₹30/kg and ₹50/kg. What ratio should he use?
  A. 3:2
  B. 2:3
  C. 2:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 3:2
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 50</div><div>Lower value = 30</div><div>Mean value = 38</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">50</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">30</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">38</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">8</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">12</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 38 and 30:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
38-30=8
\]</div><p>Difference between 50 and 38:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
50-38=12
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=12:8=3:2
\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(50-38):(38-30)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=12:8=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(50-38):(38-30)=12:8=3:2
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>

Q10. A shopkeeper compares two rates. A trader wants a fertilizer blend costing ₹75/kg by using grades priced at ₹60/kg and ₹100/kg. What ratio should he use?
  A. 5:3
  B. 3:5
  C. 2:1
  D. 5:4
Answer: 5:3
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 100</div><div>Lower value = 60</div><div>Mean value = 75</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">100</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">60</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">75</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">15</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">25</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 75 and 60:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
75-60=15
\]</div><p>Difference between 100 and 75:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
100-75=25
\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=25:15=5:3
\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(100-75):(75-60)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=25:15=5:3
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(100-75):(75-60)=25:15=5:3
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>
