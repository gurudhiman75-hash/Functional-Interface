Q1. A job is finished by 18 workers in 8 days when each works 5 hours daily. How many workers are needed for 12 days at 6 hours daily?
A. 27 workers
B. 10 workers
C. 18 workers
D. 13 workers
Answer: 10 workers
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_painting_walls_area_work
Explanation:
Write output as rate multiplied by active time.
\[
M_1D_1H_1=M_2D_2H_2
\]
Add the outputs of all active agents.
\[
18\times 8\times 5=M_2\times 12\times 6
\]
Simplify the total output.
\[
M_2=10
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
M_2=\frac{18\times 8\times 5}{12\times 6}=10
\]
Answer: 10 workers
