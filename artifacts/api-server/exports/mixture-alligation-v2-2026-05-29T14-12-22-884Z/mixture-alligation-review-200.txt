Q1. A merchant blends two varieties. A trader wants a tea blend costing ₹64/kg by using grades priced at ₹40/kg and ₹100/kg. What ratio should he use?
  A. 3:2
  B. 2:3
  C. 2:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 3:2
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 100</div><div>Lower value = 40</div><div>Mean value = 64</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">100</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">40</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">64</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">36</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 100 and 64:</p><div class="mix-math">\[100-64=36\]</div><p>Difference between 64 and 40:</p><div class="mix-math">\[64-40=24\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=36:24=3:2\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(100-64):(64-40)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=36:24=3:2\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[(D-M):(M-C)\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[(100-64):(64-40)=36:24=3:2\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>
