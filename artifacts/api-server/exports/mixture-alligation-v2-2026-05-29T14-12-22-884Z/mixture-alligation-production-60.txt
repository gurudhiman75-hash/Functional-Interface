Q1. A merchant calculates the final average. A shopkeeper mixes paint costing ₹48 per kg with paint costing ₹80 per kg to make a mixture worth ₹60 per kg. In what ratio should the two types be mixed?
  A. 5:3
  B. 3:5
  C. 2:1
  D. 5:4
Answer: 5:3
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 80</div><div>Lower value = 48</div><div>Mean value = 60</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">80</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">48</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">60</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">12</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">20</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 80 and 60:</p><div class="mix-math">\[80-60=20\]</div><p>Difference between 60 and 48:</p><div class="mix-math">\[60-48=12\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=20:12=5:3\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(80-60):(60-48)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=20:12=5:3\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[(D-M):(M-C)\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[(80-60):(60-48)=20:12=5:3\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>
