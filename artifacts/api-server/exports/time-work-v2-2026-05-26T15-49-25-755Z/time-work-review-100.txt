Q1. A and B begin a job together. After 5 days A leaves. If A and B alone take 18 and 24 days, find when the job ends.
A. 20.33 days
B. 17.33 days
C. 21.33 days
D. 13.87 days
Answer: 17.33 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_forward_leave
Explanation:
Use the governing rate-state formula.
\[
W=(r_A+r_B)t_1+r_Bt_2
\]
Substitute the given values.
\[
W_1=(4+3)\times 5
\]
Simplify to get the required value.
\[
T=5+\frac{37}{3}=17.33
\]
Therefore the required answer is obtained.
\[
Ans=17.33
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=5+\frac{W-W_1}{B}
\]
Answer: 17.33 days
