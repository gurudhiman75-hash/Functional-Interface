Q1. A invests ₹7500 for 8 months and B invests ₹4500 for 10 months. If the total profit is ₹5600, find A's share.
A. ₹7500
B. ₹2400
C. ₹3200
D. ₹2800
Answer: ₹3200
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_partnership_time_variation
Explanation:
Profit ratio is capital multiplied by time.

\[
A:B=7500\times 8:4500\times 10
\]

Simplify the effective capital ratio.

\[
A:B=60000:45000=4:3
\]

Use the total profit.

\[
4k+3k=5600,\quad k=800
\]

Find A's share.

\[
A=4k=3200
\]

Answer: ₹3200

Q2. At present, the age ratio of A to B is \(7:9\). It becomes \(4:5\) after 6 years. Find A's present age.
A. 48 years
B. 42 years
C. 54 years
D. 6 years
Answer: 42 years
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_age_future_ratio

\[
A=7k,\quad B=9k

\[
\frac{7k+6}{9k+6}=\frac{4}{5}

Solve for the multiplier.

\[

\]

Find A's present age.

\[
A=7k=42
\]

Answer: 42 years

Q500. For the same duration, A and B invest in the ratio \(2:3\). If the total profit is ₹3500, find A's share.
A. ₹700
B. ₹1750
C. ₹2100
D. ₹1400
Answer: ₹1400
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_partnership_basic
Explanation:
For equal time, profit is divided in the investment ratio.

\[
A:B=2:3
\]

Use the total profit.

\[
2k+3k=3500,\quad k=700
\]

Find A's profit share.

\[
A=2k=1400
\]

Answer: ₹1400
