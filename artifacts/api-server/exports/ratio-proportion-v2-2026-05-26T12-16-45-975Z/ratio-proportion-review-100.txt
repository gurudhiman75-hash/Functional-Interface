Q1. Two similar figures have corresponding sides in the ratio \(9:13\). Find the ratio of their areas.

B. 729:2197
C. 81:169
D. 9:13
Answer: 81:169
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_side_area_volume_ratio
Explanation:
Start with the side ratio.

\[
S_1:S_2=9:13
\]

Area ratio is the square of side ratio.

\[
A_1:A_2=9^{2}:13^{2}=81:169
\]

Answer: 81:169
