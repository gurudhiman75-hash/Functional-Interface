A:B=28:36

B:C=36:39

\[
A:B:C=28:36:39
\]

Answer: 28:36:39

Q7. With 18 workers, a job takes 90 days. Find the time needed when 27 workers do the same job.
A. 60
B. 9
C. 135
D. 90
Answer: 60
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_inverse_variation_basic
Explanation:
Workers and days vary inversely.

\[
W_1D_1=W_2D_2

D_2=\frac{18\times 90}{27}=60
\]

Answer: 60
