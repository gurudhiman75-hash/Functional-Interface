Q1. Shop A and Shop B have shares in the ratio \(2:3\). If the total stock is 200, find Shop A's share.
A. 120
B. 80
C. 40
D. 100
Answer: 80
Difficulty: Easy
Family: rp_direct_sharing
Explanation:
Write the given ratio.

\[
Shop A=2k,\quad Shop B=3k
\]

Use the total stock.

\[
2k+3k=200
\]

Solve for the common multiplier.

\[
k=\frac{200}{5}=40
\]

Compute Shop A's share.

\[
Shop A=2k=2\times 40=80
\]

Answer: 80

B. 2250
C. 1500
D. 1200
Answer: 2250
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_joint_variation
Explanation:
For joint variation, output is proportional to machines and hours.

\[
O=kMH
\]

Use the ratio form.

\[
\frac{O_2}{O_1}=\frac{M_2H_2}{M_1H_1}
\]

Compute the new output.

\[
O_2=1200\times \frac{10\times 9}{8\times 6}=2250
\]

Answer: 2250
