Q1. In a ratio \(6:13\), the second quantity is 65. What is the other quantity?
A. 65
B. 30
C. 5
D. 95
Answer: 30
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_partial_value_ratio_recovery
Explanation:
Let the common multiplier be k.

\[
A=6k,\quad B=13k
\]

Use the given part to find k.

\[
13k=65,\quad k=5
\]

Compute the required value.

\[
A=6\times 5=30
\]

Answer: 30
