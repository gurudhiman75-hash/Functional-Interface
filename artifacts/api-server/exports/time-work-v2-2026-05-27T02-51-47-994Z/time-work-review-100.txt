Q1. A team adds 15 metres to a project each day, but 5 metres are lost each day. What is the time for 150 metres?
A. 19 days
B. 15 days
C. 16 days
D. 12 days
Answer: 15 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_digging_filling_negative_work
Explanation:
Write output as rate multiplied by active time.
\[
T=\frac{W}{r_+-r_-}
\]
Add the outputs of all active agents.
\[
T=\frac{150}{15-5}
\]
Simplify the total output.
\[
T=15
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{W}{r_n}
\]
Answer: 15 days
