Q1. A trader combines the given materials. A store prepares a tea mixture of average price ₹64/kg from two lots costing ₹40/kg and ₹100/kg. In what ratio are the lots mixed?
  A. 3:2
  B. 2:3
  C. 2:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 3:2
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 100</li><li>Lower value = 40</li><li>Mean value = 64</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">100</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">40</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">64</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">36</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 100 and 64:</p><div class="mix-math">\[100-64=36\]</div><p>Difference between 64 and 40:</p><div class="mix-math">\[64-40=24\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=36:24=3:2\]</div><p>Place the lower value, mean value and higher value in the alligation cross.</p><div class="mix-math">\[\begin{array}{ccc}
C=40 & & D=100 \\
& M=64 & \\
D-M=36 & & M-C=24
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(100-64):(64-40)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=36:24=3:2\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(100-64):(64-40)=36:24=3:2</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>

\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(80-60):(60-48)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=20:12=5:3\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(80-60):(60-48)=20:12=5:3</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 90</li><li>Lower value = 50</li><li>Mean value = 65</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">90</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">50</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">65</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">15</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">25</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 90 and 65:</p><div class="mix-math">\[90-65=25\]</div><p>Difference between 65 and 50:</p><div class="mix-math">\[65-50=15\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=25:15=5:3\]</div><p>Place the lower value, mean value and higher value in the alligation cross.</p><div class="mix-math">\[\begin{array}{ccc}
C=50 & & D=90 \\
& M=65 & \\
D-M=25 & & M-C=15
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(90-65):(65-50)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=25:15=5:3\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(90-65):(65-50)=25:15=5:3</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>

C=60 & & D=100 \\
& M=75 & \\
D-M=25 & & M-C=15
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(100-75):(75-60)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=25:15=5:3\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(100-75):(75-60)=25:15=5:3</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3.
