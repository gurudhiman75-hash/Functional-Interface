Q1. A merchant calculates the final average. Cheap rice at ₹25/kg is mixed with dearer rice at ₹85/kg. If the blend is worth ₹50/kg, find the ratio?
  A. 7:5
  B. 5:7
  C. 8:5
  D. 7:6
Answer: 7:5
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 85</li><li>Lower value = 25</li><li>Mean value = 50</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">85</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">25</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">50</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">25</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">35</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 85 and 50:</p><div class="mix-math">\[85-50=35\]</div><p>Difference between 50 and 25:</p><div class="mix-math">\[50-25=25\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=35:25=7:5\]</div><p>Place the lower value, mean value and higher value in the alligation cross.</p><div class="mix-math">\[\begin{array}{ccc}
C=25 & & D=85 \\
& M=50 & \\
D-M=35 & & M-C=25
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(85-50):(50-25)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=35:25=7:5\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(85-50):(50-25)=35:25=7:5</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 7:5. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 7:5. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>

<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. C=64 & & D=100 \\
& M=76 & \\
D-M=24 & & M-C=12
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(100-76):(76-64)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=24:12=2:1\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(100-76):(76-64)=24:12=2:1</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 2:1. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>

  D. 1:1

Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 100</li><li>Lower value = 40</li><li>Mean value = 64</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">100</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">40</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">64</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">36</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 100 and 64:</p><div class="mix-math">\[100-64=36\]</div><p>Difference between 64 and 40:</p><div class="mix-math">\[64-40=24\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=36:24=3:2\]</div><p>Place the lower value, mean value and higher value in the alligation cross.</p><div class="mix-math">\[\begin{array}{ccc}
C=40 & & D=100 \\
& M=64 & \\
D-M=36 & & M-C=24
\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(100-64):(64-40)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=36:24=3:2\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(100-64):(64-40)=36:24=3:2</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>

C=40 & & D=60 \\
& M=48 & \\

\end{array}\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(60-48):(48-40)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=12:8=3:2\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><p>\[</p><p>(D-M):(M-C)</p><p>\]</p><p>\[</p><p>(60-48):(48-40)=12:8=3:2</p><p>\]</p><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2.
