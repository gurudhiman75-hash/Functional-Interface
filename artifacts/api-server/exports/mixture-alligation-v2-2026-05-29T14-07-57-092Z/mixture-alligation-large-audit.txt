Q1. A trader combines the given materials. A store prepares a lentils mixture of average price ₹48/kg from two lots costing ₹30/kg and ₹72/kg. In what ratio are the lots mixed?
  A. 4:3
  B. 3:4
  C. 5:3
  D. 1:1
Answer: 4:3
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 72</li><li>Lower value = 30</li><li>Mean value = 48</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">72</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">30</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">48</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">18</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 72 and 48:</p><div class="mix-math">\[72-48=24\]</div><p>Difference between 48 and 30:</p><div class="mix-math">\[48-30=18\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=24:18=4:3\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(72-48):(48-30)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=24:18=4:3\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[(D-M):(M-C)\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[(72-48):(48-30)=24:18=4:3\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 4:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 4:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>
