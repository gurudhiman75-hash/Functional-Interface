Q1. A merchant blends two varieties. A store prepares a fertilizer mixture of average price ₹75/kg from two lots costing ₹60/kg and ₹100/kg. In what ratio are the lots mixed?
  A. 5:3
  B. 3:5
  C. 2:1
  D. 5:4
Answer: 5:3
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 100</li><li>Lower value = 60</li><li>Mean value = 75</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">100</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">60</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">75</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">15</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">25</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 100 and 75:</p><div class="mix-math">\[100-75=25\]</div><p>Difference between 75 and 60:</p><div class="mix-math">\[75-60=15\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=25:15=5:3\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(100-75):(75-60)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=25:15=5:3\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[(D-M):(M-C)\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[(100-75):(75-60)=25:15=5:3\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></section></div>

Q12. A vessel contains a mixture. Two grades of grain cost ₹24/kg and ₹40/kg. To get a blend worth ₹30/kg, in what ratio should they be mixed?

<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><ul><li>Higher value = 40</li><li>Lower value = 24</li><li>Mean value = 30</li></ul></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram" role="img" aria-label="Alligation cross diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">40</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">30</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">6</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">10</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>Difference between 40 and 30:</p><div class="mix-math">\[40-30=10\]</div><p>Difference between 30 and 24:</p><div class="mix-math">\[30-24=6\]</div><p>Opposite differences give the required ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C_q:D_q=10:6=5:3\]</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=(40-30):(30-24)\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[C:D=10:6=5:3\]</div></section><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[(D-M):(M-C)\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[(40-30):(30-24)=10:6=5:3\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 5:3.   D. 40 litres

Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><section class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>Lower value = 0. Higher value = 75.
