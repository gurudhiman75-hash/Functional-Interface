Q1. A seller prepares the final blend. A trader wants a wheat blend costing ₹38/kg by using grades priced at ₹30/kg and ₹50/kg. What ratio should he use?
  A. 3:2
  B. 2:3
  C. 2:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 3:2
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 50</div><div>Lower value = 30</div><div>Mean value = 38</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">50</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">30</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">38</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">8</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">12</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Cross Differences</div><p>Difference between 38 and 30:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
38-30=8
\]</div><p>Difference between 50 and 38:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
50-38=12
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Interpret Ratio</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=12:8=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(50-38):(38-30)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=12:8=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(50-38):(38-30)=12:8=3:2
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>

Q9. A chemist mixes 60 kg of one liquid of density 3 kg/litre with 120 kg of another liquid of density 6 kg/litre. Find the density of the resulting mixture?

  B. 5.5 kg/litre

Answer: 4.5 kg/litre
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is not a simple average of densities. Density means mass divided by volume, so masses must first be converted into volumes.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>The useful values are shown in the calculation below.</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Volume Calculation</div><p>Density is mass divided by volume, so first convert each given mass into volume.</p><p>Volume of the first liquid is mass divided by its density.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
V_1=\frac{60}{3}=20
\]</div><p>Volume of the second liquid is found in the same way.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
V_2=\frac{120}{6}=20
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Total Volume and Mass</div><p>Now add masses and volumes separately.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
M=60+120=180,\quad V=20+20=40
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Density Calculation</div><p>Density of the mixture is total mass divided by total volume.</p><div class="mix-math">\[

Q16. A chemist mixes 60 kg of one liquid of density 2 kg/litre with 60 kg of another liquid of density 6 kg/litre. Find the density of the resulting mixture?

  B. 4 kg/litre
  C. 2 kg/litre

Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is not a simple average of densities. Density means mass divided by volume, so masses must first be converted into volumes.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>The useful values are shown in the calculation below.</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Volume Calculation</div><p>Density is mass divided by volume, so first convert each given mass into volume.</p><p>Volume of the first liquid is mass divided by its density.</p><div class="mix-math">\[

V_2=\frac{60}{6}=10
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Total Volume and Mass</div><p>Now add masses and volumes separately.</p><div class="mix-math">\[

Q17. A chemist mixes 80 kg of one liquid of density 4 kg/litre with 40 kg of another liquid of density 8 kg/litre. Find the density of the resulting mixture?

Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is not a simple average of densities. M=80+40=120,\quad V=20+5=25
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Density Calculation</div><p>Density of the mixture is total mass divided by total volume.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
\rho=\frac{120}{25}=4.8

M=80+40=120,\quad V=20+5=25

Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 4.8 kg/litre. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>

Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 120</div><div>Lower value = 72</div><div>Mean value = 90</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">120</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">72</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">90</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">18</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">30</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Cross Differences</div><p>Difference between 90 and 72:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
90-72=18

120-90=30
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Interpret Ratio</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[

C:D=30:18=5:3

Q19. A grocer is mixing the items. Two grades of sugar cost ₹20/kg and ₹30/kg. To get a blend worth ₹24/kg, in what ratio should they be mixed?

  B. 2:3
  C. 2:1
  D. 1:1
Answer: 3:2
Explanation:
<div class="mix-explanation-page"><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-concept"><div class="mix-block-title">Concept</div><p>This is an alligation question. The required mean lies between the lower and higher values, so the quantities must balance the two gaps from the mean.</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-given"><div class="mix-block-title">Given</div><div class="mix-given-list"><div>Higher value = 30</div><div>Lower value = 20</div><div>Mean value = 24</div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-diagram-block"><div class="mix-block-title">Alligation Diagram</div><div class="mix-alligation-diagram"><div class="mix-alligation-top-left">30</div><div class="mix-alligation-top-right">20</div><div class="mix-alligation-center">24</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-left">4</div><div class="mix-alligation-bottom-right">6</div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-a"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-b"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-c"></div><div class="mix-alligation-line mix-alligation-line-d"></div></div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Cross Differences</div><p>Difference between 24 and 20:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
24-20=4
\]</div><p>Difference between 30 and 24:</p><div class="mix-math">\[
30-24=6
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Interpret Ratio</div><p>Opposite differences give the required quantity ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C_q:D_q=6:4=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-working"><div class="mix-block-title">Working</div><p>The mean lies between the two prices, so compare each price with the mean.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=(30-24):(24-20)
\]</div><p>Simplify the alligation ratio.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
C:D=6:4=3:2
\]</div></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-shortcut"><div class="mix-block-title">Shortcut / Exam Method</div><p>Write the opposite differences directly.</p><div class="mix-math">\[
(D-M):(M-C)
\]</div><div class="mix-math">\[
(30-24):(24-20)=6:4=3:2
\]</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div><div class="mix-explanation-block mix-answer"><div class="mix-block-title">Final Answer</div><p>Therefore, the required answer is 3:2. Hence, the correct answer is Option (A).</p></div></div>
