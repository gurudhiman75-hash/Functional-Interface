Q1. 20 workers finish a work in 144 days. If the number of workers is 30, how many days will be required?
A. 10
B. 96
C. 216
D. 144
Answer: 96
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_inverse_variation_basic
Explanation:
Workers and days vary inversely.

\[
W_1D_1=W_2D_2
\]

Substitute the values.

\[
20\times 144=30\times D_2
\]

Solve for the days.

\[
D_2=\frac{20\times 144}{30}=96
\]

Answer: 96

Q2. Output varies jointly as the number of machines and working hours. 8 machines working for 6 hours produce 2160 units. How many units will 10 machines produce in 9 hours?
A. 2700
B. 2160
C. 3240
D. 4050
Answer: 4050
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_joint_variation
Explanation:
For joint variation, output is proportional to machines and hours.

\[
O=kMH
\]

Use the ratio form.

\[
\frac{O_2}{O_1}=\frac{M_2H_2}{M_1H_1}
\]

Compute the new output.

\[
O_2=2160\times \frac{10\times 9}{8\times 6}=4050
\]

Answer: 4050

Q3. A and B have money in the ratio \(11:7\). After ₹4800 is given by A to B, the ratio becomes \(1:1\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹21600
B. ₹26400
C. ₹16800
D. ₹31200
Answer: ₹26400
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=11k,\quad B=7k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{11k-4800}{7k+4800}=\frac{1}{1}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
1(11k-4800)=1(7k+4800)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
4k=9600,\quad k=2400
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=11k=11\times 2400=26400
\]

Answer: ₹26400

B. 100

D. 125
Answer: 80
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:

\[

\[

\]

Answer: 80
