Q1. A invests ₹4000 for 12 months and B invests ₹10000 for 6 months. If the total profit is ₹8100, find A's share.
A. ₹4050
B. ₹3600
C. ₹4000
D. ₹4500
Answer: ₹3600
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_partnership_time_variation
Explanation:
Profit ratio is capital multiplied by time.

\[
A:B=4000\times 12:10000\times 6
\]

Simplify the effective capital ratio.

\[
A:B=48000:60000=4:5
\]

Use the total profit.

\[
4k+5k=8100,\quad k=900
\]

Find A's share.

\[
A=4k=3600
\]

Answer: ₹3600
