Q1. The incomes of A and B are in the ratio \(7:8\). A's income increases by 20% and B's income increases by 10%. Find the new ratio.
A. 770:960
B. 21:22
C. 7:8
D. 27:18
Answer: 21:22
Difficulty: Medium
Family: rp_ratio_after_increase
Explanation:
Let the original incomes be ratio parts.

\[
A=7,\quad B=8
\]

Apply the percentage increases.

\[
A':B'=7\left(1+\frac{20}{100}\right):8\left(1+\frac{10}{100}\right)
\]

Simplify the new ratio.

\[
A':B'=840:880=21:22
\]

Answer: 21:22

Q2. A and B have money in the ratio \(7:5\). After ₹600 is given by A to B, the ratio becomes \(13:11\). Find A's original amount.
A. ₹7800
B. ₹6000
C. ₹8400
D. ₹9000
Answer: ₹8400
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_ratio_after_transfer
Explanation:
Let the original amounts be ratio parts.

\[
A=7k,\quad B=5k
\]

Form the equation after transfer.

\[
\frac{7k-600}{5k+600}=\frac{13}{11}
\]

Cross-multiply and solve.

\[
11(7k-600)=13(5k+600)
\]

Find the common multiplier.

\[
12k=14400,\quad k=1200
\]

Compute A's original amount.

\[
A=7k=7\times 1200=8400
\]

Answer: ₹8400

Q5. \(x\) varies directly as \(y\) and inversely as \(z\). If \(x=288\) when \(y=8\) and \(z=3\), find \(x\) when \(y=12\) and \(z=6\).
A. 432
B. 288
C. 864
D. 216
Answer: 216
Difficulty: Hard
Family: rp_combined_direct_inverse
Explanation:
Use combined direct and inverse variation.

\[
x=k\frac{y}{z}
\]

Compare the two cases.

\[
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{y_2}{y_1}\times \frac{z_1}{z_2}
\]

Compute the new value.

\[
x_2=288\times \frac{12}{8}\times \frac{3}{6}=216
\]

Answer: 216
