Q1. A takes 15 days to finish a work and B takes 20 days for the same work. In how many days can A and B finish it together?
A. 17 1/2 days
B. 15 days
C. 35 days
D. 8.57 days
Answer: 8.57 days
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_lcm_hidden_total_work
Explanation:
Take the LCM as total work.
\[
W=LCM(15,20)=60
\]
Find each worker's one-period efficiency.
\[
A=60/15=4,\quad B=60/20=3
\]
Divide total work by the combined efficiency.
\[
T=\frac{60}{4+3}=8.57
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{15\times 20}{15+20}=8.57
\]
Answer: 8.57 days
