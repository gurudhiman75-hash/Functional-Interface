Q1. A and B work by turns. A starts and completes 6 documents per turn; B completes 5. How many days will 90 documents take?
A. 16 1/3 days
B. 20.42 days
C. 11 1/3 days
D. 13 1/3 days
Answer: 16 1/3 days
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_terminal_overshoot_cycle
Explanation:
Find the work completed in one full cycle.
\[
W_{cycle}=\sum r_i
\]
Count full cycles before the last turn.
\[
W_{cycle}=6+5
\]
Add only the needed part of the final turn.
\[
T=16\frac{1}{3}
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=cycles+final\ turn
\]
Answer: 16 1/3 days
