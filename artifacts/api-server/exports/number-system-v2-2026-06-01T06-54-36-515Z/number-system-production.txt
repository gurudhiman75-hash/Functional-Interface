\[

\]

Step 2: Write the two-digit number

Hence, the number is 83.

\[
N=10\times 8+3=83
\]

Answer Verification
Digit sum of 83 is 11.

Final Answer
Therefore, the correct answer is Option (B). The required value is \(83\).

Shortcut

Find the ones digit from the digit sum, then place it.

\[
u=3
\]

\[
N=83
\]

Fast result: \(83\). Therefore, the correct answer is Option (B).

Q2. The number \(145x7\) is divisible by \(9\). What is the value of \(x\)?
A. \(1\)
B. \(2\)
C. \(0\)
D. \(3\)
Answer: A

Explanation:
Step 1: Use the divisibility rule for 9

A number is divisible by 9 when the sum of its digits is a multiple of 9.

\[
S=17
\]

\[
S+x=17+x
\]

Step 2: Form and solve the digit-sum equation

The completed digit sum must be 18, a multiple of 9.

\[
17+x=18
\]

\[
x=1
\]

Answer Verification
With \(x=1\), the digit-sum rule is satisfied.
\[
17+1=18
\]
\[
18\equiv 0\pmod{9}
\]

Final Answer
Therefore, the correct answer is Option (A). The required value is \(1\).

Shortcut

Complete the known digit sum to the next multiple of 9.

\[
x\equiv 1\pmod{9}
\]

\[
x=1
\]

Fast result: \(1\). Therefore, the correct answer is Option (A).

Q3. Between \(227\) and \(307\), how many integers are divisible by \(12\)?
A. \(8\)
B. \(6\)
C. \(9\)
D. \(7\)
Answer: D

Explanation:
Step 1: Find the first valid multiple

Therefore, the first multiple of 12 greater than 227 is 228.

\[
12\times 19=228
\]

Step 2: Find the last valid multiple

Therefore, 300 is the greatest multiple of 12 not exceeding 307.

\[
307=12\times 25+7
\]

\[
307-7=300
\]

Step 3: Count the valid multiples

The multiples form an arithmetic progression with common difference 12.

\[
\frac{300-228}{12}+1=6+1=7
\]

Answer Verification
The total number of multiples is 7.
Hence, there are 7 multiples of 12 in the given range.

Final Answer
Therefore, the correct answer is Option (D). The required value is \(7\).

Shortcut

Count multiples using quotient floors on the range limits.

\[
\left\lfloor\frac{307}{12}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\frac{226}{12}\right\rfloor
\]

\[
25-18=7
\]

Fast result: \(7\). Therefore, the correct answer is Option (D).
