Q1. Machines A, B and C make 12, 15 and 18 parts per hour. If they run for 8 hours, how many parts are made?

B. 361 items
C. 270 items
D. 360 items
Answer: 360 items
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_machine_breakdown_midway
Explanation:
Write output as rate multiplied by active time.
\[
O=\sum r_it_i
\]

\[
O=12\times 8+15\times 8+18\times 8
\]
Simplify the total output.
\[
O=360
\]

\[
O=(r_1+r_2+r_3)t
\]
Answer: 360 items

B. 13 2/3 days
C. 18 2/3 days
D. 17 2/3 days
Answer: 18 2/3 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_forward_leave
Explanation:
Split the work into timeline phases.
\[
W=(r_A+r_B)t_1+r_Bt_2
\]
Subtract the work already completed.
\[
W_1=(20+15)\times 4
\]
Use the active rate for the remaining phase.
\[
T=4+\frac{220}{15}=18\frac{2}{3}
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=4+\frac{W-W_1}{B}
\]
Answer: 18 2/3 days
