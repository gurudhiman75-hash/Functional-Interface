Q1. A alone takes 12 days and B alone takes 18 days. During a joint job, A does not work in the last 2 days and B does not work in the last 3 days. What is the total time?
A. 9 3/5 days
B. 4 3/5 days
C. 6 3/5 days
D. 12 days
Answer: 9 3/5 days
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_backward_leave
Explanation:
Write the rate relation used in this question.
\[
r_A(T-a)+r_B(T-b)=W
\]
Substitute the numbers from the statement.
\[
6(T-2)+4(T-3)=72
\]
Simplify the relation to get the answer.
\[
T=9.6
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=\frac{W+A\times 2+B\times 3}{A+B}
\]
Answer: 9 3/5 days
