Q1. A job can be finished in 20 days. If work continues for 3 days, what fraction is still incomplete?
A. 17/20
B. 17/3
C. 3/20
D. 20/17
Answer: 17/20
Difficulty: Easy
Family: tw_basic_residual_work
Explanation:
Write the rate relation used in this question.
\[
R=1-\frac{t}{T}
\]
Substitute the numbers from the statement.
\[
R=1-\frac{3}{20}
\]
Simplify the relation to get the answer.
\[
R=\frac{17}{20}=17/20
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
R=\frac{20-3}{20}=17/20
\]
Answer: 17/20

Q2. A pipe fills a tank in 10 hours. A leak slows the filling to 16 hours. What time will the leak take to empty the tank alone?
A. 24 2/3 hours
B. 20 hours
C. 21 2/3 hours
D. 26 2/3 hours
Answer: 26 2/3 hours
Difficulty: Medium
Family: pc_leak_hidden_rate
Explanation:
Write filling rates as positive and emptying rates as negative.
\[
\frac{1}{L}=\frac{1}{F}-\frac{1}{T}
\]
Find the effective tank rate.
\[
\frac{1}{L}=\frac{1}{10}-\frac{1}{16}
\]
Divide the required tank work by that rate.
\[
L=26\frac{2}{3}
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
L=\frac{FT}{T-F}
\]
Answer: 26 2/3 hours
