Q1. A filling tap adds 12 litres in an hour, then an emptying tap removes 5 litres in the next hour. The tank holds 60 litres. When will the tank become full?
A. 16 1/3 hours
B. 12 1/4 hours
C. 11 1/3 hours
D. 14 1/3 hours
Answer: 16 1/3 hours
Difficulty: Hard
Family: pc_alternating_spill_cycle
Explanation:
Write filling rates as positive and emptying rates as negative.
\[
W_{cycle}=12-5
\]
Find the effective tank rate.
\[
60\ units,\ cycle=7
\]
Divide the required tank work by that rate.
\[
T=16\frac{1}{3}
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=C_f+t_f
\]
Answer: 16 1/3 hours
