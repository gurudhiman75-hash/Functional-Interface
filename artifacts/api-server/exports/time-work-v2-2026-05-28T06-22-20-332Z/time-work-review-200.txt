Q1. A, B and C together complete a work in 10 days. A and B together can complete it in 20 days. In how many days will C alone complete the work?
A. 23 days
B. 20 days
C. 25 days
D. 26 days
Answer: 20 days
Difficulty: Medium
Family: tw_pairwise_total_abc
Explanation:
Write the rate relation used in this question.
\[
r_X=r_{team}-\sum r_i
\]
Substitute the numbers from the statement.
\[
r_X=\frac{1}{10}-\frac{1}{20}
\]
Simplify the relation to get the answer.
\[
T_X=20
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T_X=1/r_X
\]
Answer: 20 days
