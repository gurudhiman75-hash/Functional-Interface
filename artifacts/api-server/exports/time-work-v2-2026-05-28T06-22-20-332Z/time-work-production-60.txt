Q1. During the first 2 days, 1 metres of wall are painted each day. After the team changes, 4 metres are painted each day. How many days are needed for 12 metres?
A. 6 3/4 days
B. 6 1/2 days
C. 8 1/2 days
D. 4 1/2 days
Answer: 4 1/2 days
Difficulty: Hard
Family: tw_multi_phase_join_leave
Explanation:
Split the work into timeline phases.
\[
\sum r_it_i=W
\]
Subtract the work already completed.
\[
W_1=1\times 2
\]
Use the active rate for the remaining phase.
\[
T=2+\frac{10}{4}=4.5
\]
Shortcut / Exam Method:
\[
T=t_1+\frac{W-W_1}{r_2}
\]
Answer: 4 1/2 days
