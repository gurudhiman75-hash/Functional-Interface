## STAT-QL-111 — Population and sample

A researcher surveys 400 randomly selected registered voters to estimate the views of all voters in a country. What are the 400 surveyed voters?

A. Parameter
B. Population
C. Statistic
D. Sample

**Answer:** D. Sample

**Explanation:** The surveyed subset is the sample; the full group the researcher wants to describe is the population.

## STAT-QL-112 — Parameter and statistic

A value computed from the heights of 80 sampled students is used to estimate the average height of all students. What kind of value is the computed sample average?

A. Statistic
B. Census
C. Parameter
D. Sampling frame

**Answer:** A. Statistic

**Explanation:** A numerical summary of a sample is a statistic. A numerical summary of the full population is a parameter.

## STAT-QL-113 — Sampling and non-sampling errors

An interviewer records a respondent's answer incorrectly. What type of error is this?

A. Non-sampling error
B. Sampling distribution
C. Selection probability
D. Sampling error

**Answer:** A. Non-sampling error

**Explanation:** A recording mistake is a measurement or processing problem, not sampling variability; it is non-sampling error.

## STAT-QL-114 — Simple random selection probability

A simple random sample of 300 units is selected without replacement from a population of 1000. Find the inclusion probability for one specified unit.

A. 0.35
B. 0.25
C. 0.4
D. 0.3

**Answer:** D. 0.3

**Explanation:** In a simple random sample, each unit has inclusion probability n/N = 300/1000 = 0.3.

## STAT-QL-115 — Proportional stratified allocation

A population of 500 units is divided into strata. A proportional sample of size 120 is planned. Stratum H contains 150 units. How many units should be sampled from H?

A. 40
B. 36
C. 34
D. 38

**Answer:** B. 36

**Explanation:** Proportional allocation uses nH = n(NH/N) = 120×(150/500) = 36.

## STAT-QL-116 — Cluster and stratified sampling

A survey randomly selects several entire schools and collects data from every student in each selected school. Which sampling method is described?

A. Stratified sampling
B. Systematic sampling
C. Cluster sampling
D. Simple random sampling

**Answer:** C. Cluster sampling

**Explanation:** The selected groups are intact clusters, and all units in those chosen clusters are observed; this is cluster sampling.

## STAT-QL-117 — Systematic sampling interval

A systematic sample of 60 units is drawn from an ordered list of 1200. What interval k is used after choosing a random start?

A. 22
B. 20
C. 18
D. 24

**Answer:** B. 20

**Explanation:** The systematic interval is k = N/n = 1200/60 = 20.

## STAT-QL-118 — Multistage sampling

A survey first samples districts, then villages within selected districts, then households within selected villages. Which sampling design is this?

A. Convenience sampling
B. Stratified sampling
C. Cluster sampling
D. Multistage sampling

**Answer:** D. Multistage sampling

**Explanation:** Selection occurs in successive stages, moving from districts to villages to households; this is multistage sampling.

## STAT-QL-119 — Convenience sampling

A researcher interviews the first people available outside a nearby shop. Which non-probability method is used?

A. Convenience sampling
B. Purposive sampling
C. Simple random sampling
D. Quota sampling

**Answer:** A. Convenience sampling

**Explanation:** Participants are selected because they are easy to reach, which is convenience sampling.

## STAT-QL-120 — Purposive sampling

A researcher deliberately selects specialists with particular expertise because they can address the study question. Which non-probability method is used?

A. Purposive sampling
B. Simple random sampling
C. Convenience sampling
D. Quota sampling

**Answer:** A. Purposive sampling

**Explanation:** Participants are intentionally selected for specified characteristics or expertise, which is purposive sampling.

## STAT-QL-121 — Quota sampling

Interviewers recruit respondents until preset counts for age groups are filled, without random selection. Which non-probability method is used?

A. Quota sampling
B. Purposive sampling
C. Convenience sampling
D. Simple random sampling

**Answer:** A. Quota sampling

**Explanation:** Recruitment fills preset category counts without random selection, which is quota sampling.

## STAT-QL-122 — Sampling distribution and standard error

A population has standard deviation σ = 15. Assume independent draws or a negligible finite-population correction. For repeated samples of size 81, find the standard error of the sample mean.

A. 3.67
B. 5.67
C. 1.67
D. 0

**Answer:** C. 1.67

**Explanation:** For independent observations, the standard error is σ/√n = 15/√81 = 1.67. The sampling distribution is centered at the population mean.

## STAT-QL-123 — Sample size for estimating a mean

Using n ≥ (zσ/E)^2, find the minimum whole-number sample size for z = 1.96, σ = 10, and desired margin of error E = 4.

A. 25
B. 23
C. 29
D. 27

**Answer:** A. 25

**Explanation:** n ≥ (zσ/E)^2 = (1.96×10/4)² = 24.01. Round up to the next whole number: n = 25.
