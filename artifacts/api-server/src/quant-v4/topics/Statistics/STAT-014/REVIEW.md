## STAT-QL-164 — Simple aggregative index

Base-period prices total 200; current-period prices total 300. Find the simple aggregative price index.

A. 154
B. 152
C. 150
D. 148

**Answer:** C. 150

**Explanation:** Index = (Σp₁/Σp₀) × 100 = (300/200) × 100 = 150.

## STAT-QL-165 — Average of price relatives

Two commodities have price relatives 100% and 125%. Find their arithmetic mean price relative.

A. 116.5
B. 112.5
C. 110.5
D. 114.5

**Answer:** B. 112.5

**Explanation:** Average price relative = (100 + 125)/2 = 112.5%.

## STAT-QL-166 — Laspeyres price index

For a basket, Σp₁q₀ = 600 and Σp₀q₀ = 500. Find the Laspeyres price index.

A. 124
B. 120
C. 118
D. 122

**Answer:** B. 120

**Explanation:** L = (Σp₁q₀/Σp₀q₀) × 100 = (600/500) × 100 = 120.

## STAT-QL-167 — Paasche price index

For current-period quantities, Σp₁q₁ = 750 and Σp₀q₁ = 600. Find the Paasche price index.

A. 127
B. 123
C. 129
D. 125

**Answer:** D. 125

**Explanation:** P = (Σp₁q₁/Σp₀q₁) × 100 = (750/600) × 100 = 125.

## STAT-QL-168 — Fisher ideal price index

Laspeyres and Paasche price indices are 120 and 125. Find Fisher's ideal index.

A. 122.47
B. 120.47
C. 124.47
D. 126.47

**Answer:** A. 122.47

**Explanation:** F = √(L × P) = √(120 × 125) = 122.47.

## STAT-QL-169 — Value index

Base-period total value is 400 and current-period total value is 540. Find the value index.

A. 135
B. 139
C. 133
D. 137

**Answer:** A. 135

**Explanation:** Value index = (Σp₁q₁/Σp₀q₀) × 100 = (540/400) × 100 = 135.

## STAT-QL-170 — Weighted quantity index

Using base-period prices as weights, Σq₁p₀ = 720 and Σq₀p₀ = 600. Find the weighted quantity index.

A. 122
B. 118
C. 120
D. 124

**Answer:** C. 120

**Explanation:** Quantity index = (Σq₁p₀/Σq₀p₀) × 100 = (720/600) × 100 = 120.

## STAT-QL-171 — Chain-link index

A chain-linked index is 120 in year 1. The year 2 link relative is 110. Find the chain-linked index for year 2.

A. 130
B. 132
C. 134
D. 136

**Answer:** B. 132

**Explanation:** Chain index₂ = chain index₁ × link relative₂/100 = 120 × 110/100 = 132.

## STAT-QL-172 — Shifting the index base

An index is 120 for period A and 150 for period B on the old base. Find period A's index after shifting the base to period B.

A. 80
B. 78
C. 84
D. 82

**Answer:** A. 80

**Explanation:** New-base index for A = (old index A / old index B) × 100 = (120/150) × 100 = 80.

## STAT-QL-173 — Inflation from index numbers

A price index rises from 150 to 165. Find the percentage inflation over the period.

A. 10
B. 8
C. 14
D. 12

**Answer:** A. 10

**Explanation:** Inflation = ((165−150)/150) × 100 = 10%.

## STAT-QL-174 — Time-reversal test

Which condition is the time-reversal test for a price index, when the two indices use reciprocal period directions?

A. P₀₁ − P₁₀ = 0
B. P₀₁ × P₁₀ = 1 (ratio form)
C. P₀₁ + P₁₀ = 100
D. P₀₁/P₁₀ = 1 for every pair of periods

**Answer:** B. P₀₁ × P₁₀ = 1 (ratio form)

**Explanation:** A price index satisfies the test when P₀₁ × P₁₀ = 1 if expressed as ratios (or 10,000 if both are percentages).

## STAT-QL-175 — Factor-reversal test

What does the factor-reversal test require of price and quantity indices, expressed as ratios?

A. P₀₁ × Q₀₁ = V₀₁
B. P₀₁ = Q₀₁ = V₀₁
C. P₀₁ × V₀₁ = Q₀₁
D. P₀₁ + Q₀₁ = V₀₁

**Answer:** A. P₀₁ × Q₀₁ = V₀₁

**Explanation:** The product of price and quantity indices should equal the value index: P₀₁ × Q₀₁ = V₀₁.
