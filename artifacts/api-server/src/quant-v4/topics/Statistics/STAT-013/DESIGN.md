# STAT-013 — Time Series Analysis

This controlled-review package covers classical components; additive and multiplicative models; moving averages, including centered four-quarter averages from raw observations; least-squares and semi-average trend fitting; trend forecasts; seasonal indices and normalization; ratio-to-moving-average estimation; additive and multiplicative seasonal forecasts; and both forms of seasonal adjustment.

Twenty permanent QLs use STAT-QL-152–163, STAT-QL-191–194, and STAT-QL-205–208 for SSC CGL Tier II and JSO. Ratio-to-moving-average estimates average observed/centered-moving-average ratios for the same season before normalizing the full seasonal set. Quarterly indices sum to 400. Multiplicative forecasts convert percent indices into factors and assume an irregular factor of 1. Additive adjustment subtracts the seasonal effect, including when that effect is negative. The semi-average contract uses an even number of observations at equally spaced times 1 through n, with each half's mean placed at its time midpoint.

This pass addresses classical foundation coverage. Odd-length semi-average conventions, advanced cyclical analysis, ARIMA and other stochastic forecasting methods remain outside this pass.
