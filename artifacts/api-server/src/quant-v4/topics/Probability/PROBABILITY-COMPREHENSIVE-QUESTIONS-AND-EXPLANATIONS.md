## Explanation Standard

Most solutions follow three simple steps:

1. Find the total possible cases.
2. Find the required cases.
3. Divide and simplify.

For successive draws, complements, conditional probability and counting questions, only the extra step actually needed is shown.

### PRB-CP-001

#### Question 1 — PRB-QL-001 (Easy)

**Question:** A box contains 15 tokens. Of these, 6 are marked. One is chosen at random. What is the probability of choosing a marked token?

**Options:**

- **A.** 1/3
- **B.** 1/5
- **C.** 3/5
- **D.** 2/5

**Correct answer:** D. 2/5

**Explanation:** Favourable outcomes = 6. Total outcomes = 15. Probability = 6/15 = 2/5.
