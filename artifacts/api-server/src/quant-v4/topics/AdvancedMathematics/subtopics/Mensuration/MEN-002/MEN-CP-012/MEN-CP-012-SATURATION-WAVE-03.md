## Source anchors used

The saturation set is based on recurring government-exam/PYQ patterns including:

- SSC CGL: solid sphere melted into a hollow cylindrical tube; recover tube thickness;
- SSC MTS: sphere material recast into a hollow cylindrical shell; recover shell length;
- SSC MTS: solid sphere melted and drawn into a long wire with metre-to-centimetre conversion;
- SSC CGL Tier 2: N equal spheres recast into a cylindrical rod whose radius/height are multiples of the sphere radius;
- SSC CHSL: sphere recast into a cylinder with radius expressed as a multiple of the sphere radius; ask a height-to-radius ratio;
- SSC CPO: sphere recast into cylinder with a final height rounded to one decimal place;
- SSC CPO: sphere recast into a cone; recover base diameter and compare it with height;
- SSC CGL: unequal spheres melted into one sphere, including a follow-up percentage decrease in surface area;
- competitive-exam coin-to-cuboid recasting with diameter/circumference, millimetre thickness and cubic-volume conversion;
- hollow spherical shell recast into a solid cylinder, used as an ownership-boundary probe with CP-011.

These anchors are used as **pattern evidence**, not copied stems. Generated states remain original deterministic variants.

## Important merge signals

Wave 03 strengthens several likely merges:

- sphere→wire is a representation of the broader wire/drawing conservation family;
- relative-dimension N-sphere questions are a representation of count-by-volume conservation;
- ratio output and decimal rounding are representations of inverse target-dimension recovery, not independent identities;
- coin diameter/thickness is a unit-rich representation of count-by-volume conservation;
- circumference-based coin questions add a pre-step and may remain a representation unless source evidence shows it deserves a distinct reasoning family.

Wave 03 also reveals likely genuinely distinct reasoning:

- hollow-target material conservation where an inner radius/thickness must be reconstructed;
- unequal combined-source solids followed by target-root recovery;
- recasting followed by a second requested measure such as percentage change in surface area.
