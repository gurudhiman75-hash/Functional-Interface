# MEN-001 CP-005 — Composite, Shaded, Inscribed and Regular Plane Figures

## Ownership

CP-005 owns plane figures whose answer depends on decomposing, combining or subtracting standard shapes. It includes rectangle-plus-semicircle figures, stadium figures, rectangle-plus-triangle figures, L-shapes, shaded square/circle combinations, inscribed square/circle relations, regular hexagons, exposed composite boundaries and reverse recovery from a composite or shaded area.

CP-005 does not own general paths, flooring or fencing rates (CP-004), nor unit conversion, scaling, wire reshaping or same-boundary transformations (CP-006).
