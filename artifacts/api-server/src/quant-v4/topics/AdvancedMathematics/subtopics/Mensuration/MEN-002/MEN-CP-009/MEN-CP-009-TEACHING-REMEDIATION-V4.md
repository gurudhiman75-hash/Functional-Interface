# MEN-CP-009 Teaching Remediation V4

## Status

`REVIEW_CANDIDATE__NOT_APPROVED__PRODUCT_LOCKED`

## Why V4 exists

Human review of the Hindi/Punjabi V1 artifact identified a presentation problem that also applies to the English learner surface:

- explanations feel robotic and formula-first;
- the previous 2–4 line contract is too compressed for teaching;
- middle calculations are often hidden;
- a stem may tell the learner to use `π = 3.14` or `π = 22/7`, while the explanation still jumps through a symbolic `π` expression without showing where that value was substituted;
- English wording should stay simple and avoid unnecessarily formal vocabulary;
- Hindi and Punjabi should use simpler, more natural wording and be slightly more explanatory.

This feedback supersedes the earlier preference for ultra-short CP-009 explanations. The existing English V3 approval remains historical evidence, but V3 is not silently rewritten. V4 is a separate review candidate.

## English V4 contract

Authority: `MEN-CP009-STUDENT-VIEW-V4-TEACHING`

The mathematical runtime, permanent QLs, options, correct answer ownership and independent verification remain unchanged.

The learner presentation changes only wording and worked explanation display.

Required explanation shape:

1. state the relevant formula in plain language;
2. identify the given value(s);
3. substitute the actual values into the formula;
4. show the useful middle arithmetic or algebraic rearrangement;
5. finish with the answer in the question's units.

Normal target length is **4–5 connected teaching lines**. The purpose is not to add filler; each line must move the solution forward.

## Explicit π policy

When a question requires a numerical convention:

- `π = 22/7` must appear in the worked substitution;
- `π = 3.14` must appear in the worked substitution;
- the calculation must not jump from a symbolic `π` formula directly to a numerical answer.

Where `π = 3.14` produces a terminating rational display, V4 presents the learner-facing number as a normal decimal instead of an unnecessary fraction. Non-terminating exact rational results are not silently rounded unless the question authorises approximation.

## Language policy

### English

Prefer normal exam/student language such as:

- `What is ...?`
- `Use π = 3.14.`
- `Putting the values into the formula...`
- `So the volume is ...`

Avoid vocabulary-heavy generator wording when a simpler expression carries the same meaning.

### Canonical native surface-area terminology

For learner-facing Hindi and Punjabi, **surface area is expressed as “area of the surface”**, not with bookish `पृष्ठीय/ਪ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਠੀ` terminology.

| English | Hindi | Punjabi |
|---|---|---|
| Surface area | `सतह का क्षेत्रफल` | `ਸਤ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ` |
| Curved surface area | `वक्र सतह का क्षेत्रफल` | `ਵਕਰ ਸਤ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ` |
| Total surface area | `कुल सतह का क्षेत्रफल` | `ਕੁੱਲ ਸਤ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ` |

Forms such as `पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल`, `वक्र पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल`, `कुल पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल`, `ਪ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਠੀ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ`, `ਵਕਰ ਪ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਠੀ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ`, `ਕੁੱਲ ਪ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਠੀ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ` and the misspelling `ਸਤਹ` are not allowed on the learner surface. Punjabi must use `ਸਤ੍ਹਾ`.

### Hindi

Prefer simple phrases such as:

- `सतह का क्षेत्रफल`
- `वक्र सतह का क्षेत्रफल`
- `कुल सतह का क्षेत्रफल`
- `मान सूत्र में रखने पर...`

Avoid unnecessarily formal wording where simpler school/exam Hindi is clear.

### Punjabi

Prefer simple phrases such as:

- `ਸਤ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ`
- `ਵਕਰ ਸਤ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ`
- `ਕੁੱਲ ਸਤ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਖੇਤਰਫਲ`
- `ਮੁੱਲ ਸੂਤਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੱਖਣ ਤੇ...`

Avoid overly Sanskritised or bookish phrasing when a natural Punjabi sentence is available.
