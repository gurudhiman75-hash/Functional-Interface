# MEN-001 — Competitive-Exam Strategy and Explanation Polish

## Scope

This follow-up responds to the post-freeze review of MEN-001 for SSC, Banking and Punjab state-exam preparation.

It does not add or remove QLs, solve modes, mathematical states, distractor ownership or publication routes. It improves only the learner-facing explanation layer and its regression proof.

## Exam-smart shortcuts

Shortcuts are appended after the complete standard method, so conceptual proof remains available.

- isosceles triangle states expose a Pythagorean-triplet shortcut only when the generated half-base, height and equal side form an exact integer triplet;
- side-ratio triangle-area states bypass Heron's formula only when the generated actual sides form a right triangle;
- non-right states such as 5:5:6 and 13:14:15 retain the standard Heron method without a false shortcut;
- uniform linear increases use the two-dimensional successive-percentage shortcut `2p + p²/100`;
- uniform linear decreases use `2p − p²/100`.

## Explanation cleanup

- percentage steps are titled by the actual task: new area percentage, percentage increase or percentage decrease;
- equation-bearing steps no longer receive filler prose such as “Use the previous result in the next part of the calculation”;
- generic substitution prose is omitted when the displayed equation already carries the complete numerical action;
- meaningful instructional prose is retained for conceptual, conversion, inverse and geometric-construction steps.

## Mathematical display

- `Heron's formula` is protected as one LaTeX text phrase rather than rendering as `\text{Heron}'s \text{formula}`;
- square units are normalized to braced exponents such as `\text{m}^{2}` and `\text{cm}^{2}`;
- existing semantic-math and balanced-brace validation remains active.

## Acceptance proof

The focused audit verifies:

- a triplet shortcut appears for eligible QL-017 and QL-020 states;
- no triplet shortcut appears for a non-right QL-020 state;
- QL-414 and QL-415 expose the correct competitive percentage shortcuts;
- QL-414 uses specific percentage-step titles;
- the identified filler sentences are absent;
- possessive text and square-unit LaTeX are normalized.
