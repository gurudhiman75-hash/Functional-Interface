## 3. Proof sweep

For each temporary contract, the deterministic CI sweep must prove:

- 100 generated mathematical packages;
- deterministic replay for every seed;
- canonical/verifier parity;
- four unique options;
- exactly one marked correct option;
- every answer position reached;
- Easy, Medium and Hard states reached through live complexity parameters;
- complete answer semantic and conclusion;
- source and prototype ancestry;
- no permanent QL identity;
- no Question Studio, Question Bank, test or public exposure.

The review export contains at least three mathematically distinct states per temporary contract.

## 4. Difficulty construction

### Easy

- one small prime or prime-power support;
- short interval;
- visible direct factorisation;
- co-prime pair with an obvious small common factor in distractors.

### Medium

- two or three prime supports;
- repeated exponent;
- larger interval or mixed prime/composite boundaries;
- non-obvious co-prime pair.

### Hard

- three or four supports or a larger bounded candidate domain;
- inverse missing component;
- pairwise-versus-collective distinction;
- close misconception options and boundary-sensitive interval evidence.

Difficulty is derived from generated state features, not from seed labels or cosmetic number size alone.
