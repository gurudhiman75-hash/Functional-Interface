# PNC-CP-012 Discovery and Ownership

## Purpose

`PNC-CP-012 — Mixed Advanced Counting Systems` owns exam-relevant counting problems whose primary reasoning combines two or more mature P&C authorities and therefore cannot be assigned cleanly to CP-006 through CP-011.

CP-012 is bounded. It is not a miscellaneous dumping ground. Admission requires:

1. a material learner-facing distinction;
2. an exact authority;
3. an independent verifier on the admitted parameter domain;
4. a clear negative-boundary reason explaining why an earlier CP does not own the problem;
5. deterministic generation, option and explanation proof.

## Owned families

### Conditional selection followed by roles — `PNC-QL-242..246`

- category-quota committee followed by two unrestricted offices;
- woman chair with unrestricted secretary;
- woman chair and man secretary;
- compulsory-member committee followed by two offices;
- category-specific three-office assignment.

These are not CP-006-only role problems because committee membership is itself conditional. They are not CP-009-only selection problems because ordered offices materially change the authority.

### Restricted selection followed by arrangement — `PNC-QL-247..252`

- compulsory or excluded pair before a linear arrangement;
- category quota before a linear arrangement;
- category quota before circular seating;
- specified cross-category pair compulsory and adjacent in a selected circular set;
- specified inclusion before reversible-ring equivalence.

These combine selection ownership with linear, circular or dihedral arrangement ownership.

### Derangements and fixed points — `PNC-QL-253..257`

- no fixed point;
- exactly a stated number of fixed points;
- at least one fixed point;
- exactly one of two specified objects fixed;
- neither of two specified objects fixed.

The primary authority is fixed-position inclusion-exclusion or derangement recurrence, not ordinary position fixing.

### Monotone grid paths — `PNC-QL-258..261`

- unrestricted shortest paths;
- paths through a checkpoint;
- paths avoiding a checkpoint;
- paths through at least one of two ordered checkpoints.

These are sequence-selection systems represented geometrically, with decomposition or inclusion-exclusion across path events.

### Mixed grouping and distribution — `PNC-QL-262..269`

- identical objects under non-uniform upper capacities;
- identical objects under non-uniform lower and upper vectors;
- distinct objects under non-uniform capacities;
- two identical colours into labelled receivers;
- every labelled receiver non-empty across two colours;
- named equal teams followed by one captain per team;
- unnamed equal teams followed by one captain per team;
- category quota across two named teams followed by captains.

These exceed CP-011's clean single-authority boundaries by coupling non-uniform bounds, multiple object identities or grouping with subsequent role assignment.

## Deferred boundaries

Current CP-012 ownership does not admit:

- large unrestricted generating-function systems;
- arbitrary per-object eligibility matrices;
- repeated-colour necklaces requiring Burnside or Pólya enumeration;
- distribution followed by internal linear or circular arrangement when the coupled state space is not independently enumerable on a useful exam domain;
- highly artificial case systems without source evidence or a stable misconception profile.

A future contract may be added only through a new source-backed gap audit. Existing IDs and solve modes remain immutable.
