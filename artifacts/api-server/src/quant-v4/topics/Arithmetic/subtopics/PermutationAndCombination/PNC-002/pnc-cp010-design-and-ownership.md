# PNC-CP-010 Design and Ownership

## Canonical problem

`PNC-CP-010 — Circular Arrangements & Rotational Symmetry`

## Positive ownership

CP-010 owns arrangements whose primary state space is circular and whose count depends on one or more of:

- rotational equivalence around a round table;
- a fixed reference person used to remove rotational duplication;
- circular together/apart and multi-block restrictions;
- circular adjacency, opposite-seat, neighbour and directed clockwise conditions;
- exact, minimum or maximum clockwise separation;
- prescribed clockwise relative order;
- alternation or circular gap placement;
- bounded recovery from a circular count;
- ornament systems where rotation-only or rotation-plus-reflection equivalence is stated explicitly.

## Negative boundaries

CP-010 does not own:

- unrestricted linear arrangements — CP-002;
- linear blocks — CP-007;
- linear positions, gaps or alternation — CP-008;
- selection conditions — CP-009;
- grouping and distribution — CP-011;
- mixed selection-plus-circular or advanced Burnside/Pólya systems — CP-012;
- repeated-object word or multiset authority — CP-005.

Round-table seating, rotation-only displays and reflection-equivalent necklaces are separate contracts. They do not share a solver merely because all are drawn on a circle.

## Need-based QL admission

The first runtime checkpoint admitted `PNC-QL-177` through `PNC-QL-203` after the initial circular ownership audit.

The second saturation audit admitted two additional material contracts:

- `PNC-QL-204`: a specified circular group must not all occupy consecutive seats;
- `PNC-QL-205`: exactly one of two disjoint specified pairs must be adjacent.

The active range is therefore `PNC-QL-177` through `PNC-QL-205`, containing 29 QLs. These values remain discovered regression snapshots rather than predetermined quotas. Noun substitutions, changed table sizes and equivalent wording are rejected when they do not alter formula authority, evidence, verification, explanation or distractor semantics.

## Proof model

Production formulas use exact factorial, combination, product, division and complement arithmetic.

Independent verification enumerates every distinct circular permutation after fixing one reference object. Constraint predicates are applied directly to the enumerated seating. Reflection-equivalent ornament modes retain one canonical representative from each mirrored pair. Inverse modes search a bounded domain using the independent enumeration counts.

For `PNC-QL-205`, independent verification evaluates both disjoint adjacency events directly and accepts arrangements where their truth values differ. This verifies the formula `2 × (one specified pair together − both specified pairs together)` without reusing its derivation.
