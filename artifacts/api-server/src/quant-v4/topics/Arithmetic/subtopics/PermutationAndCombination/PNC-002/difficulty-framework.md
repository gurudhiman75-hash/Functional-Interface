# PNC-002 Difficulty Framework

Difficulty is structural rather than number-based.

## Easy

- one familiar block of two objects;
- direct compression into a single unit;
- no complement or interacting restriction.

## Medium

- block size greater than two;
- direct complement for “not together” or “not all consecutive”;
- two simple blocks;
- bounded recovery of one total-object parameter.

## Hard

- unequal or three simultaneous blocks;
- a required block combined with a separate apart condition;
- inverse complement counting;
- bounded recovery of block size;
- multiple correction factors whose meanings must remain visible.

## Current observed distribution

- Easy: 1
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 6
- Total: 12

This is a checkpoint snapshot, not a future quota.
