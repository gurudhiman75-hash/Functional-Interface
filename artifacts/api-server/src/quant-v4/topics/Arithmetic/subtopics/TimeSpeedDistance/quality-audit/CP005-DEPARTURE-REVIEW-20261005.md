## TSD-QL-061 · en-IN · cp005-review-v8:3:0:state-4

Coach A and Coach B travel between towns P and Q. A and B start simultaneously from opposite ends P and Q of a 360 km route, moving towards each other at constant speeds. After meeting, A takes 108 minutes to reach Q and B takes 300 minutes to reach P. What are their speeds, A first?

A. 45 km/h and 75 km/h
B. 100 km/h and 36 km/h
C. 75 km/h and 45 km/h
D. 75 km/h and 75 km/h

Answer: 75 km/h and 45 km/h

1. Let t be the time before meeting, and u and v the speeds of A and B. Their post-meeting journeys give u × tA = v × t and v × tB = u × t. Multiplying and cancelling u × v gives t² = tA × tB.
2. Time before meeting: √((1 4/5) × (5)) = 3 h
3. A's total journey time: (3) + (1 4/5) = 4 4/5 h
4. Speed of A: (360) ÷ (4 4/5) = 75 km/h
5. B's total journey time: (3) + (5) = 8 h
6. Speed of B: (360) ÷ (8) = 45 km/h
