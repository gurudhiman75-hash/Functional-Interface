# RAP-002: Canonical Problems

This document defines the independent reasoning systems for Compound Proportions & Linked Ratios.

| CP ID | Name | Core Logic | Exam Realism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RAP-CP-007 | Direct Chain Ratios | Align linked ratios such as A:B and B:C, or A:B, B:C, and C:D, into one combined chain. | Foundational advanced ratio skill. |
| RAP-CP-008 | Reverse Chain Proportions | Recover an intermediate value or missing endpoint from linked ratios and known quantities. | Common in SSC and Banking word problems. |
| RAP-CP-009 | Multi-Stage Ratio Transformations | Track a ratio through successive add/remove/transfer actions. | High-frequency SSC transformation pattern. |
| RAP-CP-010 | Conditional Partition With Ratios | Divide a total by one ratio, then subdivide selected parts by another ratio. | Banking and DI-style distribution. |
| RAP-CP-011 | Inverse Proportion Chains | Solve chains where one or more linked relations are inverse, such as work-rate or speed-time. | Formula-heavy but exam-realistic. |
| RAP-CP-012 | Ratio Comparison & Ordering | Compare two ratio chains, order values, or test equivalence after normalization. | Reasoning-heavy comparison questions. |

## CP Independence Analysis

### RAP-CP-007: Direct Chain Ratios

- **Focus**: Forward linkage.
- **Independence**: The main operation is aligning shared terms using LCM/scaling. Unlike RAP-001's simple linkage, this CP should emphasize longer chains, alternate wording, and compound alignment.

### RAP-CP-008: Reverse Chain Proportions

- **Focus**: Recovering hidden terms.
- **Independence**: Starts from endpoints or constraints and works backward to an intermediate value. The student cannot simply read the combined ratio.

### RAP-CP-009: Multi-Stage Ratio Transformations

- **Focus**: Time/state evolution.
- **Independence**: The same two or three quantities undergo multiple changes. The solver must preserve state across actions.

### RAP-CP-010: Conditional Partition With Ratios

- **Focus**: Nested allocation.
- **Independence**: A total is first divided at one level, then a selected part is divided again. This is a tree of ratio units, not one flat partition.

### RAP-CP-011: Inverse Proportion Chains

- **Focus**: Reversed dependency.
- **Independence**: At least one link in the chain inverts the ratio, e.g. speed vs. time or workers vs. days.

### RAP-CP-012: Ratio Comparison & Ordering

- **Focus**: Normalized comparison.
- **Independence**: The output may be logical rather than numeric: greater, smaller, equivalent, or ordered sequence.
