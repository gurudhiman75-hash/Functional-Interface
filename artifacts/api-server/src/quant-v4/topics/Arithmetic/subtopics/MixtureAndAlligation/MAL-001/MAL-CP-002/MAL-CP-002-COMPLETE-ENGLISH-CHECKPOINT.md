## Completion scope

The checkpoint closes the evidence-derived two-substance ratio-adjustment frontier across:

- explicit pure-component addition and removal;
- forward ratio after pure addition or removal;
- reverse ratio reconstruction;
- total-and-ratio state partition;
- one-known-component ratio scaling;
- hidden-state target adjustment from total and ratio;
- original total from before/after ratio shifts;
- one-stage homogeneous remove-and-refill in forward and inverse directions;
- homogeneous-sample removal invariance;
- operation feasibility expressed as the required operation and quantity;
- source-backed three-component coupled addition.

Counts were not prescribed in advance. The 17 permanent QLs were allocated only after the open coverage matrix was closed by executable implementation, an explicit owner boundary, a source-thin exclusion or a non-uniqueness proof.

## Preserved ownership boundaries
