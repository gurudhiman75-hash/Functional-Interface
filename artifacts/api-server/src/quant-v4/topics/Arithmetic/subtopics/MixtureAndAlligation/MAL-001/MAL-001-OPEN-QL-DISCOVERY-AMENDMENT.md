# MAL-001 Open QL Discovery Amendment

Status: **authoritative for MAL-001 design and all future implementation work**.

This amendment supersedes every earlier Mixture & Alligation planning statement that prescribes a fixed chapter total, a fixed CP allocation, a fixed solve-mode count, a fixed difficulty split, or permanent `MAL-QL-*` ranges before exhaustive discovery.

The July 2026 planning PDF remains valuable prior art, especially for its legacy-recovery analysis and provisional six-domain chapter map. Its proposed totals of 340 English QLs, 31 solve modes, CP percentages, and 32/34/34 difficulty allocation are planning estimates only and are not implementation authority.

## 1. Current identity state

```text
Package: MAL-001

Question Bank eligibility: disabled

```

No `MAL-QL-*` ID may be created during design or prototype discovery.

## 2. Governing discovery rule

A permanent QL may be created only after its owning checkpoint has completed all of the following:

1. source and concept audit;
2. mathematical-state audit;
3. task-direction audit;
4. inverse-contract audit;
5. unknown-variable audit;
6. answer-semantic and unit audit;
7. edge-case, impossibility and ambiguity audit;
8. representation and renderer audit;
9. misconception and distractor audit;
10. executable prototype proof with a canonical solver;
11. independent verifier agreement;
12. merge/split audit proving that each surviving contract is materially distinct;
13. cross-chapter ownership collision audit;
14. final gap audit showing no meaningful uncovered exam contract.

Counts remain open while any meaningful gap, collision or duplicate contract remains unresolved.

## 3. Prototype identity policy

Before freeze, candidate contracts use checkpoint-local non-permanent IDs, for example:

```text
MAL-CP001-PROT-RATIO-FROM-TWO-VALUES-AND-MEAN
MAL-CP001-PROT-MEAN-FROM-RATIO-AND-SOURCE-VALUES
MAL-CP001-PROT-MISSING-SOURCE-VALUE
```

Prototype IDs:

- are not QLs;
- are not added to chapter-wide production registries;
- are not discoverable in Question Studio;
- may be merged, split, renamed or retired without migration;
- must remain `publiclyPublishable: false`.

Permanent IDs are assigned sequentially only after checkpoint discovery is approved.

## 4. Provisional chapter-domain map

The following six domains are retained as the **starting hypothesis** because they correspond to materially different state transitions in the legacy corpus. They are not frozen CP allocations until the chapter-wide ownership audit is complete.

1. component blending to or from a target mean;
2. two-substance ratio adjustment;
3. repeated fractional replacement;
4. conserved-solute transformations;
5. dishonest adulteration by mixing;
6. multi-vessel transfer and equalisation.

A domain may be merged, split, renamed or reassigned if executable discovery proves that its mathematical state or exam task contract is not distinct.

## 5. Difficulty policy

Difficulty is instance-based and evidence-based. It is not preallocated by percentage or by position in a registry.

Difficulty must consider at least:

- number of independently necessary relations;
- forward versus inverse reconstruction;
- number of components or stages;
- whether the target is directly represented;
- algebraic depth;
- bookkeeping burden;
- exactness and representation complexity;
- presence of coupled constraints.

No QL is created merely to satisfy an Easy, Medium or Hard quota.

## 6. Language policy

English is the first executable language.

Hindi and Punjabi may have schema companions during architecture work, but they must carry explicit pending-localisation status and reject runtime generation until naturally authored, parity-checked and human-reviewed. English text must never be silently returned for a Hindi or Punjabi request.

## 7. Freeze effect

MAL-001 has no authoritative final QL count, solve-mode count, CP count or difficulty distribution yet.

Those values become authoritative only after:

- all retained domains complete executable discovery;
- cross-chapter ownership is locked;
- chapter-wide gap and duplicate audits pass;
- permanent QL allocation is explicitly approved.
