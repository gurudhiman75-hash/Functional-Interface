## 2. Open gaps

### Direct target-exam evidence required

No direct trusted fixture has yet been recovered for these learner outputs:

1. final total mixture quantity after solving an unknown component quantity;
2. difference between the two component quantities when total and target mean are given;
3. impossible or indeterminate blend/determinacy questions.

All three are mathematically constructible. That is not sufficient reason to create a QL-template family. Each would introduce a distinct answer or option contract and therefore requires direct exam evidence.

### Ownership decision still required

- static concentration blending: CP-001 weighted-source blend versus CP-004 concentration-domain ownership;
- direct vessel combination: CP-001 static blend versus CP-006 vessel-state or transfer bookkeeping.

Duplicate QLs across owners are prohibited.
