## Existing 12-prototype decision

All 12 product-approved Wave 01/V2 prototypes are retained as **distinct future task-contract candidates**, but they are not 12 independent mathematical engines.

### Core A — Free adulterant sold at the pure-product cost

Six task directions share one invariant:

- profit from known pure/adulterant quantities;
- ratio from target profit;
- adulterant quantity from pure quantity + target profit;
- pure quantity from adulterant quantity + target profit;
- final-mixture adulterant percentage from target profit;
- target profit from final-mixture adulterant percentage.

These remain separate future QL candidates because answer semantics and given/unknown structure differ.

### Core B — Free adulterant with an independent selling rate

Three task directions share one commercial ledger:

- profit from cost, composition and selling rate;
- composition ratio from cost, selling rate and target profit;
- selling rate from composition and target profit.

### Core C — Paid cheaper/adulterant ingredient

Three task directions share weighted mixture cost:

- profit from ingredient costs, quantities and selling rate;
- ingredient ratio from costs, selling rate and target profit;
- selling rate from ingredient costs, ratio and target profit.

CP-005 use of this core must preserve a genuine lower-grade/adulterant commercial composition. Neutral blends of legitimate varieties remain CP-001 mixture-value territory even when a profit percentage appears in the stem.

## New source-backed CP-005 candidate

Wave 02 recommends exactly one new contract for later executable discovery:

`MAL-CP005-CAND-PROFIT-AFTER-FREE-ADULTERATION-AND-PRICE-CHANGE`

R.S. Aggarwal Q111 combines free-water adulteration with a changed selling price and asks for the commercial result. Both the mixture composition and the price change are indispensable, so this is not merely a PNL markup question and not already represented by the pure-cost free-adulterant family.

No permanent QL is allocated in Wave 02.

## Important non-CP-005 decisions

### Paid blend: unknown quantity from one known quantity + commercial target

**Owner: MAL-CP-001.** Arun Sharma Solutions 5 and 7 first derive a target average cost from sale/profit information and then perform ordinary two-paid-item alligation. The final unknown quantity is standard CP-001 allocation.

### Paid blend: missing ingredient cost

**Owner: MAL-CP-001.** R.S. Aggarwal Q102 and Q109 reduce to a target mean followed by weighted-source-price reconstruction. The cited products are legitimate paid blends, not adulteration.

### Three paid varieties sold for profit

**Owner: MAL-CP-001 calculation boundary.** R.S. Aggarwal Q104 is a weighted multi-item blend. A sale objective does not by itself make the question dishonest mixing.

### Adulteration + false measure

**Owner: PNL-CP-005.** R.S. Aggarwal Q191 requires the false 800 ml measure. False weight/measure/short delivery stays with Profit and Loss even when adulteration is also present.

### Repeated replacement then sale

**Owner: MAL-CP-003 unless stronger evidence appears.** The governing transformation remains repeated sampling/refill.

### Target-loss and broad markup/discount symmetry

**Held.** Wave 02 does not create mathematically symmetrical families without direct exam authority. The price-increase evidence is captured only in the new adulteration-plus-price-change candidate.
