# PNL-CP-005 Distractor Contract

Status: REQUIRED

CP-005 distractors must represent identifiable reasoning errors. Arbitrary numerical offsets are prohibited.

## Approved misconception families

- Uses the nominal full-quantity cost instead of the cost of the quantity actually delivered.
- Calculates shortage percentage on delivered quantity instead of true quantity.
- Treats a 20% short delivery as exactly 20% profit when the price is unchanged; the correct profit is measured on the reduced actual cost.
- Adds declared price profit and shortage percentage directly.
- Subtracts a declared loss from the shortage gain instead of composing price and quantity multipliers.
- Uses quoted selling price as the profit-percentage base.
- Treats the amount charged as the cost price.
- Ignores the extra quantity received while buying.
- Ignores the short quantity delivered while selling.
- Adds the buying advantage and selling advantage rather than multiplying their quantity factors.
- Applies markup and discount additively.
- Applies discount to cost price instead of marked price.
- Uses full-quantity cost after the false quantity has been introduced.
- Confuses dealer profit percentage with customer overcharge percentage.
- Uses `true quantity - delivered quantity` as the overcharge percentage without changing the base.
- Reverses an inverse by multiplying by the target multiplier instead of dividing.
- Recovers the cost of the delivered quantity but forgets to scale it to the true nominal quantity.
- Returns total received quantity where delivered quantity per sale is asked, or vice versa.
- Compares two schemes using quoted prices alone and ignores quantity delivered.
- Selects the larger declared rate instead of the larger actual profit rate.

## Option-quality requirements

Every generated MCQ must have:

1. one correct option;
2. three distinct misconception-driven options;
3. no duplicate numerical values after formatting;
4. no answer-position pattern tied to solve mode;
5. no simple `answer ± constant` construction;
6. direction labels that agree with the numerical result.
