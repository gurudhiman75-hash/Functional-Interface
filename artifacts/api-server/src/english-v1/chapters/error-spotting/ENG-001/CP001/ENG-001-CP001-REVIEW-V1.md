## Easy

### 1. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-001

Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.

A. My younger brother  
B. are attending  
C. a short computer course  
D. this month.

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The error is in **“are attending”**. The subject **“My younger brother”** is singular, so it requires the singular verb form **“is attending”**. Correct sentence: **My younger brother is attending a short computer course this month.**

### 2. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-001

Choose the segment that contains a grammatical error.

A. The new machines  
B. operates  
C. without interruption  
D. during the night shift.

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The subject **“machines”** is plural, so the verb must also be plural in form: **“operate”**, not **“operates”**. Correct sentence: **The new machines operate without interruption during the night shift.**

### 3. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-002

Select the part of the sentence that contains an error.

A. Each of the applicants  
B. have received  
C. a confirmation message  
D. from the office.

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The grammatical head is **“Each”**, which is singular. The phrase **“of the applicants”** does not make the subject plural. Therefore, **“have received”** should be **“has received”**. Correct sentence: **Each of the applicants has received a confirmation message from the office.**

## Medium

### 7. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-001

Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.

A. The revised schedule  
B. for the evening buses  
C. show  
D. two additional departures.

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The subject head is **“schedule”**, which is singular. The nearby plural noun **“buses”** is inside a prepositional phrase and does not control the verb. Therefore, **“show”** should be **“shows”**. Correct sentence: **The revised schedule for the evening buses shows two additional departures.**

### 8. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-004

Choose the segment that contains a grammatical error.

A. A number of employees  
B. has requested  
C. a change in the reporting time  
D. for next week.

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** **“A number of + plural noun”** means several and takes a plural verb. Therefore, **“has requested”** should be **“have requested”**. Correct sentence: **A number of employees have requested a change in the reporting time for next week.**

### 9. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-005

Select the part of the sentence that contains an error.

A. The manager  
B. along with two assistants  
C. are reviewing  
D. the final list.

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** **“Along with two assistants”** is additional information and does not form a compound subject. The main subject **“manager”** is singular, so **“are reviewing”** should be **“is reviewing”**. Correct sentence: **The manager, along with two assistants, is reviewing the final list.**

### 10. ENG-001-QL002 · GR-SVA-006

One of the three segments may contain a grammatical error. Choose the incorrect segment, or select **No error** if the sentence is correct.

A. Neither the supervisors nor the clerk were available  
B. to verify the entries  
C. before the counter closed.

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** With **“neither...nor”**, the verb agrees with the nearer subject in the target exam convention. The nearer subject is **“the clerk”**, which is singular. Therefore, **“were available”** should be **“was available”**. Correct sentence: **Neither the supervisors nor the clerk was available to verify the entries before the counter closed.**

## Hard

### 13. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-004

Choose the segment that contains a grammatical error.

A. The number of complaints  
B. received during the first week  
C. have fallen  
D. since the new system was introduced.

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The subject is **“The number”**, which refers to one total and is singular. The plural noun **“complaints”** appears inside the **“of”** phrase. Therefore, **“have fallen”** should be **“has fallen”**. Correct sentence: **The number of complaints received during the first week has fallen since the new system was introduced.**

### 14. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-005

Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.

A. The maintenance schedule  
B. as well as the revised safety instructions  
C. were circulated  
D. to all section heads yesterday.

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** **“As well as the revised safety instructions”** does not create a plural compound subject. Agreement is controlled by the singular head **“schedule”**. Therefore, **“were circulated”** should be **“was circulated”**. Correct sentence: **The maintenance schedule, as well as the revised safety instructions, was circulated to all section heads yesterday.**

### 15. ENG-001-QL002 · GR-SVA-006

One of the three segments may contain a grammatical error. Choose the incorrect segment, or select **No error** if the sentence is correct.

A. Either the branch manager or the senior accountants is expected  
B. to present the verified figures  
C. at tomorrow's meeting.

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** In the **“either...or”** structure, the nearer subject is **“the senior accountants”**, which is plural. Therefore, **“is expected”** should be **“are expected”**. Correct sentence: **Either the branch manager or the senior accountants are expected to present the verified figures at tomorrow's meeting.**

### 16. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-007

Select the part of the sentence that contains an error.

A. The jury  
B. after examining the complete record  
C. have reached  
D. a unanimous decision.

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The phrase **“a unanimous decision”** makes the unit reading clear: the jury is acting as one body. Therefore, the singular verb **“has reached”** is required. Correct sentence: **The jury, after examining the complete record, has reached a unanimous decision.**

### 17. ENG-001-QL001 · GR-SVA-009

Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.

A. Many a skilled worker  
B. in these small workshops  
C. have learned  
D. the craft through years of practice.

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The construction **“many a + singular noun”** takes a singular verb. The plural noun **“workshops”** is only part of an intervening phrase. Therefore, **“have learned”** should be **“has learned”**. Correct sentence: **Many a skilled worker in these small workshops has learned the craft through years of practice.**

### 18. ENG-001-QL007 · GR-SVA-010

Choose the segment containing a grammatical error. If there is no error, select **No error**.

A. The list of items  
B. required for the two field teams  
C. is displayed  
D. beside the issue counter.

**Answer:** No error

**Explanation:** There is no error. The subject head is the singular noun **“list”**. The plural nouns **“items”** and **“teams”** occur inside intervening phrases, so they do not control the verb. **“Is displayed”** is correct.
