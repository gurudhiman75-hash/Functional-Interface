# ENG-007-CP001 — Core High-frequency Spelling — Source & Coverage Audit V1

Status: `REVIEW_CANDIDATE_V1__HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING__REVIEW_ONLY`

## Coverage

- 240 governed spelling pairs.
- 80 Easy / 80 Medium / 80 Hard.
- Every record contains one standard spelling and one deliberately plausible misspelling.
- Both exam directions are supported: correctly-spelt-word and misspelt-word identification.
- Distractors are drawn from other words at the same difficulty, prioritising similar trap families and word lengths.

## Editorial policy

- Accepted British/American variants must not be keyed against one another.
- A distractor must not itself be a valid alternative spelling or a different valid English word used as a spelling error.
- Random keyboard noise and visibly absurd forms are not acceptable distractors.
- Capitalisation is not used as the tested error.
- The lexical list is a human-review candidate; questionable variants must be removed rather than defended.

## Dictionary authority policy

Before chapter freeze, disputed forms are checked against major learner/general dictionaries (Cambridge, Oxford Learner's, Merriam-Webster or Collins). Where reputable dictionaries recognise competing spellings, that pair is excluded from the spelling-error bank.

## Lifecycle

Review-only. No Question Studio registration, Question Bank persistence, test/mock eligibility, public publication, automatic learner release or production promotion before explicit human approval.
