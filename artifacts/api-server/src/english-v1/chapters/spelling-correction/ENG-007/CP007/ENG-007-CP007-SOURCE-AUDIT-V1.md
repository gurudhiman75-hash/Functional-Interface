# ENG-007-CP007 — Final PYQ Residual Closure — Source & Coverage Audit V1

Status: `REVIEW_CANDIDATE_V1__HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING__REVIEW_ONLY`

## Why CP007 exists

After CP006 was approved, ENG-007 had 1,392 governed spellings. A final residual audit broadened the evidence beyond the internal ENG-004/ENG-006 donor pools and beyond the first CP006 PYQ sample.

The audit sampled:
- SSC MTS 2019 and 2020/2021 official-paper-derived spelling items;
- SSC MTS 2022 previous-year spelling questions;
- SSC CGL 2024 Tier-I spelling items;
- SSC CHSL 2024 spelling items; and
- SSC MTS 2025 papers held in February 2026.

Only words that were directly tested as a correct spelling, or were the canonical correction of a printed misspelling, were retained. Harmless correct decoy options were not bulk-imported.

## Result

- **53 genuine residual spellings** remain uncovered after CP001-CP006.
- Difficulty: **21 Easy / 25 Medium / 7 Hard**.
- Combined observed ENG-007 breadth after CP007: **1,445 unique canonical spellings**.
- The observed exam misspelling is retained wherever the source exposed one and it is safe/non-colliding.
- `fulfil/fulfill` is deliberately excluded from single-answer governance because Cambridge records `fulfil` as the UK form and `fulfill` as the US form. Neither valid regional spelling should be treated as wrong.
- Other variant-sensitive forms are likewise excluded from the wrong-answer pool.

## Representative residual families

- common vowel errors: reference, repetition, tolerance, prominent, tendency;
- transposition/sequence errors: patience, ancestors, sieve, equipment;
- doubled-letter errors: attempt, fabulous, ambulance, suggestion, excessive;
- difficult exam vocabulary: religiosity, irascible, truculent, rudimentary, coalesce, pernicious;
- recent MTS 2025/2026 forms: compatible, volatility, ludicrous, excessive, pernicious, governance.

## Question surfaces

1. Correctly spelt word: exactly one correct option plus three misspelt options.
2. Incorrectly spelt word: exactly one misspelt keyed answer among correctly spelt options.
