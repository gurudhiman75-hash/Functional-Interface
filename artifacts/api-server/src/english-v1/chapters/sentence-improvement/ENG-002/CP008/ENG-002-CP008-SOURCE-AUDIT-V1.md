## Scope

CP008 transforms the human-approved ENG-001 CP008 noun and quantifier grammar layer into Sentence Improvement questions. Grammar authority remains in ENG-001; ENG-002 owns only the intact-sentence replacement surface, distractors, No improvement balance, explanations and review artifact.

Rule families: `GR-NQN-001` through `GR-NQN-010`, covering many/much, few/a few, little/a little, fewer/less, number/amount, uncountable noun number, irregular plurals, plural-only nouns, unit expressions with mass nouns, and each/one of the + plural group noun.

## Learner surface

- intact sentence with one focused or rule-appropriate underlined target;
- no slash-separated Error Spotting parts;
- three replacement choices plus D. No improvement;
- deterministic approximately 25% No improvement and exactly five of twenty per difficulty in the review batch;
- difficulty comes from the donor sentence structure, not obscure vocabulary.

## Explanation standard

Every explanation follows: error/no-improvement → simple underlying concept → sentence-specific application → full corrected sentence. No option-by-option analysis or test-taking jargon is used.

## Review and validation

A deterministic 60-question review uses all 60 donor scenes: 20 Easy, 20 Medium and 20 Hard, covering all ten rule families. Stress validation generates 2,000 questions per difficulty and checks deterministic replay, option uniqueness, answer balance, rule/domain reachability, learner-surface integrity and review composition.
