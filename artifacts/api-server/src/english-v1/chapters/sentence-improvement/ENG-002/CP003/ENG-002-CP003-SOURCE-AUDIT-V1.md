# ENG-002-CP003 — Sentence Improvement: Articles and Determiners — Source Audit V1

Status: `IMPLEMENTED_V1__HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING__NOT_QUESTION_STUDIO_REGISTERED`

## Boundary

ENG-002 CP003 is the Sentence Improvement transformation of the closed ENG-001 CP003 Articles and Determiners authority. It reuses the approved scene/rule layer and does not create a second grammar source.

Covered rules are `GR-ART-001` through `GR-ART-010`: required determiners, `a/an` sound choice, superlatives, specified reference, zero article, professions, institutional zero article, geographical names, countability quantifiers, and singular/plural determiner agreement.

## Learner surface

Each item keeps one complete natural sentence intact and underlines exactly one article/determiner phrase. The fixed instruction is:

> Select the most appropriate option to improve the underlined part of the sentence. If no improvement is required, select 'No improvement'.

Options contain three replacement phrases plus `No improvement` at D. About 25% of production items are calibrated No-improvement cases.

## Distractors

Distractors are built from the same noun phrase and the registered article/determiner contrast. They must never invent words or change the sentence into an unrelated grammar task. Countability and noun-number distractors preserve the noun while changing only the relevant determiner/number relationship.

## Explanations

Every explanation follows the approved ENG-002 teaching order:

1. state the error or state that no improvement is needed;
2. explain the rule in very easy language;
3. apply it to the current sentence;
4. show the complete corrected sentence.

No option-by-option analysis or generic closing clutter is allowed.

## Difficulty

Difficulty remains structural and is inherited from the closed ENG-001 CP003 scene layer: direct signals at Easy, wider reference/countability dependencies at Medium, and longer institutional/geographical/general-vs-specific distinctions at Hard. Vocabulary is not used as the difficulty mechanism.
