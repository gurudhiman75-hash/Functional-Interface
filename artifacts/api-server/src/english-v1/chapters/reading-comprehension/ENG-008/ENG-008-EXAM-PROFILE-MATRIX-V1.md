# ENG-008 — Exam Profile Matrix V1

Status: `DESIGN_AUTHORITY__REVIEW_ONLY`

| Profile | Passage character | Typical engine difficulty | Primary operations |
|---|---|---|---|
| SSC Foundation | simple story/book + straightforward report | Easy–Medium | fact, local inference, theme, title, vocabulary, supported statement |
| SSC Editorial / Current Affairs | report/editorial/current-affairs style | Medium–Hard | purpose, tone, implication, summary, cause/effect, contextual vocabulary |
| Banking Prelims | readable general-interest, narrative, business/economy-light, social/science/tech-light | Easy–Medium under time pressure | fact, inference, vocab-in-context, supported statement, contextual word fit |
| Banking Mains | analytical economy/science/technology/social/editorial | Medium–Hard | deep inference, viewpoint, tone, purpose, can/cannot infer, conclusion, paragraph relation |
| Research / Survey | study/report/survey findings | Medium–Hard | findings, limitation, cause-vs-correlation, evidence/conclusion, comparison |

## Governance

- SSC genre claims are tied to official SSC syllabus wording.
- Banking genre/question-mix claims are empirical paper-profile rules, not official granular syllabus claims.
- Every passage may declare more than one eligible profile only after review.
- A passage being topically relevant is not enough; its discourse structure and question operations must fit the target profile.
- Passage sets must preserve exam-profile question-count and difficulty behaviour rather than mixing profiles indiscriminately.
