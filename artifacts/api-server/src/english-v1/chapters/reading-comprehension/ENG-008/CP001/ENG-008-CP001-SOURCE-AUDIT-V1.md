# ENG-008 CP001 — Narrative / Story-style Direct Comprehension — Source Audit V1

Status: `IMPLEMENTED_V1__HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING__REVIEW_ONLY`

## Purpose

CP001 establishes the first Reading Comprehension surface for English. It focuses only on **direct comprehension from short three-paragraph narrative passages** so that passage quality, option quality and evidence-based explanations can be reviewed before higher-order inference and editorial/report passages are added.

## Official basis

The official SSC CGL English syllabus includes **Comprehension Passage** and specifies that comprehension may include three or more paragraphs, with at least one simple passage based on a book or story. SSC CHSL also explicitly lists Comprehension Passage.

CP001 therefore uses original Examtree-authored story-like passages rather than copied external passages.

## Implemented breadth

- 12 original three-paragraph passages
- 4 Easy / 4 Medium / 4 Hard
- 4 direct-comprehension questions per passage
- 48 authored question records
- deterministic passage/question selection
- explicit evidence string for every keyed answer

Covered direct skills:
- locating a stated fact;
- identifying a stated reason;
- identifying a stated action or sequence;
- identifying a stated result/outcome;
- connecting an explicitly stated cause to its result.

## Difficulty

Difficulty is not assigned by passage length alone.

- **Easy** — short sentences, low information density, direct local evidence.
- **Medium** — more details, delayed evidence and mild cause/result tracking.
- **Hard** — denser paragraphs, competing details, cross-sentence evidence and explicit causal relationships.

CP001 does **not** claim inference, title/theme, tone, vocabulary-in-context or reference questions. Those are separate planned checkpoints.

## Quality guards

- every answer must be recoverable from the passage alone;
- distractors must remain plausible but unsupported or contradicted;
- no outside knowledge is needed;
- passages contain no copyrighted newspaper/book text;
- explanations identify the correct answer and point back to the supporting passage evidence;
- all passages contain exactly three coherent paragraphs;
- every authored question has exactly four options and one keyed answer;
- review-only lifecycle remains locked.

## Lifecycle

Human review is pending. CP001 is not registered in Question Studio and cannot write to Question Bank, tests, mocks, learner/public surfaces or production release.
