# ENG-008 — Reading Comprehension — Blueprint V1

Status: `IMPLEMENTATION_ACTIVE__REVIEW_ONLY`

## Official exam boundary

ENG-008 closes the Reading Comprehension gap identified against current official SSC English syllabi.

Official SSC references:
- CGL 2025 notice: https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_adv_cgl_2025.pdf
- CHSL 2025 notice: https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_adv_chsl_2025.pdf
- Selection Post Phase XIV 2026 notice: https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_RHQ_2026_phase_xiv.pdf

SSC explicitly lists Comprehension Passage as an English question family. The CGL notice further states that comprehension may use three or more paragraphs, with at least one simple book/story-type paragraph and other paragraphs based on current-affairs report/editorial material.

ENG-008 owns passage comprehension only. It does not duplicate ENG-001–ENG-007 grammar, vocabulary, idiom, one-word or spelling authorities except where vocabulary is tested in passage context.

## Core principles

- passages are authored/licensed/public-domain authorities, never copied from copyrighted coaching material;
- every question is answerable from explicit passage evidence or a governed inference;
- every authority stores an evidence span or evidence rationale;
- distractors must be passage-plausible, not random;
- no question may require outside GK unless the passage itself supplies the fact;
- difficulty is driven by evidence distance, paraphrase, inference depth and discourse structure rather than obscure vocabulary alone;
- deterministic generation and replay;
- review-only Question Studio lifecycle until explicit human approval;
- no fixed final passage-count target; closure is audit-driven.

## Planned checkpoints

### CP001 — Foundation RC authority model
Short and medium passages establishing:
- factual retrieval;
- inference;
- main idea / central theme;
- suitable title;
- vocabulary in context;
- supported-statement selection.

Genres: narrative/story, report/expository, editorial/argumentative.

### CP002 — Literary / Story comprehension
Character motive, sequence, implicit cause, narrator attitude, contextual vocabulary, theme and title.

### CP003 — Report / Current-affairs-style comprehension
Data-free report passages, cause/effect, stated findings, comparison, implication, summary and vocabulary in context.

### CP004 — Editorial / Argumentative comprehension
Claim/evidence, tone, author purpose, assumption, inference, strengthening/weakening interpretation and conclusion.

### CP005 — Long multi-paragraph SSC comprehension sets
Three-or-more-paragraph passage sets, mixed question bundles, cross-paragraph evidence and hard evidence synthesis.

### CP006+ — Gap-audit checkpoint only if justified
No arbitrary numeric target. New checkpoints require a material exam-relevant omission found by the breadth audit.

## Difficulty contract

**Easy**
- answer stated directly;
- one evidence location;
- low paraphrase distance.

**Medium**
- moderate paraphrase;
- local inference;
- main idea/title;
- vocabulary in context;
- supported statement.

**Hard**
- cross-sentence or cross-paragraph synthesis;
- implicit conclusion;
- tone/purpose distinction;
- close distractors differing by scope or evidence.
