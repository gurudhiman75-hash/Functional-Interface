# ENG-008 CP005 — Research / Survey / Report Reading Comprehension — Source Audit V1

Status: `HUMAN_APPROVED__QUESTION_STUDIO_REVIEW_ONLY`

## Purpose

CP005 covers research-style and survey/report passages that require careful interpretation of findings, limits and evidence. It is designed for the analytical side of competitive-exam RC without requiring outside subject knowledge.

## Passage contract

- 6 original passages
- 380–520 words
- 6–8 paragraphs
- study/survey/report structure
- all facts required for answering are contained in the passage
- no live current-affairs claim is required as outside knowledge

Domains:
- workplace productivity study
- public transport survey
- sleep and study-habit research
- retail consumer survey
- environmental field study
- digital-learning evaluation

## Question families

- `RS-F01` — Direct finding
- `RS-F02` — Inference from results
- `RS-F03` — Main conclusion / best summary
- `RS-F04` — Limitation / scope
- `RS-F05` — Correlation vs causation
- `RS-F06` — Evidence vs interpretation
- `RS-F07` — Competing explanation / confound
- `RS-F08` — Practical implication / justified recommendation

Each passage carries all 8 families: **6 × 8 = 48 atomic authorities**.

## Difficulty

- Medium: direct findings, local inference, best summary, practical implication.
- Hard: limitation, correlation-vs-causation, competing explanation, scope-sensitive interpretation.
- No Easy authorities.

## Distractor rules

- overclaim beyond sample or period;
- causal claim from correlational evidence;
- reverse direction of relationship;
- confuse reported result with proposed explanation;
- ignore comparison group or baseline;
- generalise beyond sampled population;
- treat a limitation as proof the study is useless;
- recommend action stronger than the evidence supports.
