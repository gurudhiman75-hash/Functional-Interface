# ENG-008 CP002 — SSC Editorial & Current-Affairs Reading Comprehension — Source Audit V1

Status: `HUMAN_APPROVED__QUESTION_STUDIO_REVIEW_ONLY`

## Purpose

CP002 implements the SSC editorial / current-affairs side of Reading Comprehension after CP001 established the simpler story/report foundation.

The checkpoint is passage-led, evidence-led and fully authored inside Examtree. It does not reproduce newspaper/editorial/PYQ text.

## Official requirement traced

The SSC English syllabus explicitly includes Comprehension Passage and describes comprehension material that may include current-affairs reports/editorials in addition to simpler book/story passages.

CP002 therefore governs two passage classes:
- editorial / argumentative;
- current-affairs-style report / expository.

## Passage breadth

**12 original passages**
- 8 editorial / argumentative
- 4 current-affairs-style report / expository

Four editorial passages were preserved as seed material during CP001 restructuring and are now independently governed/reviewed under CP002. Eight additional passages expand the breadth.

## Question families

- `RC2-F01` — Direct factual/detail retrieval
- `RC2-F02` — Inference
- `RC2-F03` — Main idea / best summary
- `RC2-F04` — Tone / attitude
- `RC2-F05` — Author purpose
- `RC2-F06` — Implication / conclusion
- `RC2-F07` — Cause-effect / supported or unsupported claim
- `RC2-F08` — Vocabulary / phrase in context

Each passage carries one authority from every family: **12 × 8 = 96 atomic RC authorities**.

## Difficulty contract

- **Easy:** direct detail or low-distance contextual vocabulary.
- **Medium:** local inference, summary, cause-effect, purpose, supported claim.
- **Hard:** tone distinctions, scope-sensitive implications/conclusions, cross-paragraph synthesis.

Difficulty is driven by evidence distance and distractor closeness, not rare vocabulary.

## Editorial controls

- no outside GK needed to answer;
- no live claim is used as the keyed answer authority;
- current-affairs-style passages are original synthetic reports about plausible public issues, not copied journalism;
- one defensible answer only;
- distractors must be topic-plausible and differ by evidence, scope, cause/effect or author position;
- explanations identify the passage idea that supports the answer;
- no option-by-option filler.

## Validation

The checkpoint must prove:
- 12 unique passages;
- 96 unique question authorities;
- all 8 families exercised 12 times;
- both passage classes represented;
- 4 unique options per question;
- deterministic replay;
- all Easy / Medium / Hard bands reachable;
- 9,600-question soak;
- no downstream lifecycle leakage.
