# ENV-CP-020 — Integrated Environment GK

**Chapter:** ENV-001 Environment & Ecology  
**CP:** ENV-CP-020 Integrated Environment GK  
**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE  
**Review version:** V1  
**Runtime registration:** blocked pending project-owner approval

## Purpose

ENV-CP-020 is the capstone of the 20-CP Environment & Ecology blueprint. It introduces no major new content area. Instead, it tests whether learners can connect facts and concepts already established across ENV-CP-001 through ENV-CP-019.

## Scope

- ecology relationships, food chains and nutrient cycles
- ecosystem/biome features linked with biodiversity concepts
- biodiversity hotspots, threatened species and conservation approaches
- protected areas linked with important Indian sites/species
- pollution sources linked with pollutant effects
- atmosphere, ozone and climate-change distinctions
- Indian environmental laws linked with institutions and functions
- biodiversity-governance bodies linked across national/state/local levels
- Project Tiger, NTCA, tiger reserves and monitoring tools
- Ramsar wetlands linked with states, wetland types and ecological features
- international conventions linked with their subjects/mechanisms
- mixed multi-pair environment identification across the complete chapter

## Ownership boundary

CP020 is an integration layer only. The underlying factual authority remains with CP001–CP019. Any factual correction discovered here must be fixed in the owning CP as well, not silently redefined only in CP020.

## Deferred / excluded

- new current-affairs environment facts
- current counts, rankings, office-holders or latest designations
- facts not already covered or intentionally deferred by CP001–CP019
- obscure legal sections, COP trivia or changing scheme details
- Question Studio/runtime registration before explicit approval

## Learner-facing rules

- short SSC/Banking-style stems
- simple language even when reasoning depth is Medium/Hard
- plausible distractors drawn from the same environmental neighbourhood
- explanations state the cross-topic connection clearly in simple language
- no option-by-option filler
- no internal CP/QL/source labels in learner-facing text
- Hard difficulty comes from close distinctions, two-step associations and multi-pair matching

## Review contract

- 12 QLs
- 4 questions per QL
- 48 questions total
- Easy / Medium / Hard represented
- all four correct-answer positions inside every QL
- deterministic generation
- source/fact provenance on every item
- every QL integrates material from multiple earlier CPs
- review-only; no runtime registration before explicit project-owner approval
