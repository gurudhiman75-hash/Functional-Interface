# ENV-CP-019 — Ramsar Sites & Wetlands

**Chapter:** ENV-001 Environment & Ecology

**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE

## Scope

ENV-CP-019 covers stable, exam-relevant wetland concepts and important Indian Ramsar-site facts:

- Ramsar wetland definition and broad inland/coastal/human-made wetland types
- India's entry into the Ramsar Convention and its first designated sites
- Chilika Lake and its restoration/Montreux Record history
- Keoladeo National Park and Montreux Record context
- Loktak Lake, phumdis and Keibul Lamjao linkage
- Wular Lake and the Jhelum system
- Harike Wetland and major Punjab Ramsar-site identification
- Deepor Beel and East Kolkata Wetlands
- Sambhar Lake and Kolleru Lake
- Vembanad-Kol Wetland and Tsomoriri
- Ramsar site–state / wetland-type matching
- Montreux Record concept at stable historical level

## Ownership boundary

ENV-CP-017 owns treaty names, adoption years and international treaty mechanics. ENV-CP-019 owns wetland concepts and India-focused Ramsar-site knowledge. ENV-CP-010 remains the general Indian protected-area layer.

## Deferred / excluded

- current number of Ramsar Sites in India or worldwide
- latest Ramsar designations and changing state-wise site counts
- current area totals and mutable rankings
- current office-holders, COP hosts and current Ramsar Secretariat details
- changing ecological-status assessments or current management statistics
- exhaustive lists of every Ramsar Site

## Learner-facing rules

- short, direct SSC/Banking-style stems
- simple language even for Medium/Hard
- distractors from the same wetland/site neighbourhood
- explanations add one useful location, type, ecological feature or historical distinction
- no source names or internal QL labels in learner-facing text
- no option-by-option explanation filler
- Hard items use close site-state, feature-site or Montreux distinctions rather than mutable trivia
