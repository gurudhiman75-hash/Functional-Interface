## Scope

ENV-CP-017 covers the high-yield international environmental agreements most commonly tested in competitive exams:

- Ramsar Convention on Wetlands
- CITES / Washington Convention
- Convention on Migratory Species / Bonn Convention
- Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD)
- United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC)
- Kyoto Protocol
- Paris Agreement
- Vienna Convention and Montreal Protocol for ozone protection, including the Kigali Amendment at basic level
- Basel Convention on hazardous wastes
- Stockholm Convention on Persistent Organic Pollutants
- Minamata Convention on Mercury
- integrated treaty-topic, chronology and place-name matching

## Ownership boundary

ENV-CP-013 owns ozone science. ENV-CP-014 owns climate-change science, mitigation and adaptation. ENV-CP-017 owns the international treaty layer, including treaty names, fixed adoption years/places, core objectives and major mechanisms. It does not repeat the Indian-law or Indian-institution layers from CP015/CP016.

## Deferred / excluded

- current party counts and current ratification rankings
- current COP numbers, hosts, presidents and latest negotiation outcomes
- current Ramsar-site counts by country or state
- mutable nationally determined contributions and current emissions targets
- detailed article numbers, annex schedules and amendment-by-amendment legal text
- obscure secretariat staffing or office-holder trivia

## Learner-facing rules

- short, direct SSC/Banking-style stems
- simple language even for Medium/Hard
- distractors from the same treaty neighbourhood
- explanations add one useful treaty distinction, objective or application
- no source names or internal QL labels in learner-facing text
- no option-by-option explanation filler
- Hard items use chronology, close treaty distinctions or multi-pair matching rather than obscure legal trivia
