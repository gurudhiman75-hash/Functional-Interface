## Scope

Included:
- food chain and food web
- grazing and detritus food chains
- trophic levels
- direction of energy flow
- producers and successive consumers in a chain
- ecological pyramids: number, biomass and energy
- predator–prey relation at basic exam level
- simple applied food-chain sequences

Deferred:
- nutrient cycles (ENV-CP-004)
- biome-specific ecology (ENV-CP-005)
- biodiversity and conservation (ENV-CP-006 onward)

## Design rules

1. Stems stay short and exam-like.
2. Explanations directly state the ecological rule being tested.
3. Chain-sequence distractors use plausible biological orders.
4. Trophic-level questions do not rely on obscure species facts.
5. The pyramid of energy is always upright; number and biomass may vary by ecosystem.
6. Energy flow is one-way through trophic levels.
7. Standard instruction stems may repeat when the tested relation differs.
8. V1 is the approved learner-facing wording baseline.
