## Scope

ENV-CP-018 covers stable, exam-relevant Project Tiger and tiger-reserve facts:

- Project Tiger launch in 1973 and its conservation objective
- Project Tiger as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change
- the original nine Project Tiger reserves and stable reserve–state matching
- tiger-reserve core / buffer strategy
- critical/core habitat versus buffer/peripheral management distinction
- NTCA statutory authority for Project Tiger and its role under the Wild Life (Protection) Act framework
- Tiger Conservation Plans and broad NTCA approval/oversight functions
- ecologically unsustainable land-use controls and tourism/conservation standards at exam level
- M-STrIPES: purpose, full form and broad monitoring functions
- tiger monitoring, patrolling and management tools
- tiger reintroduction examples such as Sariska and Panna at a stable conceptual level
- integrated Project Tiger, NTCA, M-STrIPES and reserve–state identification

## Ownership boundary

ENV-CP-009 owns protected-area categories. ENV-CP-010 owns important protected areas generally. ENV-CP-016 owns the broader Indian environmental-institutions layer. ENV-CP-018 owns the dedicated Project Tiger / tiger-reserve layer.
