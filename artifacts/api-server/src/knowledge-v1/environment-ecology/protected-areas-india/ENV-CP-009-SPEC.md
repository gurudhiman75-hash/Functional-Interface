## Learner-facing rules

1. Stems use direct competitive-exam formats from V1.
2. Explanations use one short fact or distinction.
3. Under the Act, protected area means a National Park, sanctuary, conservation reserve or community reserve.
4. Conservation reserves are declared on Government-owned land, especially land adjacent to protected areas or linking protected areas, after consultation with local communities.
5. Community reserves may be declared on private or community land when a community or individual volunteers to conserve wildlife and habitat.
6. A community reserve cannot already be part of a National Park, sanctuary or conservation reserve.
7. A conservation reserve management committee advises the Chief Wild Life Warden.
8. A community reserve management committee is responsible for conserving, maintaining and managing the community reserve.
9. Biosphere reserve is not one of the four categories in the Act's statutory definition of protected area.
10. No learner question relies on changing national totals.
11. Review generators remain `runtimeRegistered: false` until approval.
