**Chapter:** ENV-001 Environment & Ecology

**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE

## Purpose

ENV-CP-021 is an audit-remediation extension created after the chapter-wide ENV-CP-001 through ENV-CP-020 exhaustive review found important Static GK gaps that were not owned by any existing Environment CP.

It does not duplicate Polity-owned constitutional environment provisions. It adds only missing Environment-owned exam material.

## Included coverage

- ecological succession
- primary versus secondary succession
- pioneer species and climax-community concept
- Environmental Impact Assessment purpose
- EIA Notification, 2006 and prior environmental-clearance concept
- distinction between EIA and routine operational pollution monitoring
- Project Elephant launch and broad objectives
- elephant habitat, corridor and human-elephant conflict concepts
- Chipko movement
- Appiko movement
- Save Silent Valley movement
- Khejarli/Bishnoi tree-protection tradition
- National Action Plan on Climate Change, 2008
- original eight-mission NAPCC framework
- National Solar Mission
- National Water Mission
- Green India Mission
- National Mission on Sustainable Habitat
- integrated cross-domain Environment GK questions

## Ownership boundary

- ENV-CP-014 continues to own climate-change science, mitigation and adaptation concepts.
- ENV-CP-015 continues to own the main Indian environmental Acts, NGT and pollution-control institutions.
- ENV-CP-018 remains exclusively Project Tiger / tiger reserves.
- ENV-CP-021 owns the previously orphaned NAPCC static-GK layer, Project Elephant basics, ecological succession, EIA basics and major Indian environmental movements.
- Article 48A and Article 51A(g) remain owned by the Polity chapter and are not duplicated here.
