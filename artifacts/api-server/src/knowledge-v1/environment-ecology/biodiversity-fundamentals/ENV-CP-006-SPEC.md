## Scope

Included:
- biodiversity meaning
- genetic diversity
- species diversity
- ecosystem diversity
- species richness
- native species
- endemic species
- exotic/alien species
- invasive alien species
- keystone species
- flagship species
- close distinctions among these terms

Deferred:
- biodiversity hotspots and hotspot criteria (ENV-CP-007)
- IUCN categories and species conservation (ENV-CP-008)
- protected areas (ENV-CP-009 onward)
- mutable species status, population counts or current lists

## Design rules

1. Stems stay short and exam-like.
2. Explanations state the tested distinction directly.
3. Genetic, species and ecosystem diversity must not be conflated.
4. Endemic means naturally restricted to a particular geographic area; native means naturally occurring in an area.
5. Alien/exotic means introduced outside the natural range; not every alien species is invasive.
6. Invasive alien species spread outside their native range and cause or are likely to cause ecological harm.
7. Keystone species have an ecological effect disproportionately large relative to their abundance.
8. Flagship species are selected as symbols to build public support for conservation; flagship status does not itself imply a keystone ecological role.
9. Species richness means the number of species present in an area.
10. Standard instruction stems may repeat when the tested relation differs.
11. All review questions remain `runtimeRegistered: false` until approval.
