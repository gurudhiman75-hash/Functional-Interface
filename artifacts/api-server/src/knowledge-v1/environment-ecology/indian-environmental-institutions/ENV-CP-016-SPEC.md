# ENV-CP-016 — Indian Environmental Institutions

**Chapter:** ENV-001 Environment & Ecology  
**CP:** ENV-CP-016 Indian Environmental Institutions  
**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE  
**Review version:** V1  
**Runtime registration:** blocked pending project-owner approval

## Scope

ENV-CP-016 covers stable, exam-relevant Indian environmental institutions and their functions without repeating the basic environmental-law layer frozen in ENV-CP-015.

- CPCB / SPCB / PCC: national, state and Union Territory pollution-control roles at comparison level only
- National Biodiversity Authority (NBA)
- State Biodiversity Boards (SBBs)
- Biodiversity Management Committees (BMCs) and People’s Biodiversity Registers (PBRs)
- National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA)
- Central Zoo Authority (CZA)
- Wildlife Crime Control Bureau (WCCB)
- Forest Survey of India (FSI)
- Botanical Survey of India (BSI)
- Zoological Survey of India (ZSI)
- Wildlife Institute of India (WII)
- integrated institution–function identification

## Ownership boundary

ENV-CP-015 owns Act-year, Act-purpose and broad CPCB/SPCB/NGT legal basics. ENV-CP-016 deepens the institutional layer and therefore avoids recycling those direct questions. Detailed international conventions, treaty secretariats and global bodies remain reserved for ENV-CP-017.

## Deferred / excluded

- current office-holders and current board membership
- current numbers of BMCs, tiger reserves, zoos, regional offices or projects
- current species population estimates or changing rankings
- detailed treaty mechanics, COP chronology and international institutions (ENV-CP-017)
- obscure section-number memorisation
- short-lived schemes, portals or current notifications unless later authorized

## Learner-facing rules

- short, direct SSC/Banking-style stems
- simple language even for Medium/Hard
- distractors from the same institutional neighbourhood
- explanations add one useful distinction, function or application
- no source names or internal QL labels in learner-facing text
- no option-by-option explanation filler
- Hard items increase relation depth through close institution-function distinctions, not obscure trivia
