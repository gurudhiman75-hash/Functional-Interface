## Stability rule
Concepts such as density, distribution, growth, age composition, sex ratio, literacy, migration and settlement types may be permanent.
All numeric counts, percentages, rankings and state comparisons must be explicitly tied to Census 2011 unless a later official census becomes authoritative.
