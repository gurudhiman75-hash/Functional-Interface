## Scope

CP008 covers high-frequency Indian hill, range and peak relations that belong to physiography. It avoids obscure peak trivia and keeps difficulty in comparison and relation-composition rather than difficult wording.

### Qualified coverage

- Aravali Hills: highly eroded, southwest–northeast trend from Gujarat towards Delhi
- Guru Shikhar as the highest peak of the Aravali range
- Vindhya and Satpura relation to the Narmada valley
- Western Ghats and Eastern Ghats as the western/eastern edges of the Deccan Plateau
- Western Ghats higher and more continuous than Eastern Ghats
- Eastern Ghats discontinuous and cut by rivers flowing to the Bay of Bengal
- Sahyadri as a common name for the Western Ghats
- Nilgiri Hills where the Western and Eastern Ghats meet in the south
- Anaimalai and Cardamom Hills as southern hill sections of the Western Ghats system
- Anamudi, Doddabetta and Mahendragiri as standard exam peaks
- Mahadev Hills and Maikal Range with the Satpura system
- Kaimur Range as an eastern extension of the Vindhyan system
