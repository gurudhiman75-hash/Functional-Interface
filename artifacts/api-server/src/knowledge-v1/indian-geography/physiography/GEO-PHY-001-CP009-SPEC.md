Status: REVIEW CANDIDATE V1

## Scope

CP009 covers high-frequency Indian mountain passes, valleys and gaps used in school and competitive-exam geography.

### Qualified coverage

- Zoji La: Kashmir Valley–Ladakh relation and Srinagar–Leh route
- Banihal Pass in the Pir Panjal system on the Jammu–Kashmir Valley approach
- Rohtang Pass linking Kullu with Lahaul–Spiti
- Shipki La in Himachal Pradesh and the Sutlej entry corridor
- Nathu La in Sikkim on the India–Tibet/China route
- Khardung La north of Leh on the route towards Nubra Valley
- Kashmir Valley between the Greater Himalaya and Pir Panjal; Jhelum river relation
- Kullu Valley and the Beas river
- Kangra Valley between the Dhauladhar and Shiwalik systems
- Dehra Dun as a longitudinal valley between the Lesser Himalaya and Shiwaliks
- Palghat (Palakkad) Gap between the Nilgiri and Anaimalai hills, linking Kerala and Tamil Nadu
- Bhor Ghat and Thal Ghat as important Western Ghats routes in Maharashtra

## Language rule

Difficulty must come from relation composition, not difficult wording. Internal source/review/generator language must never appear in learner-facing text.
