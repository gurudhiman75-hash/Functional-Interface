## Scope

CP004 covers the high-frequency structure of the Peninsular Plateau:

- old crystalline, igneous and metamorphic tableland
- Gondwana origin and broad landform character
- Deccan Trap as a volcanic-origin black-soil region
- Central Highlands and Deccan Plateau
- Narmada as the broad dividing reference between the two regions
- Malwa, Bundelkhand, Baghelkhand and Chotanagpur associations needed to understand the Central Highlands
- Chambal, Sind, Betwa and Ken drainage direction across the Central Highlands
- Deccan Plateau shape, northern base and eastward slope
- Mahadev Hills, Kaimur Hills and Maikal Range as eastern extensions of the Deccan Plateau
- northeastern plateau extension, including its fault separation from the Chotanagpur Plateau
- Western Ghats versus Eastern Ghats as the western/eastern edges of the Deccan Plateau

Detailed peak trivia is intentionally left to CP008. State-association drilling is left to CP010.
