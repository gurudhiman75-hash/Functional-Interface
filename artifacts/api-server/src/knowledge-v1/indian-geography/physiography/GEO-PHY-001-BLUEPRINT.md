## 1. Goal

Build an exam-standard, source-governed Indian Physiography chapter that covers the physical divisions and their high-frequency subregions without relying on current/live facts. The chapter must support direct, reverse, pair, statement, match and mixed-composition questions while keeping explanations simple and beginner-readable.

## 2. Source policy

Primary educational authority: NCERT `Contemporary India-I`, Chapter 2 — Physical Features of India. Official Survey of India / Geological Survey / government sources may be used as corroborating authorities where an NCERT statement needs more precise geographic framing. Runtime web/LLM output is never truth authority.

## 4. Major physical divisions used as the chapter spine

1. The Himalayan Mountains
2. The Northern Plains
3. The Peninsular Plateau
4. The Indian Desert
5. The Coastal Plains
6. The Islands

These six divisions are the canonical high-level learner categories. Subdivisions are introduced only in the owning checkpoint.

## 5. Question-quality rules

- Standard exam instructions may repeat; semantic question payloads must not.
- Every CP review batch should expose at least six distinct payloads per QL where the source corpus supports it.
- Explanations must state the decisive fact directly, not discuss option-analysis strategy.
- No internal source/generator/review terminology in learner-facing content.
- Avoid obscure trivia merely to manufacture difficulty.
- Hard questions should come from relation composition, comparison and multi-statement reasoning over already-qualified facts.
- Negative questions require a bounded/complete canonical relation set; absence alone is never proof.
- Learner-facing geographic proper nouns must use conventional school/exam forms.

## 6. Difficulty model

- Easy: one-hop identification or direct association.
- Medium: reverse association, correct/incorrect pair, two-clue identification, or two-statement reasoning.
- Hard: cross-division comparison, three-statement count, multi-relation matching or mixed integration.

## 8. CP001 target

`GEO-PHY-001-CP001 — Major Physiographic Divisions` establishes the six-division framework, basic formation/character descriptions, and major contrast relations used by later checkpoints. It must not pre-empt detailed range, plateau, coastal or island facts owned by later CPs.
