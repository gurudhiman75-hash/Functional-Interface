## Scope

CP007 covers the two major island groups of India and the standard school/competitive-exam facts used to distinguish them.

### Qualified coverage

- Lakshadweep in the Arabian Sea, close to the Malabar Coast
- Lakshadweep as a group of small coral islands
- Kavaratti as the administrative headquarters of Lakshadweep
- Pitti island and its bird sanctuary
- Andaman and Nicobar Islands in the Bay of Bengal
- north–south chain and Andaman/Nicobar division
- Ten Degree Channel separating the Andaman and Nicobar groups
- Andaman and Nicobar islands as larger, more numerous and more scattered than Lakshadweep
- Andaman and Nicobar islands as elevated parts of submarine mountains
- Barren Island and active volcanism
- equatorial climate and thick forest cover of the Andaman and Nicobar group
