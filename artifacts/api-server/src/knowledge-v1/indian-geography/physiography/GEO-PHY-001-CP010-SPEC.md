# GEO-PHY-001 CP010 — Plateau–Hill–State Associations

Status: REVIEW CANDIDATE V1  
Chapter: `GEO-PHY-001 — Indian Physiography & Physical Divisions`  
Permanent QLs: `GEO-PHY-001-QL-082` to `GEO-PHY-001-QL-090`

## Scope

CP010 tests the state and regional location of important plateaus and hill groups already established by the physiography chapter. It does not treat a regional physical boundary as if it were an exact political boundary; wording uses `mainly`, `extends into`, `border region` or `meeting area` where needed.

### Qualified coverage

- Malwa Plateau — mainly western Madhya Pradesh, extending into southeastern Rajasthan
- Bundelkhand — northern Madhya Pradesh and southern Uttar Pradesh
- Baghelkhand — mainly northeastern Madhya Pradesh, extending into southeastern Uttar Pradesh
- Chota Nagpur Plateau — mainly Jharkhand, with nearby extensions
- Ranchi Plateau — Jharkhand
- Meghalaya Plateau — Meghalaya; Garo, Khasi and Jaintia Hills west to east
- Karbi Anglong and North Cachar Hills — Assam
- Deccan Plateau — large parts of Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh
- Karnataka Plateau — Karnataka
- Telangana Plateau — Telangana
- Bastar Plateau — mainly southern Chhattisgarh
- Nilgiri Hills — Tamil Nadu–Kerala–Karnataka meeting area
- Anaimalai Hills — Tamil Nadu–Kerala border region
- Cardamom Hills — mainly Kerala, extending towards Tamil Nadu

## Language and quality rules

- Keep stems short, direct and exam-like.
- Difficulty comes from relation composition, not difficult wording.
- Do not imply exact state boundaries for physical regions when the source supports only a broad regional location.
- Use plausible geography distractors, not obviously unrelated filler.
- Explanations state the decisive location fact directly.
- No source, generator or review terminology appears in learner-facing text.

## Source policy

NCERT `Contemporary India-I`, Chapter 2 is the educational spine. NCERT `India: Physical Environment` and government geography references may corroborate regional/state location where Chapter 2 does not state the political location precisely.
