## Scope

CP003 owns the Northern Plains as a distinct physiographic unit, including:

- formation by alluvium brought by the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra river systems and their tributaries;
- broad regional divisions: Punjab Plains, Ganga Plain and Brahmaputra Plain;
- Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar and Khadar;
- exam-standard contrasts among these relief/alluvial belts.

Detailed river-system facts remain owned by `GEO-RIV-001`. CP003 uses rivers only as geographic boundaries or formation agents for the plains.
