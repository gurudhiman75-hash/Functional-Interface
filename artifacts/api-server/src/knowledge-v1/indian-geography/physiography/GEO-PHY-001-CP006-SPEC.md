# GEO-PHY-001 CP006 — Coastal Plains

Status: **REVIEW-ONLY CANDIDATE**

QL range: `GEO-PHY-001-QL-046` to `GEO-PHY-001-QL-054`

## Scope

CP006 covers the standard school/competitive-exam facts for India’s coastal plains:

- coastal strips along the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal;
- Western Coastal Plain between the Western Ghats and Arabian Sea;
- narrow nature of the western coast;
- Konkan, Kannad Plain and Malabar Coast;
- Eastern Coastal Plain along the Bay of Bengal;
- wide and level nature of the eastern coast;
- Northern Circar and Coromandel Coast;
- large east-coast deltas of the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri;
- Chilika Lake and its position in Odisha south of the Mahanadi delta;
- standard west/east comparisons: submerged vs emergent coast, delta development and Malabar backwaters (kayals).
