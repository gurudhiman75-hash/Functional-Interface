## Scope

CP001 establishes the basic controls that explain broad climatic differences across India and the basic monsoon character of India's climate. Detailed monsoon mechanism, seasons and regional rainfall distribution belong to later checkpoints.

The checkpoint must cover the standard climate controls at least once: latitude, altitude, pressure and winds, distance from sea, ocean currents and relief.

## Ownership boundary

- CP001 may explain the broad effect of the Himalayas and relief, but detailed regional rainfall paths belong to later rainfall/monsoon CPs.
- CP001 may state that warm or cold ocean currents can modify nearby coastal temperatures, especially with onshore winds; it does not require obscure current names.
- CP001 may use seasonal pressure change only as a basic control. Detailed ITCZ, jet-stream and ENSO mechanism belongs to later CPs.
