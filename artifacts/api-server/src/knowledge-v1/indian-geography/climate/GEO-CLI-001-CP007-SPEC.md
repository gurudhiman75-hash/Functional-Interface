## Scope

CP007 owns the broad distribution and year-to-year variability of rainfall in India:

- QL055 — average rainfall and broad spatial variation
- QL056 — high and medium rainfall belts
- QL057 — low and inadequate rainfall belts
- QL058 — rainfall variability and coefficient of variation
- QL059 — low-variability rainfall regions
- QL060 — high-variability rainfall regions

## Source authority

Primary authority: NCERT `India: Physical Environment`, Climate chapter, especially `Distribution of Rainfall`, `Variability of Rainfall`, and `Monsoons and the Economic Life in India`.

Supporting authority: NCERT `Contemporary India-I`, Climate chapter.

Key governed facts include:

- India's average annual rainfall is about 125 cm, but spatial variation is very large.
- High-rainfall areas broadly exceed 200 cm annually.
- Medium-rainfall areas broadly receive 100-200 cm.
- Low-rainfall areas broadly receive 50-100 cm.
- Inadequate-rainfall areas receive below 50 cm.
- Rainfall variability is measured using coefficient of variation: standard deviation divided by mean, multiplied by 100.
- In some places annual rainfall may deviate roughly 20-50 per cent from the mean.
- Areas with variability below 25 per cent generally receive more than 100 cm annually.
- Areas with variability above 50 per cent generally receive less than 50 cm annually.
- Much of the remaining country broadly falls in the 25-50 per cent variability range and 50-100 cm rainfall range.
- Rainfall variability can produce droughts or floods and strongly affects rain-fed agriculture.
- Agricultural success depends on rainfall being timely and adequately distributed, not only on the annual total.
- Sudden intense monsoon rain can increase soil erosion.

## Ownership boundary

CP007 does not own:

- detailed orographic causes, rain-shadow mechanisms or fine regional rainfall gradients — CP008;
- detailed western disturbances, jet streams or ENSO — CP009;
- advancing monsoon onset and branch movement — CP005;
- retreating monsoon and cyclonic rain — CP006.

Regional examples are used only to establish the broad rainfall and variability classes owned by CP007.
