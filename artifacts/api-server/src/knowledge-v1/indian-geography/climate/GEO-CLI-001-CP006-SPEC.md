## Source authority

Primary authority: NCERT `India: Physical Environment`, Climate chapter, especially the section `Season of Retreating Monsoon`.

Supporting authority: NCERT `Contemporary India-I`, Climate chapter.

Key governed facts include:

- October and November are the principal retreating-monsoon months.
- The southwest monsoon weakens as the low-pressure trough shifts southward.
- Withdrawal starts from western Rajasthan and progresses southward.
- Retreating-monsoon weather can include clear skies, warmth, humidity and `October heat`.
- Northern India is generally dry while the eastern/southeastern Peninsula receives important rain.
- Northeast winds can gain moisture while crossing the Bay of Bengal before reaching the Tamil Nadu / Coromandel coast.
- Many rain-bearing cyclonic depressions originate near the Andaman Sea and cross the eastern coast of the southern Peninsula.
- The Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri delta regions are especially exposed to destructive cyclones.
- Comparable cyclonic storms are less frequent in the Arabian Sea.
