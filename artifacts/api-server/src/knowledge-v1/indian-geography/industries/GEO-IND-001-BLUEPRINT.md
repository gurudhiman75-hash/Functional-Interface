# GEO-IND-001 — Industries & Industrial Geography of India

Status: IMPLEMENTATION IN PROGRESS — REVIEW ONLY

## 1. Design rule

This package is breadth-driven. There is no preset QL count, CP count or chapter question total.

Semantic coverage is defined first; stable semantic QL IDs are assigned from that coverage. Numeric QL ranges are not an authoring authority.

## 2. Source authority

### Foundation
- NCERT Class X Geography — Manufacturing Industries.
- NCERT Class XII India: People and Economy — Manufacturing Industries / industrial regions.

### Sector authorities
- DPIIT annual reports and industrial-policy/corridor material.
- Ministry of Steel.
- Ministry of Textiles.
- Department of Chemicals & Petrochemicals.
- Department of Fertilizers.
- Ministry of Heavy Industries.
- Ministry of Food Processing Industries.
- Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises where location/classification facts genuinely belong.
- Relevant PSU/company official material only for durable plant/location facts.

### Stability rule
Permanent QLs may own durable facts such as:
- raw-material/industry relationship
- industrial-location factors
- classic industrial centres and belts
- integrated steel plants and long-lived plant locations
- textile centres and regional specialisation
- public/private/cooperative classification where stable
- agro-based vs mineral-based relationships
- industrial regions and map relationships
- pollution/industrial-environment concepts at static-GK depth

Annual output, export value, current rank, capacity, employment and market-share figures are dated-only and never silently promoted to timeless static facts.
