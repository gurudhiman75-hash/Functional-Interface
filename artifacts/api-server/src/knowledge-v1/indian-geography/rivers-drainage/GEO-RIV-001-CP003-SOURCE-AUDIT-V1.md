## 2. Primary official coverage

### NMCG — Course of Ganga

### NMCG — Hydrology of Ganga Basin
Use for:

- Farakka split and Padma / Bhagirathi-Hooghly course only where wording is durable and unambiguous

### District Prayagraj, Government of Uttar Pradesh
Use for:
- current place name Prayagraj

## 3. Exam-frequency evidence

Competitive-exam Geography repeatedly tests:
- Bhagirathi + Alaknanda → Ganga at Devprayag
- Gangotri Glacier / Gaumukh
- Panch Prayag matching
- Yamuna source and Ganga confluence
- left-bank versus right-bank tributaries
- Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi and Sone associations
- Kosi as the “Sorrow of Bihar”
- Chambal / Betwa / Ken as Yamuna tributaries
- correct/incorrect pair questions
- short joining chains and statement questions

This evidence controls question-family emphasis, not factual truth.

## 4. Explicit exclusions from initial CP003

The first CP003 authority excludes:
- changing dam/project status
- pollution rankings or current water-quality claims
- discharge / annual water-yield numbers
- exact river lengths and basin-area figures where measurement conventions vary
- flood-loss statistics
- folklore-only or mythological river claims as geographic truth
- disputed or weakly sourced micro-tributary facts
- city-on-river coverage that belongs primarily to CP012 unless needed to anchor a confluence

## 5. Source-discrepancy rule

When official sources use different levels of precision or legacy place names:
- store the stable common geography relation;
- preserve source wording in provenance;
- use the current official place name in learner-facing text when independently corroborated;
- do not manufacture extra precision.

Example: older hydrology sources may say the Yamuna meets the Ganga at Allahabad. CP003 may use **Prayagraj** in learner-facing text because the current district authority identifies the Ganga-Yamuna confluence there, while preserving the older locator in provenance.

## 6. Initial coverage gate

CP003 should not enter question review until the canonical corpus covers at least:
- Bhagirathi / Gangotri / Gaumukh
- Alaknanda + Bhagirathi → Ganga at Devprayag
- all five Panch Prayag relations
- Haridwar plains-entry anchor
- Bay of Bengal outfall
- Yamuna source + Prayagraj confluence
- Ramganga, Gomti, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi and Sone
- major left/right bank relations
- Chambal, Betwa and Ken as Yamuna tributaries
- Kosi formation and “Sorrow of Bihar”

## 7. Editorial standard

Use simple exam English:
- short direct stems
- familiar river/place names
- difficulty from relation depth, not wording
- concise question-specific explanations
- no filler such as “associated with the source” or “matches the reviewed relation”
- no shortcut/trap sections
- statement explanations must state the corrected fact directly
