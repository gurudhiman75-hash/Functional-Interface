## Approved systems

### Godavari
Use durable facts only:
- rises in the Nashik/Trimbak region of Maharashtra
- east/south-east flowing peninsular system
- drains to the Bay of Bengal
- Pravara, Manjra/Manjira and Darna as important right-bank tributaries
- Purna, Pranhita, Indravati and Sabari as important left-bank tributaries
- Wainganga + Wardha relation to Pranhita
- Godavari also known as Dakshin Ganga

### Krishna
- rises just north of Mahabaleshwar in the Western Ghats, Maharashtra
- flows east to the Bay of Bengal downstream of Vijayawada
- Ghataprabha, Malaprabha and Tungabhadra as principal right-bank tributaries
- Bhima, Musi and Munneru as principal left-bank tributaries
- Tungabhadra formed by Tunga + Bhadra

### Mahanadi
- source convention in the Sihawa hills / Dhamtari district of Chhattisgarh
- flows east to the Bay of Bengal
- Seonath, Hasdeo, Mand and Ib as left-bank tributaries
- Ong, Tel and Jonk as right-bank tributaries

### Cauvery (Kaveri)
- rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri range, Karnataka
- drains to the Bay of Bengal in Tamil Nadu
- Harangi, Hemavati, Shimsha and Arkavati as left-bank tributaries
- Lakshmantirtha, Kabini, Suvarnavati, Bhavani, Noyyal and Amaravati as right-bank tributaries
- Cauvery/Kaveri spelling treated as an alias pair, not separate rivers

### Pennar
- rises in the Chenna Kasava hill / Nandidurg range in Karnataka
- east-flowing to the Bay of Bengal
- Jayamangali, Kunderu and Sagileru as left-bank tributaries
- Chitravati, Papagni and Cheyyeru as right-bank tributaries

### Other important east-flowing systems
- Brahmani: South Koel upper-reach convention; Sankh + Koel formation relation; Bay of Bengal
- Baitarani: source in Keonjhar/Kendujhar hills; Salandi and Matai tributaries; Bay of Bengal
- Subarnarekha: source near Nagri, Ranchi district; Kanchi, Kharkai and Karkari tributaries; Bay of Bengal

## Discrepancy handling

### Mahanadi source wording
CWC identifies the source near Farsiya village in Dhamtari district, while Chhattisgarh district sources use the Sihawa hills convention. These are stored as compatible source-place/source-region relations rather than collapsed into invented precision.

### Cauvery / Kaveri
Government/CWC sources commonly use `Cauvery`; exam and textbook usage also commonly uses `Kaveri`. The engine stores one river entity with both accepted labels.

### Godavari ranking and numerical claims
Exact ranking/length/catchment questions are excluded from CP005 because conventions and measurement datasets can differ. `Dakshin Ganga` is retained as a stable nickname with Maharashtra government provenance.

### Brahmani wording
CWC material describes South Koel as the upper-reach naming, while Odisha government material explicitly describes Sankh + Koel joining near Rourkela/Vedavyas to form Brahmani. Formation questions use the Odisha government formation relation; generic source questions use the reviewed CWC/state wording.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not generate CP005 questions from:
- exact river lengths or basin-area percentages
- current reservoir storage/flood status
- current inter-state disputes or tribunal allocations
- changing project status
- disputed superlatives unless independently locked in a later CP
- dams/projects/reservoir matching (reserved for CP010)
- city-on-river matching (reserved for CP012)
- obscure minor tributaries used only to inflate difficulty

## Editorial standard

- Natural competitive-exam English.
- Difficulty from relation depth, not obscure phrasing.
- Prefer river-for-river, source-for-source and tributary-for-tributary distractors.
- Explanations state the correct relation directly, then add at most one useful connected fact.
- Avoid `associated with`, `matches the reviewed relation`, `exam trap`, `shortcut`, and similar machine/editorial commentary.
- Maps/photos/diagrams are optional manual editorial attachments only.
