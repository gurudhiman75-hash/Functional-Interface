## Scope

Build a deterministic, source-backed Static GK package for the Ganga river system, focused on durable relations repeatedly tested in SSC, Railway, Banking, State and UPSC-style objective exams.

## Canonical knowledge domains

### A. Ganga headwaters
- Bhagirathi as source stream
- Gangotri Glacier and Gaumukh
- Alaknanda + Bhagirathi
- Devprayag formation/name anchor

### B. Panch Prayag
- Vishnuprayag — Dhauliganga + Alaknanda
- Nandprayag — Nandakini + Alaknanda
- Karnaprayag — Pindar + Alaknanda
- Rudraprayag — Mandakini + Alaknanda
- Devprayag — Bhagirathi + Alaknanda

### C. Ganga main-course anchors
- enters the plains at Haridwar
- flows to the Bay of Bengal
- selected durable Farakka / Padma / Bhagirathi-Hooghly relations only where needed

### D. Major tributaries
- Yamuna
- Ramganga
- Gomti
- Ghaghara
- Gandak
- Kosi
- Sone

### E. Yamuna system
- Yamunotri Glacier / Banderpoonch source area
- joins Ganga at Prayagraj
- Chambal, Betwa and Ken as important tributaries

### F. High-frequency tributary anchors
- Ramganga → Ganga near Kannauj
- Gomti → Ganga at Audihar
- Ghaghara → Ganga near Chapra
- Gandak → Ganga near Patna
- Kosi → Ganga west of Manihari
- Sone → Ganga near Arrah
- Kosi formed by Sun Kosi + Arun Kosi + Tamur Kosi
- Kosi = “Sorrow of Bihar”

## Difficulty

### Easy
- one direct source/course relation
- one major tributary relation
- familiar confluence or place

### Medium
- reverse association
- left/right bank discrimination
- Panch Prayag matching
- close same-system distractors
- two linked facts

### Hard
- multi-hop river relation
- ordered confluence chain
- statement combinations
- close tributary/source/place discrimination

Hard wording must remain simple.

## Distractor rules

Prefer same-class distractors:
- river for river
- glacier/source place for source place
- confluence place for confluence place
- real river candidates for bank-side questions
- Panch Prayag place for Panch Prayag place

Every distractor must be checked against the canonical relation graph so no second correct answer is introduced.

## Explanation standard

Explanations should:
1. state the correct relation immediately;
2. add one useful connected fact where it improves understanding;
3. correct a false statement directly when applicable;
4. remain simple and exam-like.

Preferred example:
> The Bhagirathi and Alaknanda meet at Devprayag. From this confluence, the river is known as the Ganga.

Avoid generic phrases such as “this matches the reviewed relation,” “associated with the source,” “exam trap,” tautological pair explanations, and unnecessary shortcut commentary.

## Reviewed fact normalization

Bhagirathi and Alaknanda are modeled as joining at Devprayag to form the Ganga. Bhagirathi must not be represented as a tributary of Alaknanda merely because a source sentence states that it joins the Alaknanda there.
