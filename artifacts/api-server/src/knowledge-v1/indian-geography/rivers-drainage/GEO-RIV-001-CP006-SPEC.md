## Canonical domains

### A. Sources and terminal drainage
- Narmada — Amarkantak/Maikal — Gulf of Khambhat/Arabian Sea
- Tapi — Satpura region — Arabian Sea/Gulf of Khambhat
- Mahi — Vindhyan region — Gulf of Khambhat
- Sabarmati — Aravalli Hills, Rajasthan — Gulf of Khambhat
- Luni — western Aravalli near Ajmer — Rann of Kutch
- Netravati and selected Kerala/Karnataka rivers — Arabian Sea

### B. Structural geography
- Narmada and Tapi as major west-flowing rift-valley rivers
- Narmada between Vindhya and Satpura ranges
- Luni as an inland/terminal-drainage contrast to Arabian-Sea rivers

### C. Major tributaries and bank side
- Narmada: Tawa, Hiran and selected major tributaries
- Tapi: Purna, Girna, Aner and selected bank-side relations
- Mahi: Som, Anas, Panam
- Sabarmati: Wakal, Harnav, Hathmati, Watrak, Sei
- Luni: Jojari versus major left-bank tributaries
- Netravati: Kumaradhara
