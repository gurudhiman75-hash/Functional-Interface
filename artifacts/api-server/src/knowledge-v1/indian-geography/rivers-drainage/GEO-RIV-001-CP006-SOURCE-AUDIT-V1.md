# GEO-RIV-001-CP006 — West-flowing Peninsular Rivers — Source Audit V1

## Primary truth authorities

1. **CWC Water Year Book 2020-21 — Narmada Basin**
   - Narmada origin at Amarkantak/Maikal hills
   - westward course between Vindhya and Satpura ranges
   - Gulf of Cambay/Khambhat–Arabian Sea outfall
   - tributary and bank-side relations including Tawa and Hiran

2. **CWC Water Quality Assessment — Narmada Basin**
   - Amarkantak Plateau source convention
   - Gulf of Khambat near Bharuch outfall

3. **CWC Tapi Water Year Book / Tapi appraisal material**
   - Tapi system and tributary classification
   - right-bank: Vaki, Gomai, Arunavati, Aner
   - left-bank: Nesu, Buray, Panjhra, Bori, Girna, Vaghur, Purna, Mona, Sipna

4. **CWC Mahi Water Year Book**
   - Mahi system
   - Som as right-bank tributary
   - Anas and Panam as left-bank tributaries

5. **CWC Sabarmati basin material**
   - origin in Aravalli Hills, Rajasthan
   - Gulf of Khambhat outfall
   - passes through Ahmedabad
   - Wakal, Harnav, Hathmati, Watrak as left-bank tributaries; Sei as right-bank tributary

6. **CWC Hydrological Data (Unclassified) Book 2021**
   - Luni source on western slopes of Aravalli near Ajmer
   - Luni outfall in Rann of Kutch
   - Jojari as the notable right-bank tributary; main remaining tributaries are left-bank
   - west-coast independent systems including Netravati, Bharathapuzha, Periyar, Pamba and others
   - Netravati source/course/outfall and Kumaradhara relation

## Scope rules

Include only durable geography:
- source/origin region
- receiving sea/gulf/rann
- major interstate/state course relations
- major tributaries and explicit bank side
- well-established city/course association
- rift-valley discrimination for Narmada and Tapi
- independent west-coast river identification

Exclude:
- changing project status
- reservoir levels/current flood data
- exact discharge and rainfall values
- exact lengths/catchment areas unless needed only as provenance context
- disputed superlatives
- weak folklore/nickname claims without official support

## Editorial conventions

- Use **Tapi** as the primary label; `Tapti` may appear only as a familiar alternate spelling in an explanation or option when useful.
- Use **Gulf of Khambhat** as the modern wording; source locators may preserve `Gulf of Cambay`.
- Treat **Rann of Kutch** as Luni's terminal drainage relation; do not force it into an Arabian-Sea mouth template.
- Use `left-bank` / `right-bank` consistently.
- Difficulty must come from relation depth and close same-class distractors, not obscure wording.
