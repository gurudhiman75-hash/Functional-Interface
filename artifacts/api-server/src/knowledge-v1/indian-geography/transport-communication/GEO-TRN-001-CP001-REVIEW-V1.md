## Road network hierarchy and functions

Semantic QL: `ROAD-NETWORK-HIERARCHY`

### Q1. Which roads connect major cities, state capitals, ports and important economic centres across states?

A. National Highways
B. Village roads
C. District roads only
D. Private factory roads

**Answer:** National Highways

**Explanation:** National Highways form the principal inter-state road network linking major cities, ports, capitals and strategic centres.

### Q2. Which roads primarily connect important places within a state?

A. State Highways
B. National Highways only
C. Border roads only
D. International highways

**Answer:** State Highways

**Explanation:** State Highways connect state capitals with district headquarters and other important centres within the state.

### Q3. Which roads connect villages with nearby towns and markets?

A. Rural roads
B. National Highways
C. Expressways only
D. Port roads

**Answer:** Rural roads

**Explanation:** Rural roads provide local access between villages, markets, service centres and larger road networks.

### Q4. Consider the statements: I. National Highways carry major inter-state traffic. II. Rural roads provide local village connectivity. Which is correct?

A. Both I and II are correct
B. Only I is correct
C. Only II is correct
D. Neither I nor II is correct

**Answer:** Both I and II are correct

**Explanation:** The road hierarchy reflects different spatial functions, from national movement to local access.

### Q5. Which pair is correctly matched in road classification?

A. National Highway — major inter-state corridor
B. Rural road — national trunk route
C. State Highway — international sea route
D. Expressway — village path

**Answer:** National Highway — major inter-state corridor

**Explanation:** National Highways are India's principal inter-state road corridors.

### Q6. A road links a state capital with several district headquarters. Which category fits best?

A. State Highway
B. Village road
C. Border track only
D. International waterway

**Answer:** State Highway

**Explanation:** Connecting a state capital with district-level centres is a standard State Highway function.

## Expressways and access-controlled roads

Semantic QL: `EXPRESSWAYS-AND-ACCESS-CONTROL`

### Q25. What distinguishes an access-controlled expressway from an ordinary road?

A. Entry and exit are restricted to designated points
B. It has no road surface
C. It allows access from every field
D. It carries only bicycles

**Answer:** Entry and exit are restricted to designated points

**Explanation:** Access control reduces conflict points and allows higher-speed long-distance movement.

### Q26. Why are expressways important for inter-city transport?

A. They reduce travel time through high-capacity controlled-access design
B. They increase intersection conflicts
C. They serve only village lanes
D. They eliminate freight movement

**Answer:** They reduce travel time through high-capacity controlled-access design

**Explanation:** Limited access, grade separation and higher design standards improve travel efficiency.

### Q27. Consider the statements: I. Expressways are designed for high-speed movement. II. Access is usually controlled. Which is correct?

A. Both I and II are correct
B. Only I is correct
C. Only II is correct

**Answer:** Expressway — controlled access

## Road density, terrain and relief

Semantic QL: `ROAD-DENSITY-TERRAIN-AND-RELIEF`

### Q43. Why is road construction generally easier in plains than in mountains?

A. Plains require fewer steep gradients and major engineering works
B. Mountains are always flat
C. Plains have no rivers
D. Roads cannot cross plains

**Answer:** Plains require fewer steep gradients and major engineering works

**Explanation:** Flat relief reduces construction difficulty and cost compared with steep mountainous terrain.

### Q44. Which physical factor can reduce road density in high mountain regions?

A. Steep relief
B. Flat terrain
C. Dense markets
D. Urbanisation

**Answer:** Steep relief

**Explanation:** Steep slopes, snow and landslides increase construction and maintenance difficulty.

### Q45. Consider the statements: I. Relief influences road density. II. Population and economic activity also influence road development. Which is correct?

A. Both I and II are correct
B. Only I is correct
C. Only II is correct
D. Neither I nor II is correct

**Answer:** Both I and II are correct

**Explanation:** Road networks reflect both physical geography and human demand.

### Q46. Which region is generally more favourable to a dense road network?

A. Level, densely populated plain
B. High glaciated mountain belt
C. Deep ocean
D. Uninhabited desert core only

**Answer:** Level, densely populated plain

**Explanation:** Flat terrain and strong settlement/economic demand favour denser roads.

### Q47. Why can bridge construction strongly affect road connectivity in alluvial plains?

### Q48. A region has steep slopes, landslides and heavy snowfall. Which transport challenge is most likely?
