## Railway network and transport functions

Semantic QL: `RAILWAY-NETWORK-FUNCTIONS`

### Q1. Which transport mode is especially suitable for moving large volumes of passengers and bulk freight over long distances on land?

A. Railways
B. Village paths
C. Pipelines only
D. Inland ferries only

**Answer:** Railways

**Explanation:** Railways combine high carrying capacity with long-distance efficiency for both passengers and freight.

### Q2. Why are railways important to India's economy?

A. They connect production regions, markets, cities and ports
B. They serve only tourism
C. They move no freight
D. They replace all roads

**Answer:** They connect production regions, markets, cities and ports

**Explanation:** Railways integrate major economic regions through large-scale movement of people and goods.

### Q3. Consider the statements: I. Railways carry bulk freight. II. Railways also support long-distance passenger movement. Which is correct?

A. Both I and II are correct
B. Only I is correct
C. Only II is correct
D. Neither I nor II is correct

**Explanation:** Both functions are central to India's railway network.

### Q4. Which pair is correctly matched in transport geography?

A. Railway — high-capacity land transport
B. Railway — sea transport
C. Railway — crude-oil pipeline
D. Railway — air corridor

**Answer:** Railway — high-capacity land transport

**Explanation:** Railways are a high-capacity land transport system.

### Q5. Which commodity is especially suited to bulk rail movement?

A. Coal
B. Electronic mail
C. Natural gas through a pipeline
D. Air passengers

**Answer:** Coal

**Explanation:** Heavy bulk commodities such as coal are efficiently moved by rail over long inland distances.

### Q6. A transport system carries coal, steel, grain and millions of passengers across long inland routes. Which mode is it?

B. Pipeline only

**Answer:** Railways

**Explanation:** The mix of bulk freight and mass passenger movement strongly indicates rail transport.

## Railway gauges and network standardisation

Semantic QL: `RAILWAY-GAUGES`

### Q7. Which railway gauge is the dominant standard on the Indian Railways network?

A. Broad gauge
B. Metre gauge only
C. Narrow gauge only
D. Monorail gauge

**Answer:** Broad gauge

**Explanation:** Broad gauge forms the overwhelming majority of the modern Indian Railways network.

### Q8. Why does gauge standardisation improve railway operations?

A. It reduces breaks of gauge and allows smoother movement of trains
B. It increases transshipment
C. It prevents through running
D. It eliminates stations

**Answer:** It reduces breaks of gauge and allows smoother movement of trains

**Explanation:** A common gauge allows trains and freight to move across wider networks without changing rolling stock.

### Q9. Consider the statements: I. Broad gauge is dominant in India. II. Converting smaller gauges can improve network integration. Which is correct?

A. Both I and II are correct
B. Only I is correct
C. Only II is correct
D. Neither I nor II is correct

**Answer:** Both I and II are correct

**Explanation:** Gauge conversion reduces operational fragmentation.

### Q10. Which pair is correctly matched in railway gauge geography?

A. Broad gauge — dominant Indian Railways standard
B. Narrow gauge — dominant national standard
C. Metre gauge — only gauge in India
D. Broad gauge — inland waterway

**Answer:** Broad gauge — dominant Indian Railways standard

**Explanation:** Broad gauge is the main standard across the Indian Railways system.

### Q11. What problem occurs when two connected railway lines use different gauges?

A. Break of gauge
B. Port congestion only
C. Air turbulence
D. Pipeline leakage

**Answer:** Break of gauge

**Explanation:** Passengers or freight may need to be transferred between trains where gauges differ.

### Q12. A programme converts metre-gauge routes to broad gauge. What is the main network benefit?

A. Better interoperability
B. More breaks of gauge
C. Less route continuity
D. Elimination of freight

**Answer:** Better interoperability

**Explanation:** A common gauge improves through movement and operational integration.

## Railway electrification and traction
