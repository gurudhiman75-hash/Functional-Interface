## Tea & Coffee

### 1. Which climate is favourable for tea cultivation?

A. Warm, humid conditions with frequent rainfall
B. Cold arid conditions with frost
C. Permanent snow cover
D. Hot desert climate without moisture

**Answer:** A. Warm, humid conditions with frequent rainfall

**Explanation:** Tea grows well in a warm and humid environment with frequent rainfall or showers through much of the growing season. A long moist period supports repeated leaf growth and plucking.
