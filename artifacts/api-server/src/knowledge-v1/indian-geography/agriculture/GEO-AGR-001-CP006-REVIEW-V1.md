## 1. Kharif crops are generally sown with the onset of which season in India?

A. Southwest monsoon season
B. Winter season
C. Retreating winter period
D. Early spring season

**Answer:** A. Southwest monsoon season

**Explanation:** Kharif crops are sown when the southwest monsoon begins and moisture becomes available for field preparation. Their growing period therefore coincides with the rainy season.

## 104. Which problem is more closely linked with excessive canal irrigation than with lack of water?

A. Drought from zero irrigation
B. No soil moisture at all
C. Permanent frost
D. Waterlogging and salinity

**Answer:** D. Waterlogging and salinity

**Explanation:** Excess canal water combined with poor drainage can raise the water table and concentrate salts near the surface. These problems come from too much poorly managed water rather than shortage.
