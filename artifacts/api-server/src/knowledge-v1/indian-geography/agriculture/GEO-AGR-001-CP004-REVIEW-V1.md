## Irrigation, Cropping Pattern & Agricultural Inputs

### 1. Which is a common source of irrigation in Indian agriculture?

A. Wells and tube-wells
B. Sea tides
C. Glacial ice on fields
D. Only natural dew

**Answer:** A. Wells and tube-wells

**Explanation:** Wells and tube-wells draw groundwater for use on farms and are important irrigation sources in many parts of India. Canals and tanks are other common irrigation systems.
