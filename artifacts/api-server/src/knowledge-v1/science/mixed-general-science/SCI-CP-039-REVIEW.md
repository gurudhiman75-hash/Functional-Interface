## Q01 · Easy · Measurement, motion and mechanics mix

Which SI unit is used for force?

A. newton
B. joule
C. watt
D. pascal

**Answer:** A. newton

**Explanation:** Force is measured in newtons in the SI system. One newton is the force needed to accelerate a mass of one kilogram by one metre per second squared.
