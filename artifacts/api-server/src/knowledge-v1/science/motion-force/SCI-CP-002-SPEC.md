## Scope

- distance, displacement, speed and velocity
- average speed and basic distance-speed-time calculations
- acceleration, retardation and direction change
- uniform straight-line motion and circular-motion concept
- Newton's first, second and third laws
- inertia of rest, motion and direction
- momentum and conservation of momentum
- friction, lubrication, tyres and rolling/sliding distinction
- simple `F = ma` applications
- familiar real-life applications such as buses, seat belts, walking and recoil

Detailed work-energy-power belongs to `SCI-CP-003`; gravitation belongs to `SCI-CP-004`.
