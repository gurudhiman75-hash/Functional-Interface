## Purpose

V2 keeps the essential Atomic Structure core and absorbs the missing Atoms & Molecules foundation so Chemistry has a complete bridge from matter to periodic classification without creating an extra CP ID.

## Scope

- electron, proton and neutron: charge, relative mass and location
- atomic number and mass number
- neutral atoms and simple ions
- isotopes and isobars
- shell-wise electronic configuration for the first 20 elements
- valence electrons and simple valency
- atom, molecule, cation and anion
- molecules of elements and compounds
- formula writing from simple ionic charges/valencies
- common polyatomic ions at general-exam depth
- ionic versus covalent bonding at basic recognition level
- law of constant proportions
- atomic mass unit and relative atomic mass
- molecular mass and formula unit mass
- Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr and Chadwick
- Dalton's atomic theory at familiar school/exam depth

Groups, periods and periodic trends belong to `SCI-CP-013`. Detailed reaction chemistry belongs to `SCI-CP-014` onward.
