## Q01 · Easy · QL 1

### English

Who discovered cells while examining a thin slice of cork?

A. Robert Hooke
B. Anton van Leeuwenhoek
C. Robert Brown
D. Theodor Schwann

**Answer:** A. Robert Hooke

**Explanation:** Robert Hooke observed box-like compartments in cork in 1665 and called them cells. His observation led to the use of the term 'cell' in biology.

## Q02 · Easy · QL 1

### English

According to cell theory, which of the following statements is correct?

A. All cells contain chloroplasts
B. All living organisms are made of cells
C. Every cell has a cell wall
D. Cells arise only in plants

**Answer:** B. All living organisms are made of cells

**Explanation:** Cell theory states that living organisms are composed of cells and that the cell is the basic unit of life. It also recognizes that new cells arise from pre-existing cells.

## Q04 · Medium · QL 1

### English

Which of the following shows the correct order of biological organization from simpler to more complex?

A. Tissue → Cell → Organ system → Organ
B. Cell → Organ → Tissue → Organ system
C. Organ → Tissue → Cell → Organ system
D. Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ system

**Answer:** D. Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ system

**Explanation:** Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs work together in organ systems. This is the usual order from simpler to more complex organization.

B. Only cells contain water in living organisms

## Q07 · Easy · QL 2

### English

Which of the following is absent in a typical prokaryotic cell?

A. Cell membrane
B. Ribosomes
C. Membrane-bound nucleus
D. Cytoplasm

**Answer:** C. Membrane-bound nucleus

**Explanation:** Prokaryotic cells do not have a true nucleus enclosed by a nuclear membrane. Their genetic material lies in a nucleoid region, while cell membrane, cytoplasm and ribosomes are present.

### English

If ribosomes in a cell stop functioning, which immediate effect will occur?

A. Osmosis through the plasma membrane will stop completely
B. Existing DNA will disappear at once
C. The cell wall will instantly dissolve
D. New protein synthesis will stop

**Answer:** D. New protein synthesis will stop

**Explanation:** Ribosomes are the sites where amino acids are assembled into proteins. If ribosomes stop functioning, production of new proteins is directly halted.
