# SCI-CP-038 — Scientific Instruments

Status: REVIEW-ONLY CANDIDATE V1
Chapter: `SCI-001`
CP: `SCI-CP-038`

## Scope
- common scientific instruments and the quantities they measure
- basic working principles only where exam-relevant
- temperature, pressure, humidity and weather instruments
- electrical, optical, sound and earthquake instruments
- medical and physiological instruments
- common laboratory measuring tools
- navigation, altitude and astronomy instruments
- chemical and environmental measuring instruments
- integrated instrument-selection reasoning

## Ownership boundaries
- detailed physical laws and device theory → `SCI-CP-001` to `SCI-CP-010`
- detailed human physiology → `SCI-CP-021` to `SCI-CP-029`
- this CP focuses on instrument identification, measured quantity, use and simple principle

- Easy 18 / Medium 30 / Hard 12
- A/B/C/D = 15 each

- plausible same-domain distractors
- Hard = familiar instrument selection/reasoning, not obscure apparatus trivia
- explanations should be slightly fuller but simple, usually 2–3 sentences
- source and fact provenance on every item
