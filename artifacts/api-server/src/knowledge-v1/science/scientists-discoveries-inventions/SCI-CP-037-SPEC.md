Status: REVIEW-ONLY CANDIDATE V1

## Scope
- high-frequency scientists, laws, discoveries and inventions for one-day exams
- classical physics, electricity, magnetism, optics and modern physics
- chemistry and atomic theory
- cell discovery, microbiology, genetics and evolution
- medicine, vaccination and physiology
- familiar communication and technology inventions
- major Indian scientists and their well-known contributions
- careful distinction between discovery, law/theory and invention

## Editorial rules
- avoid false single-person attribution where a discovery was collaborative
- use school/exam-standard wording for familiar historical associations
- do not use obscure dates as difficulty
- explanations clarify the contribution rather than merely repeat the answer
