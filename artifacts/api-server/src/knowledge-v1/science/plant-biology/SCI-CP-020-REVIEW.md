## Q01 · Easy · Plant tissues and meristems

Which meristem is mainly responsible for increase in the length of a root or shoot?

A. Apical meristem
B. Lateral meristem
C. Cork cambium
D. Permanent tissue

**Answer:** A. Apical meristem

**Explanation:** Apical meristems occur near root and shoot tips. Their actively dividing cells increase the length of the plant body.

**Source fact:** `PLANT-MERISTEM-APICAL`

## Q02 · Easy · Plant tissues and meristems

Which plant tissue is made of thick-walled dead cells and mainly provides mechanical strength?

A. Parenchyma
B. Sclerenchyma
C. Collenchyma
D. Meristem

**Answer:** B. Sclerenchyma

**Explanation:** Sclerenchyma cells usually have thick, lignified walls and are dead at maturity. They provide strong mechanical support to plant parts.

**Source fact:** `PLANT-TISSUE-SCLERENCHYMA`

## Q03 · Medium · Plant tissues and meristems

Parenchyma tissue commonly performs which function in plants?

A. Long-distance transport of sugars only
B. Formation of pollen grains
C. Storage of food and water
D. Opening and closing of stomata

**Answer:** C. Storage of food and water

**Explanation:** Parenchyma is a simple living tissue that often stores food and water. In green parts, parenchyma containing chloroplasts can also carry out photosynthesis.

**Source fact:** `PLANT-TISSUE-PARENCHYMA`

## Q04 · Medium · Plant tissues and meristems

Which tissue gives flexible support to young stems and leaf stalks?

A. Sclerenchyma
B. Xylem vessel
C. Phloem fibre only

**Answer:** D. Collenchyma

**Explanation:** Collenchyma cells have unevenly thickened walls and provide support without making young plant parts rigid. This allows bending without breaking.

**Source fact:** `PLANT-TISSUE-COLLENCHYMA`

## Q05 · Medium · Plant tissues and meristems

Increase in the girth of a woody stem is mainly due to the activity of:

A. Lateral meristem
