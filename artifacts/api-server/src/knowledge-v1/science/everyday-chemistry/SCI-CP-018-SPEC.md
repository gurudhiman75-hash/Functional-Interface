# SCI-CP-018 — Everyday Chemistry

> Status: REVIEW-ONLY CANDIDATE V1
> Chapter: SCI-001 — General Science
> CP: SCI-CP-018

## Scope
- common household and industrial chemicals and common names
- LPG, CNG, biogas, oxygen, carbon dioxide and acetylene uses
- fertilizers and major plant nutrients
- common polymers, plastics and synthetic fibres
- common hydrated salts and familiar chemical names
- food preservatives, acids and antioxidants
- hard water, soap scum and simple water-treatment chemistry
- vulcanization, glass, cement and ceramics

## Exclusions
- detailed acid/base chemistry → SCI-CP-015
- metals, alloys and corrosion → SCI-CP-016
- carbon bonding and named organic reactions → SCI-CP-017
- environmental pollution depth → SCI-CP-034
