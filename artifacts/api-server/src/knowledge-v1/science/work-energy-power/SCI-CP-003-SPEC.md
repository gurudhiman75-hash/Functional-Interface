# SCI-CP-003 — Work, Energy & Power

Status: REVIEW CANDIDATE V1
Chapter: `SCI-001 — General Science`
CP: `SCI-CP-003`

## Scope

Competitive-exam level coverage of:

- mechanical work and conditions for positive, negative and zero work
- joule and basic work calculations
- kinetic, potential and common energy transformations
- kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy
- conservation of energy and mechanical energy
- power, watt and basic power calculations
- simple machines, mechanical advantage, inclined plane and fixed pulley
- everyday applications and mixed numerical reasoning

Exclude advanced derivations, calculus, rotational work-energy, elastic-constant numericals and JEE/NEET-level mechanics.
