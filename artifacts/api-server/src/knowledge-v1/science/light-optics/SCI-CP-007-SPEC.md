# SCI-CP-007 — Light & Optics — Review Candidate V1

## Scope
Exam-level reflection, refraction, mirrors, lenses, human eye and everyday optics for SSC, Railways, Police and state one-day exams.

## QL contract
1. Laws of reflection
2. Plane mirrors and lateral inversion
3. Spherical mirrors and basic image properties
4. Concave-mirror applications and focal relations
5. Refraction and refractive index
6. Lenses, focal length and power
7. Human eye and vision defects
8. Dispersion, rainbow and atmospheric scattering
9. Statement I/II synthesis
10. Mixed light and optics application

## Review batch
- 60 questions total; 6 per QL.
- Difficulty: Easy 18 / Medium 30 / Hard 12.
- Answer positions: A15 / B15 / C15 / D15.
- Hard questions test discrimination/application, not obscure theory.
- Explanations stay concise and beginner-friendly.
- No runtime registration, Question Bank write, mock/test eligibility, or learner publication before explicit approval.

## Source anchors
- NCERT Science VII — Light
- NCERT Science X — Light: Reflection and Refraction
- NCERT Science X — Human Eye and the Colourful World

The CP is review-ready only when its qualification test, review export and API build pass. Human approval remains mandatory before promotion.
