## Q01 · Easy · Balanced diet and nutrient groups

A diet that supplies all essential nutrients in suitable amounts is called a:

A. Balanced diet
B. Liquid diet
C. Fasting diet
D. Protein-only diet

**Answer:** A. Balanced diet

**Explanation:** A balanced diet provides carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water and fibre in appropriate proportions.

**Source fact:** `BALANCED-DIET`

B. Carbohydrates
C. Minerals
D. Water

**Answer:** B. Carbohydrates

**Explanation:** Carbohydrates are the body's principal quick energy source in a normal diet.

**Source fact:** `CARBOHYDRATES-ENERGY`
