# SCI-CP-017 — Carbon & Common Compounds

Status: REVIEW-ONLY CANDIDATE V2
Chapter: `SCI-001`
CP: `SCI-CP-017`

## Scope
- tetravalency, covalent bonding and catenation
- chains, branches and rings at school-exam depth
- diamond, graphite and fullerene
- saturated and unsaturated hydrocarbons
- alkanes, alkenes, alkynes and homologous-series relations
- basic functional groups and naming: halo, alcohol, aldehyde, ketone and carboxylic acid
- combustion, oxidation, addition, hydrogenation, substitution and bromine-water unsaturation test
- ethanol and ethanoic acid
- esterification and saponification
- soap, detergent, micelles and hard-water cleaning
- simple structural-isomer idea where directly useful

## Exclusions
- advanced organic mechanisms and stereochemistry
- extended IUPAC nomenclature beyond familiar exam compounds
- industrial polymer chemistry → `SCI-CP-018`

## Source authority
- `NCERT-SCIENCE-X-CARBON-COMPOUNDS`
- `NIOS-SECONDARY-SCIENCE-CARBON-COMPOUNDS`
