## Q01 · Easy · Blood and its components

Which liquid component makes up the largest part of blood?

A. Plasma
B. Platelets
C. Red blood cells
D. White blood cells

**Answer:** A. Plasma

**Explanation:** Plasma is the fluid part of blood. It carries cells, nutrients, hormones, wastes and dissolved substances around the body.

**Source fact:** `BLOOD-PLASMA`

## Q02 · Easy · Blood and its components

Which of the following is a formed element of blood?

A. Plasma
B. Red blood cell
C. Water
D. Dissolved glucose

**Answer:** B. Red blood cell

**Explanation:** Red blood cells are formed elements suspended in plasma. White blood cells and platelets are also formed elements.

**Source fact:** `BLOOD-FORMED-ELEMENTS`

## Q03 · Medium · Blood and its components

Plasma helps transport which substances through the body?

A. Only oxygen
B. Only platelets
C. Nutrients, hormones and wastes
D. Only carbon dioxide

**Answer:** C. Nutrients, hormones and wastes

**Explanation:** Plasma carries many dissolved substances, including nutrients, hormones, salts and metabolic wastes.

**Source fact:** `PLASMA-TRANSPORT`

## Q04 · Medium · Blood and its components

Which statement best distinguishes plasma from serum?

A. Serum contains more red blood cells than plasma
B. Plasma contains no water
C. Serum is the solid part of blood
D. Serum is plasma without most clotting factors

**Answer:** D. Serum is plasma without most clotting factors

**Explanation:** Serum is the liquid left after blood clots, so it lacks fibrinogen and several clotting factors found in plasma.

**Source fact:** `PLASMA-SERUM`

## Q05 · Medium · Blood and its components

**Explanation:** Blood carries heat from active tissues and redistributes it, helping the body maintain a more stable temperature.

## Q10 · Medium · Red blood cells, haemoglobin and oxygen transport
