## Q01 · Easy · Disease basics and classification

A disease that can spread from one person to another is called:

A. Communicable disease
B. Deficiency disease
C. Genetic trait
D. Nutritional disorder only

**Answer:** A. Communicable disease

**Explanation:** Communicable diseases are caused by infectious agents and can spread directly or indirectly between hosts.

**Source fact:** `COMMUNICABLE-DEFINITION`

## Q02 · Easy · Disease basics and classification

Which of the following is a non-communicable disease?

A. Tuberculosis
B. Diabetes mellitus
C. Measles
D. Cholera

**Answer:** B. Diabetes mellitus

**Explanation:** Diabetes mellitus does not spread from one person to another and is classed as a non-communicable disease.

**Source fact:** `NONCOMMUNICABLE-DIABETES`

## Q03 · Medium · Disease basics and classification

What distinguishes an infectious disease from a deficiency disease?

A. It always results from lack of vitamins
B. It cannot spread
C. It is caused by a pathogen
D. It never produces fever

**Answer:** C. It is caused by a pathogen

**Explanation:** Infectious diseases are caused by disease-producing organisms or agents, while deficiency diseases result from inadequate nutrients.
