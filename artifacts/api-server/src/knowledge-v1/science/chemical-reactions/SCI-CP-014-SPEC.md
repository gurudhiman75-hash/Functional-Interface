# SCI-CP-014 — Chemical Reactions

Status: REVIEW-ONLY CANDIDATE V2
Chapter: `SCI-001`
CP: `SCI-CP-014`

## Scope

- evidence that a chemical reaction has occurred
- chemical equation notation, conservation of atoms and balancing
- combination and decomposition reaction patterns
- thermal, electrolytic and photochemical decomposition
- displacement, double displacement and precipitation
- oxidation, reduction and redox at school/general-exam depth
- exothermic and endothermic reactions
- corrosion and rancidity only as familiar oxidation applications
- integrated equation/observation/reaction-type reasoning

Detailed acid-base chemistry belongs to `SCI-CP-015`. Detailed reactivity-series and corrosion-prevention chemistry belongs to `SCI-CP-016`.
