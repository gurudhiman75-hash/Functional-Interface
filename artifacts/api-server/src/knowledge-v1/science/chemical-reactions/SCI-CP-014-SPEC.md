# SCI-CP-014 — Chemical Reactions

Status: REVIEW-ONLY CANDIDATE V1
Chapter: `SCI-001`
CP: `SCI-CP-014`

## Scope

- visible signs that a chemical reaction has occurred
- writing and balancing simple chemical equations
- law of conservation of mass in chemical equations
- combination and decomposition reactions
- thermal, electrolytic and photochemical decomposition
- displacement and double-displacement reactions
- precipitation reactions
- oxidation, reduction and redox reactions
- exothermic and endothermic reactions
- corrosion and rancidity as familiar oxidation effects
- simple mixed applications suitable for one-day competitive exams

Detailed acids, bases and salts belong to `SCI-CP-015`; metals and non-metals belong to `SCI-CP-016`.
