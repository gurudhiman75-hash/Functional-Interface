## Objective
Fill the critical V2 gap in medieval cultural history with exam-relevant coverage of Sufism, Bhakti, early Sikh development and the connected growth of languages, literature, music, painting and architecture.

## Scope
- Sufi concepts, institutions and major silsilahs;
- Chishti and Suhrawardi traditions;
- Bhakti foundations, Nirguna/Saguna traditions and major saints;
- Maharashtra, Bengal and north Indian Bhakti;
- Guru Nanak, early Sikh institutions, Guru Arjan and the Khalsa;
- Persian, Urdu/Dakhni and regional literary growth;
- qawwali, Mughal music and painting;
- Sultanate and Mughal architectural synthesis.

## Deliberate exclusions
- eighteenth-century Sikh political expansion already covered in HIS-CP-011;
- disputed invention claims and low-value hagiographic trivia;
- detailed monument inventories better suited to a separate art-history package;
- repeated political chronology already frozen in Sultanate and Mughal CPs.

## Sources
1. NIOS History 315 Lesson 14 — Cultural Developments in Medieval India.
2. NIOS Indian Culture & Heritage 223 Chapter 9 — Religion and Philosophy in Medieval India.
3. NCERT Our Pasts II — Devotional Paths to the Divine.
