## Objective
Close the verified V2 gaps in colonial education, press/public-sphere policy, connecting constitutional developments and underrepresented nineteenth-century reform movements without duplicating the V1 core.

## Scope
- Orientalist institutions and the Anglicist turn;
- Macaulay's Minute and downward filtration;
- Wood's Dispatch and Hunter Commission;
- university expansion and Curzon's 1904 measure;
- press regulation from Wellesley to Ripon;
- Vernacular Press Act and Arms Act of 1878;
- limited representative reforms in 1861 and 1892;
- Young Bengal, Paramhansa Mandali, Phule/Satyashodhak, Pandita Ramabai;
- Theosophical Society and Annie Besant;
- education, print and reform as foundations of a modern public sphere.
