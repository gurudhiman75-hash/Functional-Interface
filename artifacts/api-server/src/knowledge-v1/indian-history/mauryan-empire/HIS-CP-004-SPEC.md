# HIS-CP-004 — Mauryan Empire

## Scope
This content point covers the Mauryan Empire from Chandragupta Maurya through Ashoka, with emphasis on rulers, expansion, historical sources, Ashokan inscriptions, dhamma, administration, provincial centres, military organisation, economy and the limits of imperial control.

## Source policy
Canonical facts must be traceable to authoritative educational or official material. V1 uses NCERT Themes in Indian History I and NIOS History 315. Source names are provenance metadata only and must not appear in learner-facing stems.

## Question design
- realistic SSC/Railway/Banking/state-exam wording
- 4 options with same-class plausible distractors
- simple beginner-friendly explanations generated from canonical facts
- Easy = direct but meaningful recall
- Medium = associations, administration, inscriptions and applied factual understanding
- Hard = chronology, comparison, source reasoning and multi-statement synthesis
- no option-by-option explanation unless needed
- no internal wording such as `school-level`, `this CP`, `review batch` or `canonical fact`

## Historical caution
- Do not describe Mauryan administration as perfectly uniform across the empire.
- Do not infer that every place with an Ashokan inscription was necessarily under identical administrative control.
- Treat the Arthashastra carefully: NCERT states that parts were probably composed by Kautilya/Chanakya and tradition connects him with Chandragupta.
- Avoid unsupported exact numerical claims about the army as universal fact when they depend on Megasthenes.
- Kalinga-war consequences should follow the inscriptional evidence: Ashoka expresses repentance over suffering and increased commitment to dhamma.
- Avoid turning dhamma into a synonym for Buddhism; its stated ethical principles are broader.
