## Objective
Fill the verified V2 gap in early medieval north Indian political history without repeating the Rashtrakuta/Chola material already frozen in HIS-CP-007 or Sultanate administration in HIS-CP-008.

## Scope
- Gurjara-Pratihara rise, Vatsaraja, Kannauj and Mihira Bhoja;
- Pala foundation, expansion, Buddhist patronage and Vikramashila;
- emergence and legitimization of Rajput states;
- Tomaras and Chauhans in twelfth-century Delhi;
- Mahmud of Ghazni's raids and their political effect;
- Muhammad Ghori's territorial advance;
- First and Second Battles of Tarain and Chandawar.

## Deliberate exclusions
- Rashtrakuta political history already covered in HIS-CP-007;
- detailed Sultanate rulers, administration and iqta system already covered in HIS-CP-008;
- medieval economy/society expansion reserved for HIS-CP-020;
- Bhakti-Sufi and broader medieval culture reserved for HIS-CP-021.
