# HIS-CP-003 — Mahajanapadas, Jainism & Buddhism

Status: REVIEW-ONLY V1

## Scope

- rise of janapadas and sixteen Mahajanapadas
- major Mahajanapadas, capitals and political forms
- sixth-century BCE urban and economic background
- Jainism: Mahavira, Triratna, five vows, anuvrata, anekantavada, language and sects
- Buddhism: major life places, Middle Path, Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, language, sangha, council/sect basics, literature and art

## Source policy

Canonical facts are source-backed. Sources are metadata only and must not appear in question stems. V1 uses official NIOS History 315 Lesson 5 plus UNESCO records for Lumbini and Bodh Gaya.

## Content policy

- use stable school/exam facts; avoid fragile exact-date trivia where standard references differ
- no absolute claims on debated doctrinal or sectarian chronology
- keep stems concise and natural
- use same-class distractors
- keep explanations simple and beginner-friendly
- Hard questions must require comparison, sequence or multi-statement reasoning rather than obscure recall
