# HIS-CP-016 — National Movement 1919–1947

## Scope
Review-only knowledge checkpoint covering the final phase of the Indian national movement from the Rowlatt Satyagraha to independence and Partition in 1947.

### Included
- Rowlatt Satyagraha and Jallianwala Bagh
- Khilafat and Non-Cooperation
- Chauri Chaura and withdrawal of Non-Cooperation
- Swaraj Party, revolutionary activity and Simon Commission
- Nehru Report, Lahore Congress and Purna Swaraj
- Dandi March and Civil Disobedience
- Gandhi-Irwin Pact, Round Table Conference and Karachi resolutions
- Government of India Act 1935, 1937 elections and 1939 resignations
- Individual Satyagraha, Cripps Mission and INA
- Quit India
- Royal Indian Navy uprising
- Cabinet Mission, Interim Government, Mountbatten Plan, Independence and Partition

## Historical hygiene
- do not present independence as the result of one movement or one leader alone
- distinguish constitutional politics, mass movements, revolutionary activity and military efforts
- distinguish the Cripps proposals, Cabinet Mission and Mountbatten Plan
- do not conflate the Government of India Act 1935 with independence
- treat Partition as a political process accompanied by large-scale communal violence and displacement
- avoid unsupported claims about motives where sources are contested
- keep learner-facing language neutral, factual and exam-oriented
