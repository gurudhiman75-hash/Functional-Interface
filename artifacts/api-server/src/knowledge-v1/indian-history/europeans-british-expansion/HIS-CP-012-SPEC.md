# HIS-CP-012 — Europeans in India & British Expansion

## Scope
Review-only History checkpoint covering European trading powers in India and the political expansion of the English East India Company up to 1857.

Primary coverage:
- Portuguese, Dutch, French and English trading presence
- Anglo-French rivalry and Wandiwash
- Bengal from Plassey to Buxar and the Diwani
- Mysore and Company expansion
- Subsidiary Alliance
- Anglo-Maratha expansion
- Ranjit Singh, Anglo-Sikh wars and Punjab
- Doctrine of Lapse and major annexations
- chronology, comparison and expansion mechanisms

## Source hierarchy

## Historical hygiene
- Do not treat Plassey as a simple test of battlefield superiority; political conspiracy was central.
- Keep Buxar distinct from Plassey and link the 1765 Diwani to the post-Buxar settlement.
- Distinguish Doctrine of Lapse annexations from Awadh's 1856 annexation on alleged misgovernment grounds.
- Do not imply that the Third Carnatic War removed all French possessions from India; Wandiwash ended French political competition for supremacy, while some settlements remained/restored.
- Keep detailed land-revenue, constitutional, judicial and economic policy for HIS-CP-013.
- Avoid fragile or source-conflicted claims about the exact first English factory chronology.
