# HIS-CP-002 — Vedic Age

## Scope

HIS-CP-002 covers the Early and Later Vedic periods at competitive-exam depth:

- Vedic literature: Rigveda, Samaveda, Yajurveda, Atharvaveda, Brahmanas, Aranyakas and Upanishads
- broad chronology and geographical spread
- Sapta-Sindhu and important Vedic river names
- Early Vedic pastoral economy and key terms
- Later Vedic agriculture, iron use and settlement growth
- family, clan, jana, gotra and varna changes
- major Rigvedic deities and Later Vedic religious change
- sabha, samiti, rajan, purohita and other political institutions
- clear Early Vedic vs Later Vedic comparison

## Question families

1. Vedic texts and basic terms
2. Vedas and their contents
3. chronology and geographical spread
4. river-name and region mapping
5. Early Vedic economy and terminology
6. Later Vedic economy, iron and settlements
7. social institutions and change
8. religion and philosophical ideas
9. polity and offices
10. comparison and multi-statement synthesis

Each family contributes six review questions in V1.

## Difficulty

- Easy: direct high-frequency relations and basic textbook facts
- Medium: confusable terms, river mapping, social/economic change and institution matching
- Hard: polity/religion synthesis, Early–Later comparison and multi-statement questions

Hard questions must remain solvable from standard exam knowledge. Obscure ritual trivia is excluded.

## Language standard

Explanations must be short, direct and beginner-friendly. They should explain the fact in plain English instead of repeating the answer in formal textbook wording.

## Historical guardrails

- Do not generate speculative race-based claims.
- Do not turn debated Aryan-origin or migration interpretations into absolute-answer MCQs.
- Do not treat broad textbook dates as exact event dates.
- Do not imply that social institutions were identical throughout the entire Vedic period.
- Questions comparing Early and Later Vedic life must preserve the period distinction.
- Distractors should come from the same semantic class whenever possible.

## Source policy

Canonical facts in V1 are grounded in NIOS History 315, Lesson 4: *The Vedic Age (1500 BC–600 BC)*. The NIOS chapter portal is retained as an official resource reference.

## Lifecycle

V1 is review-only. It must not be registered for Question Studio runtime or Question Bank persistence until the human review gate is explicitly approved.
