# HIS-CP-015 — National Movement 1885–1919

## Scope
Review-only knowledge checkpoint for the national movement from the formation of the Indian National Congress through the 1919 constitutional transition.

### Included
- early political associations and formation of the Congress
- Moderate phase, methods, demands and economic critique
- Partition of Bengal, Swadeshi and boycott
- radical nationalism, Surat split and revolutionary organisations
- Muslim League and Morley–Minto reforms
- Ghadar and wartime revolutionary activity
- Home Rule Leagues
- Congress reunion and Lucknow Pact
- Gandhi's return and early satyagrahas before the post-1919 mass phase
- Montagu–Chelmsford reforms as the boundary transition

### Reserved for HIS-CP-016
- Rowlatt Act agitation
- Jallianwala Bagh
- Khilafat and Non-Cooperation
- Civil Disobedience
- constitutional politics after 1919
- Quit India and developments up to 1947
