# HIS-CP-009 — Vijayanagara, Bahmani & Regional Kingdoms

## Scope
Review-only History checkpoint covering the major regional states that rose during the weakening of Delhi Sultanate authority, with Vijayanagara and the Bahmani Sultanate as the core focus.

Primary coverage:
- Bahmani foundation, provinces and central-provincial control
- Mahmud Gawan and Bahmani noble politics
- breakup of the Bahmani kingdom into Deccan Sultanates
- Vijayanagara foundation and dynastic sequence
- Krishnadeva Raya and the early sixteenth-century high point
- Vijayanagara military organisation and the amara-nayaka system
- Bahmani-Vijayanagara rivalry over Raichur, trade and resources
- Jaunpur and Kashmir
- Bengal and Gujarat
- broad causes for the rise and interaction of regional states

## Historical hygiene
- Do not reduce Vijayanagara-Bahmani rivalry to a simple Hindu-Muslim conflict; territorial, commercial and strategic interests were central.
- Use NCERT to avoid the inaccurate claim that Krishnadeva Raya founded the Tuluva dynasty; he belonged to it.
- Keep detailed art and architecture outside this History CP except when directly tied to rulers or political context.
- Avoid colourful court legends, physical descriptions and miracle-like anecdotes unless historically necessary and well supported.
- Avoid fragile 'first/greatest/only' claims unless standard and well supported.
- Keep eighteenth-century successor states outside this CP.
