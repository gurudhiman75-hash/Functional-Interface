# HIS-CP-014 — Revolt of 1857 & Socio-Religious Reform

## Status
Review-only V1 checkpoint. Do not register with Question Studio runtime or Question Bank persistence before human approval.

## Scope
This checkpoint covers:
- political, economic, social and military causes of the Revolt of 1857;
- Meerut, Delhi and the spread through north and central India;
- major leaders and civilian participation;
- competing interpretations of the revolt, reasons for its failure and its consequences;
- Rammohan Roy, Brahmo Samaj and Vidyasagar;
- Ramakrishna Mission and Prarthana Samaj;
- Arya Samaj, DAV/Gurukul strands and shuddhi;
- selected Muslim reform movements and Sayyid Ahmad Khan's educational initiatives;
- the wider and sometimes complex impact of nineteenth-century reform movements.

## Boundaries
- Do not reduce 1857 to a single cause or only to the cartridge issue.
- Do not state a contested interpretation of the revolt as the only valid interpretation.
- Keep detailed colonial economic policy in HIS-CP-013.
- Keep post-1885 organised nationalism for HIS-CP-015 and HIS-CP-016.
- Treat reform movements as historically diverse; avoid presenting one religious doctrine as inherently superior or inferior.
- Use source names only in provenance metadata, never in learner-facing stems or explanations.
