# HIS-CP-013 — British Administration & Economic Policies

## Status
Review-only candidate. `runtimeRegistered=false` until human approval.

## Scope
- Revenue farming and Permanent Settlement
- Ryotwari and Mahalwari settlements
- Regulating Act 1773 and Pitt's India Act 1784
- Charter Acts of 1813 and 1833
- Company bureaucracy and district administration
- Judicial organisation and legal principles
- Colonial economic phases and free trade
- Commercialisation of agriculture
- Railways in the colonial economy
- Deindustrialisation and drain-of-wealth critique

## Boundary
- Detailed British territorial conquest belongs to HIS-CP-012.
- Revolt of 1857 and socio-religious reform belong to HIS-CP-014.
- National movement belongs to HIS-CP-015 and HIS-CP-016.

## Review design
- 60 questions
- 10 QLs × 6
- Easy 18 / Medium 30 / Hard 12
- Answer positions A/B/C/D = 15 each
- Hard questions use chronology, comparison and multi-fact synthesis rather than obscure trivia.
- Stems use short, natural exam English.
- Explanations are simple and direct.
