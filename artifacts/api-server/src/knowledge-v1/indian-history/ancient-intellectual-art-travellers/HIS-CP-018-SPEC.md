## Objective
Fill verified V2 gaps in ancient art, architecture, learning, travellers, literature, medicine, science and technology without duplicating the frozen CP005–CP007 core.

## Scope
- Buddhist stupas, chaityas and viharas
- Mathura, Gandhara and Amaravati sculpture
- Gupta painting and structural temple architecture
- Pallava and Chalukya architectural supplements
- Fa Hien, Hsuan Tsang and Nalanda
- selected literary supplements
- Aryabhata, Varahamihira and technological evidence
- Sushruta and ancient surgery
- Nagarjuna's Rasaratnakara in metallurgical experimentation

## Deliberate exclusions
Political chronology already covered in CP005–CP007 is not repeated. Medieval Bhakti-Sufi cultural coverage remains reserved for HIS-CP-021. Disputed or oversimplified invention claims are excluded.
