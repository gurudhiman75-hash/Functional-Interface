# HIS-CP-008 — Delhi Sultanate

## Scope
Review-only History checkpoint covering the establishment, consolidation, expansion, administration and decline of the Delhi Sultanate.

Primary coverage:
- Qutbuddin Aibak and Iltutmish
- Raziya and Balban
- Khalji dynasty and Alauddin Khalji
- Tughlaq dynasty and Muhammad Tughlaq
- Sultanate central administration
- iqta and local administration
- Sayyids and Lodis
- Mongol pressure, Timur and political decline

## Source hierarchy
1. NIOS History 315 Lesson 9 — Establishment and Expansion of the Delhi Sultanate
2. NIOS History 315 Lesson 12 — Administrative System and Institutions
3. NCERT — Delhi: 12th to 15th Century for sequence/cross-checking

Sources are provenance only. Source names must not appear in learner-facing stems or explanations.

## Historical hygiene
- Do not present the Padmini/Chittor romantic narrative as settled historical fact.
- Distinguish Alauddin Khalji's tribute-oriented southern policy from the Tughlaq move toward more direct Deccan control.
- Present Muhammad Tughlaq's policies with their stated administrative aims and practical outcomes, not as caricatures.
- Avoid unsupported 'first/greatest/most advanced' claims.
- Keep detailed architecture/culture outside this CP except where needed for direct political-history identification.
