# HIS-CP-006 — Gupta & Post-Gupta India

## Scope
Gupta rise and rulers, Samudragupta, Chandragupta II, Huna pressure and Gupta decline, Gupta administration, taxation, economy, land grants, literature, religion, science, northern successor states and Harshavardhana.

Detailed Chalukya/Pallava and later South-Indian dynastic coverage is reserved for HIS-CP-007.

## Source policy
Canonical facts are grounded in official NIOS History 315 Lesson 7. Source labels remain provenance metadata and must not leak into learner-facing stems or explanations.

- Real SSC/Railway/Banking/state-exam style stems.
- Same-semantic-class distractors.

- Avoid detailed Art & Culture expansion beyond direct historical context.
