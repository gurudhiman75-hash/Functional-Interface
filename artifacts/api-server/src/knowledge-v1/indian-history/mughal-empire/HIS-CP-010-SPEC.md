# HIS-CP-010 — Mughal Empire

## Scope
Review-only History checkpoint covering Mughal political expansion, major ruler transitions, major challenges and core administrative institutions from Babur to Aurangzeb.

Primary coverage:
- Babur and the establishment of Mughal rule
- Humayun, Sher Shah and the Afghan interlude
- Akbar's accession, expansion and Rajput policy
- Jahangir and Shah Jahan
- Aurangzeb, Deccan expansion and major political challenges
- Mughal central and provincial administration
- mansabdari and jagirdari systems

## Historical hygiene
- Do not reduce Mughal political history to religious explanations where political, military or fiscal causes are more direct.
- Distinguish Shah Jahan's 1636 Deccan suzerainty arrangements from Aurangzeb's later annexation of Bijapur and Golconda.
- Do not treat legends or courtly claims as settled facts without qualification.
- Avoid fragile army-size trivia and unnecessary exact figures.
- Keep architecture, painting and detailed cultural history outside this CP except where essential to a political-history fact.
- Present mansab and jagir as administrative institutions, not hereditary landed ownership in general.
