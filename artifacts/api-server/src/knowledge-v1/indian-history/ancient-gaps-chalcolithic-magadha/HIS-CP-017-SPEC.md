## Objective
Fill two verified V1 coverage gaps without rewriting the frozen CP001–CP016 corpus: non-Harappan Chalcolithic cultures and the rise of Magadha before the Mauryan transition.

## Scope
- Chalcolithic meaning, chronology, Banas, Kayatha, Malwa and Jorwe cultures;
- sites, pottery, copper/stone tools, craft evidence and Daimabad bronzes;
- agriculture, cattle rearing, hunting/fishing, crops, houses and settlement planning;
- Bimbisara, Ajatashatru, Udayin, Shishunaga, Kalashoka and the Nandas;
- Anga, Kashi, Vaishali, Avanti and Kalinga associations;
- Pataliputra and geographical/resource factors behind Magadha's rise.

## Deliberate exclusions
Harappan urban facts already frozen in HIS-CP-001, general Mahajanapada/capital coverage in HIS-CP-003, and Mauryan rulers/administration in HIS-CP-004 are not duplicated.

## Sources
1. NIOS History 315 Lesson 3 — The Harappan Civilization, sections on non-Harappan Chalcolithic communities.
2. NIOS History 315 Lesson 5 — From Janapadas to Empire, section on the rise of Magadha.
