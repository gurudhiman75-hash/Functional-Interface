## Objective
Fill the verified V2 gap in medieval economic and social history without duplicating the political and administrative facts already frozen in HIS-CP-008 through HIS-CP-011.

## Scope
- expansion of cultivation, food/cash crops and irrigation;
- land-revenue assessment methods and peasant society;
- textile, mineral and metal crafts;
- spinning, weaving, paper, artillery and household technologies;
- jajmani, balutedar, dadni and royal karkhana production;
- local, regional and inter-regional trade;
- Karwanis, banjaras, sarrafs, dalals, hundis and commercial insurance.

## Deliberate exclusions
- iqta/jagir definitions and central administrative offices already covered in CP008/CP010;
- dynastic political chronology already covered in CP008–CP011;
- Bhakti, Sufi and cultural history reserved for HIS-CP-021;
- low-value lists of every local craft or market centre.

## Primary source
NIOS History 315 Lesson 13 — Economy.

## Question design
- 60 questions; 10 QLs × 6
- Easy 18 / Medium 30 / Hard 12
- all 60 canonical facts represented
- direct exam-style stems
- no unnecessary “best describes” or “associated with”
- same-class distractors
- simple contextual explanations
- review-only; runtime unchanged
