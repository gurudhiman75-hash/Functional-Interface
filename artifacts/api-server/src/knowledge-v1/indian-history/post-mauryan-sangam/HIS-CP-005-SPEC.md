# HIS-CP-005 — Post-Mauryan India & Sangam Age

## Scope
- Shungas and immediate post-Mauryan transition
- Indo-Greeks, Shakas and Parthians at exam-relevant depth
- Kushana rulers, titles, trade and limited period-context art references
- Kharavela and Hathigumpha
- Satavahana polity, administration and economy
- Uttarapatha, Dakshinapatha and Indo-Roman trade
- Sangam literature, Chola-Chera-Pandya centres, ports, tinais, economy and society

## Source policy
Primary canonical source: official NIOS History 315 Lesson 6, cross-supported for Sangam-as-source framing by NIOS Lesson 1. Source names remain provenance metadata and must not appear in learner stems.

## Editorial rules
- Keep stems concise and exam-like.
- Use same-class plausible distractors.
- Avoid fragile fixed dates or disputed identifications when a stable concept tests the same learning goal.
- Do not ask a fixed 78 CE Kanishka/Shaka-era question.
- Do not use unqualified “first/oldest/largest” claims such as the Kaveri dam as the earliest dam.
- Art-history facts are limited to period identification; detailed art belongs in Art & Culture.
- Explanations are short, direct and beginner-friendly.

## Review design
10 QLs × 6 questions = 60 questions.
Difficulty target: Easy 18, Medium 30, Hard 12.
Hard questions must require comparison, association or multi-statement synthesis rather than obscure trivia.

## Lifecycle
Review-only until explicit human approval. No Question Studio runtime registration or Question Bank persistence before approval.
