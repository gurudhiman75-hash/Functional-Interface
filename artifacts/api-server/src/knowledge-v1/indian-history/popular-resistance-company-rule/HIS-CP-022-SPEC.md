## Objective
Fill the critical V2 gap in popular resistance before and beyond the Revolt of 1857 without duplicating the dedicated 1857 block already frozen in HIS-CP-014.

## Scope
- colonial agrarian and tribal grievances;
- Sanyasi-Fakir and Rangpur resistance;
- Poligar, Mappila, Jat and Koli uprisings;
- Bhil, Kol and Santhal resistance;
- Indigo Rebellion;
- Birsa-Munda movement;
- Deccan Riots of 1875;
- nature, limits and significance of early peasant and tribal resistance.
