## 1. Freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse throughout India is guaranteed by:

**QL:** `POL-027-QL-001`  
**Difficulty:** Easy

A. Article 301
B. Article 302
C. Article 304
D. Article 307

**Answer:** A. Article 301

**Explanation:** Article 301 declares trade, commerce and intercourse throughout India free, but the freedom remains subject to the other provisions of Part XIII.

---

## 2. Which Part of the Constitution deals with trade, commerce and intercourse within India?

**QL:** `POL-027-QL-001`  
**Difficulty:** Easy

A. Part XII
B. Part XIII
C. Part XIV
D. Part IXB

**Answer:** B. Part XIII

**Explanation:** Part XIII contains Articles 301 to 307 and deals specifically with trade, commerce and intercourse within the territory of India.

---

## 3. The freedom under Article 301 extends throughout:

**QL:** `POL-027-QL-001`  
**Difficulty:** Easy

A. Only inter-State trade
B. Only Union Territories
C. The territory of India
D. Only notified trade zones

**Answer:** C. The territory of India

**Explanation:** Article 301 uses the broad expression throughout the territory of India, so its constitutional freedom is not limited only to inter-State transactions.

## 4. Is the freedom declared by Article 301 absolute?
