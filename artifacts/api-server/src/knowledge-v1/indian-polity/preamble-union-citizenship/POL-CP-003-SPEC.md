# POL-CP-003 — Preamble, Union & Citizenship

**Chapter:** POL-001 Indian Polity  
**Lifecycle:** APPROVED / CONTENT-FROZEN  
**Runtime registration:** DEFERRED to Polity Question Studio integration pass

## Scope

This CP covers three constitutional blocks:

1. the Preamble;
2. Part I — The Union and its Territory (Articles 1–4);
3. Part II — Citizenship at the commencement of the Constitution (Articles 5–11).

It deliberately excludes current citizenship-policy/current-affairs questions and does not treat the Citizenship Act's changing statutory rules as Static GK facts inside this CP.

## Canonical content

### Preamble
- Justice, Liberty, Equality and Fraternity with their constitutional wording;
- present description: Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic;
- original 1949 wording versus the Forty-second Amendment changes;
- insertion of Socialist and Secular;
- change from unity of the Nation to unity and integrity of the Nation;
- adoption date as stated in the Preamble.

### Articles 1–4
- Article 1: India, that is Bharat; Union of States; components of the territory of India;
- Article 2: admission or establishment of new States;
- Article 3: formation of new States and alteration of area, boundaries or names; President's recommendation and reference to affected State Legislature for views;
- Article 4: First/Fourth Schedule and consequential provisions; qualifying laws under Articles 2/3 are not deemed amendments for Article 368 purposes.

### Articles 5–11
- Article 5: citizenship at commencement and domicile-linked qualifying routes;
- Article 6: migrants from Pakistan to India, including the 19 July 1948 distinction and registration route;
- Article 7: migrants to Pakistan and the resettlement/permanent-return permit exception;
- Article 8: certain persons of Indian origin residing outside India and diplomatic/consular registration;
- Article 9: voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship;
- Article 10: continuance of citizenship subject to parliamentary law;
- Article 11: Parliament's power to legislate on acquisition, termination and other citizenship matters.

## Quality rules

- Do not ask current Citizenship Act/current policy questions in this CP.
- Do not imply that Article 3 requires the affected State Legislature's binding consent; the Constitution requires referral for expressing its views.
- Do not describe an Article 4 law as an Article 368 constitutional amendment.
- Keep Article 5 citizenship explicitly anchored to commencement of the Constitution.
- Keep Article 6's pre/post-19 July 1948 routes distinct.
- Keep Article 7's migration-to-Pakistan rule distinct from Article 6's migration-from-Pakistan rule.
- Keep Article 8's diplomatic/consular registration distinct from Articles 6 and 7.
- No invented Article numbers, fake citizenship categories or random cross-domain distractors.
- The generic `On which date did the following occur...` wrapper rejected in CP-002 remains prohibited.
