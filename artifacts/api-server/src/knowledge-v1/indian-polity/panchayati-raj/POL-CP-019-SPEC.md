# POL-CP-019 — Panchayati Raj

**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE — V1  
**Runtime registration:** BLOCKED pending English approval

## Scope

This CP covers the constitutional Panchayati Raj framework created by the Constitution (Seventy-third Amendment) Act, 1992, principally Part IX (Articles 243–243O) and the Eleventh Schedule.

## Constitutional coverage

- Article 243 — definitions
- Article 243A — Gram Sabha
- Article 243B — Panchayat structure and the 20-lakh intermediate-tier exception
- Article 243C — composition and chairpersons
- Article 243D — SC/ST, women and backward-class reservation
- Article 243E — five-year term and re-election after dissolution
- Article 243F — membership disqualifications and minimum age protection at 21
- Article 243G — powers, planning, social justice and State-law devolution
- Article 243H — taxes, assigned revenues, grants and funds
- Article 243I — State Finance Commission
- Article 243J — accounts and audit
- Article 243K — State Election Commission and election safeguards
- Article 243L — Union-territory application
- Article 243M — territorial and State exceptions
- Article 243N — transitional continuation of older laws and Panchayats
- Article 243O — bar to ordinary court interference in Panchayat electoral matters
- Eleventh Schedule — all 29 functional matters

## Accuracy guardrails

- the 73rd Amendment provisions came into force on 24 April 1993;
- Part IX covers Articles 243–243O;
- intermediate Panchayats may be omitted where State population does not exceed 20 lakh;
- Article 243D requires at least one-third of directly elected seats for women and allows backward-class reservation by State law;
- Panchayat term is five years, with normal reconstitution within six months of dissolution;
- a fresh election is unnecessary where less than six months of the old term remained;
- age below 25 alone does not disqualify a Panchayat candidate who has attained 21;
- Article 243G enables State-law devolution and does not itself automatically transfer all 29 Eleventh Schedule matters;
- State Finance Commission is constituted by the Governor every five years after the initial commission;
- Panchayat elections are under the State Election Commission; the State Election Commissioner has High-Court-Judge-style removal protection;
- Article 243M contains Scheduled/tribal-area and specified State/area exceptions;
- Article 243O channels election challenges into election petitions and bars court challenges to covered delimitation laws;
- the Eleventh Schedule contains 29 matters.
