## Scope

This CP covers the formation and working of the Constituent Assembly, key office-holders and committees, the Objectives Resolution, the drafting process, adoption/signing/commencement, selected composition statistics and high-yield constitutional influences.

It does not duplicate POL-CP-001's colonial-Act timeline except where the Government of India Act, 1935 is tested as a constitutional source/influence.

- adoption, signing and commencement as separate events
- selected, unambiguous constitutional influences

## Ambiguity policy

Some secondary sources use different counting conventions for the Assembly's total working days or the elapsed duration of constitution-making. This CP does not ask a context-free question such as “How many days did the Constituent Assembly sit?” where competing conventions could make the item ambiguous.

Where the figure `165` is used, the stem explicitly ties it to the Bombay High Court Constituent Assembly ready reckoner. The `114` days spent considering the Draft Constitution is handled under the same authority.
