# POL-CP-009 — Prime Minister & Union Council of Ministers

**Lifecycle:** APPROVED / CONTENT-FROZEN — V1  
**Runtime registration:** READY FOR POLITY PACKAGE BINDING

## Scope

This CP covers the Prime Minister and Union Council of Ministers as constitutional institutions. Current office-holders and current Cabinet composition are excluded.

### Core Articles
- Article 74 — Council of Ministers to aid and advise the President
- Article 75 — appointment and other provisions as to Ministers
- Article 77 — conduct of business of the Government of India
- Article 78 — duties of the Prime Minister towards the President
- Article 88 — rights of Ministers in Parliament
- Article 352(3) — constitutional meaning of Union Cabinet for the Emergency provision

## High-yield coverage

- Prime Minister appointed by the President
- other Ministers appointed by the President on the Prime Minister's advice
- Prime Minister as head of the Council of Ministers
- collective responsibility to Lok Sabha
- six-consecutive-month parliamentary membership rule
- 15% Union Council size cap, including the Prime Minister
- Ninety-first Amendment link
- anti-defection bar on ministerial appointment under Article 75(1B)
- oaths of office and secrecy before the President; Third Schedule
- pleasure clause, stated without presenting it as unlimited personal presidential discretion
- government business in the President's name under Article 77
- Prime Minister's information duties under Article 78
- Ministers' speaking/participation rights under Article 88 and the voting limitation
- Cabinet as the Cabinet-rank group within the wider Council, using Article 352(3)
- ministerial salaries and allowances under Article 75

## Accuracy boundaries

- collective responsibility is stated specifically as responsibility to Lok Sabha;
- the six-month rule is membership of either House of Parliament;
- the 15% cap is based on total Lok Sabha membership and includes the Prime Minister;
- Article 88 is not treated as an independent voting right;
- the Cabinet/Council distinction is anchored to the constitutional Cabinet definition in Article 352(3);
- the pleasure clause is kept within the parliamentary-system context;
- current office-holders and current ministry size are excluded.
