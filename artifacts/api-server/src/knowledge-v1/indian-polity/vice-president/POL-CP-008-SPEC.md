# POL-CP-008 — Vice-President

**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE — HELPFUL EXPLANATIONS V3

## Scope

This CP covers Articles 63–71 with emphasis on Vice-President-specific exam traps and comparison with the President only where needed for clarity.

### Core coverage
- Article 63 office of Vice-President
- Article 64 ex officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha
- Article 65 acting as President / discharging presidential functions
- Article 66 election, electoral college and qualifications
- Article 67 term, resignation and removal
- Article 68 vacancy election
- Article 69 oath
- Article 70 other contingencies
- Article 71 election disputes

## Language standard

Keep necessary constitutional terms such as electoral college, Rajya Sabha and removal resolution, but simplify the English around them. Prefer direct questions, common verbs and short sentences.

## Explanation standard

Every question receives its own explanation. It first identifies the exact correct answer and then explains the constitutional rule behind it. Important President/Vice-President distinctions are added only when they make the answer clearer. Generic QL-level paragraphs and repeated explanations are not accepted.

Mechanical gates:
- explanation minimum 18 words;
- exact answer must appear in the explanation;
- explanations inside the same QL must be unique;
- repeated answers such as `No` still require question-specific reasoning;
- no unnecessary option-by-option analysis.

## High-yield distinctions

- nominated MPs vote in the Vice-Presidential election; State Legislatures do not
- qualification is for election to Rajya Sabha, not Lok Sabha
- resignation goes to the President
- removal starts in Rajya Sabha and is not presidential impeachment
- no specific removal ground is stated in Article 67
- casual vacancy gives a fresh full five-year term
- while acting as President, the Vice-President does not perform Rajya Sabha Chairman duties
