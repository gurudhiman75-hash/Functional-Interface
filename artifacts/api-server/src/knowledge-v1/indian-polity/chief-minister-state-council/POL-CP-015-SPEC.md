# POL-CP-015 — Chief Minister & State Council of Ministers

**Lifecycle:** APPROVED / CONTENT-FROZEN — V1  
**Runtime registration:** DEFERRED to Polity integration checkpoint

## Scope

This CP covers the Chief Minister and State Council of Ministers centred on Articles 163, 164, 166 and 167, with Article 177 included for Ministers' legislative participation rights. The Advocate-General as a constitutional authority remains owned by POL-CP-022; State Legislature structure remains for POL-CP-016. POL-CP-014 may cross-link only Governor-facing interactions needed to explain the Governor's role.

## Constitutional coverage

- Article 163 — Council of Ministers with Chief Minister at head; aid and advice; constitutionally required discretion; advice-inquiry bar
- Article 164(1) — Chief Minister appointment; appointment of other Ministers on Chief Minister's advice; pleasure formula
- Article 164 proviso — tribal-welfare Minister in Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh and Odisha
- Article 164(1A) — 15% ministry ceiling and minimum 12 Ministers
- Article 164(1B) — ministerial disqualification after specified Tenth Schedule disqualification
- Article 164(2) — collective responsibility to the Legislative Assembly
- Article 164(3) — oaths of office and secrecy
- Article 164(4) — six-consecutive-month rule for a non-member Minister
- Article 164(5) — salaries and allowances
- Article 166 — State Government business, authentication and allocation among Ministers
- Article 167 — Chief Minister's information and Council-submission duties toward the Governor
- Article 177 — Ministers' speaking and participation rights in the State Legislature; no voting right by virtue of Article 177 alone

## Accuracy guardrails

- Chief Minister is formally appointed by the Governor;
- other Ministers are appointed by the Governor on the Chief Minister's advice;
- Article 163 discretion is not treated as a general personal policy power;
- courts cannot inquire into whether or what ministerial advice was tendered to the Governor under Article 163(3);
- ministry ceiling is 15% of Assembly membership with a minimum of 12 including the Chief Minister;
- Article 164(1B) Tenth Schedule ministerial bar is kept distinct from the six-month non-member rule;
- collective responsibility is to the Legislative Assembly, including in bicameral States;
- non-member ministerial tenure cannot exceed six consecutive months without entering the Legislature;
- Article 166 formal expression in the Governor's name is not confused with personal gubernatorial decision-making;
- Article 167 information duties are kept distinct from Article 163 aid and advice;
- Article 177 participation rights do not by themselves create voting rights.
