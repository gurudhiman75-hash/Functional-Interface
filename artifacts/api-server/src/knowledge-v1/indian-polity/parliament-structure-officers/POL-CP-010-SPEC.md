# POL-CP-010 — Parliament Structure & Officers

**Lifecycle:** APPROVED / CONTENT-FROZEN — V1  
**Runtime registration:** DEFERRED to Polity integration checkpoint

## Scope

This CP covers Articles 79–98: constitutional structure of Parliament, basic membership rules, sessions and presidential addresses, and officers of both Houses. Detailed legislative procedure, Bills and financial procedure remain reserved for POL-CP-011.

## Core coverage

- Article 79 — Parliament consists of the President, Rajya Sabha and Lok Sabha
- Article 80 — Rajya Sabha composition, nomination and State election method
- Article 81 — Lok Sabha composition
- Article 82 — readjustment after census
- Article 83 — duration of Houses
- Article 84 — qualifications for Parliament membership
- Article 85 — sessions, prorogation and dissolution
- Articles 86–87 — presidential addresses and messages
- Article 88 — participation rights of Ministers and Attorney-General
- Articles 89–92 — Rajya Sabha Chairman and Deputy Chairman
- Articles 93–96 — Lok Sabha Speaker and Deputy Speaker
- Article 97 — salaries and allowances of presiding officers
- Article 98 — Parliament secretariat

## Accuracy boundaries

- Rajya Sabha is treated as a continuing House; the House itself is not dissolved;
- State representatives in Rajya Sabha are elected by elected MLAs through proportional representation by single transferable vote;
- Article 84 minimum ages remain 30 for Rajya Sabha and 25 for Lok Sabha;
- Article 85 uses the constitutional six-month session-gap rule;
- Article 88 participation is not treated as an independent voting right;
- Deputy Chairman and Speaker removal majorities are stated as a majority of all the then members of the relevant House;
- both removal processes preserve the fourteen-day notice rule;
- the Speaker's post-dissolution continuity is preserved;
- the Chairman's no-vote rule during Vice-President removal is kept distinct from the Speaker's first-instance vote during Speaker removal;
- Bills, Money Bills, joint-sitting procedure, Budget and financial procedure are deferred to POL-CP-011.
