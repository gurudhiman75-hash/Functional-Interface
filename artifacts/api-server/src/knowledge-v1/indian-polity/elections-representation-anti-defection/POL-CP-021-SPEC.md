## Scope

CP021 owns the static constitutional and election-law layer for:

- Articles 324–329: Election Commission, common electoral roll, adult suffrage, legislative election powers and election-petition boundary;
- basic Election Commission election jurisdiction;
- Representation of the People Act, 1950 versus Representation of the People Act, 1951;
- electoral-roll eligibility and ordinary-residence concepts at general-exam depth;
- delimitation basics;
- direct versus indirect election distinctions needed for Parliament;
- SC/ST legislative-seat reservation under Articles 330 and 332;
- political-party registration under section 29A at static-GK depth;
- election petitions and basic corrupt-practice identification;
- Tenth Schedule anti-defection rules;
- 52nd and 91st Amendment links;
- independent and nominated-member rules;
- merger exception, removal of the former split exception, and presiding-officer decision mechanics.

## Exclusions

- current Election Commissioners or office-holders;
- live voter counts, turnout, party recognition counts or election results;
- current party symbols or party-specific political facts;
- state-by-state mutable election schedules;
- litigation trivia beyond stable constitutional principles.
