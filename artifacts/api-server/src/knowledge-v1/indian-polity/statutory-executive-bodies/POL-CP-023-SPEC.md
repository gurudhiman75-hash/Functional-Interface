## Scope

CP023 covers high-yield Union-level statutory and executive institutions that remain outside CP022's constitutional-body layer:

- NITI Aayog: 1 January 2015 Cabinet resolution, policy-think-tank role, cooperative federalism and Governing Council basics;
- National Human Rights Commission and State Human Rights Commissions under the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 as amended in 2019;
- Central Vigilance Commission under the Central Vigilance Commission Act, 2003;
- Central Information Commission and State Information Commissions under the Right to Information Act, 2005;
- Lokpal and Lokayukta framework under the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013;
- Central Bureau of Investigation: 1 April 1963 executive resolution, Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946 powers, Sections 5–6 State-consent framework and CVC superintendence link.

## Ownership boundaries

- Constitutional bodies and constitutional authorities remain in POL-CP-022.
- Election administration and anti-defection remain in POL-CP-021.
- Current chairpersons, commissioners, office-holders, vacancy counts and current case statistics are excluded.
- Mutable organisation charts, current work allocation and current State-by-State consent status are excluded.
- Detailed criminal procedure, corruption offences and RTI exemptions are outside this CP except where a basic distinction is necessary.
