## English

### COM-005-QL-001

#### 1. Which network usually covers a school building?

A. LAN
B. WAN
C. MAN
D. PAN

Answer: A. LAN

Explanation: A LAN (Local Area Network) covers a limited area such as a building.

#### 2. Which network connects devices across different cities?

A. PAN
B. WAN
C. LAN
D. CAN

Answer: B. WAN

Explanation: A WAN (Wide Area Network) covers a large geographical area.

#### 3. A personal connection between a phone and a smartwatch is usually a:

A. LAN
B. MAN
C. PAN
D. WAN

Answer: C. PAN

Explanation: A PAN (Personal Area Network) connects devices around one person.

#### 4. Which network is larger than a LAN but smaller than a WAN in common classification?

A. PAN
B. WAN
C. SAN
D. MAN

Answer: D. MAN

Explanation: A MAN (Metropolitan Area Network) generally covers a city or metropolitan area.
