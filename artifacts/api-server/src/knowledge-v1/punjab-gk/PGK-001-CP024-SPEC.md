## Scope

CP024 covers stable Punjab GK on major fairs and festivals, their places and historical significance, and selected heritage sites of Amritsar, Anandpur Sahib, Jalandhar and Patiala.

CP023 owns folk dance/music/craft. CP024 may mention Baisakhi or other festivals only for fair/heritage context. CP025 owns personalities and sports.

## Learner-facing rules

- Use direct Punjab recruitment-exam stems.
- Explanations normally contain two short, useful sentences.

- Avoid mechanical qualifiers such as `traditionally`, `mainly`, `generally`, `commonly`, `widely`, `usually` and `best described` when direct wording works.

## Qualified coverage

- Hola Mohalla is centred on Sri Anandpur Sahib and includes martial displays and cultural activity.
- Takht Sri Keshgarh Sahib at Anandpur Sahib marks the site where Guru Gobind Singh created the Khalsa in 1699 on Baisakhi.
- Virasat-e-Khalsa is a heritage museum at Anandpur Sahib; it opened to the public in November 2011 and was designed by Moshe Safdie.
- Maghi fair at Sri Muktsar Sahib commemorates the Forty Mukte connected with the Battle of Khidrana/Muktsar.
- Shaheedi Jor Mela at Fatehgarh Sahib commemorates the martyrdom of the younger Sahibzadas and Mata Gujri and is held in December.
- Baisakhi is a major harvest festival of Punjab and also marks the 1699 creation of the Khalsa at Anandpur Sahib.
- Harballabh Sangeet Sammelan is held at Jalandhar; the first Sammelan was held in 1875 at Shri Devi Talab.
- Baba Sodal Mela is held at Jalandhar in the month of Bhadon/September.
- Kila Raipur Rural Olympics/Sports Festival is identified with Kila Raipur in Ludhiana district.
- Amritsar heritage coverage includes Sri Harmandir Sahib, Jallianwala Bagh, Gobindgarh Fort and Ram Tirath.
- Gobindgarh Fort was strengthened on modern lines by Maharaja Ranjit Singh.
- Ram Tirath lies west of Amritsar and is identified with the Valmiki/Ramayana tradition.
- Patiala heritage coverage includes Qila Mubarak, Sheesh Mahal and Moti Bagh Palace.
- Qila Mubarak forms the historic core of Patiala; Baba Ala Singh built the pacca qila after founding the city.
- Sheesh Mahal stands behind Moti Bagh Palace and houses museum/art collections.
