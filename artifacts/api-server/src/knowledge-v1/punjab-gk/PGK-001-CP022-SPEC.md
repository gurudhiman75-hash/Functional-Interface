## Scope

CP022 covers high-value Punjabi literature GK: early/classical Punjabi poets, the qissa tradition and Waris Shah, Bhai Vir Singh, Nanak Singh, Amrita Pritam, Shiv Kumar Batalvi, Gurdial Singh, and selected modern author-work/award relations.

The CP tests literary identification rather than literary criticism. CP021 owns language/script mechanics; CP023 owns folk culture, music and dance.

## Qualified coverage

- Baba Farid is among the earliest major poets represented in the Punjabi literary tradition.
- Shah Hussain, Sultan Bahu and Bulleh Shah are major names in classical Punjabi Sufi poetry; Bulleh Shah primarily used the kafi form.
- Waris Shah was an eighteenth-century Punjabi Sufi poet whose `Heer`/`Heer Ranjha` is a landmark Punjabi qissa based on the Heer-Ranjha romance.
- Bhai Vir Singh wrote `Sundari` and `Rana Surat Singh`; his poetry work `Mere Sainya Jio` received the Sahitya Akademi Award for Punjabi in 1955.
- Nanak Singh was a major Punjabi novelist. `Ik Miyan Do Talwaran` received the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1961; `Chitta Lahu` is another major novel.
- Amrita Pritam wrote the Punjabi novel `Pinjar` and the poem `Ajj Aakhaan Waris Shah Nu`. Her poetry collection `Sunehure` received the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1956. She received the Jnanpith Award in 1981.
- Shiv Kumar Batalvi wrote the verse-play `Loona`, which received the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1967.
- Gurdial Singh wrote `Marhi Da Deeva`; his novel `Adh Chanani Raat` received the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1975. He received the Jnanpith Award for Punjabi in 1999.
- Selected Sahitya Akademi relations used for wider modern literature: Mohan Singh — `Wadda Vela` (1959, poetry); Balwant Gargi — `Rangmanch` (1962); Kulwant Singh Virk — `Naven Lok` (1968, short stories); Dalip Kaur Tiwana — `Eho Hamara Jiwana` (1971, novel).
