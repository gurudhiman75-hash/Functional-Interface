## PGK-001-QL-021 — Five rivers, ancient names and present-day distinction

### PGK-001-CP004-Q001 — Easy
**Which set contains the five rivers from which Punjab gets its name?**

A. Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum
B. Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Ghaggar and Yamuna
C. Beas, Ravi, Chenab, Indus and Yamuna
D. Sutlej, Ravi, Jhelum, Ghaggar and Indus

**Answer:** Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum

**Explanation:** The five traditional rivers are Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum. The name Punjab literally refers to the land of five waters.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q002 — Easy
**Which of the following is NOT one of the traditional five rivers of Punjab?**

A. Ghaggar
B. Ravi
C. Chenab
D. Jhelum

**Answer:** Ghaggar

**Explanation:** Ghaggar is an important seasonal river, but it is not one of the traditional five rivers that give Punjab its name.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q003 — Easy
**Of the traditional five rivers, which three flow through present-day Indian Punjab?**

A. Sutlej, Beas and Ravi
B. Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum
C. Sutlej, Chenab and Jhelum
D. Beas, Chenab and Jhelum

**Answer:** Sutlej, Beas and Ravi

**Explanation:** Sutlej, Beas and Ravi are the three rivers from the traditional five that flow through present-day Indian Punjab.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q004 — Medium
**Which pair from the traditional five does not flow through present-day Indian Punjab?**

A. Chenab and Jhelum
B. Ravi and Beas
C. Sutlej and Ravi
D. Beas and Sutlej

**Answer:** Chenab and Jhelum

**Explanation:** Chenab and Jhelum are the two traditional Punjab rivers that do not flow through present-day Indian Punjab.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q005 — Easy
**What is the ancient name of the Sutlej River?**

A. Shutudri
B. Vipasa
C. Purushni
D. Askini

**Answer:** Shutudri

**Explanation:** Sutlej was known as Shutudri in ancient tradition. Sutudri and Shatadru are also encountered as spelling variants.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q006 — Medium
**Vipasa was the ancient name of which river?**

A. Beas
B. Ravi
C. Chenab
D. Jhelum

**Answer:** Beas

**Explanation:** Vipasa was the ancient name of the Beas. It is also commonly written as Vipasha.

## PGK-001-QL-022 — Ravi, Beas and Sutlej relations

### PGK-001-CP004-Q007 — Easy
**Which three are known as the eastern rivers of the Indus system?**

A. Ravi, Beas and Sutlej
B. Chenab, Jhelum and Indus
C. Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum
D. Beas, Jhelum and Indus

**Answer:** Ravi, Beas and Sutlej

**Explanation:** Ravi, Beas and Sutlej are the three eastern rivers of the Indus system.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q008 — Easy
**Which of the following is NOT one of the three eastern rivers?**

A. Chenab
B. Ravi
C. Beas
D. Sutlej

**Answer:** Chenab

**Explanation:** The eastern-river group consists of Ravi, Beas and Sutlej. Chenab belongs to the western group.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q009 — Medium

B. Chenab, Jhelum and Ravi

D. Sutlej, Chenab and Ravi

**Answer:** Sutlej, Beas and Ravi

**Explanation:** Sutlej, Beas and Ravi are both the three eastern rivers and the three members of the traditional five that flow through present-day Indian Punjab.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q010 — Medium
**Which pair consists entirely of eastern rivers?**

A. Ravi and Beas
B. Chenab and Jhelum
C. Jhelum and Sutlej
D. Indus and Ravi

**Answer:** Ravi and Beas

**Explanation:** Ravi and Beas are both eastern rivers. Sutlej is the third river in this group.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q011 — Medium
**Which river was known as Purushni in ancient times?**

A. Ravi
B. Beas
C. Sutlej
D. Jhelum

**Answer:** Ravi

**Explanation:** Ravi was known as Purushni in ancient tradition. Parushni is another common spelling of the same name.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q012 — Medium
**Askini was the ancient name of which river?**

A. Chenab
B. Jhelum
C. Ravi
D. Beas

**Answer:** Chenab

**Explanation:** Chenab was known as Askini in ancient tradition. The name is also commonly written as Asikni.

## PGK-001-QL-023 — Harike river confluence

### PGK-001-CP004-Q013 — Easy
**The Beas joins which river at Harike?**

A. Sutlej
B. Ravi
C. Chenab
D. Ghaggar

**Answer:** Sutlej

**Explanation:** The Beas joins the Sutlej at Harike. Their confluence is one of Punjab's important river landmarks.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q014 — Easy
**Which two rivers meet at Harike?**

A. Beas and Sutlej
B. Ravi and Beas
C. Ravi and Sutlej
D. Ghaggar and Sutlej

**Answer:** Beas and Sutlej

**Explanation:** Harike lies at the confluence of the Beas and Sutlej rivers.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q015 — Medium
**After the Beas joins the Sutlej at Harike, which river name continues downstream?**

A. Sutlej
B. Beas
C. Ravi
D. Chenab

**Answer:** Sutlej

**Explanation:** The Beas joins the Sutlej at Harike. The combined flow continues downstream as the Sutlej.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q016 — Medium
**Which statement about the Beas is correct?**

A. It joins the Sutlej at Harike.
B. It joins the Ravi at Harike.
C. It is not one of the traditional five rivers.
D. It forms the Ghaggar floodplain.

**Answer:** It joins the Sutlej at Harike.

**Explanation:** Beas is one of Punjab's traditional five rivers and joins the Sutlej at Harike.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q017 — Medium
**Which river pair forms both a doab boundary and a confluence at Harike?**

A. Beas and Sutlej
B. Ravi and Chenab
C. Chenab and Jhelum
D. Ravi and Beas

**Answer:** Beas and Sutlej

**Explanation:** Beas and Sutlej bound the Bist Doab and also meet at Harike.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q018 — Hard
**A river pair bounds Bist Doab and meets at Harike. Which pair is it?**

A. Sutlej and Beas
B. Beas and Ravi
C. Ravi and Chenab
D. Chenab and Jhelum

**Answer:** Sutlej and Beas

**Explanation:** Bist Doab lies between Sutlej and Beas. These same two rivers meet at Harike.

## PGK-001-QL-024 — Ghaggar and seasonal drainage

### PGK-001-CP004-Q019 — Easy
**Ghaggar is mainly what type of river in Punjab?**

A. Seasonal river
B. Perennial glacier-fed river
C. Tidal river
D. Deltaic distributary

**Answer:** Seasonal river

**Explanation:** Ghaggar is mainly a seasonal river. Its flow increases greatly during the rainy season.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q020 — Easy
**The flow of the Ghaggar generally becomes much stronger during the:**

A. Rainy season
B. Winter dry season
C. Late spring only
D. Entire year equally

**Answer:** Rainy season

**Explanation:** Ghaggar carries much more water during the rainy season and can flood after heavy rainfall.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q021 — Medium
**Which description best fits the Ghaggar?**

A. A seasonal river with much higher flow during rains
B. A permanently frozen Himalayan river
C. A coastal tidal channel
D. A river that remains equally full throughout the year

**Answer:** A seasonal river with much higher flow during rains

**Explanation:** Ghaggar is seasonal and responds strongly to monsoon rainfall. Its lower stretches may remain dry for much of the year.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q022 — Medium
**Which river is correctly matched with its general flow character in Punjab?**

A. Ghaggar — Seasonal
B. Sutlej — Tidal
C. Ravi — Coastal
D. Beas — Desert-only

**Answer:** Ghaggar — Seasonal

**Explanation:** Ghaggar is a seasonal river in Punjab, with much stronger flow during the rains.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q023 — Medium
**Which statement is correct about Ghaggar and the traditional five rivers of Punjab?**

A. Ghaggar is a seasonal river but is not one of the traditional five rivers.
B. Ghaggar replaces Ravi in the traditional five.
C. Ghaggar is another name for Sutlej.
D. Ghaggar is one of the three eastern rivers.

**Answer:** Ghaggar is a seasonal river but is not one of the traditional five rivers.

**Explanation:** Ghaggar is an important seasonal river, but it is separate from the traditional five-river naming of Punjab.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q024 — Hard
**Consider the following statements about Ghaggar:
I. It is seasonal.
II. Its flow rises strongly during the rainy season.
III. It is one of the traditional five rivers that give Punjab its name.
Which of the statements given above are correct?**

A. I only
B. I and II only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III

**Answer:** I and II only

**Explanation:** Ghaggar is seasonal and becomes much fuller during rains. It is not part of the traditional five-river set.

## PGK-001-QL-025 — Doab meaning and structure

### PGK-001-CP004-Q025 — Easy
**What is meant by the term 'Doab'?**

A. Land between two rivers
B. Land above a mountain pass
C. A seasonal river channel
D. A river delta at the sea

**Answer:** Land between two rivers

**Explanation:** A doab is the tract of land lying between two rivers.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q026 — Easy
**Which geographical term is used for a tract of land between two rivers?**

A. Doab
B. Kandi
C. Bet
D. Choe

**Answer:** Doab

**Explanation:** The term doab refers to land between two rivers.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q027 — Medium
**Which pair of rivers bounds Bist Doab?**

A. Sutlej and Beas
B. Beas and Ravi
C. Ravi and Chenab
D. Chenab and Jhelum

**Answer:** Sutlej and Beas

**Explanation:** Bist Doab lies between the Sutlej and Beas rivers.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q028 — Medium
**Bari Doab lies between which two rivers?**

A. Beas and Ravi
B. Sutlej and Beas
C. Ravi and Chenab
D. Chenab and Jhelum

**Answer:** Beas and Ravi

**Explanation:** Bari Doab is the tract between the Beas and Ravi rivers.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q029 — Medium
**Which two doabs share the Beas as one of their boundary rivers?**

A. Bist Doab and Bari Doab
B. Bari Doab and Rachna Doab
C. Rachna Doab and Chaj Doab
D. Chaj Doab and Sind Sagar Doab

**Answer:** Bist Doab and Bari Doab

**Explanation:** Beas forms one boundary of Bist Doab and the other boundary of Bari Doab.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q030 — Hard
**Which sequence correctly follows adjacent doabs from the Sutlej-Beas tract toward the Chenab-Jhelum tract?**

A. Bist → Bari → Rachna → Chaj
B. Bari → Bist → Chaj → Rachna
C. Chaj → Rachna → Bari → Bist
D. Bist → Rachna → Bari → Chaj

**Answer:** Bist → Bari → Rachna → Chaj

**Explanation:** The sequence follows adjoining river pairs: Sutlej-Beas, Beas-Ravi, Ravi-Chenab and Chenab-Jhelum.

**Rachna Doab lies between which two rivers?**

A. Ravi and Chenab
B. Beas and Ravi
C. Chenab and Jhelum
D. Sutlej and Beas

**Answer:** Ravi and Chenab

**Explanation:** Rachna Doab lies between the Ravi and Chenab rivers.

**Chaj Doab lies between which two rivers?**

A. Chenab and Jhelum
B. Ravi and Chenab
C. Beas and Ravi
D. Sutlej and Beas

**Answer:** Chenab and Jhelum

**Sind Sagar Doab lies between which two rivers?**

A. Jhelum and Indus
B. Chenab and Jhelum
C. Ravi and Chenab

**Answer:** Jhelum and Indus

B. Bist Doab

**Explanation:** Rachna Doab lies between Ravi and Chenab. Ravi also forms the western boundary of Bari Doab.

A. Chaj Doab

## PGK-001-QL-027 — River-doab synthesis

### PGK-001-CP004-Q037 — Hard
**Consider the following pairs:
I. Bist Doab — Sutlej and Beas
II. Bari Doab — Beas and Ravi
III. Rachna Doab — Ravi and Chenab
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?**

A. I only
B. I and II only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III

**Answer:** I, II and III

**Explanation:** All three pairs are correct and form a continuous sequence of adjoining river tracts.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q038 — Hard
**Consider the following pairs:
I. Chaj Doab — Chenab and Jhelum
II. Sind Sagar Doab — Jhelum and Indus
III. Bist Doab — Beas and Ravi
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?**

A. I only
B. I and II only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III

**Answer:** I and II only

**Explanation:** Chaj and Sind Sagar are correctly matched. Bist Doab lies between Sutlej and Beas, not Beas and Ravi.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q039 — Hard
**A doab is bounded by the same two rivers that meet at Harike. Which doab is it?**

A. Bist Doab
B. Bari Doab
C. Rachna Doab
D. Chaj Doab

**Answer:** Bist Doab

**Explanation:** Beas and Sutlej meet at Harike, and the land between these two rivers is Bist Doab.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q040 — Hard
**Which of the following ancient-name pairs is correctly matched?**

A. Jhelum — Vitasta
B. Chenab — Vipasa
C. Ravi — Askini
D. Beas — Purushni

**Answer:** Jhelum — Vitasta

**Explanation:** Jhelum was known as Vitasta in ancient tradition. Vipasa belongs to Beas, Askini to Chenab and Purushni to Ravi.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q041 — Hard
**Consider the following pairs:
I. Sutlej — Shutudri
II. Beas — Vipasa
III. Ravi — Purushni
IV. Chenab — Askini
V. Jhelum — Vitasta
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?**

A. I, II and III only
B. II, III and IV only
C. I, III, IV and V only
D. I, II, III, IV and V

**Answer:** I, II, III, IV and V

**Explanation:** All five pairs are correctly matched. These are the standard ancient names of Punjab's five traditional rivers.

### PGK-001-CP004-Q042 — Hard
**Which sequence correctly matches four adjoining doabs with their river pairs?**

A. Bist—Sutlej/Beas; Bari—Beas/Ravi; Rachna—Ravi/Chenab; Chaj—Chenab/Jhelum
B. Bist—Beas/Ravi; Bari—Ravi/Chenab; Rachna—Chenab/Jhelum; Chaj—Sutlej/Beas
C. Bist—Chenab/Jhelum; Bari—Sutlej/Beas; Rachna—Beas/Ravi; Chaj—Ravi/Chenab
D. Bist—Jhelum/Indus; Bari—Chenab/Jhelum; Rachna—Beas/Ravi; Chaj—Sutlej/Beas

**Answer:** Bist—Sutlej/Beas; Bari—Beas/Ravi; Rachna—Ravi/Chenab; Chaj—Chenab/Jhelum

**Explanation:** The four doabs follow adjoining river pairs from Sutlej-Beas through Chenab-Jhelum.
