## Scope

CP004 builds the river and doab relation layer of Punjab GK. It covers the five-river name set, their ancient names, the rivers flowing through present-day Punjab, the Ravi-Beas-Sutlej relation, Harike confluence, Ghaggar as a seasonal river, doab meaning and the standard named doabs of the Punjab plains.

Dams, barrages, canals and irrigation projects belong primarily to CP005. CP004 may mention Harike only as a river-confluence fact; barrage/project depth is deferred.

## Qualified coverage

- Historical five-river set: Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum.
- Ancient-name mapping for the five traditional rivers:
  - Sutlej — Shutudri; accepted spelling variants include Sutudri/Shatadru.
  - Beas — Vipasa; Vipasha/Vipas accepted as variants.
  - Ravi — Purushni; Parushni accepted as a variant.
  - Chenab — Askini; Asikni accepted as a variant.
  - Jhelum — Vitasta.
- Of the traditional five, Sutlej, Beas and Ravi flow through present-day Indian Punjab.
- Ravi, Beas and Sutlej form the three eastern rivers.
- Beas joins Sutlej at Harike.
- Ghaggar is a seasonal river in Punjab and becomes much fuller during the rainy season.
- `Doab` means land between two rivers.
- Bist Doab: Sutlej-Beas.
- Bari Doab: Beas-Ravi.
- Rachna Doab: Ravi-Chenab.
- Chaj Doab: Chenab-Jhelum.
- Sind Sagar Doab: Jhelum-Indus side of the Punjab plains.
- Region-place relations are used only where already accepted in CP003.
