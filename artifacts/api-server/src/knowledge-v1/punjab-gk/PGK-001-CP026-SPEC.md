## Scope

CP026 is the district-level consolidation layer for present-day Punjab. It covers all 23 districts through stable, high-value links among geography, heritage, industries, institutions, products and notable places. It avoids turning the chapter into a list of minor local trivia.

Earlier CPs retain ownership of the full thematic treatment of rivers, dams, archaeology, Sikh history, freedom movements, industries, wildlife and heritage. CP026 tests where those facts belong at district level and combines them across regions.

## Learner-facing rules

- Use direct Punjab recruitment-exam stems.
- Explanations normally contain two short, useful sentences.
- Keep learner text source-free.
- Difficulty comes from district-place and district-feature relations, not obscure local facts.
- Use same-domain district distractors.
- Prefer durable places, institutions, industries and historical relations.
- Avoid current office-holders, current rankings, changing project values and mutable district statistics.
- Historical geography must not be silently presented as present-day district geography.
- Hard questions should combine several verified district relations.

## District coverage

All 23 present-day districts are represented:

Amritsar; Barnala; Bathinda; Faridkot; Fatehgarh Sahib; Fazilka; Ferozepur; Gurdaspur; Hoshiarpur; Jalandhar; Kapurthala; Ludhiana; Malerkotla; Mansa; Moga; Pathankot; Patiala; Rupnagar; Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar (S.A.S. Nagar); Sangrur; Shaheed Bhagat Singh Nagar; Sri Muktsar Sahib; Tarn Taran.

## Qualified coverage

- Amritsar: Sri Harmandir Sahib and Jallianwala Bagh.
- Tarn Taran: Harike Wetland; Harike lies downstream of the Beas-Sutlej confluence.
- Gurdaspur: Kalanaur and Dera Baba Nanak; Kalanaur is connected with Akbar's installation in 1556.
- Pathankot: Ranjit Sagar Dam at Shahpurkandi on the Ravi.
- Jalandhar: major sports-goods manufacturing cluster.
- Kapurthala: Pushpa Gujral Science City, Sultanpur Lodhi and Jagatjit Palace.
- Hoshiarpur: decorative wood inlay handicraft.
- Shaheed Bhagat Singh Nagar: Khatkar Kalan, ancestral village of Shaheed Bhagat Singh.
- Ludhiana: Punjab Agricultural University and major bicycle/bicycle-parts manufacturing.
- Moga: major food-processing activity including the Nestle plant listed by the district administration.
- Barnala: prominent towel/textile and combine-manufacturing industries.
- Sangrur: Sunam, birthplace of Shaheed Udham Singh.
- Malerkotla: district identity derived from the former princely state of Maler Kotla.
- Patiala: Netaji Subhas National Institute of Sports, Qila Mubarak and Old Moti Bagh Palace.
- Fatehgarh Sahib: Mandi Gobindgarh steel rolling industry and district Sikh heritage.
- S.A.S. Nagar: IISER Mohali and major Mohali institutions/landmarks.
- Rupnagar: Ropar archaeological site and museum; the site was the first Harappan site excavated in independent India.
- Bathinda: Qila Mubarak and Takht Sri Damdama Sahib at Talwandi Sabo.
- Mansa: Punjab cotton belt / 'area of white gold' identity.
- Faridkot: city name tradition connected with Baba Farid.
- Ferozepur: National Martyrs Memorial at Hussainiwala.
- Fazilka: Abohar Wildlife Sanctuary and blackbuck conservation.
- Sri Muktsar Sahib: Khidrana battle, Chali Mukte and Mukta Minar.
