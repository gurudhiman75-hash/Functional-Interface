## Qualified coverage

- Milkha Singh was an Indian track athlete called the Flying Sikh. He finished fourth in the 400 metres at the 1960 Rome Olympics and represented India at the 1956, 1960 and 1964 Olympic Games.
- Pakistan's President Ayub Khan gave Milkha Singh the Flying Sikh title after his Lahore race.
- Balbir Singh Sr. won Olympic hockey gold as a player in 1948, 1952 and 1956. He captained India's 1956 Melbourne gold-medal team and scored five goals in the 1952 Olympic final.
- Ajit Pal Singh, born at Sansarpur in Punjab, captained India to the 1975 Men's Hockey World Cup title in Kuala Lumpur. His World Cup medal sequence was bronze in 1971, silver in 1973 and gold in 1975.
- Pargat Singh, born at Mithapur in Punjab, captained India at the 1992 Barcelona and 1996 Atlanta Olympic Games.
- Manpreet Singh, also born at Mithapur, captained India's men's hockey bronze-medal team at Tokyo 2020.
- Harmanpreet Singh, born in Punjab, captained India's men's hockey bronze-medal team at Paris 2024.
- Gurbachan Singh Randhawa won decathlon gold at the 1962 Asian Games, finished fifth in the 110 metres hurdles at the 1964 Tokyo Olympics, received the Arjuna Award in 1961 and received the Padma Shri in 2005.
- Paramjeet Singh broke Milkha Singh's long-standing Indian 400 metres record in 1998 and received the Maharaja Ranjit Singh Award in 2006.
- The Maharaja Ranjit Singh Award is a Punjab Government sports honour for high sporting achievement.
