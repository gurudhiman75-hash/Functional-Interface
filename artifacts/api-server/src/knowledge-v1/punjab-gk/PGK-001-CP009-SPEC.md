## Scope

CP009 covers stable Punjab economy and industry GK: major industrial centres and products, Ludhiana manufacturing, Jalandhar sports/hand-tool industries, Mandi Gobindgarh secondary steel, Batala casting and machine tools, Guru Gobind Singh Refinery at Bathinda, cooperative institutions such as MARKFED and MILKFED/Verka, and PSIEC industrial infrastructure.

Current GSDP shares, export values, turnover, rankings, plant capacities and year-sensitive industrial statistics are excluded from learner questions unless explicitly versioned.

## Learner-facing rules

- Stems must be short, direct and recruitment-exam grade.
- Do not use filler such as `associated with`, `linked with`, `known for`, `closely related to` or source-note wording.
- Do not cite departments, reports, websites or source names in learner text.
- Keep explanations natural and normally 1–2 sentences.
- Difficulty must come from industry-centre, institution-function and multi-fact relations rather than obscure company trivia.

## Qualified coverage

- Ludhiana is a major Punjab manufacturing centre for hosiery/knitwear, bicycles, sewing machines, hand tools and auto components.
- Jalandhar is a major centre for sports goods, hand tools, leather/tanning, casting and surgical goods.
- Mandi Gobindgarh is one of Punjab's oldest secondary-steel clusters and is strongly identified with steel re-rolling.
- Batala has a long-established casting-iron and machine-tools industry.
- Guru Gobind Singh Refinery is located at Bathinda and is operated by HPCL-Mittal Energy Limited (HMEL).
- HMEL is a joint venture promoted by Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited and Mittal Energy Investments.
- MARKFED is the Punjab State Cooperative Supply and Marketing Federation Limited; it was registered in 1954.
- MILKFED is the Punjab State Cooperative Milk Producers' Federation Limited; it came into existence in 1973.
- Verka is the flagship dairy brand of MILKFED.
- PSIEC stands for Punjab Small Industries & Export Corporation Limited and develops industrial focal points/infrastructure in Punjab.
