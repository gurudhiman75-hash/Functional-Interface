## Scope

CP016 covers the First Anglo-Sikh War (1845–46), the 1846 treaty settlement and regency transition, the Second Anglo-Sikh War (1848–49), and the annexation of Punjab in 1849.

CP015 owns Maharaja Ranjit Singh and the Sikh Empire during his reign. CP017 owns later reform and freedom movements. CP016 may use the post-1839 succession background only where it directly explains the wars.

## Qualified coverage

- After Ranjit Singh's death in 1839, the Lahore state entered a period of political instability; the young Duleep Singh became Maharaja and Rani Jindan served as Regent.
- The First Anglo-Sikh War was fought in 1845–46.
- The Khalsa army crossed the Sutlej in December 1845; the first major battle was fought at Mudki on 18 December 1845.
- Ferozeshah was fought on 21–22 December 1845.
- Aliwal was fought on 28 January 1846; Sir Harry Smith led the British force and Ranjodh Singh Majithia led the Sikh force.
- Sobraon was fought on 10 February 1846 and was the final major battle of the First Anglo-Sikh War.
- Sir Hugh Gough was Commander-in-Chief of the British forces in India during the First Anglo-Sikh War; Henry Hardinge was Governor-General.
- The Treaty of Lahore was signed on 9 March 1846 and ended the First Anglo-Sikh War.
- Under the Treaty of Lahore, the Lahore state ceded the Jalandhar Doab between the Beas and Sutlej and accepted a war indemnity.
- The Treaty of Amritsar was signed on 16 March 1846 between the East India Company and Gulab Singh. It recognised Gulab Singh as Maharaja of Jammu and Kashmir under the treaty settlement.
- The Treaty/Agreement of Bhairowal was concluded in December 1846. During Duleep Singh's minority, British supervision over the Lahore administration became much stronger through the Resident and Council of Regency.
- The Second Anglo-Sikh War was fought in 1848–49.
- The conflict grew from the Multan revolt led by Diwan Mulraj in 1848; Sher Singh Attariwala later joined the rebellion.
- Important Second War actions include Ramnagar (22 November 1848), Chillianwala (13 January 1849) and Gujrat (21 February 1849).
- Multan surrendered in January 1849 after the siege.
- Gujrat was the decisive final major battle of the Second Anglo-Sikh War.
- The remaining Sikh forces surrendered at Rawalpindi in March 1849.
- Punjab was annexed by the East India Company on 29 March 1849 during Lord Dalhousie's tenure as Governor-General.
- Maharaja Duleep Singh was deposed after annexation.
