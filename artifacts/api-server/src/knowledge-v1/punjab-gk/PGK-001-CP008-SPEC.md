## Scope

CP008 covers stable Punjab agriculture GK: crop seasons, the wheat-rice system, cotton geography, maize and other important field crops, horticultural belts, Punjab Agricultural University and Green Revolution relations, and crop-diversification concepts.

Current acreage, production, procurement shares, rankings and year-specific crop statistics are excluded from learner questions unless explicitly versioned.

## Qualified coverage

- Wheat is a major Rabi crop; rice/paddy, cotton and maize are major Kharif crops.
- Rapeseed-mustard and gram/chickpea are important Rabi crops.
- Wheat-rice is Punjab's dominant cereal rotation.
- Punjab Agricultural University is located at Ludhiana and played a major role in the Green Revolution.
- Cotton cultivation is concentrated in south-western Punjab, especially Bathinda, Mansa, Fazilka and Sri Muktsar Sahib.
- Kharif maize is important in Punjab; PAU recommendations include maize suited to Kandi areas.
- Sugarcane is an important commercial crop and PAU maintains dedicated sugarcane research.
- Abohar/Fazilka is a major kinnow and cotton belt.
- The arid-irrigated horticultural zone includes south-western districts and is well suited to kinnow and other citrus fruits.
- Kandi horticulture includes fruits such as guava, ber, amla, mango and galgal.
- Crop diversification away from excessive wheat-rice dependence is used to reduce pressure on groundwater and broaden farm income sources.
- Direct-seeded rice and short-duration rice are water-saving approaches compared with conventional long-duration transplanted paddy systems.
