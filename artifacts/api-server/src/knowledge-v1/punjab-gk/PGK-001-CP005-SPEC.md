# PGK-001 CP005 — Dams, Barrages & Canals

Status: HUMAN APPROVED / FROZEN V1

Permanent QLs: `PGK-001-QL-028` to `PGK-001-QL-034`

## Scope

CP005 builds Punjab's high-value water-infrastructure relation layer: major dams and their rivers, reservoir/alias relations, important headworks and barrages, canal-source relations, and selected multipurpose-project links.

Detailed river geography and doabs remain in CP004. Climate, soils and natural resources begin in CP006.

## Learner-facing rules

- Do not cite source names, departments, reports, websites or document titles in stems or explanations.
- Keep stems exam-like and concise.
- Keep explanations natural: decisive fact first, then one useful relation.
- Avoid project-status wording that can become time-sensitive.
- No routine option-by-option analysis.
- Use only stable static relations in the learner pool.

## Qualified coverage

- Bhakra Dam — Sutlej; concrete gravity dam; reservoir known as Gobind Sagar.
- Pong/Beas Dam — Beas; earth-core gravel-shell dam; reservoir known as Maharana Pratap Sagar.
- Ranjit Sagar Dam/Thein Dam — Ravi; Pathankot district; 600 MW installed capacity.
- Shahpurkandi Dam — Ravi; downstream of Ranjit Sagar and upstream of Madhopur Headworks; balancing-regulation relation.
- Nangal structure — mass-concrete barrage in the Bhakra-Nangal system on the Sutlej.
- Harike Headworks — at the Beas-Sutlej confluence; linked with the Ferozepur Feeder system.
- Madhopur Headworks — Ravi; source/headworks relation for the Upper Bari Doab Canal and Kashmir Canal.
- Ropar/Rupnagar Headworks — Sutlej; Sirhind Canal takes off from here.
- Sirhind Canal — Sutlej/Ropar Headworks relation.
- Upper Bari Doab Canal (UBDC) — Madhopur Headworks/Ravi relation.
- Nangal Hydel Channel — part of the Bhakra-Nangal water-conductor system.
- Beas-Sutlej Link — diverts Beas water toward the Sutlej system.

## Distractor rule

Use other Punjab/Punjab-system dams against dams, rivers against rivers, headworks against headworks and canals against canals. Avoid obviously unrelated filler.

## Explanation rule

Normally 1–2 short sentences. Explanations should clarify the dam-river, reservoir, headworks, canal or project relation without citing the evidence source.
