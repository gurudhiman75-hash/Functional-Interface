## Scope

CP018 covers the administrative transformation of Punjab after independence: the 1947 Partition, East and West Punjab, the need for a new capital, formation of PEPSU, the 1956 merger of PEPSU with Punjab, and the Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966.

The chapter is historical and administrative. It does not evaluate political movements or current inter-state disputes.

## Learner-facing rules

- Use direct Punjab recruitment-exam stems.
- Explanations should normally contain two short, useful sentences.
- Keep `1947`, `1948`, `1956` and `1966` conceptually distinct.
- Avoid source-note language and filler such as `associated with`, `linked with`, `known for`, `formed the framework`.
- Do not ask casualty totals, communal-composition trivia, current disputes or partisan interpretations.
- Hard questions should test chronology, place-status changes, legislation and administrative relations.

## Qualified coverage

- The former Punjab province was divided between India and Pakistan in 1947; East Punjab became part of India and West Punjab became part of Pakistan.
- Lahore became part of Pakistan, leaving Indian Punjab without its former provincial capital.
- The Chandigarh site near the Shivalik foothills was approved in 1948; the foundation stone was laid in 1952.
- Chandigarh served as Punjab's capital before the 1966 reorganisation.
- PEPSU stands for Patiala and East Punjab States Union and was inaugurated on 15 July 1948.
- PEPSU was formed from eight princely states: Patiala, Kapurthala, Jind, Nabha, Faridkot, Malerkotla, Nalagarh and Kalsia.
- PEPSU merged with Punjab under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956; the merger took effect on 1 November 1956.
- The PEPSU High Court was merged into the Punjab High Court after the 1956 reorganisation.
- The Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966 was enacted on 18 September 1966.
- The Act's appointed day was 1 November 1966.
- Haryana was formed from specified territories of the existing Punjab.
- Chandigarh became a Union Territory and continued as the capital of both Punjab and Haryana.
- Specified hill territories were transferred from Punjab to Himachal Pradesh.
