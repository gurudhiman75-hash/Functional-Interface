# ECO-CP-013 — Government Budget

## Objective

Build a Static-GK question pool on the constitutional structure, parliamentary procedure and principal documents of the Union Budget without encoding current-year figures, allocations or tax proposals.

## Included

- Union Budget and Annual Financial Statement (AFS);
- Article 112 and the President causing the AFS to be laid before both Houses;
- Consolidated Fund of India, Contingency Fund of India and Public Account of India;
- Articles 266 and 267 at concept level;
- charged versus voted expenditure;
- Demands for Grants and Article 113;
- Lok Sabha's voting role in Demands for Grants;
- Appropriation Bill and Article 114;
- Finance Bill and its tax-proposal role under Article 110;
- supplementary, additional and excess grants under Article 115;
- Vote on Account, Vote of Credit and Exceptional Grant under Article 116;
- Expenditure Budget, Receipt Budget, Expenditure Profile and Budget at a Glance;
- Budget-document and constitutional-process distinctions;
- mixed statement and sequence questions.

## Excluded

- current Budget allocations or scheme outlays;
- current number of Demands for Grants;
- current tax rates/slabs and current Finance Bill proposals;
- current deficit/debt values;
- current Contingency Fund corpus;
- current ministry-wise expenditure or receipts;
- detailed tax-system classification reserved for ECO-CP-014;
- political commentary or evaluation of a particular Budget.

## Editorial rules

- Every normal MCQ stem must be a complete exam-style question and end in `?`.
- Do not use prompt fragments ending in `:` such as `Article 112 deals with:` or `is known as:`.
- Keep language concise and natural for SSC/Banking/State exams.
- Use plausible same-domain distractors.
- Explanations should normally give the deciding rule plus one short reason or application.
- Statement explanations must identify which statement is true or false and why.
- Hard questions must test close constitutional/document/process distinctions, not obscure trivia or wording length.
