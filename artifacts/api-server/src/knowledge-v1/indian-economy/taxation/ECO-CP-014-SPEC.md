# ECO-CP-014 — Taxation

## Objective

Build a Static-GK question pool on the Indian tax system using stable economic and constitutional concepts rather than current tax rates, slabs, exemptions or Budget-year proposals.

## Included

- meaning and broad purpose of taxation;
- direct versus indirect taxes;
- tax burden/shifting and basic incidence distinction;
- progressive, proportional and regressive tax structures;
- income tax and corporation tax as direct-tax examples;
- customs duty as an indirect tax on imported/exported goods;
- GST introduction on 1 July 2017 and Constitution (101st Amendment) Act, 2016;
- GST as a destination-based consumption tax and value-added tax with input-tax-credit logic;
- dual GST model;
- CGST plus SGST/UTGST on intra-State supplies;
- IGST on inter-State supplies;
- Article 246A legislative power for GST;
- Article 269A inter-State GST levy/collection and apportionment;
- GST Council under Article 279A;
- GST Council composition and recommendation role at a static constitutional level;
- close direct/indirect, origin/destination and intra/inter-State distinctions.

## Excluded

- current income-tax slabs, rebates, surcharge or cess rates;
- current corporate-tax rates;
- current GST rate slabs and item-wise rates;
- current customs tariff rates;
- current GST exemptions, threshold limits or composition-scheme limits;
- current GST Council meeting decisions or pending proposals;
- current cess rates or compensation arrangements;
- current filing dates/forms/compliance procedures;
- detailed tax administration and litigation procedure.

## Question architecture

12 QLs / 44 generated English review questions:

1. Taxation purpose and direct/indirect distinction — 4

3. Tax burden, shifting and incidence — 4
4. GST history and constitutional foundation — 4
5. Destination-based GST, value addition and input tax credit — 4
6. CGST and SGST/UTGST on intra-State supplies — 4
7. IGST and inter-State supplies — 4
8. GST Council and Articles 246A/269A/279A — 4
9. Customs duty basics — 3
10. Income tax and corporation tax as direct taxes — 3
11. Statement evaluation — 3
12. Mixed close distinctions — 3

Total: 44.

## Editorial rules

- Use short, natural SSC/Banking/State-exam stems.
- Normal MCQs must be complete questions ending in `?`; terminal-colon prompt fragments are not allowed.
- Explanations should normally use 1–2 clear sentences: deciding rule plus reason/application.
- Use plausible same-domain distractors.
- Difficulty is assigned per generated variant.
- Hard questions must test close distinctions or multi-fact reasoning, not current rates or obscure compliance trivia.
