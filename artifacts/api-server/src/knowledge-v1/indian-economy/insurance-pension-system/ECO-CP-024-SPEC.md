# ECO-CP-024 — Insurance & Pension System

## Coverage-gap authority

Added by the Economy exhaustive coverage-gap audit because the earlier financial-institutions specification explicitly deferred insurance and pension institutions, while later packs did not pick them up.

## Scope

- insurance as risk pooling;
- life versus general insurance;
- life-insurance nationalisation and LIC in 1956;
- general-insurance nationalisation effective 1 January 1973 and GIC;
- R. N. Malhotra Committee;
- IRDA Act, 1999 and IRDAI statutory framework;
- IRDAI regulation, policyholder protection, investment and solvency functions;
- reinsurance concept;
- Interim PFRDA in 2003;
- NPS introduction in 2004 and voluntary all-citizen extension in 2009;
- PFRDA Act, 2013 / statutory status from 2014;
- IRDAI versus PFRDA distinctions.

## Static boundary

Exclude current office-holders, current premium rates, current insurer rankings, current solvency numbers, live market shares, current NPS returns/assets and changing contribution or withdrawal limits.

## Review architecture

28 questions / 7 QLs. Difficulty is variant-based. Stems must be short exam-grade questions; explanations use the deciding fact and a short reason.
