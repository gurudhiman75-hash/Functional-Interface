### 1. Easy
Which crisis directly triggered India's major economic reforms in 1991?

A. Balance-of-payments crisis  
B. Agricultural surplus crisis  
C. Stock-market boom  
D. Bank nationalisation crisis

**Answer:** A. Balance-of-payments crisis

**Explanation:** India faced a severe balance-of-payments crisis in 1991. It became the immediate trigger for major reforms.

### 2. Medium
Why did India need urgent stabilisation measures in 1991?

A. To address the immediate external and macroeconomic crisis  
B. To introduce Five-Year Plans  
C. To nationalise all private firms  
D. To end foreign trade

**Answer:** A. To address the immediate external and macroeconomic crisis

**Explanation:** Stabilisation was needed to handle the immediate crisis. Structural reforms addressed deeper long-term problems.

### 4. Hard
Which statement correctly distinguishes liberalisation from globalisation?

A. Liberalisation reduces domestic controls, while globalisation increases integration with the world economy  
B. Both mean only sale of public-sector shares  
C. Globalisation means more domestic licensing  
D. Liberalisation means closing the economy to trade

**Answer:** A. Liberalisation reduces domestic controls, while globalisation increases integration with the world economy

**Explanation:** Liberalisation mainly reduces internal controls. Globalisation links the economy more closely with world trade and investment.

### 5. Easy
What was a major feature of the New Industrial Policy of 1991?

A. Industrial licensing was abolished for most industries  
B. Industrial licensing was extended to every industry  
C. Private industry was banned  
D. Foreign investment was completely prohibited

**Answer:** A. Industrial licensing was abolished for most industries

**Explanation:** The 1991 policy removed industrial licensing for most industries. Only a limited set remained under compulsory licensing.

### 6. Medium
What is meant by industrial delicensing?

A. Removing the need for government licences for most industries  
B. Closing all public-sector firms  
C. Banning imports  
D. Fixing all industrial prices by law

**Answer:** A. Removing the need for government licences for most industries

**Explanation:** Delicensing removed compulsory licences from most industries. It reduced administrative control over industrial decisions.
