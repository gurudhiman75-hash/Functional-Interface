# ECO-CP-016 — Economic Reforms of 1991

**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE ONLY  
**Runtime:** NOT REGISTERED

## Objective

Cover the stable exam-relevant facts and distinctions around India's 1991 economic reforms using short SSC/Banking/State-exam stems and simple 1-2 sentence explanations.

## Static scope

Included:
- 1991 balance-of-payments crisis as the immediate reform backdrop;
- stabilisation versus structural reform;
- LPG: liberalisation, privatisation and globalisation;
- New Industrial Policy, 1991;
- industrial delicensing and reduced administrative controls;
- change in public-sector role and disinvestment concept;
- foreign-investment liberalisation;
- trade liberalisation and reduction of quantitative restrictions;
- exchange-rate adjustment and movement toward a market-determined system;
- LERMS as a transitional dual-exchange-rate system;
- current-account convertibility milestone;
- Narasimham Committee and financial-sector reforms;
- FERA-to-FEMA direction as part of the liberalised foreign-exchange framework;
- close distinctions among liberalisation, privatisation and globalisation.

Excluded:
- current FDI caps or sector-specific automatic-route limits;
- current customs/tariff rates;
- current disinvestment targets or receipts;
- current exchange rate or reserve levels;
- current privatisation pipeline;
- current banking capital/CRR/SLR values;
- live trade restrictions, sanctions or policy notifications.

## Editorial rules

- Ask the tested fact directly.
- Avoid editorial framing and terminal-colon prompts.
- Normal stems should end in `?`.
- Explanations should normally be 1-2 short sentences.
- Hard questions must test genuine distinctions or chronology, not a single date.
