# ECO-CP-021 — International Economic Institutions

## Scope

Static exam-grade coverage of major international economic institutions and their functions.

Included:
- Bretton Woods background;
- IMF purpose, surveillance/lending basics and SDR concept;
- World Bank / IBRD / IDA distinctions;
- WTO establishment and core trade functions;
- ADB role and regional focus;
- AIIB role and infrastructure focus;
- New Development Bank origin and purpose;
- development-bank versus monetary/trade institution distinctions;
- institution-to-function and headquarters-level stable facts where useful.

Excluded:
- current office-holders;
- current membership counts;
- live quotas/voting shares;
- current lending volumes;
- current SDR valuation;
- current project values and rankings.

Editorial rules: short complete exam questions, plausible same-domain distractors, simple 1-2 sentence explanations, and genuine distinction-based Hard variants.
