# ECO-CP-023 — Important Economic Events & Milestones

## Purpose

Final Indian Economy consolidation pack covering major post-independence economic turning points and their chronology.

## Scope

- RBI nationalisation on 1 January 1949;
- Planning Commission establishment in 1950 and SBI constitution in 1955;
- nationalisation of 14 major commercial banks in 1969 and 6 more in 1980;
- NABARD establishment in 1982;
- 1991 balance-of-payments crisis and the shift to economic reforms;
- SEBI creation in 1988 and statutory status in 1992;
- exchange-rate reform sequence: LERMS 1992, unified market-determined rate 1993, current-account convertibility 1994;
- WTO membership from 1 January 1995;
- FRBM Act, 2003 and its commencement in 2004;
- NITI Aayog constitution on 1 January 2015, replacing the Planning Commission framework;
- Constitution (101st Amendment) Act, 2016 and GST launch on 1 July 2017;
- chronology, event-purpose and close milestone distinctions.

## Static boundary

Exclude current economic indicators, live policy rates, current bank ownership statistics, current WTO disputes, current FRBM targets, current GST rates/collections, current NITI office-holders, and other volatile figures.
