# ECO-CP-027 — Derivatives & Risk Management

## Coverage-gap authority

CP019 reserved deeper derivatives content but did not include even a stable recognition layer. This pack closes that omission without entering advanced pricing or trading strategy.

## Scope

- derivative and underlying concept;
- futures contracts;
- options and option premium;
- call and put options;
- basic hedging/risk-management use;
- futures/options/hedging distinctions and short applications.

## Static boundary

Exclude current derivatives turnover, margin rules, contract lot sizes, expiry calendars, position limits, current index levels, option-pricing formulas and trading recommendations.

## Review architecture

20 questions / 5 QLs.
