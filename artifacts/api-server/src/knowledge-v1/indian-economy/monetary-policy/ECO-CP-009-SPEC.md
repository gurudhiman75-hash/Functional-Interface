# ECO-CP-009 — Monetary Policy

**Chapter:** ECO-001 Indian Economy  
**CP:** ECO-CP-009 Monetary Policy  
**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE  
**Runtime registration:** blocked pending human approval

## Scope

Included:
- primary objective of monetary policy: price stability while keeping growth in mind;
- Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) statutory structure and voting basics;
- policy repo rate concept;
- Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) concept;
- Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) concept;
- Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) and corridor logic;
- Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) at concept level;
- Bank Rate at concept level;
- Open Market Operations (OMOs);
- liquidity injection versus absorption;
- expansionary/easing versus contractionary/tightening policy direction;
- basic transmission logic from policy/liquidity changes to borrowing, spending and inflation pressure;
- stable distinction between SDF floor, repo middle and MSF ceiling.

Excluded:
- current repo/SDF/MSF/Bank Rate/CRR/SLR values;
- current MPC stance or latest policy decision;
- current inflation target/tolerance numbers;
- names of current MPC members;
- current WACR or liquidity data;
- detailed variable-rate auction operations;
- detailed commercial-bank balance-sheet/credit-creation mechanics reserved for ECO-CP-010.

## Language rules

- Keep stems short and exam-like.
- Explanations should normally be one or two simple sentences.
- Give the deciding rule plus a short reason/application.
- Statement questions must explain why each relevant statement is true or false.
- Same-domain distractors only.
- Avoid manufacturing difficulty through long wording.

## Difficulty rule

Difficulty belongs to the generated variant, not permanently to the QL.

- **Easy:** direct instrument/function identification.
- **Medium:** one-step policy-direction or liquidity application.
- **Hard:** close instrument distinctions, corridor reasoning, or multi-link transmission inference.

A statement format or numerical/rate reference alone does not make an item Hard.
