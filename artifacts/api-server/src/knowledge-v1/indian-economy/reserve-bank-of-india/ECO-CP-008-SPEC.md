# ECO-CP-008 — Reserve Bank of India

## Scope

Included:
- RBI as India's central bank and monetary authority;
- Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 and commencement of operations on 1 April 1935;
- Central Office originally in Calcutta and permanently moved to Mumbai in 1937;
- original private ownership and nationalisation with effect from 1 January 1949;
- core monetary-authority role at concept level, without current policy settings;
- sole right to issue banknotes under Section 22 of the RBI Act, with the one-rupee-note exception kept conceptually clear;
- Government of India responsibility for minting coins and RBI's distribution role;
- banker to the Central Government and, by agreement, State Governments;
- public-debt-management role at introductory level;
- banker to banks and inter-bank settlement role;
- lender-of-last-resort concept;
- regulation and supervision of the banking/financial system at introductory level;
- manager of foreign exchange under FEMA, 1999 at role level;
- regulator/supervisor of payment and settlement systems at role level;
- developmental/promotional role at introductory level.
