# ECO-CP-017 — Agriculture & Indian Economy

## Purpose

Build a static-GK review pack on the economic structure, institutions and major policy concepts of Indian agriculture.

## Included scope

1. Economic role of agriculture and allied activities.
2. Land-reform concepts: abolition of intermediaries, tenancy reform, land ceilings and consolidation.
3. Green Revolution: HYV technology, irrigation, fertilisers, wheat/rice emphasis, food-security gains and regional concentration.
4. Kharif, Rabi and Zaid crop seasons at basic exam level.
5. Minimum Support Price (MSP) and Commission for Agricultural Costs & Prices (CACP).
6. Procurement, Food Corporation of India (FCI), buffer stocks and Public Distribution System (PDS).
7. Agricultural credit, cooperative/RRB channels and NABARD refinance role.
8. Crop insurance and PMFBY at concept level.
9. Agricultural marketing, APMC concept and e-NAM.
10. Productivity drivers: irrigation, quality seed, research, mechanisation and institutional credit.
11. Diversification into allied activities such as dairy, fisheries and horticulture.
12. Mixed statements and close distinctions.

## Static-GK boundary

Do not encode:
- current MSP amounts or current crop-wise procurement quantities;
- current agricultural growth rates, GVA shares, employment shares or rankings;
- current foodgrain/horticulture/milk/fish output values;
- current PMFBY premium rates, claim values, enrolment numbers or operational deadlines;
- current e-NAM mandi counts, trade values or live State coverage;
- current NABARD refinance amounts or credit targets;
- current buffer-stock quantities or issue prices;
- current scheme allocations or beneficiary counts.

## Editorial rules

- Use short SSC/Banking/State-exam style stems.
- Ordinary stems must be complete questions ending in `?`.
- Avoid editorial framing and terminal-colon prompts.
- Distractors must be plausible and from the same topic/domain.
- Explanations should normally be 1-2 short sentences: deciding fact plus minimum reason.
- Hard questions must test close distinctions, chronology or multi-concept application rather than simple date recall.

- 12 QLs
- 44 deterministic English questions
- Review-only; not runtime registered
