# Indian Economy — Per-Variant Difficulty Policy

This policy applies to every `ECO-CP-*` package.

## Core rule

Difficulty belongs to the generated question variant, not permanently to its QL.

A QL may therefore produce more than one difficulty band when the reasoning demand changes.

## Difficulty meaning

### Easy
- direct recognition or recall;
- one familiar concept with no close comparison;
- simple classification from an obvious example;
- no more than one direct relationship.

### Medium
- apply a concept to a short scenario;
- distinguish two related concepts;
- combine two classification dimensions;
- reverse a familiar relationship;
- perform one simple calculation after choosing the correct rule.

### Hard
- discriminate among closely related concepts;
- combine two or more relationships;
- evaluate statements where a near-miss distinction matters;
- classify a less obvious case using more than one dimension;
- infer the required aggregate/relationship before solving.

## Binding rules

1. `Hard` must not mean obscure trivia.
2. A statement-format question is not automatically Hard.
3. A direct-looking question is not automatically Easy if close concepts must be separated.
4. Numerical Economy questions remain concept-led and light; arithmetic alone does not make a question Hard.
5. Major concept families should expose more than one difficulty level where their logic supports it.
6. Difficulty assignment must be deterministic for the same generated variant.
7. Review QA must verify Easy, Medium and Hard coverage and at least one mixed-difficulty QL in conceptually rich CPs.
