### 1. Easy
Which activity is part of manufacturing?

A. Converting raw materials into finished or semi-finished goods  
B. Only buying and selling shares  
C. Only collecting taxes  
D. Only providing bank deposits

**Answer:** A. Converting raw materials into finished or semi-finished goods

**Explanation:** Manufacturing changes raw materials or components into goods through production processes.

### 2. Medium
How does industrial development support economic growth?

A. By raising value addition, employment and production capacity  
B. By ending all services  
C. By replacing agriculture completely  
D. By fixing every market price

**Answer:** A. By raising value addition, employment and production capacity

**Explanation:** Industry adds value to inputs and creates production capacity and jobs.

### 3. Easy
Which policy framework is closely linked with the mixed-economy industrial structure of the 1950s?

A. Industrial Policy Resolution, 1956  
B. New Industrial Policy, 1991  
C. SEZ Act, 2005  
D. MSMED Act, 2006

**Answer:** A. Industrial Policy Resolution, 1956

**Explanation:** The 1956 Industrial Policy Resolution structured industries around public, mixed and private roles.

### 4. Hard
Which comparison between the 1956 and 1991 industrial policies is correct?

A. 1956 stressed a larger public-sector role, while 1991 reduced controls and widened private participation  
B. 1956 abolished licensing, while 1991 expanded it to all industries  
C. Both policies reserved every industry for the public sector  
D. 1991 replaced private industry with central planning

**Answer:** A. 1956 stressed a larger public-sector role, while 1991 reduced controls and widened private participation

**Explanation:** The 1956 framework gave the State a larger industrial role. The 1991 policy reduced licensing and widened private participation.

### 5. Easy

**Explanation:** The 1991 policy removed compulsory licensing for most industries.

### 11. Hard
Which statement correctly distinguishes IIP from GDP?

A. IIP tracks industrial production volume, while GDP measures value added across the economy

**Answer:** A. IIP tracks industrial production volume, while GDP measures value added across the economy

**Explanation:** IIP is a production-volume index for industry. GDP is a broader measure of economic value added.
