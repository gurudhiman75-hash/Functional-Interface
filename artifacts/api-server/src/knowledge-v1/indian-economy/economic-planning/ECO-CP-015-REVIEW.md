### 1. Easy · Early planning
Which body was set up by the Indian National Congress in 1938 as an early planning initiative?

A. National Planning Committee  
B. National Development Council  
C. Planning Commission  
D. NITI Aayog

**Answer:** A. National Planning Committee

**Explanation:** The National Planning Committee was set up in 1938, well before the post-independence Five-Year Plan system began.

---

### 2. Medium · Planning Commission origin
How was the Planning Commission established in 1950?

A. By a Government of India Resolution  
B. By a constitutional amendment  
C. By an Act of Parliament creating a statutory body  
D. By a Reserve Bank notification

**Answer:** A. By a Government of India Resolution

**Explanation:** The Planning Commission was created by Government Resolution on 15 March 1950. It was not created by the Constitution or a separate statute.

### 3. Hard · Planning Commission vs NDC
Which distinction between the Planning Commission and the National Development Council is correct?

A. The Commission formulated and reviewed plans, while the NDC served as a broader Centre-State development forum  
B. The NDC issued currency, while the Commission regulated banks  
C. The Commission was a court, while the NDC was a tax authority  
D. Both institutions performed only monetary-policy functions

**Answer:** A. The Commission formulated and reviewed plans, while the NDC served as a broader Centre-State development forum

**Explanation:** The Planning Commission was the apex advisory planning body, whereas the NDC was designed as a forum for Centre-State cooperation around national development and Plan implementation.

### 4. Easy · First Plan
Which sector received the strongest emphasis in the First Five-Year Plan?

A. Agriculture  
B. Heavy basic industry  
C. Information technology  
D. Capital-market liberalisation

**Answer:** A. Agriculture

**Explanation:** Food shortage and inflation made agriculture a central priority of the First Plan, along with price stability, power and transport.

### 5. Hard · First vs Second Plan
A question contrasts an agriculture-first plan with a later heavy-industry plan. Which pairing is correct?

A. First Plan—agriculture; Second Plan—heavy and basic industries  
B. First Plan—heavy industry; Second Plan—agriculture only  
C. First Plan—liberalisation; Second Plan—Rolling Plan  
D. First Plan—NITI Aayog; Second Plan—Plan Holiday

**Answer:** A. First Plan—agriculture; Second Plan—heavy and basic industries

**Explanation:** The First Plan responded to food and agricultural constraints, while the Second Plan shifted strongly toward rapid industrialisation and basic and heavy industries.

### 7. Easy · Third Plan
What broad objective was central to the Third Five-Year Plan?

A. Building a self-reliant and self-generating economy  
B. Ending all public-sector investment  
C. Creating NITI Aayog  
D. Introducing GST

**Answer:** A. Building a self-reliant and self-generating economy

**Explanation:** The Third Plan aimed to move the economy toward self-reliance and self-generation, while giving renewed importance to agriculture.

### 8. Medium · Plan Holiday
What does the term Plan Holiday refer to in Indian planning history?

A. The three Annual Plans from 1966 to 1969  
B. The entire period from 1951 to 1956  
C. The Twelfth Plan period  
D. The NITI Aayog period after 2015

**Answer:** A. The three Annual Plans from 1966 to 1969

**Explanation:** After the Third Plan, India used three Annual Plans during 1966-69 before the Fourth Plan began in 1969. This interval is commonly called the Plan Holiday.

### 9. Hard · Third-to-Fourth transition
Which sequence correctly describes the transition after the Third Plan?

A. Third Plan ended in 1966 → three Annual Plans → Fourth Plan began in 1969  
B. Third Plan ended in 1966 → NITI Aayog began → Fourth Plan began in 2015  
C. Third Plan ended in 1966 → Rolling Plan began immediately → Fifth Plan began in 1969  
D. Third Plan ended in 1966 → Eighth Plan began → Fourth Plan followed

**Answer:** A. Third Plan ended in 1966 → three Annual Plans → Fourth Plan began in 1969

**Explanation:** The normal Five-Year cycle paused after the Third Plan. Three Annual Plans covered 1966-69, after which the Fourth Plan started in 1969.

### 12. Hard · Rolling Plan
