# ECO-CP-015 — Economic Planning in India

## Purpose

Build a Static-GK question pool on the evolution, institutions, chronology and major themes of economic planning in India, from pre-independence planning ideas through the Planning Commission/Five-Year Plan era to NITI Aayog.

## Included scope

- pre-independence planning milestones at introductory exam level;
- Planning Commission establishment on 15 March 1950 by Government Resolution;
- Planning Commission's advisory/resource-assessment/plan-formulation role;
- National Development Council as a Centre-State planning forum from 1952;
- First Five-Year Plan (1951-56): agriculture, price stability, power/transport and Harrod-Domar framework;
- Second Plan (1956-61): Mahalanobis strategy and heavy/basic industry;
- Third Plan (1961-66): self-reliant/self-generating economy and renewed agriculture priority;
- Annual Plans of 1966-69 / Plan Holiday;
- Fourth Plan (1969-74): growth with stability and progressive self-reliance;
- Fifth Plan (1974-79): removal of poverty and self-reliance; early termination in 1978;
- Rolling Plan period (1978-80);
- Sixth Plan (1980-85): poverty/unemployment reduction, technology modernisation and expanding economy;
- Seventh Plan (1985-90): food, work and productivity;
- Annual Plans of 1990-91 and 1991-92 before the Eighth Plan;
- Eighth Plan (1992-97) in the post-1991 reform setting;
- Ninth Plan (1997-2002): growth with social justice and equality and a more facilitative State role;
- Tenth Plan (2002-07): monitorable development targets and broader human-development outcomes;
- Eleventh Plan (2007-12): faster and more inclusive growth;
- Twelfth Plan (2012-17): faster, more inclusive and sustainable growth;
- transition from Planning Commission to NITI Aayog on 1 January 2015;
- NITI Aayog's strategic-policy/think-tank and cooperative-federalism role;
- centralised Five-Year planning versus increasingly indicative planning;
- chronology, close distinctions and mixed reasoning across planning phases.

## Explicit exclusions

- current NITI Aayog Vice-Chairperson, CEO, members or special invitees;
- current Governing Council attendance, meeting count or latest agenda;
- current NITI schemes, rankings, indices or live programme metrics;
- plan target/actual growth-rate percentages and historical rupee outlays as rote-value questions;
- detailed 1991 reform measures reserved for ECO-CP-016;
- detailed agriculture/industry policy reserved for ECO-CP-017 and ECO-CP-018.

## Review architecture

- 12 QLs;
- 44 deterministic English review questions;
- Easy/Medium/Hard assigned per generated variant;
- complete exam-question stems ending in `?` for ordinary MCQs;
- same-domain plausible distractors;
- explanations use deciding fact plus short reason/application;
- runtime registration remains blocked until separate promotion approval.
