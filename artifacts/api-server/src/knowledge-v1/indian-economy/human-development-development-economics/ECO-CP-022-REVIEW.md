### 1. Easy
What is the main focus of human development?

A. Increasing national income alone  
B. Expanding people's choices and capabilities  
C. Increasing exports alone  
D. Reducing taxes alone

**Answer:** B. Expanding people's choices and capabilities

**Explanation:** Human development focuses on people's choices, capabilities and opportunities. Income is only one part of development.

**Answer:** C. Mahbub ul Haq

**Explanation:** Mahbub ul Haq is closely associated with the human-development approach. Amartya Sen's capability ideas strongly influenced it.

### 8. Hard

### 10. Hard
Can an economy grow without an equal improvement in human development?

A. No, because HDI measures only GDP  
B. No, growth and human development are always identical

**Answer:** C. Yes, if income rises but health, education or opportunities improve little

### 12. Hard

### 13. Easy
What does a Lorenz curve show?

A. The exchange rate over time  
B. The distribution of income or consumption across the population  
C. The inflation rate over time  
D. The tax rate on companies

**Answer:** B. The distribution of income or consumption across the population

**Explanation:** A Lorenz curve compares cumulative population shares with cumulative income or consumption shares.

### 14. Hard

**Explanation:** A larger gap from the equality line indicates greater inequality and a higher Gini measure.
