# ECO-CP-022 — Human Development & Development Economics

## Scope

Static-GK treatment of human development and core development-economics indicators.

### Included

1. Human development concept and 1990 Human Development Report.
2. Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen's capability influence.
3. HDI dimensions: health, education and standard of living.
4. HDI indicators: life expectancy at birth, mean years of schooling, expected years of schooling and GNI per capita.
5. HDI as a composite measure and its distinction from GDP/income alone.
6. Economic growth versus broader economic development.
7. GNI per capita and purchasing-power-parity comparison logic.
8. Lorenz curve and Gini index.
9. Absolute versus relative poverty.
10. Multidimensional Poverty Index: health, education and living standards.
11. Human capital and capability/development distinctions.
12. Applied statement and mixed-indicator questions.

## Static boundary

Exclude:
- current HDI ranks, values or country classifications;
- current MPI values, poverty headcounts or country rankings;
- current GNI per-capita figures;
- current Gini values;
- current poverty lines or dollar thresholds;
- current life-expectancy or schooling values;
- current UNDP/World Bank annual rankings.

## Editorial rules

- 12 QLs / 44 deterministic English review questions.
- Review only; no runtime registration.
- Short SSC/Banking/State-exam style stems.
- Ask the tested fact directly; do not put the explanation in the stem.
- Ordinary stems end in `?`; no terminal-colon fragments.
- Explanations normally use 1-2 short sentences.
- Hard variants test close distinctions or applied interpretation, not verbosity.
