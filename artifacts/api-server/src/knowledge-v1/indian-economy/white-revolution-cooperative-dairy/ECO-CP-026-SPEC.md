# ECO-CP-026 — White Revolution & Cooperative Dairy

## Coverage-gap authority

Added because CP017 covered Green Revolution, crop policy and allied activities but did not cover the White Revolution, Operation Flood, NDDB or the cooperative dairy transformation.

## Scope

- NDDB origin in 1965;
- Operation Flood launch in 1970 and three-phase chronology;
- production, rural-income and consumer-price objectives;
- National Milk Grid;
- village producer cooperatives and Anand model;
- White Revolution;
- Dr. Verghese Kurien;
- chronology and institution-function distinctions.

## Static boundary

Exclude live milk-output figures, current cooperative counts, current procurement values, current dairy-scheme allocations and current office-holders.

## Review architecture

20 questions / 5 QLs.
