# ECO-CP-028 — Fiscal Federalism & Finance Commission

## Coverage-gap authority

Public Finance and Budget packs covered deficits, funds and Union Budget procedure, but did not substantively cover Union-State fiscal transfers or the constitutional Finance Commission.

## Scope

- Article 280;
- Presidential constitution of the Finance Commission;
- Chairman plus four members;
- five-year periodicity or earlier constitution;
- Union-State distribution of divisible net tax proceeds;
- allocation among States;
- grants-in-aid principles;
- augmentation of State funds for Panchayats and Municipalities;
- other matters referred in the interests of sound finance;
- Article 281 parliamentary laying requirement;
- Finance Commission versus GST Council/monetary-policy distinctions.

## Static boundary

Exclude current Finance Commission membership, current award percentages, current grants, current terms-of-reference details and live State allocations.

## Review architecture

24 questions / 6 QLs.
