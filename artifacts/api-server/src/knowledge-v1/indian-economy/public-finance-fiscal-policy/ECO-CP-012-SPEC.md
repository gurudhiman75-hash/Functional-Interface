# ECO-CP-012 — Public Finance & Fiscal Policy

## Objective

Build a Static-GK question pool on public finance and fiscal policy using stable concepts, classifications and deficit relationships rather than current Budget figures or live deficit ratios.

## Included

- public finance meaning and Government revenue/expenditure/borrowing;
- fiscal policy meaning and objectives;
- expansionary and contractionary fiscal policy;
- tax and non-tax revenue at concept level;
- revenue receipts versus capital receipts;
- debt-creating versus non-debt capital receipts;
- revenue expenditure versus capital expenditure;
- fiscal deficit, revenue deficit and primary deficit;
- non-debt receipts and Government borrowing requirement;
- public debt and interest burden at introductory level;
- FRBM Act, 2003: fiscal discipline, transparency, debt/deficit sustainability and medium-term framework;
- automatic stabilisers at introductory level;
- simple one-step deficit calculations;
- close concept distinctions and statement questions.

## Excluded

- current fiscal deficit/revenue deficit/primary deficit ratios or rupee values;
- current public-debt ratio;
- current Budget allocations, subsidies or tax collections;
- current FRBM numerical targets/escape-clause values;
- current tax rates or slabs;
- detailed Union Budget procedure/documents reserved for ECO-CP-013;
- detailed tax-system classification reserved for ECO-CP-014.

## Question architecture

12 QLs / 44 generated English review questions:

1. Public finance and fiscal-policy concepts — 4
2. Expansionary versus contractionary fiscal policy — 4
3. Revenue receipts and capital receipts — 4
4. Revenue expenditure and capital expenditure — 4
5. Fiscal deficit concept/application — 4
6. Revenue deficit concept/application — 4
7. Primary deficit concept/application — 4
8. Debt/non-debt receipts and public borrowing — 4
9. FRBM concept and purpose — 3
10. Automatic stabilisers — 3
11. Statement evaluation — 3
12. Mixed deficit/classification distinctions — 3

Total: 44.

## Editorial rules

- Use short, natural SSC/Banking/State-exam stems.
- Explanations should normally use 1–2 clear sentences: deciding rule plus reason/application.
- Numerical explanations must show the short working.
- Do not merely repeat the answer.
- Use plausible same-domain distractors.
- Difficulty is assigned per generated variant, not per QL.
- Hard questions must require close classification or multi-relationship reasoning; simple arithmetic alone is not Hard.
- Current values, live Budget data and changing statutory targets must not enter the static pool.

## Runtime

Review-only until English approval and a separate runtime/Question Studio promotion decision.
