# ECO-CP-002 — Economic Systems & Sectors
## Human Review Set V1

**Status:** REVIEW ONLY — not runtime registered

### 1
An economy where public and private sectors work together is called:

A. Capitalist economy  
B. Socialist economy  
C. Mixed economy  
D. Closed economy

**Answer:** C. Mixed economy

**Explanation:** A mixed economy has both public and private sectors.

### 2
Private ownership and a major role for markets are features of:

A. Capitalist economy  
B. Socialist economy  
C. Mixed economy  
D. Subsistence economy

**Answer:** A. Capitalist economy

**Explanation:** A capitalist economy mainly relies on private ownership and markets.

### 3
State ownership or control with planned production is mainly linked with:

A. Mixed economy  
B. Socialist economy  
C. Capitalist economy  
D. Informal economy

**Answer:** B. Socialist economy

**Explanation:** In a socialist economy, the state has a major role in ownership and planning.

### 4
Growing wheat belongs to:

A. Primary sector  
B. Secondary sector  
C. Tertiary sector  
D. Public sector

**Answer:** A. Primary sector

**Explanation:** Farming uses natural resources directly, so it is part of the primary sector.

### 5
Extracting coal from a mine belongs to:

A. Tertiary sector  
B. Secondary sector  
C. Primary sector  
D. Private sector

**Answer:** C. Primary sector

**Explanation:** Mining extracts natural resources, so it is a primary-sector activity.

### 6
Making sugar from sugarcane belongs to:

A. Primary sector  
B. Secondary sector  
C. Tertiary sector  
D. Public sector

**Answer:** B. Secondary sector

**Explanation:** Sugarcane is processed into sugar, so the activity is secondary.

### 7
Producing steel in a plant belongs to:

A. Secondary sector  
B. Primary sector  
C. Tertiary sector  
D. Unorganised sector

**Answer:** A. Secondary sector

**Explanation:** Manufacturing belongs to the secondary sector.

### 8
Providing banking services belongs to:

A. Primary sector  
B. Secondary sector  
C. Tertiary sector  
D. Public sector only

**Answer:** C. Tertiary sector

**Explanation:** Banking provides a service, so it is tertiary.

### 9
Transporting goods by truck belongs to:

A. Secondary sector  
B. Tertiary sector  
C. Primary sector  
D. Private sector only

**Answer:** B. Tertiary sector

**Explanation:** Transport is a service activity.

### 10
A government-owned enterprise belongs to:

A. Private sector  
B. Public sector  
C. Primary sector  
D. Tertiary sector

**Answer:** B. Public sector

**Explanation:** Public and private sectors are based on ownership. Government ownership means public sector.

### 11
A factory owned by private individuals belongs to:

A. Public sector  
B. Private sector  
C. Primary sector  
D. Organised sector only

**Answer:** B. Private sector

**Explanation:** Private ownership makes it part of the private sector.

### 12
A worker in a registered factory with formal service rules works in the:

A. Organised sector  
B. Unorganised sector  
C. Primary sector  
D. Public sector

**Answer:** A. Organised sector

**Explanation:** Formal and recorded employment conditions are features of the organised sector.

### 13
A casual worker hired day to day without formal service benefits works in the:

A. Public sector  
B. Organised sector  
C. Unorganised sector  
D. Secondary sector only

**Answer:** C. Unorganised sector

**Explanation:** The job lacks formal service conditions, so it is unorganised.

### 14
A privately owned hospital provides health services. Which classification is correct?

A. Tertiary sector; Private sector  
B. Primary sector; Private sector  
C. Tertiary sector; Public sector  
D. Secondary sector; Public sector

**Answer:** A. Tertiary sector; Private sector

**Explanation:** Health care is a service, while ownership is private.

### 15
A government-owned transport service is correctly classified as:

A. Primary sector; Public sector  
B. Tertiary sector; Public sector  
C. Tertiary sector; Private sector  
D. Secondary sector; Public sector

**Answer:** B. Tertiary sector; Public sector

**Explanation:** Transport is tertiary. Government ownership makes it public sector.

### 16
Which pair is correctly matched?

A. Primary sector — banking  
B. Secondary sector — steel production  
C. Tertiary sector — mining  
D. Primary sector — retail trade

**Answer:** B. Secondary sector — steel production

**Explanation:** Steel production is manufacturing, so it belongs to the secondary sector.

### 17
Which pair is correctly matched?

A. Tertiary sector — hospital services  
B. Primary sector — textile factory  
C. Secondary sector — fishing  
D. Tertiary sector — coal mining

**Answer:** A. Tertiary sector — hospital services

**Explanation:** Health care is a service and belongs to the tertiary sector.

### 18
Which pair is incorrectly matched?

A. Primary sector — fishing  
B. Secondary sector — sugar manufacturing  
C. Tertiary sector — banking  
D. Primary sector — steel production

**Answer:** D. Primary sector — steel production

**Explanation:** Steel production is a secondary-sector activity.

### 19
Consider the statements:

I. Farming belongs to the primary sector.  
II. Banking belongs to the tertiary sector.

Which is correct?

A. I only  
B. II only  
C. Both I and II  
D. Neither I nor II

**Answer:** C. Both I and II

**Explanation:** Farming is primary, while banking is tertiary.

### 20
Consider the statements:

I. Manufacturing belongs to the secondary sector.  
II. Mining belongs to the tertiary sector.

Which is correct?

A. I only  
B. II only  
C. Both I and II  
D. Neither I nor II

**Answer:** A. I only

**Explanation:** Manufacturing is secondary, but mining is primary.

### 21
Which statement is correct?

A. Primary and public mean the same thing.  
B. Tertiary and private mean the same thing.  
C. Primary, secondary and tertiary classify activity; public and private classify ownership.  
D. Ownership decides whether an activity is primary or tertiary.

**Answer:** C. Primary, secondary and tertiary classify activity; public and private classify ownership.

**Explanation:** These are two different classification systems.

### 22
Which statement is correct?

A. Every service belongs to the public sector.  
B. A service can be in either the public or private sector.  
C. Every private activity is secondary.  
D. Every government activity is primary.

**Answer:** B. A service can be in either the public or private sector.

**Explanation:** Tertiary tells the type of activity. Public/private tells ownership.

### 23
Which statement is correct?

A. Organised means public sector only.  
B. Unorganised means primary sector only.  
C. Organised and unorganised describe work conditions.  
D. Organised and tertiary mean the same thing.

**Answer:** C. Organised and unorganised describe work conditions.

**Explanation:** They classify employment conditions, not the type of economic activity.

### 24
A street vendor working outside formal employment is generally placed in the:

A. Organised sector  
B. Unorganised sector  
C. Public sector  
D. Secondary sector

**Answer:** B. Unorganised sector

**Explanation:** The work is outside a formal employment structure.
