# ECO-CP-002 — Economic Systems & Sectors

**Chapter:** ECO-001 Indian Economy  
**CP:** ECO-CP-002 Economic Systems & Sectors  
**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE  
**Runtime registration:** blocked pending human approval  
**Languages:** English review surface; facts remain locale-neutral.

## Scope

This CP covers:
- capitalist, socialist and mixed economies
- primary, secondary and tertiary sectors
- public and private sectors
- organised and unorganised sectors
- formal and informal work at basic exam level
- classification through common occupations and economic activities

## Design rules

1. Stems must be short and direct.
2. Explanations should usually be one or two short sentences.
3. Sector questions should use realistic work/activity examples, not only definitions.
4. System questions should test the main organising feature, not political ideology.
5. Distractors must come from the same classification family.
6. Do not treat every government activity as primary-sector activity; ownership and activity are separate classifications.
7. Do not treat every service as public-sector activity; tertiary and public/private are separate classifications.
8. Current employment shares or GDP shares are excluded from Static GK.
9. Review items remain `runtimeRegistered: false` until approval.
