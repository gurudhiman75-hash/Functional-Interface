# ECO-CP-020 — External Sector & Balance of Payments

## Objective
Build a Static-GK review pool on India's external sector and balance of payments using durable concepts only.

## Scope
- Balance of Payments meaning and structure
- current account: goods, services, income and transfers
- trade balance versus current-account balance
- visibles versus invisibles
- capital/financial flows: direct investment, portfolio investment, loans and banking capital
- FDI versus FPI
- foreign-exchange reserves and BoP adjustment
- exchange-rate appreciation/depreciation
- devaluation/revaluation versus market movement
- current-account convertibility and India's 1994 milestone
- LERMS/unified market-determined exchange-rate transition at introductory level
- export/import effects of currency movement as directional concepts

## Excluded volatile content
- current rupee exchange rate
- current forex-reserve amount
- current CAD/trade-deficit/BoP values
- current FDI/FPI inflows
- current export/import values or rankings
- live intervention amounts or exchange-rate forecasts

## Review architecture
44 questions across 12 QLs:
1. BoP meaning and structure — 3
2. Current account — 4
3. Trade balance versus current account — 4
4. Invisibles: services, income and transfers — 4
5. Capital/financial flows — 4
6. FDI versus FPI — 4
7. Exchange-rate appreciation/depreciation — 4
8. Devaluation/revaluation distinction — 3
9. Forex reserves and BoP adjustment — 4
10. Convertibility and 1994 milestone — 3
11. LERMS/exchange-rate reform chronology — 3
12. Mixed statements and applied distinctions — 4

## Editorial rules
Use short SSC/Banking/State-exam stems ending in `?`. Avoid review-like framing and terminal-colon prompts. Explanations should normally be 1–2 short sentences containing the deciding fact and minimum reason.
