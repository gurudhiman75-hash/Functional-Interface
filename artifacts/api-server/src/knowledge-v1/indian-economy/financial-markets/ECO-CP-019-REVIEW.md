### 1. Easy
Which market mainly deals in short-term funds and instruments?

A. Money market  
B. Capital market  
C. Commodity spot market  
D. Real-estate market

**Answer:** A. Money market

**Explanation:** The money market mainly deals in short-term funds and instruments.

### 2. Hard
A company needs long-term ownership capital, while a bank needs overnight liquidity. Which markets fit these needs?

A. Capital market for the company; money market for the bank  
B. Money market for both  
C. Capital market for both  
D. Money market for the company; capital market for the bank

**Answer:** A. Capital market for the company; money market for the bank

**Explanation:** Long-term ownership funds come from the capital market. Overnight liquidity belongs to the money market.

### 3. Easy
Where are new securities first issued to investors?

A. Primary market  
B. Secondary market  
C. Call-money market  
D. Repo market

**Answer:** A. Primary market

**Explanation:** New securities are issued by the issuer in the primary market.

### 4. Hard
Which statement correctly distinguishes the primary and secondary markets?

A. The primary market raises new funds for issuers, while the secondary market provides trading in existing securities  
B. Both markets only trade existing securities  
C. The secondary market alone creates new company shares  
D. The primary market is only for government taxes

**Answer:** A. The primary market raises new funds for issuers, while the secondary market provides trading in existing securities

**Explanation:** The primary market is for new issues. The secondary market provides liquidity and trading after issue.

### 5. Easy
What does an equity share represent?

A. Ownership in a company  
B. A fixed bank deposit  
C. A government tax claim  
D. A short-term interbank loan

**Answer:** A. Ownership in a company

**Explanation:** An equity share represents an ownership interest in the issuing company.

### 6. Medium
Which statement correctly compares equity and debt securities?

A. Equity represents ownership, while debt represents a repayment obligation  
B. Both always provide fixed returns  
C. Debt gives ownership voting rights by definition  
D. Equity must be repaid at a fixed maturity

**Answer:** A. Equity represents ownership, while debt represents a repayment obligation

**Explanation:** Equity is ownership capital. Debt creates a contractual claim for repayment.

### 12. Medium
What does NAV represent in a mutual fund?

A. The per-unit value of the scheme  
B. The stock exchange index level

**Explanation:** NAV is the per-unit value of a mutual-fund scheme after accounting for assets and liabilities.

### 13. Hard
Why can a mutual fund help an investor diversify?

A. It can spread money across several securities or asset classes  
B. It guarantees that no investment can lose value

### 14. Easy
Who issues Treasury Bills in India?

A. Government of India  
B. Private companies  
C. Mutual funds  
D. Stock exchanges

**Answer:** A. Government of India

**Explanation:** Treasury Bills are short-term debt instruments issued by the Government of India.

### 15. Medium
How do Treasury Bills normally provide a return to investors?

A. They are issued at a discount and redeemed at face value  
B. They pay equity dividends  
C. They provide voting rights  
D. They are redeemed below their issue price by design

**Answer:** A. They are issued at a discount and redeemed at face value

**Explanation:** T-bills are zero-coupon securities. The return comes from the difference between issue price and face value.

### 16. Medium
Which statement correctly distinguishes a Treasury Bill from a dated Government security?

A. A Treasury Bill is short-term and zero-coupon, while a dated G-Sec has a longer maturity  
B. Both are equity shares  
C. Dated G-Secs are issued only by private firms  
D. Treasury Bills give company ownership

**Answer:** A. A Treasury Bill is short-term and zero-coupon, while a dated G-Sec has a longer maturity

**Explanation:** T-bills are short-term zero-coupon Government instruments. Dated G-Secs are longer-term Government debt securities.

### 19. Hard
Which comparison is correct?

A. Commercial Paper is unsecured corporate short-term borrowing, while a Certificate of Deposit is issued by eligible banks or financial institutions  
B. Commercial Paper gives company ownership, while a Certificate of Deposit gives voting rights  
C. Both are long-term equity instruments  
D. Certificates of Deposit are issued only by stock exchanges

**Answer:** A. Commercial Paper is unsecured corporate short-term borrowing, while a Certificate of Deposit is issued by eligible banks or financial institutions

**Explanation:** CP is an unsecured short-term borrowing instrument. CDs are negotiable money-market instruments issued by eligible institutions.

### 20. Hard
Which statement correctly distinguishes repo from call money?

A. Repo is secured by securities, while call money is unsecured  
B. Call money is secured by equity shares, while repo is always unsecured  
C. Both are long-term capital-market instruments  
D. Repo creates company ownership, while call money creates voting rights

**Answer:** A. Repo is secured by securities, while call money is unsecured

**Explanation:** Repo uses securities as collateral support. Call money is an unsecured short-term market.

### 21. Easy
What is an Initial Public Offer?

A. A company's first public issue of shares  
B. Trading of old shares between investors  
C. An overnight bank loan  
D. A Treasury Bill auction

**Answer:** A. A company's first public issue of shares

**Explanation:** An IPO is the first public issue of a company's shares in the primary market.

### 22. Hard
A company raises money through an IPO, and its shares later trade on an exchange. Which sequence is correct?

A. Primary market first, secondary market later  
B. Secondary market first, primary market later  
C. Money market first, repo market later  
D. Call market first, primary market later

**Answer:** A. Primary market first, secondary market later

**Explanation:** The IPO raises funds in the primary market. Later trading takes place in the secondary market.

### 23. Medium
Consider the statements. I. New securities are sold in the primary market. II. Existing securities are traded in the secondary market. Which option is correct?

A. I only  
B. II only  
C. Both I and II  
D. Neither I nor II

**Answer:** C. Both I and II

**Explanation:** Both statements are correct. The primary market handles new issues, while the secondary market handles later trading.

### 24. Hard
Which combination correctly matches the instrument with its main market feature?

A. Equity—ownership; T-bill—short-term Government debt; CP—unsecured short-term borrowing; repo—secured short-term funding  
B. Equity—Government debt; T-bill—company ownership; CP—secured long-term bond; repo—mutual fund  
C. Equity—bank deposit; T-bill—equity share; CP—government tax; repo—depository  
D. Equity—call loan; T-bill—mutual fund unit; CP—stock index; repo—IPO

**Answer:** A. Equity—ownership; T-bill—short-term Government debt; CP—unsecured short-term borrowing; repo—secured short-term funding

**Explanation:** The four instruments serve different roles: ownership, Government short-term debt, unsecured borrowing and secured funding.
