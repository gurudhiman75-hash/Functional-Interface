# ECO-CP-019 — Financial Markets

## Scope

Static-GK review pack for the structure, institutions and major instruments of Indian financial markets.

Included:
- money market versus capital market;
- primary versus secondary securities market;
- equity shares versus debt securities;
- SEBI role and statutory status;
- stock exchanges and basic trading-market role;
- demat accounts, depositories and Depository Participants;
- mutual funds, diversification and NAV basics;
- Government securities, dated G-Secs and Treasury Bills;
- Commercial Paper, Certificates of Deposit and call/notice money basics;
- repo as a secured short-term funding transaction;
- IPO/public issue basics and primary-market fund raising;
- risk, return, liquidity and diversification concepts;
- mixed institutional/instrument distinctions.

Excluded:
- live Sensex/Nifty/index levels;
- current bond yields, repo rate or money-market rates;
- current mutual-fund NAVs, returns, AUM or rankings;
- current market capitalisation, IPO proceeds or trading volumes;
- current CP/CD outstanding amounts;
- live G-Sec auction cut-offs;
- current listing, settlement or issue-size thresholds that can change;
- derivatives detail beyond basic recognition; reserve deeper derivatives content for a later specialist pack if needed.

## Editorial rules

- Use short SSC/Banking/State-exam style stems.
- Ordinary stems must be complete questions ending in `?`.
- Avoid prompt fragments ending in `:` and review-like framing.
- Explanations should normally be 1-2 short sentences with the deciding fact and minimum reason.
- Easy questions may test direct concepts; Medium questions should test application/classification; Hard questions must use close distinctions, multi-instrument comparison or statement logic rather than simple recall.
