# ECO-CP-011 — Financial Institutions

## Objective

Build a Static-GK question pool on major Indian development financial institutions and NBFC basics, with stable institutional roles and history rather than current office-holders, current balance-sheet values or live scheme data.

## Included

- Development Financial Institution (DFI) concept;
- IFCI as India's first DFI and its 1948 origin;
- NABARD: 1982, agriculture/rural development, refinance and institutional-development role;
- SIDBI: 1990, MSME promotion/finance/development;
- Export-Import Bank of India: Act 1981, operations 1982, export-import finance and coordination;
- National Housing Bank: Act 1987, establishment 1988, housing-finance development role;
- transfer of HFC regulatory powers from NHB to RBI in 2019 as a stable institutional distinction;
- NaBFID: 2021, long-term infrastructure-finance role and market-development role;
- NBFC basic meaning and bank-vs-NBFC distinction;
- direct institution-to-sector matching;
- close distinctions and statement questions.

## Excluded

- current heads/chairpersons/MDs;
- current authorised/paid-up capital;
- latest loan sanctions/disbursements/profits;
- latest institution rankings or market shares;
- current scheme names, allocations or beneficiaries;
- current NBFC asset-size thresholds and dynamic regulatory layers;
- detailed insurance/pension/mutual-fund institutions reserved for later financial-market coverage;
- international institutions reserved for ECO-CP-021.

## Editorial rules

- Use short, natural SSC/Banking/State-exam stems.
- Explanations should normally use 1–2 clear sentences: deciding rule plus reason/application.
- Do not merely repeat the answer.
- Use plausible same-domain distractors.
- Difficulty is per generated variant, not per QL.
- Hard questions must test close institutional distinctions, multi-fact sequence or role changes—not obscure trivia or long wording.
- Current values, current leaders and volatile regulatory thresholds must not enter the static pool.

## Runtime

Review-only until English approval and a separate runtime/Question Studio promotion decision.
