### 1. Easy · DFI concept
What is the main purpose of a Development Financial Institution?

A. Provide or catalyse long-term development finance  
B. Issue currency notes  
C. Collect income tax  
D. Run stock exchanges

**Answer:** A. Provide or catalyse long-term development finance

**Explanation:** A DFI is designed to fill long-term financing gaps in development-oriented sectors. It is not a currency-issuing or tax-collection institution.

---

### 2. Medium · DFI distinction
Which feature best separates a DFI from an ordinary retail-banking role?

A. Only accepting salary accounts  
B. Specialised development and long-term financing focus  
C. Only issuing debit cards  
D. Only handling cash withdrawals

**Answer:** B. Specialised development and long-term financing focus

**Explanation:** The deciding feature is the specialised development-finance mandate. Ordinary retail banking focuses more directly on routine deposits, payments and customer loans.

### 3. Easy · IFCI
Which institution was established in 1948 as India's first Development Financial Institution?

A. NABARD  
B. SIDBI  
C. IFCI  
D. NaBFID

**Answer:** C. IFCI

**Explanation:** IFCI was established on 1 July 1948 as India's first DFI. Its original purpose was to meet long-term finance needs of industry.

### 4. Medium · IFCI role
IFCI was originally created mainly to meet which need?

A. Retail payment services  
B. Long-term finance for industry  
C. Crop insurance only  
D. Currency-note printing

**Answer:** B. Long-term finance for industry

**Explanation:** IFCI was created when long-term industrial finance was scarce. Its original development role was therefore tied to industry rather than ordinary retail banking.

### 19. Easy · NBFC
An NBFC is best described as:

A. Any manufacturing company  
B. A company principally engaged in specified financial activities but not a banking company  
C. A department of RBI  
D. A stock exchange only

**Answer:** B. A company principally engaged in specified financial activities but not a banking company

**Explanation:** NBFCs are companies whose principal business is financial activity such as lending or investment. They are not banking companies merely because they provide finance.

---

### 20. Hard · NBFC vs bank
Why should an NBFC not automatically be called a commercial bank?

A. It cannot conduct any financial activity  
B. It is always a government department  
C. It is a non-banking financial company with a different legal and regulatory status  
D. It only finances exports

**Answer:** C. It is a non-banking financial company with a different legal and regulatory status

**Explanation:** NBFCs perform important financial functions, but they are legally distinct from banking companies. Similar lending activity does not make the two institution types identical.

Which sequence is chronologically correct?

C. IFCI 1948 → NHB 1982 → EXIM 1988 → NABARD 1990 → SIDBI 2021

**Answer:** A. IFCI 1948 → NABARD/EXIM 1982 → NHB 1988 → SIDBI 1990 → NaBFID 2021

### 24. Hard · DFI vs NBFC
Which statement best distinguishes a sector-specific DFI from an NBFC category?

A. DFI and NBFC always mean exactly the same thing  
B. A DFI describes a development-finance mandate, while NBFC describes a non-banking financial-company status  
C. Every DFI must be a commercial bank  
D. Every NBFC is created by a special Act of Parliament

**Answer:** B. A DFI describes a development-finance mandate, while NBFC describes a non-banking financial-company status

**Explanation:** DFI refers to a development-oriented financing role, whereas NBFC is a legal/regulatory category for non-banking financial companies. The concepts can overlap in some cases but are not identical.
