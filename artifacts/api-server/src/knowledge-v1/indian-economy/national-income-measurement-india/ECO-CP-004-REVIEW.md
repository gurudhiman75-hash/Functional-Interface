# ECO-CP-004 — National Income Measurement in India
## Human Review Set V1

**Status:** REVIEW ONLY — not runtime registered  
**Difficulty:** assigned per question variant

---

### 1. Easy · Method identification
Which method adds the value created at each stage of production?

A. Income method  
B. Value added method  
C. Expenditure method  
D. Price index method

**Answer:** B. Value added method

**Explanation:** The value added method sums value added at each production stage.

---

### 2. Easy · Method identification
Which method adds incomes generated from production?

A. Expenditure method  
B. Value added method  
C. Income method  
D. Price index method

**Answer:** C. Income method

**Explanation:** The income method adds incomes generated by production.

---

### 3. Medium · Method application
An estimate adds household consumption, government consumption, investment and net exports. Which method is used?

A. Value added method  
B. Income method  
C. Expenditure method  
D. Price index method

**Answer:** C. Expenditure method

**Explanation:** These are final expenditure components.

---

### 4. Hard · Method application
Sales are recorded at several production stages. Which method best avoids counting the same input twice?

A. Income method  
B. Value added method  
C. Expenditure method  
D. Price index method

**Answer:** B. Value added method

**Explanation:** It counts only the value created at each stage.

---

### 5. Easy · Final/intermediate
Flour bought by a bakery to make bread is:

A. Final  
B. Intermediate  
C. Transfer payment  
D. Net export

**Answer:** B. Intermediate

**Explanation:** The flour is used to produce another good.

---

### 6. Medium · Final/intermediate
A new machine bought by a factory for production is treated as:

A. Final  
B. Intermediate  
C. Transfer payment  
D. Net export

**Answer:** A. Final

**Explanation:** A capital good bought for investment is a final use.

---

### 7. Medium · Double counting
A bakery's bread value already includes flour. Counting both full values would cause:

A. Depreciation  
B. Double counting  
C. Deflation  
D. Net exports

**Answer:** B. Double counting

**Explanation:** The flour is already included in the value of bread.

---

### 8. Hard · Double counting
Cotton becomes yarn and then cloth. Adding the full value of all three as final output would:

A. Count intermediate output more than once  
B. Remove depreciation  
C. Measure net exports  
D. Convert real GDP into nominal GDP

**Answer:** A. Count intermediate output more than once

**Explanation:** Earlier-stage output is already included in the later product's value.

---

### 9. Easy · Value added
Output is 500 and intermediate consumption is 200. Value added is:

A. 200  
B. 300  
C. 500  
D. 700

**Answer:** B. 300

**Explanation:** 500 - 200 = 300.

---

### 10. Medium · Value added
Output is 900 and value added is 540. Intermediate consumption is:

A. 360  
B. 540  
C. 900  
D. 1,440

**Answer:** A. 360

**Explanation:** 900 - 540 = 360.

---

### 11. Easy · Expenditure method
Household spending on final goods and services is recorded as:

A. Private final consumption expenditure  
B. Government final consumption expenditure  
C. Net exports  
D. Operating surplus

**Answer:** A. Private final consumption expenditure

**Explanation:** Household final consumption is recorded under private final consumption expenditure.

---

### 12. Medium · Expenditure method
Exports minus imports are called:

A. Capital formation  
B. Net exports  
C. Mixed income  
D. Compensation of employees

**Answer:** B. Net exports

**Explanation:** Net exports = exports - imports.

---

### 13. Easy · Income method
Wages and salaries are recorded as:

A. Compensation of employees  
B. Mixed income  
C. Net exports  
D. Capital formation

**Answer:** A. Compensation of employees

**Explanation:** Wages and salaries are compensation paid to employees.

---

### 14. Hard · Income method
A family-run shop uses the owner's labour and capital together. Its combined return is best treated as:

A. Compensation of employees  
B. Operating surplus  
C. Mixed income  
D. Net exports

**Answer:** C. Mixed income

**Explanation:** Mixed income is used when labour and capital income of a self-employed unit cannot be separated clearly.

---

### 15. Medium · GVA to GDP
Sum of GVA is 900 and taxes less subsidies on products are 60. GDP is:

A. 840  
B. 900  
C. 960  
D. 1,020

**Answer:** C. 960

**Explanation:** 900 + 60 = 960.

---

### 16. Hard · GVA to GDP
Sum of GVA is 700, product taxes are 60 and product subsidies are 20. GDP is:

A. 660  
B. 680  
C. 740  
D. 760

**Answer:** C. 740

**Explanation:** Net product taxes = 60 - 20 = 40; GDP = 700 + 40 = 740.

---

### 17. Easy · Base year
A base year is mainly used as:

A. A reference year for constant-price comparison  
B. The latest Budget year  
C. A year with the highest GDP  
D. A year used only for tax rates

**Answer:** A. A reference year for constant-price comparison

**Explanation:** A base year provides a common reference for constant-price estimates.

---

### 18. Medium · Base year
Why is a statistical base year revised from time to time?

A. To increase GDP automatically  
B. To update data, methods and economic structure  
C. To change tax rates  
D. To remove all price data

**Answer:** B. To update data, methods and economic structure

**Explanation:** Base revision keeps the statistical framework closer to the changing economy.

---

### 19. Easy · India institution
National Accounts Statistics in India are published by:

A. Ministry of Finance  
B. Reserve Bank of India  
C. Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation  
D. NITI Aayog

**Answer:** C. Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation

**Explanation:** MoSPI publishes India's National Accounts Statistics.

---

### 20. Medium · India institution
For official Indian national-accounts estimates, which ministry is the main statistical source?

A. Ministry of Corporate Affairs  
B. Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation  
C. Ministry of Labour and Employment  
D. Ministry of Commerce and Industry

**Answer:** B. Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation

**Explanation:** MoSPI is the official ministry responsible for national-accounts statistics.

### 21. Medium · Statements
Consider the statements:

I. The income method adds incomes generated in production.  
II. The expenditure method adds final expenditure on goods and services.

Which is correct?

A. I only  
B. II only  
C. Both I and II  
D. Neither I nor II

**Answer:** C. Both I and II

**Explanation:** Both statements correctly describe the two methods.

---

### 22. Hard · Statements
Consider the statements:

I. GDP can be obtained from GVA by adding taxes on products and subtracting subsidies on products.  
II. A capital good bought for final investment must always be treated as intermediate consumption.

Which is correct?

A. I only  
B. II only  
C. Both I and II  
D. Neither I nor II

**Answer:** A. I only

**Explanation:** The GDP-GVA relation is correct. A capital good bought for investment is a final use.

---

### 23. Medium · Concept distinction
Which statement is correct?

A. Every machine is an intermediate good.  
B. Every food item is a final good.  
C. Final or intermediate status depends on how the good is used.  
D. Only services can be final output.

**Answer:** C. Final or intermediate status depends on how the good is used.

**Explanation:** The same type of good can be final or intermediate depending on its use.

---

### 24. Hard · Concept distinction
Which statement correctly separates GVA from GDP?

A. GDP adds taxes less subsidies on products to the sum of GVA.  
B. GDP is always lower than GVA by depreciation.  
C. GVA equals exports minus imports.  
D. GDP and GVA differ only because of population.

**Answer:** A. GDP adds taxes less subsidies on products to the sum of GVA.

**Explanation:** GDP = Sum of GVA + taxes on products - subsidies on products.
