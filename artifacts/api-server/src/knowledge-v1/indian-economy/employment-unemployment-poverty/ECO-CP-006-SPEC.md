# ECO-CP-006 — Employment, Unemployment & Poverty

**Chapter:** ECO-001 Indian Economy  
**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE  
**Runtime:** blocked pending human approval

## Scope

Included:
- labour force, worker and unemployed concepts;
- LFPR, WPR and unemployment-rate relationships;
- self-employed, regular wage/salaried and casual labour;
- seasonal, disguised, frictional, structural and cyclical unemployment;
- absolute, relative and multidimensional poverty;
- poverty line and poverty headcount ratio;
- major poverty-estimation expert groups: Alagh, Lakdawala, Tendulkar and Rangarajan;
- MGNREGA at stable static-GK level: 2005 law and up to 100 days of rural wage-employment guarantee.

Excluded:
- current unemployment rate, LFPR or WPR values;
- current poverty ratio or MPI values/rankings;
- current poverty-line rupee values;
- current MGNREGA wage rates, beneficiaries or Budget allocation;
- volatile scheme performance and current state rankings.

## Language and explanation rules

- Stems must be short and exam-like.
- Explanations should normally use two short steps: state the deciding rule, then connect it to the question.
- Statement questions must explain why the relevant statements are true or false.
- Numerical questions must show the short working.
- Do not make Hard questions difficult through vocabulary; use deeper classification or linked concepts instead.

## Difficulty model

Difficulty is assigned per generated variant, not permanently by QL.

- **Easy:** direct definition, formula identification or clear scenario.
- **Medium:** one-step classification/application or close distinction.
- **Hard:** two linked ideas, close competing unemployment/poverty concepts, or multi-step interpretation.
