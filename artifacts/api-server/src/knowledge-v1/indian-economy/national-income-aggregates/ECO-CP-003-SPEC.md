# ECO-CP-003 — National Income & Aggregates

**Chapter:** ECO-001 Indian Economy  
**CP:** ECO-CP-003 National Income & Aggregates  
**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE  
**Runtime registration:** blocked pending human approval  
**Languages:** English review surface; facts remain locale-neutral.

## Scope

This CP covers:
- GDP, GNP, NDP and NNP
- gross vs net
- domestic vs national
- depreciation
- net factor income from abroad (NFIA)
- nominal GDP and real GDP
- per-capita income
- value added
- market price and factor cost at exam-foundation level
- simple one-step aggregate relationships

Detailed measurement methods, double counting, final/intermediate goods and transfer payments are reserved for ECO-CP-004.

## Design rules

1. Stems must be short and exam-like.
2. Explanations should show only the required relationship or one short reasoning step.
3. Formula questions must be derived from structured relationships, not memorised question strings.
4. Numerical items must remain one-step concept checks, not Quant problems.
5. Gross/net and domestic/national must be tested separately and together.
6. Current GDP values, growth rates and base-year values are excluded.
7. "National income" wording must avoid ambiguity; when traditional exam terminology is used, it must explicitly say that NNP at factor cost is meant.
8. Review items remain `runtimeRegistered: false` until approval.

## Core relationships

- `NDP = GDP - Depreciation`
- `GDP = NDP + Depreciation`
- `GNP = GDP + NFIA`
- `GDP = GNP - NFIA`
- `NNP = GNP - Depreciation`
- `GNP = NNP + Depreciation`
- `Per-capita income = National income / Population`
- `Value added = Value of output - Intermediate consumption`
- `Market price = Factor cost + Net indirect taxes` (traditional exam relationship)
- `Net indirect taxes = Indirect taxes - Subsidies`
