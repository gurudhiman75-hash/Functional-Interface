### 1
GDP after deducting depreciation becomes:

A. GNP  
B. NDP  
C. NNP  
D. NFIA

**Answer:** B. NDP

**Explanation:** NDP = GDP - Depreciation.

### 2
GNP after deducting depreciation becomes:

A. GDP  
B. NDP  
C. NNP  
D. Real GDP

**Answer:** C. NNP

**Explanation:** NNP = GNP - Depreciation.

### 3
GDP plus net factor income from abroad gives:

A. NDP  
B. GNP  
C. NNP  
D. Nominal GDP

**Answer:** B. GNP

**Explanation:** GNP = GDP + NFIA.

### 4
Which formula is correct?

A. NDP = GDP + Depreciation  
B. NDP = GDP - Depreciation  
C. NDP = GNP + NFIA  
D. NDP = GDP + Population

**Answer:** B. NDP = GDP - Depreciation

**Explanation:** Net domestic product is GDP after depreciation is deducted.

### 5
Which formula is correct?

A. GNP = GDP + NFIA  
B. GNP = GDP - Depreciation  
C. GNP = NDP - NFIA  
D. GNP = GDP / Population

**Answer:** A. GNP = GDP + NFIA

**Explanation:** Add net factor income from abroad to GDP to get GNP.

### 6
Which formula is correct?

A. NNP = GNP + Depreciation  
B. NNP = GDP + NFIA + Depreciation  
C. NNP = GNP - Depreciation  
D. NNP = GDP / Population

**Answer:** C. NNP = GNP - Depreciation

**Explanation:** NNP is GNP after depreciation is deducted.

### 7
What is deducted from a gross measure to get the corresponding net measure?

A. NFIA  
B. Depreciation  
C. Population  
D. Subsidies

**Answer:** B. Depreciation

**Explanation:** Net measures exclude depreciation.

### 8
What converts GDP into GNP?

A. Depreciation  
B. Population  
C. Net factor income from abroad  
D. Subsidies

**Answer:** C. Net factor income from abroad

**Explanation:** GNP = GDP + NFIA.

### 9
GDP measured at current prices is called:

A. Real GDP  
B. Nominal GDP  
C. NDP  
D. GNP

**Answer:** B. Nominal GDP

**Explanation:** Nominal GDP uses current prices.

### 10
GDP measured at constant prices is called:

A. Nominal GDP  
B. Real GDP  
C. NNP  
D. GNP

**Answer:** B. Real GDP

**Explanation:** Real GDP uses constant prices.

### 11
National income is 800 and population is 100. Per-capita income is:

A. 6  
B. 8  
C. 10  
D. 16

**Answer:** B. 8

**Explanation:** 800 / 100 = 8.

### 12
National income is 1,500 and population is 300. Per-capita income is:

A. 3  
B. 5  
C. 7  
D. 10

**Answer:** B. 5

**Explanation:** 1,500 / 300 = 5.

### 13
Output is worth 500 and intermediate inputs are 200. Value added is:

A. 200  
B. 300  
C. 500  
D. 700

**Answer:** B. 300

**Explanation:** Value added = 500 - 200 = 300.

### 14
Output is worth 900 and intermediate inputs are 350. Value added is:

A. 350  
B. 450  
C. 550  
D. 1,250

**Answer:** C. 550

**Explanation:** Value added = 900 - 350 = 550.

### 15
GDP is 500 and depreciation is 40. NDP is:

A. 460  
B. 500  
C. 540  
D. 440

**Answer:** A. 460

**Explanation:** NDP = 500 - 40 = 460.

### 16
GDP is 600 and NFIA is 20. GNP is:

A. 580  
B. 600  
C. 620  
D. 640

**Answer:** C. 620

**Explanation:** GNP = 600 + 20 = 620.

### 17
GNP is 700 and depreciation is 50. NNP is:

A. 650  
B. 700  
C. 750  
D. 600

**Answer:** A. 650

**Explanation:** NNP = 700 - 50 = 650.

### 18
Which pair is correctly matched?

A. GDP — GDP + NFIA  
B. GNP — GDP + NFIA  
C. NDP — GNP - Depreciation  
D. NNP — GDP - Depreciation

**Answer:** B. GNP — GDP + NFIA

**Explanation:** GNP = GDP + NFIA.

### 19
Which pair is correctly matched?

A. Nominal GDP — constant prices  
B. Real GDP — current prices  
C. Real GDP — constant prices  
D. Per-capita income — national income × population

**Answer:** C. Real GDP — constant prices

**Explanation:** Real GDP is measured at constant prices.

### 20
Consider the statements:

I. NDP = GDP - Depreciation.  
II. GNP = GDP + NFIA.

Which is correct?

A. I only  
B. II only  
C. Both I and II  
D. Neither I nor II

**Answer:** C. Both I and II

**Explanation:** Both relationships are correct.

### 21
Consider the statements:

I. NNP = GNP - Depreciation.  
II. Real GDP is measured at current prices.

Which is correct?

A. I only  
B. II only  
C. Both I and II  
D. Neither I nor II

**Answer:** A. I only

**Explanation:** NNP subtracts depreciation. Real GDP uses constant prices.

### 22
Which statement is correct?

A. Gross measures exclude depreciation.  
B. Net measures include depreciation twice.  
C. Gross measures include depreciation; net measures exclude it.  
D. Gross and net differ only because of population.

**Answer:** C. Gross measures include depreciation; net measures exclude it.

**Explanation:** Depreciation is the difference between gross and net measures.

### 23
Which statement is correct?

A. Domestic and national differ only because of depreciation.  
B. National measures adjust domestic measures for NFIA.  
C. National always means current prices.  
D. Domestic always means constant prices.

**Answer:** B. National measures adjust domestic measures for NFIA.

**Explanation:** NFIA is used to move from domestic to national measures.

### 24
Factor cost is 400 and net indirect taxes are 30. Market price is:

A. 370  
B. 400  
C. 430  
D. 30

**Answer:** C. 430

**Explanation:** Market price = 400 + 30 = 430.
