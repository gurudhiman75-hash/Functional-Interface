### 3. Medium
Which aggregate is obtained by adding net factor income from abroad to GDP?

A. NDP  
B. GNP  
C. NNP  
D. GDP

**Answer:** B. GNP

**Explanation:** NFIA adjusts a domestic aggregate to the corresponding national aggregate. Therefore, GNP = GDP + NFIA.
