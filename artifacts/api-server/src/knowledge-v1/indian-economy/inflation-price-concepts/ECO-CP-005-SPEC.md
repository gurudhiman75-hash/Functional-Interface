# ECO-CP-005 — Inflation & Price Concepts

**Chapter:** ECO-001 Indian Economy  
**CP:** ECO-CP-005 Inflation & Price Concepts  
**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE  
**Runtime registration:** blocked pending human approval

## Scope

Included:
- inflation, deflation and disinflation;
- demand-pull and cost-push inflation;
- purchasing power and real-value effects;
- expected effects on fixed-income groups, borrowers and lenders;
- Consumer Price Index (CPI), Wholesale Price Index (WPI) and GDP deflator at concept level;
- headline and core inflation;
- price-index and inflation-rate relationships;
- simple one-step numerical applications;
- base-effect concept at introductory level.

Excluded:
- current CPI/WPI/inflation values;
- current inflation target or tolerance band;
- current index weights or base-year memorisation;
- current food/fuel contribution;
- forecasts;
- arbitrary percentage cut-offs for creeping/walking/running inflation where sources vary.

## Language rules

- Keep stems short and exam-like.
- Explanations should normally be one or two simple sentences.
- Do not make wording harder to manufacture difficulty.
- Use same-domain distractors.
- Avoid unnecessary textbook introductions.

## Difficulty rule

Difficulty is assigned to the generated variant, not fixed to the QL.

- **Easy:** direct identification or one clear relation.
- **Medium:** one-step application, close distinction or simple scenario.
- **Hard:** multiple linked ideas, close competing concepts or a scenario requiring two inferences.

Statement format alone does not make a question Hard. Numerical format alone does not make a question Hard.
