## Scope

This CP covers stable, exam-relevant concepts about the Indian banking system:

- scheduled-bank meaning and broad bank classification;
- core banking intermediation: accepting deposits and extending credit;
- savings/current/fixed/recurring deposit distinctions at concept level;
- Regional Rural Banks: 1975 origin, RRB Act 1976, rural-credit purpose;
- short-term rural cooperative credit structure: State Cooperative Bank, District Central Cooperative Bank, PACS;
- Small Finance Banks versus Payments Banks;
- secured versus unsecured lending and collateral;
- basic deposit/credit creation logic;
- NPA concept and the standard 90-day overdue rule for ordinary term loans;
- priority-sector lending concept and major stable categories;
- DICGC deposit-insurance concept without encoding the current rupee ceiling;
- statement and mixed distinction questions.

## Deliberately excluded

- current number/list/ranking of banks, RRBs, SFBs or Payments Banks;
- current ownership of individual banks and latest mergers/amalgamations;
- current deposit/loan interest rates;
- current PSL target percentages/sub-target percentages;
- current DICGC insurance ceiling;
- current NPA ratios and bank-wise asset-quality figures;
- detailed NBFC/all-India financial-institution coverage reserved for ECO-CP-011;
- detailed monetary-policy instruments already covered in ECO-CP-009.

## Difficulty rules

- Easy: direct definitions and clean one-step identification.
- Medium: realistic bank-product or institutional scenarios, one linked inference, simple first-round reserve/lending working.
- Hard: close distinctions involving two institutional rules or multi-step reasoning.
- A question is not Hard merely because it is a statement question or contains arithmetic.

## Explanation standard

- Normally one or two short sentences.
- Give the deciding rule plus why it applies.
- For close distinctions, explicitly state the key difference.
- For statement questions, explain why the relevant statements are true or false.
- For numerical credit-creation items, show the small calculation.
- Avoid option-by-option commentary unless needed to resolve ambiguity.

## Static-GK gate

Every generated item must remain valid without live bank counts, live interest rates, current PSL percentages, current DICGC rupee limits, current NPA ratios or current institution names.
