# ECO-CP-001 — Basic Economic Concepts

**Chapter:** ECO-001 Indian Economy  
**CP:** ECO-CP-001 Basic Economic Concepts  
**Lifecycle:** REVIEW CANDIDATE  
**Runtime registration:** blocked pending human approval  
**Languages in this checkpoint:** English review surface only; canonical facts are locale-neutral.

## Scope

This CP establishes the basic vocabulary needed by later Economy CPs.

Included concepts:
- scarcity and choice
- opportunity cost
- production, consumption and distribution
- goods and services
- factors of production: land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship
- factor rewards: rent, wages, interest and profit
- utility
- demand and supply
- market
- microeconomics and macroeconomics at introductory level

Excluded from this CP:
- detailed elasticity
- market structures
- national-income aggregates
- inflation
- banking and monetary policy
- changing/current economic data

## Design goals

1. Questions must feel like SSC/Banking/State-exam Economy questions, not textbook prompts.
2. Direct-definition items are permitted, but the CP must also test application through short realistic scenarios.
3. Factors of production must be tested through both classification and factor-reward relationships.
4. Scarcity and opportunity cost must be represented as choice/trade-off ideas, not memorised wording only.
5. Distractors must come from the same conceptual family.
6. Explanations must be short, simple and beginner-friendly.
7. No volatile/current economic figures are allowed.
8. Review questions remain `runtimeRegistered: false` until human approval.

## Sources

Primary authority for the concepts is NCERT introductory economics material. Factor-of-production terminology is also anchored to NCERT material that explicitly identifies land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship as production inputs. Source IDs are stored separately from the question wording so source enrichment cannot alter question logic.
