# Future Extension Contracts

Future systems must extend production contracts rather than mutate them.

## Adaptive Pedagogy

May add new coaching modes and ranking strategies. Must preserve semantic answer rendering, reasoning graph integrity, and pedagogical-flow validation.

## Analytics

May consume signatures, topology metadata, metrics, and golden hashes. Must not depend on raw English prose as a semantic source.

## PYQ Imitation

May add scenario and rhythm profiles. Must preserve canonical reasoning and topology contracts.

## Difficulty Ladders

May adjust selection and sequencing. Must not alter mathematical validity or semantic formatting.

## Coaching Modes

May add new realization styles. Must consume semantic intents and reasoning graphs.

## Future Indian Languages

Must implement native-language renderers from semantic intents. Direct English translation is not a supported architecture.

## Student Modeling

May consume traps, branches, topology variants, and misconception metadata. Must not rewrite canonical structures.
