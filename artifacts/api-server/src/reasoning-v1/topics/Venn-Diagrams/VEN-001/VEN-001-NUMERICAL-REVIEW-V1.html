VEN-CP005 · Two-set counts

1 · VOLUNTEERING · onlyA · Easy

A = people who participate in literacy drives; B = people who participate in clean-up drives. In a survey of 47 people, 11 participate in literacy drives; 40 participate in clean-up drives; 8 participate in literacy drives and clean-up drives. How many people participate in literacy drives but do not participate in clean-up drives?

  1. 3
  2. 47
  3. 43
  4. 11
Answer and step-by-step explanation

A. 3

Only A = 11 − 8 = 3; Only B = 40 − 8 = 32. At least one = 11 + 40 − 8 = 43. None = 47 − 43 = 4. Required count = 3 = 3.

LaTeX: n(A\cup B)=n(A)+n(B)-n(A\cap B)

1 · VOLUNTEERING · percentage-count · Medium

A = people who participate in literacy drives; B = people who participate in clean-up drives. In a survey, 60% belong to A, 50% belong to B, and 15% belong to neither. In a survey of 500 people, How many people participate in literacy drives and clean-up drives?

  1. 425
  2. 300
  3. 125
  4. 500
Answer and step-by-step explanation

C. 125

Both-group percentage = 60 + 50 − (100 − 15) = 25%. 500 × 25/100 = 125.

LaTeX: n(A\cap B)=N(p_A+p_B+p_0-100)/100
