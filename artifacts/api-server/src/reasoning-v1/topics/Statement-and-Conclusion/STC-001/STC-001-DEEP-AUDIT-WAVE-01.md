## Wave 01 focus

A new learner-facing guard checks the generated surface itself rather than only scheduler/saturation properties.

Audit sample:

```text
6 QLs × 3 locales × 12 seeds = 216 learner surfaces
```

For every surface it verifies:

- review-only lifecycle and release locks;
- non-trivial stem;
- two non-trivial conclusions;
- four unique answer-code options;
- valid correct answer index;
- explanation depth;
- explicit resolution of both Conclusion I and Conclusion II;
- no internal generator/audit vocabulary leakage;
- no mechanical stem wording such as “associated with”, “most closely linked”, “broad” or “best describes”;
- visible stem diversity;
- all four answer positions reachable;
- multiple surface archetypes represented.
