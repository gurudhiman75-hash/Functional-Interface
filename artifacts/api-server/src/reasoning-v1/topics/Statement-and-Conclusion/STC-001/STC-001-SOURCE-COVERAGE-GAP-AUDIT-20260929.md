# STC-001 — Source Coverage and Gap Audit

Status: **NO CONVENTIONAL SEMANTIC GAP FOUND IN CURRENT V2.2 CONTRACT**

Date: 2026-09-29

## Scope

This audit compares the frozen six-QL Statement & Conclusion engine with recurring target-exam forms.

Novelty is excluded.

The audit separates:

- non-syllogistic Statement & Conclusion;
- syllogism;
- inequality;
- Statement & Inference;
- Statement & Assumption;
- Statement & Argument;
- Cause & Effect;
- Course of Action.

Those adjacent chapters are not counted as STC gaps.

## Current STC semantic contracts

The six permanent QLs cover:

- direct propositional entailment and unsupported-extra rejection;
- polarity and overclaim control;
- conditional/converse/inverse/denying-antecedent traps;
- modal strength and cautious possibility/certainty language;
- comparative/metric relations;
- temporal order and trend relations.

The V2.2 editorial layer exposes these through eight learner-facing surface archetypes per QL, including:

- one-line fact;
- public notice;
- everyday observation;
- survey/report;
- quoted claim;
- rule/eligibility;
- numeric snapshot;
- contrast/concession;
- conditional trigger;
- advice/warning;
- forecast/outlook;
- direct comparison;
- event sequence.

## Recent SSC evidence

Recent SSC CGL 2025 Statement & Conclusion questions use the expected non-syllogistic contract:

- one short factual/policy statement;
- two conclusions I and II;
- four answer classes: Only I / Only II / Both / Neither;
- direct consequence vs unsupported overreach;
- scope/absolute-word traps;
- cautious modal conclusions such as "may" or "can";
- cause/effect language only when supported by the statement.

Decision: **COVERED** by STC V2.2.

The current QL family already has explicit modal, overclaim, polarity, conditional, comparison and temporal/trend authority for these forms.

## Punjab-state evidence

Punjab Police 2025 syllabus explicitly includes **Statements and Conclusions** under Mental Ability and Logical Reasoning.

The syllabus does not establish a materially different answer contract from the standard non-syllogistic form.

Decision: **COVERED / NO NEW PUNJAB-SPECIFIC QL JUSTIFIED**.

Punjabi is already a first-class frozen locale in V2.2.

## Banking evidence

Banking Mains critical reasoning continues to include conclusion-oriented questions, but current evidence also mixes these with broader single-best-option Critical Reasoning, assumption, strengthen/weaken and decision-making formats.

Those broader Banking CR forms are not automatically equivalent to the frozen STC four-way two-conclusion contract.

Decision:

```text
two-conclusion non-syllogistic STC:   COVERED
single-best passage conclusion:       SOURCE WATCH
assumption/strengthen/weaken:          OTHER CHAPTER / CR FAMILY
syllogistic conclusions:              SYL-001
inequality conclusions:                inequality chapter
new permanent STC QL required now:     NO
```

Do not create a Banking-specific STC authority merely because a broader Critical Reasoning question asks for "a conclusion". Reopen only on recurring target-exam evidence for a genuinely different Statement & Conclusion contract.
