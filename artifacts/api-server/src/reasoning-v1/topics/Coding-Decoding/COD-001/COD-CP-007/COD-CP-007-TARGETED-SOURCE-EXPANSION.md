## 2. Current family decisions

### 2.1 Uniform modular digit translation

Decision: **retain as the only currently admitted CP-007 family**.

Evidence:

- direct uploaded-source example;
- material decimal-wrap and leading-zero semantics;
- five executable prototype tasks;
- independent forward/inverse proof;
- mathematical and editorial saturation.

### 2.2 Arbitrary digit-to-digit substitution

Decision: **remain source-pending**.

A fixed bijection over digits is mathematically valid, but the current evidence does not establish a recurring target-exam format distinct from:

- the simpler uniform decimal translation;
- CP-001 direct substitution with a different cosmetic source alphabet.

A future prototype requires direct recurring digit-domain evidence and must demonstrate a solve contract that cannot be absorbed by CP-001's generic mapping foundation.

### 2.3 Digit-to-symbol bijection

Decision: **remain source-pending**.

General symbol coding is already represented by CP-001 when letters or words map to symbols. A CP-007 family survives only when:

- the source domain is materially numeric;
- leading zero, repetition or decimal-token semantics matter;
- the task is not an unconditional letter lookup table;
- the symbols are not operators or relations;
- direct recurring exam evidence supports the digit-domain form.

No new prototype is admitted in this cycle.

### 2.4 Position-dependent digit transformation

Decision: **remain source- and collision-pending**.

Odd/even, alternating or indexed digit shifts are formally possible, but they strongly mirror CP-004 position-dependent letter transforms. A new CP-007 family requires both:

1. recurring direct digit-domain evidence; and
2. executable proof that decimal wrap, leading-zero handling or another numeric invariant creates a materially different student solve contract.

### 2.5 Pure digit permutation

Decision: **exclude from CP-007 as a separate family**.

Reversing, rotating, swapping or otherwise permuting positions is already the operation owned by CP-005. Replacing letters with digits does not change the reasoning operation.

Numeric-string presentation properties such as a leading zero remain renderer/serialization requirements and do not by themselves create a new QL.

Reopen only if a source-backed format combines permutation with a material digit-specific transformation. Such a composite would require a separate ownership audit against CP-006.

### 2.6 Alphanumeric dual-channel transformation

Decision: **remain source-pending**.

A mixed source string in which letters and digits follow separate channels may be a real format, but the current evidence does not prove recurrence. It also has high collision risk with:

- CP-003/004 letter transformations;
- the admitted CP-007 digit transformation;
- CP-006 multi-stage or multi-rule composites.

A future candidate must show that both channels are active and that the question is not merely two existing QLs concatenated.

### 2.7 Mixed-token direct substitution

Decision: **presumptively merge with CP-001 unless new evidence proves otherwise**.

A fixed map over letters, digits and symbols has the same basic hidden state and correctness predicate as direct substitution. Mixed character classes alone do not create a new reasoning operation.

Retain as a CP-007 candidate only if parsing, inverse uniqueness, answer shape or source-domain semantics materially change the student task.

## 4. What would reopen a pending family

A pending family may be reopened only with a concrete source pack containing:

- at least two independent target-exam examples or one clearly recurring format set;
- complete question and answer context;
- source year/exam metadata where available;
- enough examples to distinguish the candidate from an existing CP;
- evidence that both forward and inverse directions occur, where inverse retention is proposed.

The source pack must be followed by a non-permanent executable prototype and a collision audit. It cannot directly create permanent IDs.
