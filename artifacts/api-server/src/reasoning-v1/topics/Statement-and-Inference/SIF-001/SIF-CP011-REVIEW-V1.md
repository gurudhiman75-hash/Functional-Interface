# SIF-CP011 — Multiple-Factor Inference — Review V1

**Status:** Human-review candidate; not frozen  
**Chapter:** SIF-001 — Statement & Inference  
**Difficulty:** Medium to Hard  
**Delivery:** Review only; Question Bank, tests, mocks and public delivery remain locked

## Review focus

- combine two or more stated facts before judging support;
- distinguish satisfying prerequisites from completion of a later action;
- connect comparisons, records and scope across sentences;
- reject claims that add unsupported cause, time, population or status.

**Authority pool:** 24 trilingual scenarios across eight families. **Distribution:** 12 Medium / 12 Hard; 12 supported-I / 12 supported-II. Each family contributes three scenarios.

## Scenario review

## Q1 · PERMIT-APPLICATION

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Family:** ELIGIBILITY_INTERSECTION  
**Context:** PUBLIC_ADMINISTRATION

**Statement:** The permit office issues a site-work permit only after the planning unit approves the drawing and the applicant pays the inspection fee. The planning unit approved Rao's drawing, and the receipt shows that the fee was paid. The permit register has not yet been updated.

**Inference I:** Rao's application meets both stated conditions required before the office can issue the permit.

**Inference II:** The permit has already been issued to Rao.

**Answer:** Only I follows.
