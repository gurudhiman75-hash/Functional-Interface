## Review focus

- interpret percentages, counts, simple ratios, changes, rankings, frequencies and part-whole figures stated in prose;
- compare figures directly without turning the task into lengthy arithmetic or a data-interpretation calculation;
- keep claims within the reported period, group and measure; distinguish a raw count from a rate;
- avoid unsupported causes, projections and claims about people or outcomes the report does not describe.

## Q1 · SIF-CP009-PERCENTAGE_COMPARISON-BRANCH-RECEIPTS

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM
**Context:** BANKING
**Data family:** PERCENTAGE_COMPARISON
**Answer state:** ONLY_I

**Instruction:** Which inference is supported by the information given?

**Statement:** In a survey of 200 customers at a bank branch, 64% preferred digital receipts. 21% preferred printed receipts. The remaining responses were not divided by preference in the report.

**Inference I:** More surveyed customers preferred digital receipts than printed receipts.
**Inference II:** Every respondent who did not prefer digital receipts preferred printed receipts.

A. Only Inference I follows
B. Only Inference II follows
C. Either Inference I or II follows
D. Neither Inference I nor II follows
E. Both Inference I and II follow

**Answer:** A. Only Inference I follows

**Explanation:** The reported digital preference, 64%, is higher than the printed preference, 21%. The report does not say how the other responses were divided. Therefore, only Inference I follows.
