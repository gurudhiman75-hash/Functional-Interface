- equivalent-pair selection is mechanically viable but still lacks an exact official mixed fixture;
- the inherited count was format-driven rather than discovered from student solve contracts.

## 5. Cross-topic freeze-blocker resolution

### CP-005 and CP-006

Executable bridges already prove that pure alphabet/cluster matches explain only a component. Every admitted CP-008 relation requires the displayed numeric and letter domains together.

### Numeric analogy

A unified numeric matcher is still unavailable, but the final per-group ownership proof is sufficient for allocation proposal:

- letter-group-to-number families have no numeric input relation to delegate;
- single-letter position square is explicitly driven by an alphabet position;
- all mixed-token families require changed letters as part of the correct answer;
- mutating or ignoring the letter domain destroys the complete registered relation;
- numeric-only reasoning cannot reconstruct the complete typed answer.

A future shared numeric matcher remains a production-hardening task, not a blocker for the allocation proposal.

### CP-007

Permanent CP-008 generators must use non-word letter groups/clusters or reject enabled meaningful words. No admitted prototype relies on lexical meaning or word structure.

### Coding-Decoding

Student stems must retain analogy grammar. Terms such as `coded as`, `encode`, `decode`, code-table recovery and common-code fragments are prohibited. The final allocation audit includes a grammar guard.

### CP-009

All admitted contexts use one stable pair-local operation. Pair-index-dependent, progressive or meta-relational transformations remain delegated to CP-009.
