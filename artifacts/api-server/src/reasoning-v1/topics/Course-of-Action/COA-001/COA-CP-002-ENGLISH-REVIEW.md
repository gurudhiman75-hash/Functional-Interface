## Q1 — COA-QL-001 — Easy

**Statement:** Streetlights on one residential lane have stopped working after the local distribution box developed a fault, while lights on nearby lanes are functioning normally.

**Courses of Action:**  
I. The maintenance team should inspect and repair the faulty distribution box and check the affected streetlights before restoring the circuit.  
II. The maintenance team should replace every streetlight bulb on the affected lane before testing the faulty distribution box.

**Answer: A — Only I follows.**

**Explanation:** The distribution-box fault is already identified. Repairing it directly addresses the failure. Replacing all bulbs first targets components not shown to be the cause and delays the direct repair.

## Q2 — COA-QL-001 — Medium

**Statement:** One ATM at a bank branch accepts cards but repeatedly fails to dispense cash because its cash-dispensing unit is jamming during transactions.

**Courses of Action:**  
I. The branch should ask customers to change their ATM PINs before using the machine again.  
II. The branch should take the faulty ATM out of service, repair the dispensing unit and direct customers to another working machine meanwhile.

**Answer: B — Only II follows.**

**Explanation:** The problem is a mechanical jam, not customer PINs. Taking the machine out of service and repairing the dispensing unit directly addresses the fault while another ATM keeps service available.

## Q3 — COA-QL-001 — Medium

**Explanation:** Clearing and scaling the notification queue fixes the known bottleneck. An immediate acknowledgement is also a useful temporary safeguard while email delivery is delayed.

## CP002 review checklist

Reviewers should check:

- direct-remedy questions actually test correction of the stated problem rather than general desirability;
- preventive questions distinguish prevention from merely reacting after failure;
- rejected actions remain plausible enough to require reasoning;
- Hard items are harder because two actions are credible or multiple failure points exist, not because the English is harder;
- explanations are simple, specific and beginner-friendly;
- both actions are judged independently;
- no answer can be guessed from words such as `immediately`, `investigate`, `ban`, or from action length;
- no question requires outside specialist knowledge.

No CP002 learner-facing surface is frozen until explicit human approval.
