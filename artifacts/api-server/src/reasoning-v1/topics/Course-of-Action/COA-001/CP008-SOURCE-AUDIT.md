# COA-001 / CP008 — Source Saturation + Exam-realness Audit

Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**

Approved by product owner on 2026-09-17.

## Audit conclusion

The existing semantic families cover the material learner operations found in the audited Course-of-Action sources, but the presentation layer had two real gaps:

1. **three-course combination questions** are materially present in official-paper reproductions and require a dedicated renderer;
2. **five-code two-course questions with `Either I or II`** are materially present in banking memory-based reproductions and require a dedicated exclusive-alternative pair authority rather than inference from ordinary independent courses.

No source evidence from this pass justified reviving `COA-QL-007` as a separate semantic family. Paired-course structure remains presentation metadata.

## Approved architecture

- `TWO_ACTION_FOUR_WAY`: source-supported core presentation.
- `TWO_ACTION_FIVE_CODE`: source-supported banking profile; `Either I or II` is allowed only from dedicated mutually-exclusive alternative authority.
- `THREE_ACTION_COMBINATION`: source-supported core variant with independent I/II/III evaluation.
- `SINGLE_BEST_ACTION`: outside core COA; Decision Making / situational-judgment boundary.
- QL001–QL006 retained.
- QL007 retired from future semantic expansion and preserved only as legacy presentation/calibration identifier.
- QL008 retained for sequence/dependency.
- QL009 retained for strict integrated reasoning.
- No additional permanent semantic QL is required.

## Exam-realness rules

- difficulty must come from reasoning, not long or difficult English;
- strict action may be correct when justified by the facts;
- verification before punishment remains represented;
- immediate relief and preventive follow-up may both follow;
- broad shutdowns, blanket punishments and unrelated desirable actions remain valid distractor families;
- source-supported social/civic/public-administration cases must remain represented alongside banking and operational domains;
- conventional direction wording may repeat without being treated as semantic-template duplication.
