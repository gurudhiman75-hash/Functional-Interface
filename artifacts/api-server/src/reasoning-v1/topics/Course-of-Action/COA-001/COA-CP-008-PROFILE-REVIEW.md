# COA-001 / COA-CP-008 — Source-backed Profile Review

Status: **HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED**

Scope: CP008 source-backed presentation expansion only. Existing CP001–CP007 semantic authorities are not rewritten.

## What to review

CP008 adds two presentation capabilities supported by the source census:

1. `TWO_ACTION_FIVE_CODE`, including a genuine `Either I or II` class only for dedicated mutually-exclusive alternative authorities;
2. `THREE_ACTION_COMBINATION`, using independently judged Courses I, II and III and authored combination options.

`COA-QL-007` is retired from future semantic expansion. It remains only as a legacy identifier for approved CP001 calibration material.

## Five-code / Either calibration

### Q1 — Medium — Exam administration

**Statement:** An examination venue becomes unavailable on the morning before a scheduled session. A prepared backup venue of sufficient capacity is available, and the examination body also has authority to reschedule the session if the backup cannot be used.

**Course I:** Shift the affected session to the prepared backup venue after notifying candidates and confirming the required arrangements.

**Course II:** If the backup venue cannot be activated in time, reschedule the affected session and issue a clear revised notice.

**Options:** Only I / Only II / Either I or II / Neither / Both

**Answer:** Either I or II.

**Why:** Both are valid resolution routes, but for the same session only one route is ultimately selected.

### Q2 — Hard — Digital service

**Statement:** A critical internal application runs on an obsolete server that must be retired this month. Two fully funded replacement paths are approved: managed cloud or a supported on-premise system. Only one production platform is required.

**Course I:** Migrate to the approved cloud platform, test the migration and retire the obsolete server after successful cutover.

**Course II:** Install the approved supported on-premise replacement, test the application and retire the obsolete server after successful cutover.

**Answer:** Either I or II.

**Why:** Each is a complete valid replacement path. Implementing both would duplicate the same production replacement rather than form complementary actions.

### Q3 — Medium — Transport

**Statement:** A bridge is closed for urgent repair. Two separately approved diversion routes can each carry the normal traffic, but traffic control requires selecting one signed diversion.

**Course I:** Open and clearly sign the first approved diversion route.

**Course II:** Open and clearly sign the second approved diversion route.

**Answer:** Either I or II.

**Why:** Either approved route solves the temporary access problem, but both cannot simultaneously be the single official diversion required by the stated constraint.

### Q4 — Hard — Workplace

**Statement:** A small company has exhausted its operating funds and cannot continue in its present form. It can either complete an orderly closure and settle liabilities from available assets, or accept a credible acquisition offer that takes over the business and liabilities.

**Course I:** Proceed with an orderly closure under the applicable process.

**Course II:** Accept the credible acquisition route if its terms lawfully transfer the business and outstanding liabilities.

**Answer:** Either I or II.

**Why:** Both are valid resolution paths, but the same business cannot simultaneously complete an independent wind-down and be transferred as a going concern.

## Three-action combination calibration

### Q5 — Civic service

A public drain is repeatedly blocked because residents dump household waste into it. The drain is partly clogged and nearby waste-collection points can be provided.

I. Post guards permanently along the entire drain.  
II. Clear the blockage and provide marked waste-collection points.  
III. Enforce existing anti-dumping rules against repeated violations after the disposal arrangement is available.

**Answer:** Only II and III follow.

### Q6 — Health service

A district reports a sharp seasonal rise in mosquito-borne fever. Hospitals expect more patients over the next month.

I. Treat a political debate about responsibility as the main immediate response before health-control work begins.  
II. Intensify mosquito-control measures and issue prevention guidance.  
III. Check essential hospital supplies and staffing plans for the expected rise.

**Answer:** Only II and III follow.

### Q7 — Examination administration

Candidates complain that several questions may be outside the notified syllabus. The paper, syllabus and expert panel are available, but the claim is not verified.

I. Appoint a subject panel to compare disputed questions with the notified syllabus.  
II. Cancel the examination immediately before the disputed questions are checked.  
III. If a material syllabus error is confirmed, apply the approved corrective remedy, including re-examination if necessary.

**Answer:** Only I and III follow.

### Q8 — Recruitment / public administration

A recruitment drive experienced dangerous crowding and one serious injury. The cause is not yet established and more candidates are scheduled over the next two days.

I. Suspend all officials immediately before reviewing records and arrangements.  
II. Examine the crowd-control arrangements, entry records and sequence of events promptly.  
III. Stagger the remaining reporting schedule and adjust the entry layout before the next candidates arrive.

**Answer:** Only II and III follow.

### Q9 — Crowd management

A city expects an unusually large festival crowd in a compact market area. Entry routes are limited and police and medical teams are available.

I. Monitor entry and temporarily slow or redirect arrivals when safe capacity is reached.  
II. Strengthen crowd and traffic management at main access points.  
III. Alert nearby emergency medical teams and keep emergency access lanes clear.

**Answer:** I, II and III follow.

### Q10 — Transport disruption

Dense fog is causing repeated air and rail delays across a region. Operators can issue live updates, but there is no basis for assuming every service must stop for the whole day.

I. Suspend all air and rail services in the region for the entire day regardless of later improvement.  
II. Give passengers timely warnings and advise them to check current service status.  
III. Treat immediate installation of new navigation equipment everywhere as the first response to today's disruption.

**Answer:** Only II follows.

## Review checklist

- Does `Either I or II` appear only when both courses are individually sensible but mutually exclusive as a pair?
- Are ordinary independent courses prevented from accidentally becoming `Either`?
- Do three-action questions judge I/II/III independently rather than relying on memorised combination codes?
- Are combination options plausible and non-predictive?
- Are stems concise and exam-like?
- Are explanations simple enough for beginners?
- Does CP008 remain presentation expansion rather than a new semantic QL?

**CP008 is not frozen until explicit human approval.**
