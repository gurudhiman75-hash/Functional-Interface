## Final human-review defects found and remediated

### 1. CP-003 — ambiguous pump distractor

A probable-cause item for falling supply-line pressure offered both a stopped main pump and a pressure-control valve stuck partly closed on the **main line**. Both were defensible from the learner-visible observation.

Remediation:

- the distractor is now a pressure-control valve on a **small side branch**;
- its scope is explicitly insufficient for the wider supply-line pressure fall;
- `cp003-editorial-safety.test.ts` permanently rejects a return to the ambiguous main-line wording.

Result: **PASS**.

### 2. CP-007 — decisive evidence hidden from learner

False-causation items previously displayed only the two outcomes while the explanation used two independent causes hidden in canonical metadata. That made `common cause` versus `association/no causation` underdetermined from the stem.

Remediation:

- the reviewed CP-007 layer now exposes the two independent causal facts as learner-visible additional information;
- both effects and both supported causes are visible;
- locale parity is retained;
- `cp007-visible-evidence.test.ts` verifies that decisive evidence never becomes hidden again.

Result: **PASS**.

### 3. CP-009 — answer-leading explicit chain cue

Some relation-type questions stated that the information established `P → Q → R → S` and then asked whether P was a direct or indirect cause of S. This made the intended inference too mechanical.

Remediation:

- the explicit arrow-chain sentence is removed from reviewed relation-type items;
- all four event statements remain visible;
- learners infer the causal relationship from the event facts;
- CP-009 QA rejects any reappearance of the explicit answer-leading cue.

Result: **PASS**.
