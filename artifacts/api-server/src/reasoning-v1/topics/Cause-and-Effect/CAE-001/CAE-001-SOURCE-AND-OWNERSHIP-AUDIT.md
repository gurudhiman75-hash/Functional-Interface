These links establish discovery direction only. They are not copied into the runtime, and they do not alone authorise public release. Before freeze, CAE-001 needs a dated exam/source ledger, source-pattern census, scenario saturation audit, and human editorial approval.

## Current review boundary

The graph validates causal structure, unique answers, temporal order, and locale parity. It cannot by itself establish that a real-world claim is factually true or that a natural-language rendering is idiomatic enough for a final exam corpus. CAE-001 therefore remains deterministic Question Studio review content only.
