# NS-PRM-001 Difficulty Framework

## Status

Design package only.

## Easy

Characteristics:

- Prime Check
- Small Ranges
- Small Prime Positions

Approved CP emphasis:

- CP-001
- CP-002 with small ranges
- CP-003 with small ranges
- CP-004 with small ranges
- CP-006 with small numbers
- CP-007 with small numbers
- CP-008 with positions 1-25

## Medium

Characteristics:

- Prime Counting
- Prime Search
- Moderate Ranges

Approved CP emphasis:

- CP-002
- CP-003
- CP-004
- CP-005 with moderate ranges
- CP-006
- CP-007
- CP-008 with positions 26-100

## Hard

Characteristics:

- Large Ranges
- Prime Summation
- Large Prime Positions

Approved CP emphasis:

- CP-002 with large ranges
- CP-005
- CP-006 with larger numbers
- CP-007 with larger numbers
- CP-008 with positions 101-500

## Distribution Target

- Easy: 40%
- Medium: 40%
- Hard: 20%

## Implementation Gate

Difficulty bands are human-owned. Future runtime may load, validate, register, enforce, and audit these bands after approval.
