# NS-SURD-001 Canonical Problems

This package defines exactly eight canonical problems (CPs) for Surds and Rationalization.

## CP01: Simplification By Extracting Perfect Powers
**Description:** Convert a surd into its simplest form by extracting the largest possible perfect square (for square roots) or perfect cube (for cube roots) from the radicand.
- **Topology:** $\sqrt{n} \to a\sqrt{b}$ or $\sqrt[3]{n} \to a\sqrt[3]{b}$.
- **Method:** Prime factorization or finding the largest perfect power factor.

## CP02: Addition And Subtraction Of Like Surds
**Description:** Simplify an expression involving the sum or difference of surds by identifying and combining like terms.
- **Topology:** $a\sqrt{n} \pm b\sqrt{n}$ or expressions that require simplification before combining (e.g., $\sqrt{8} + \sqrt{18}$).
- **Method:** Simplify all surds first, then combine coefficients of terms with identical radicals.

## CP03: Multiplication And Division Of Surds
**Description:** Multiply or divide surds and simplify the resulting expression.
- **Topology:** $\sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$ or $\frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} = \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}$.
- **Method:** Combine under a single radical sign if indices match, then simplify.

## CP04: Mixed Surd Expressions
**Description:** Simplify multi-step expressions involving multiple arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division) on surds.
- **Topology:** $a\sqrt{n} + b\sqrt{m} \times \sqrt{k}$ or $\frac{\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{c}}$.
- **Method:** Apply order of operations (BODMAS), simplify individual surds, and collect like terms.

## CP05: Comparison And Ordering Of Surds
**Description:** Determine which of two or more surds is larger, or arrange a set of surds in ascending/descending order.
- **Topology:** Compare $a\sqrt{b}$ and $c\sqrt{d}$, or order $\{\sqrt{2}, \sqrt[3]{3}, \sqrt[4]{4}\}$.
- **Method:** Square/cube the values to remove radicals, or bring coefficients inside the radical.

## CP06: Rationalization Of Monomial Denominators
**Description:** Remove a single-term radical from the denominator of a fraction.
- **Topology:** $\frac{k}{\sqrt{n}}$ or $\frac{k}{a\sqrt{n}}$.
- **Method:** Multiply numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{n}$.

## CP07: Rationalization Of Binomial Denominators
**Description:** Remove a two-term radical expression from the denominator of a fraction using conjugates.
- **Topology:** $\frac{k}{a \pm \sqrt{b}}$ or $\frac{k}{\sqrt{a} \pm \sqrt{b}}$.
- **Method:** Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator.

## CP08: Surd Identity Evaluation
**Description:** Use algebraic identities to simplify or evaluate surd expressions.
- **Topology:** $(\sqrt{a} \pm \sqrt{b})^2$ or $(\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b})(\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b})$.
- **Method:** Recognize and apply identities like $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ and $(a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2$.
