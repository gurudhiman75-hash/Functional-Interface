# NS-SURD-001 Difficulty Framework

Difficulty is determined by the number of steps required, the magnitude of radicands, the root index, and the presence of fractions.

## Easy
- Single-step simplification of square roots with small radicands ($< 100$).
- Addition/subtraction of surds where the radical part is already identical.
- Direct multiplication of two simple square roots.
- Rationalizing a denominator with a single small prime radicand (e.g., $1/\sqrt{2}$).

## Medium
- Multi-step simplification involving larger radicands or cube roots.
- Addition/subtraction requiring simplification of all terms first (e.g., $\sqrt{8} + \sqrt{50} - \sqrt{18}$).
- Mixed multiplication and division.
- Rationalizing binomial denominators with integer terms (e.g., $1/(2+\sqrt{3})$).
- Comparing two surds with coefficients.

## Hard
- Complex mixed expressions with multiple operations and fractions.
- Rationalizing binomial denominators where both terms are radicals (e.g., $1/(\sqrt{5}-\sqrt{2})$).
- Evaluating identities involving fractional coefficients or larger surds.
- Ordering more than three surds with different root indices (requires finding LCM of indices).
- Multi-step problems where the final answer requires significant arithmetic simplification.
