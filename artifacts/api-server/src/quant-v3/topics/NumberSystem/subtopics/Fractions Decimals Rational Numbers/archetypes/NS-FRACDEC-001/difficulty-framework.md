# Difficulty Framework

## Easy

- Proper fractions with small denominators.
- Terminating decimals with one or two decimal places.
- Two-term arithmetic expressions.
- Comparison of two values.
- HCF/LCM of two simple fractions.
- Pure recurring decimals with one repeated digit.

## Medium

- Improper and mixed fraction conversion.
- Three-term arithmetic expressions.
- Decimal and fraction mixed expressions.
- Ordering three or four rational values.
- Fractions that reduce before decimal testing.
- Mixed recurring decimals.
- HCF/LCM of three fractions.

## Hard

- Large denominators.
- Multiple operations with mixed fractions and decimals.
- Close rational comparisons.
- Recurring decimals with multi-digit repetends.
- HCF/LCM of fractions requiring simplification first.
- HCF/LCM of fractions requiring conversion before formula use.

## Difficulty Drivers

- Number of rational values.
- Denominator size.
- Whether simplification is required before applying the main rule.
- Decimal-place count.
- Recurring-block length.
- Whether the expression mixes fractions, decimals and mixed fractions.
- Whether the final output is a simplified fraction, converted fraction, evaluated value, ordered list, decimal nature or HCF/LCM result.
