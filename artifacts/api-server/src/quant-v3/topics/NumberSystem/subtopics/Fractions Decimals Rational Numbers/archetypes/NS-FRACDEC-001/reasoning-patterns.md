# Reasoning Patterns

## RP-001: HCF Fraction Reduction

Divide numerator and denominator by their HCF to reduce a fraction to lowest terms.

Used by: CP-001 and as support in CP-003, CP-006, CP-009.

## RP-002: Quotient-Remainder Fraction Conversion

Use quotient and remainder to convert an improper fraction into a mixed fraction, or expand the whole-number component to convert a mixed fraction into an improper fraction.

Used by: CP-002.

## RP-003: Common Denominator Arithmetic

Use common denominators for addition/subtraction and numerator-denominator multiplication rules for multiplication/division.

Used by: CP-003.

## RP-004: Decimal Place Value Arithmetic

Align decimal places or convert decimals into fractions before evaluation.

Used by: CP-003 and CP-006.

## RP-005: Common Basis Comparison

Convert rational values to a common denominator or decimal approximation sufficient for ordering.

Used by: CP-004.

## RP-006: Fraction To Decimal Division

Divide numerator by denominator and identify terminating or recurring output.

Used by: CP-005.

## RP-007: Decimal Place Value To Fraction

Convert a terminating decimal into a denominator that is a power of 10, then simplify.

Used by: CP-006.

## RP-008: Recurring Decimal Elimination

Use powers of 10 to shift recurring blocks, subtract equations and solve for the fraction.

Used by: CP-007.

## RP-009: Denominator Prime-Factor Test

Reduce the fraction and check whether the denominator has only prime factors 2 and 5.

Used by: CP-008.

## RP-010: Fraction HCF/LCM Decomposition

For fractions in simplest form:

- HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators.
- LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators.

Used by: CP-009.
