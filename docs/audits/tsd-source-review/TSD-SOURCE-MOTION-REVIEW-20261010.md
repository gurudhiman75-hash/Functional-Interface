## TSD-SOURCE-VECTOR · en

Source: TASTE-Q78

A boat can travel at 20 km/h in still water. A river flows at 6 km/h. The boat is steered to land directly opposite its starting point. What is its speed across the river?

A. 14 km/h
B. 26 km/h
C. 2√91 km/h
D. 2√109 km/h

**Answer: C. 2√91 km/h**

1. The upstream component of the boat's velocity must cancel the 6 km/h current.
2. Across-river speed²=20²−6²=364; speed=2√91 km/h.

## TSD-SOURCE-BOAT-RATIO · en

Source: TASTE-Q107

A boat makes a round trip between two points on a riverbank. Doubling its speed in still water reduces the total travel time by 75%; the current remains unchanged. What is the ratio of its original speed in still water to the current speed?

A. √7:2
B. 2√5:3
C. 3:2
D. √6:√2

**Answer: A. √7:2**

1. Let the original boat speed be u and current speed c. For one-way distance D, T=2 Du/(u²−c²).
2. New time/original time=2(u²−c²)/(4 u²−c²)=1/4.
3. 4 u²=7 c², so u:c=√7:2.

Two sounds are produced 12 minutes apart at the same place. A passenger on a train approaching that place hears them 11 minutes 30 seconds apart. Sound travels at 330 m/s. What is the train's speed?

A. 110/23 m/s
B. 330/7 m/s
C. 660/23 m/s
D. 330/23 m/s

**Answer: D. 330/23 m/s**

1. The sound waves are 330×720 m apart. Their closing speed with the train is 330+v.
2. (330+v)×690=330×720; v=330×30/690=330/23 m/s.

Two cars 12 km apart approach each other at 60 km/h each. A bird flies at 120 km/h from the first car to the second, back to the first, and then to the second again. How far has the bird flown when it reaches the second car for the second time?

A. 100/9 km
B. 12 km
C. 104/9 km
D. 32/3 km

**Answer: C. 104/9 km**

1. The bird closes each gap at 120+60=180 km/h. The gaps before its three flights are 12,4 and 4/3 km; flight times are 1/15,1/45 and 1/135 hours.
